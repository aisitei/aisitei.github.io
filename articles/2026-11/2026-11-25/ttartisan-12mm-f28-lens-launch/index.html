주요 특징

고성능 STM 스테핑 모터를 탑재하여 눈동자 AF, 얼굴 AF 등 기능을 지원하며 부드러운 자동 초점 경험을 제공합니다. 336g의 가벼운 디자인으로 브리딩 현상을 최적화했으며, 클릭 스톱 방식의 조리개 링을 갖추고 있습니다. 최소 초점 거리는 0.18m이며, 필터 구경은 82mm입니다.

호환성 및 기타 기능

이 렌즈는 Sony E, Nikon Z, Panasonic L 마운트 버전으로 제공됩니다. Type-C 인터페이스가 있는 후면 캡이 장착되어 있어 향후 펌웨어 업그레이드를 지원합니다.

Introduction

Brightin Star today launched its first full-frame ultra-wide automatic focus lens, the AF 12mm F2.8, priced at ¥2,780.

Lens Specifications

  • Angle of View: 122°
  • Optical Structure: 10 groups, 16 elements
  • Special Lenses: 2 aspherical lenses, 3 extra-low dispersion (ED) lenses
  • Motor: High-performance STM stepping motor
  • Weight: 336g
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.18m
  • Filter Diameter: 82mm
  • Aperture Ring: Click-stop mechanism

Key Features

This lens offers an impressive visual effect and excellent image quality with its wide 122° angle of view. It features a sophisticated optical structure of 10 groups and 16 elements, including two aspherical lenses and three extra-low dispersion (ED) lenses, designed to reproduce true colors and delicate details.

Equipped with a high-performance STM stepping motor, the lens supports advanced functions such as eye AF and face AF, providing a smooth automatic focusing experience. Its lightweight 336g design optimizes carrying comfort, and it includes a click-stop aperture ring. Furthermore, it boasts a minimum focus distance of 0.18m and an 82mm filter diameter.

Compatibility and Functionality

The lens is available in versions for the Sony E, Nikon Z, and Panasonic L mounts. It features a rear cap with a Type-C interface, ensuring support for future firmware upgrades.

Summary

The Brightin Star AF 12mm F2.8 delivers outstanding image quality through its ultra-wide 122° angle of view and advanced optical design. Its combination of a lightweight body, smooth focusing performance via the STM motor, and versatile compatibility across Sony E, Nikon Z, and Panasonic L mounts makes it highly usable. The inclusion of a Type-C interface further enhances its longevity by supporting future firmware upgrades.
