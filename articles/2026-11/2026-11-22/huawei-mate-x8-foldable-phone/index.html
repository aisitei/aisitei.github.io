Key Features

In addition to general functions, the Huawei Mate X8 incorporates a virtual cursor control area located in the bottom right corner of the screen. Users can slide or tap this designated area to control the on-screen cursor. This feature allows for one-handed operation from any location on the display—such as the top, left, or upper right corners—that might otherwise be difficult to reach.
