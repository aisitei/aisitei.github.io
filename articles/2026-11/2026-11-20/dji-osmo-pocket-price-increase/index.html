DJI Announces Price Hikes for Key Products

In an official statement, DJI announced that it has implemented price increases following careful evaluation due to continuous and significant rises in the costs of core components such as semiconductors and storage. The new pricing structure took effect starting at 00:00 on November 18, 2026. Multiple kits for the Osmo Pocket 4, Osmo Pocket 4P, and Osmo Nano have seen price adjustments, with increases reaching up to 560 yuan (approximately $83).

DJI did not disclose a list of all affected products beforehand, advising customers to refer to the official store page for accurate pricing.
