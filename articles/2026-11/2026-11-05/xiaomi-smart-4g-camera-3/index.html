카메라

  • 양방향 음성: 양방향 실시간 음성 지원, 언제든지 가족과 소통 가능

수평 360° + 수직 109°의 넓은 시야각을 지원하여 광범위한 화면을 촬영하고 사각지대를 줄여줍니다. 가정, 유아, 반려동물 등 다양한 시나리오 감시를 지원하며, 시나리오별 AI 감지 기능을 탑재하여 상황 발생 시 즉시 메시지를 푸시합니다. 양방향 실시간 음성 통화를 지원하여 언제든지 가족과 소통할 수 있습니다.

저장 및 연결성

AOR 올데이 초절약 녹화 기술은 "움직임과 정지 구분, 두 마리 토끼를 잡는" 녹화 전략을 구현하여 하루 종일 유효한 화면을 보존하는 동시에 불필요한 영상이 microSD 카드 저장 공간을 차지하는 것을 줄여 스마트하게 저장 공간을 절약합니다.

Product Introduction

The Xiaomi Smart 4G Camera 3 has begun crowdfunding on JD.com, with a listed price of 199 Yuan.

This new product features built-in slots for both China Telecom and China Unicom SIM cards, preventing signal loss due to single carrier incompatibility and ensuring smooth switching to the best available signal. It offers free 4G data and is not restricted by Wi-Fi or wired networks.

Camera Specifications

  • Aperture: f/1.6
  • Pixel Count: 5 Megapixels
  • Resolution: 3K Ultra HD Video
  • Sensor: Built-in high-sensitivity image sensor
  • Night Vision: Equipped with multiple 940nm infrared auxiliary lights

It is equipped with a large aperture lens of f/1.6, which effectively suppresses screen noise due to high light intake, providing clear and clean footage while achieving 3K Ultra HD video. Furthermore, it includes a high-sensitivity image sensor and multiple 940nm infrared auxiliary lights.

Key Features

  • Field of View: Horizontal 360° + Vertical 109°
  • Monitoring Functionality: Supports various scenarios such as homes, infants, and pets.
  • AI Detection: Equipped with AI detection for specific scenarios, pushing immediate messages when an event occurs.
  • Two-Way Audio: Supports two-way real-time voice communication, allowing connection with family at any time.

It supports a wide field of view of 360° horizontally and 109° vertically, enabling the capture of extensive areas and reducing blind spots. It supports monitoring for various scenarios—such as homes, infants, and pets—and features scenario-specific AI detection that pushes immediate messages when an event is detected. Additionally, it supports two-way real-time voice calls, allowing users to communicate with family anytime.

Storage and Connectivity

The AOR All-Day Ultra-Saving Recording technology implements a recording strategy that "catches both movement and stillness," preserving valid footage throughout the day while minimizing unnecessary video data occupying microSD card storage space for smart storage efficiency.

Furthermore, this new product supports Xiaomi HyperOS Smart Connection, enabling seamless integration with other Mijia products to achieve intelligent connectivity. It supports family sharing and device management, allowing users to view the camera feed on various smart devices such as phones, tablets, and Xiaomi TVs.

Summary

The Xiaomi Smart 4G Camera 3 offers stable connectivity through dual SIM card support and delivers clear video using a 3K resolution and f/1.6 large aperture lens. Its combination of 360-degree field of view and AI detection enables effective monitoring in diverse environments, while the AOR recording technology enhances storage space efficiency.
