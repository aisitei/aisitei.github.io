Design and Display

The Huawei Star OSTA O1 Pro measures 6.68mm in thickness and weighs approximately 200g. It is available in three colors: Star Purple, Star Cloud White, and Starlie Black.

It features a 6.39-inch 16:9.5 ratio 1.5K LTPS bar type display, characterized by extremely thin 4-sided bezels of 1.05mm.

Camera

The rear camera system is equipped with a 200Mp 1/1.28-inch RYYB main camera, a 50Mp 3.7x RYYB periscope telephoto camera, and the Hongfeng lens.

Performance and Battery

The device is powered by the Kirin 9030S processor and offers options up to 16GB RAM and 1TB storage. It features a built-in 7000mAh battery and supports both 66W wired charging and wireless charging.

Overview

The Huawei Star OSTA O1 Pro combines a thin, light design with a 1.5K LTPS bar type display. It is powered by the Kirin 9030S processor and large-capacity battery, while also featuring a powerful camera system that includes a high-resolution RYYB main camera and a periscope telephoto lens.

Summary

The Huawei Star OSTA O1 Pro is positioned as a premium device with a thin 6.68mm body and a 1.5K LTPS display. It is built around the Kirin 9030S processor, offering up to 1TB of storage, and includes a large 7000mAh battery for extended use. Its camera system is highlighted by a powerful setup featuring a 200Mp RYYB main lens and a periscope telephoto camera.
