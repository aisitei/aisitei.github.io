Design and Display

The Huawei Starburst OSTA O1 Pro features a unique wide bar-type design. It has a thickness of 6.68mm, weighs approximately 200g, and is available in three colors: Starburst Purple, Nebula White, and Starry Sky Black.

The display is equipped with a 6.39-inch 16:9.5 ratio 1.5K LTPS bar-type panel, boasting a screen-to-body ratio of 96.1%. It features extremely thin all-around bezels measuring 1.05mm.

Camera

The rear camera system includes a 200Mp 1/1.28-inch RYYB main camera, a 50Mp 3.7x RYYB periscope telephoto camera, and the Red Maple lens.

Performance and Battery

It is powered by the Kirin 9030S processor, offering options up to 16GB RAM and 1TB storage. The phone features a large 7000mAh battery, supporting 66W wired charging and wireless charging.

This device is the first to feature HarmonyOS 7 AI Pioneer Edition, positioning it as the first HarmonyOS phone born for AI. It provides new AI interactions and system-level proactive AI intelligence from the moment the screen is turned on, acting as a long-term companion that grows with the user.
