Redmi M100 Pro 가격 및 출시

샤오미는 Redmi M100 Pro 12GB + 256GB 모델을 중국에서 2,499위안(약 350달러)에 판매하고 있습니다. 색상은 블랙, 화이트, 시안, 퍼플로 제공되며, 퍼플 색상에는 가죽 느낌의 옵션도 포함됩니다. M 시리즈 Redmi 스마트폰인 만큼, 이 모델은 중국 내수용으로만 출시될 것으로 예상됩니다.

하드웨어 측면에서는 Dimensity 7300-Ultra를 탑재한 Redmi Note 14 Pro의 리브랜딩 모델로 보입니다. 이는 M100과 글로벌 Redmi 17 5G의 전략과 동일합니다. 또한, 이전 소프트웨어 데이터베이스에서 M100 Pro가 'malachite Note 14 Pro' 코드명과 연결되었던 정보와도 일치합니다. 중국에서 M 시리즈 리브랜딩 모델을 구매하시겠습니까, 아니면 글로벌 Note 브랜드를 고수하시겠습니까?

Redmi M100 Pro发布：主要规格与特点

数月前，Redmi发布了M系列智能手机 Redmi M100。现在，该机型以 Redmi M100 Pro的名称回归。这款中端智能手机搭载MediaTek Dimensity 7300-Ultra芯片组和6.67英寸1.5K OLED屏幕，目前已在小米中国商店发售。其规格与Redmi在中国大陆以外地区销售的硬件高度一致。

Redmi M100 Pro详细配置与功能

  • 显示屏：6.67英寸曲面OLED，分辨率2712 × 1220，最大刷新率120Hz，局部峰值亮度高达3,000尼特，采用Gorilla Glass Victus 2保护。
  • 尺寸与重量：机身厚度约8.2mm，重量190g。
  • 防水/防尘等级：IP68级。
  • 操作系统：HyperOS。
  • 音频系统：立体声扬声器。
  • 触觉反馈：X轴振动马达。
  • 安全功能：屏幕内置指纹识别。
  • 其他功能：红外遥控发射器（IR Blaster）、NFC。
  • 处理器：MediaTek Dimensity 7300-Ultra。
  • 内存（RAM）：12GB LPDDR4X。
  • 存储空间：256GB UFS 2.2。
  • 后置摄像头：
    • 主摄：5000万像素，带OIS（光学防抖）。
    • 超广角：800万像素。
    • 微距：200万像素。
  • 前置摄像头：2000万像素。
  • 电池容量：5,500mAh。
  • 充电支持：45W有线快充。

Redmi M100 Pro价格与上市信息

小米目前在中国大陆以2,499元人民币（约合350美元）的价格销售 Redmi M100 Pro 12GB + 256GB版本。该机型提供黑色、白色、青色和紫色四种颜色，其中紫色版本还包含皮革质感可选配件。作为M系列Redmi智能手机，这款机型预计仅在中国大陆市场发售。

从硬件角度看，它被认为是 Redmi Note 14 Pro的重新品牌化型号，核心搭载了Dimensity 7300-Ultra芯片组。这一策略与M100以及全球Redmi 17 5G的战略是相同的。此外，根据此前的软件数据库信息，M100 Pro曾被关联到代号“malachite Note 14 Pro”，这也印证了其定位。

总结

Redmi M100 Pro是一款中端智能手机，搭载MediaTek Dimensity 7300-Ultra芯片组和1.5K OLED显示屏。它具备出色的配置，包括5,500mAh大电池、45W快充以及一套强大的摄像头系统。该机型被定位为Redmi Note 14 Pro的重新品牌化版本，主要面向中国大陆市场销售。

Redmi M100 Pro Launch: Key Specifications and Features

A few months ago, Redmi unveiled the Redmi M100, a smartphone in its M series. Now, it has returned with another model called the Redmi M100 Pro. This mid-range smartphone is already on sale at the Xiaomi China Store, featuring the MediaTek Dimensity 7300-Ultra chipset and a 6.67-inch 1.5K OLED display. Its specifications are nearly identical to hardware that Redmi is already selling in regions outside of China.

Redmi M100 Pro Specs and Features

  • Display: 6.67-inch curved OLED, 2712 × 1220 resolution, up to 120Hz refresh rate, up to 3,000 nits local peak brightness, Gorilla Glass Victus 2
  • Dimensions and Weight: Approximately 8.2mm thick, 190g
  • Water/Dust Resistance: IP68 rating
  • Operating System: HyperOS
  • Audio: Stereo speakers
  • Haptics: X-axis vibration motor
  • Security: In-display fingerprint recognition
  • Other Features: IR blaster, NFC
  • Processor: MediaTek Dimensity 7300-Ultra
  • RAM: 12GB LPDDR4X
  • Storage: 256GB UFS 2.2
  • Rear Camera:
    • Main: 50MP, OIS (Optical Image Stabilization)
    • Ultra-wide: 8MP
    • Macro: 2MP
  • Front Camera: 20MP
  • Battery: 5,500mAh
  • Charging: 45W wired charging

Redmi M100 Pro Pricing and Availability

Xiaomi is selling the Redmi M100 Pro 12GB + 256GB model in China for ¥2,499 (approximately $350 USD). It is available in Black, White, Cyan, and Purple; the Purple color option includes a leather-feel finish. As an M series Redmi smartphone, this model is expected to be launched exclusively for the Chinese domestic market.

From a hardware perspective, it appears to be a rebranded version of the Redmi Note 14 Pro, which features the Dimensity 7300-Ultra. This strategy aligns with the approach taken by the M100 and the global Redmi 17 5G. Furthermore, this matches information from previous software databases that linked the M100 Pro to the codename 'malachite Note 14 Pro'. The question remains: should consumers buy the M series rebranded model in China, or stick with the global Note brand?

Summary

The Redmi M100 Pro is a mid-range smartphone powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 7300-Ultra chipset and featuring a 1.5K OLED display. It offers robust specifications, including excellent camera hardware, a large battery, and fast charging capabilities. The device is positioned as a rebranded model of the Redmi Note 14 Pro and will be launched for the Chinese domestic market.
