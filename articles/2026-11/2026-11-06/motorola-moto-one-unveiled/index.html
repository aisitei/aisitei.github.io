Overview

According to a report from IT之家 on November 5th, the IT media outlet GSMArena reported yesterday (November 4th) that Motorola is preparing a new smartphone in its One series, based on the latest certification information from the Wireless Power Consortium (WPC).

The certification details show that this model passed Qi certification under the name "moto one," with the model number XT2771-1. It supports up to 15W of Qi wireless charging. While the certification page revealed the device's exterior design for the first time, the official final name and release information have not yet been confirmed.

Specifications

  • Model Name: XT2771-1
  • Geekbench Test Records: 4 tests
  • Expected Processor: Qualcomm 5th Gen Snapdragon 8
  • RAM: 12GB
  • Operating System: Android 16
  • Highest Geekbench Score:
    • Single Core: 2,109 points
    • Multi Core: 8,586 points

IT之家 noted that since the test model may not have reached mass production optimization, the performance of the actual released product could be even better. They included relevant screenshots with their report.

Summary

Motorola has received Qi wireless charging certification for a model named XT2771-1 (or "moto one"), supporting up to 15W wireless charging. The device is expected to feature the Qualcomm 5th Gen Snapdragon 8 chipset, 12GB of RAM, and run on Android 16. While initial Geekbench test scores were reported, the article cautions that these performance figures are preliminary and the final product may see further enhancements.
