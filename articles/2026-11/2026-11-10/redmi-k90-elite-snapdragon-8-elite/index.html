Design and Display

The Redmi K90 至尊版 is available in Shadow Black, Space Silver, and Sky Blue colors, and has received IP68/IP69 ratings.

It features a 6.83-inch M10 material 1.5K resolution 165Hz bar-type display (with a maximum brightness of 3500 nits) and incorporates large R corner design. The smartphone uses an anodized aluminum CNC middle frame to emphasize its flagship feel.

Performance

The device is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 至尊版 (Extreme Edition) processor and includes a D2 independent display chip. It supports LPDDR5X Ultra memory and UFS 4.1 flash storage, and features an integrated low-noise (32dB) active cooling fan.

The entire device is certified with IP66/IP68/IP69 dustproof and waterproof ratings, while the dedicated fan unit supports IPX8/IPX9 dustproofing and waterproofing.

Battery and Other Features

This smartphone is equipped with an 8550mAh single-cell Xiaomi Jinxiang battery. It supports 100W wired charging and 22.5W wireless reverse charging, and provides direct motherboard power supply during gaming sessions. Additional features include Bose tuned 1115X symmetric dual speakers, a 0815 X-axis haptic motor, 3D ultrasonic fingerprint recognition, landscape screen network optimization, and specialized game sound effects.
