소개

이 기기는 Unisoc T9100 프로세서를 탑재하고 8GB RAM과 256GB 저장 공간을 제공합니다. 6000mAh 배터리(33W 유선 충전 지원)를 내장하고 있으며, Android 16 시스템을 기반으로 합니다.

Introduction

According to sources on IT Home reported by smashx_60 on October 19, specifications for the HMD Vibe 2 Max have been revealed. This device is aimed at the mid-range market and is expected to launch in overseas markets soon.

Design and Display

The phone will be available in three colors: black, red, and green, and supports an IP64 rating. The front features a 6.78-inch FHD+ 120Hz IPS LCD panel (with a maximum brightness of 1500 nits) and is equipped with a 50MP selfie camera. The rear cameras include a 108MP main camera and an 8MP ultra-wide-angle camera.

Performance and Battery

It is powered by the Unisoc T9100 processor, offering 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage. The device features a large 6000mAh battery (supporting 33W wired charging) and runs on Android 16.

Overview

The HMD Vibe 2 Max is positioned as a mid-range smartphone, featuring the Unisoc T9100 processor, 8GB of RAM, and a 6.78-inch FHD+ 120Hz display with a 108MP main camera. It boasts a massive 6000mAh battery with support for 33W fast charging, along with an IP64 dust and water resistance rating.

Summary

The HMD Vibe 2 Max is revealed as a mid-range smartphone targeting global markets. Key specifications include the Unisoc T9100 processor, 8GB RAM, and a large 6000mAh battery with 33W fast charging support. It features a 6.78-inch FHD+ 120Hz display, a 108MP main camera setup, and an IP64 rating.
