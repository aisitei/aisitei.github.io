Design

The device is equipped with the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 至尊版 processor and a D2 independent display chip, supporting LPDDR5X Ultra memory and UFS 4.1 flash storage. Additionally, it includes a low-noise (32dB) active cooling fan.
