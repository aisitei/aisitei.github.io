Product Overview

The Sony ECM-AX10 smartphone spatial audio microphone is currently available on JD.com for 1,499 RMB. This new product connects to smartphones via a Type-C interface and provides features for capturing and editing realistic spatial audio.

Design and Features

This device boasts a lightweight design of just 24g and a size optimized for smartphone use, allowing users to infuse video footage with genuine on-site realism. It is equipped with a built-in 6-axis motion sensor that detects the microphone's orientation and automatically adjusts between portrait and landscape modes.

Audio Performance

The ECM-AX10 features four omnidirectional microphone pickup heads, ensuring uniform capture of details from deep bass to high treble. It encodes clear audio through advanced 3D sound image positioning. Furthermore, the inclusion of professional tuning parameters is designed to deliver "excellent sound right out of the box."

Accessories and Compatibility

The package includes various accessories such as a windscreen, cold shoe microphone stand, Type-C extension cable, and a pad. The main unit also features physical buttons that allow for quick switching between modes like Spatial Mode and Stereo Mode. It is compatible with the dedicated app, "Spatial Audio and Video Tool," enabling users to capture and edit immersive soundscapes anytime, anywhere.

Overall Review

The Sony ECM-AX10 offers high-quality spatial audio recording capabilities to smartphone users through its combination of a light 24g design and Type-C connectivity. Featuring four omnidirectional microphones and a 6-axis motion sensor, it captures immersive sound in diverse environments while providing convenient editing experiences via its dedicated application.
