에이서 Sospiro A70: 듀얼 디스플레이 5G 스마트폰 공개

지난 7월, 에이서는 후면에 보조 스크린을 탑재한 듀얼 디스플레이 스마트폰 Sospiro A15를 공개했습니다. 이번에 에이서의 라틴 아메리카(LATAM) 지부에서 유사한 디자인의 또 다른 스마트폰을 발표했습니다. 4G LTE 모델이었던 A15와 달리, A70은 5G 연결을 지원합니다. Sospiro A70에 대해 알아야 할 모든 것을 소개합니다.

에이서 Sospiro A70 사양 및 기능

  • 후면 카메라: 6400만 화소 (AI 센서, LED 플래시 포함)

Acer Sospiro A70：双显示屏 5G 智能手机发布

去年 7 月，Acer 发布了搭载后置辅助屏幕的双显示屏智能手机 Sospiro A15。此次，Acer 的拉丁美洲（LATAM）分部宣布推出另一款具有相似设计的智能手机。与此前定位为 4G LTE 模型的 A15 不同，A70 支持 5G 连接。本文将为您介绍关于 Sospiro A70 的所有细节。

Acer Sospiro A70 规格和功能

Acer Sospiro A70 配备了一块 6.67 英寸 HD+ IPS 显示屏，分辨率为 720 x 1600 像素，支持 120Hz 刷新率，并采用水滴刘海设计。其后置辅助显示屏尺寸为 1.88 英寸，分辨率为 240 x 284 像素，使用了 TFT 技术。

该双显示屏设计在拍照方面非常实用：用户可以通过这个小屏幕实时预览使用后置主摄像头拍摄的照片，从而更好地构图和自拍。相机规格如下：

  • 后置摄像头：6400 万像素（包含 AI 传感器、LED 闪光灯）
  • 前置摄像头：1600 万像素（包含专用闪光灯）

值得注意的是，后置辅助显示屏帮助用户使用高分辨率的主摄像头来构图自拍，而非依赖于前置传感器。

该智能手机运行 Android 16 系统，并由支持 5G 的 Unisoc T8200 芯片驱动。它配备了 8GB RAM 和 256GB 内置存储空间，并通过虚拟 RAM 支持可额外增加高达 12GB，宣称总内存容量达到 20GB。此外，存储空间还可通过 microSDXC 卡扩展至最高 1TB。

A70 配备了 5,000mAh 电池并支持快速充电。连接选项包括 5G、NFC、Wi-Fi、蓝牙 5.2、GPS 和 3.5mm 耳机插孔。安全功能方面，集成了面部识别和侧边指纹传感器。设备尺寸为 165.95 x 77.1 x 8.35 毫米，重量为 210克，并提供 IP64 等级的防尘防水等级。

Acer Sospiro A70 定价与上市信息

截至目前，Acer 尚未公布 Sospiro A70 的售价。这款手机似乎主要瞄准拉丁美洲市场，当地的产品页面列出了红色、黑色和白色三种颜色选择。

总结

Acer Sospiro A70 是一款继承了后置辅助显示屏双屏设计的全新智能手机，并升级至支持 5G 连接。它最大的亮点在于能够利用高分辨率的后置主摄像头进行自拍构图，提供了独特的拍照体验。该机型搭载 Unisoc T8200 芯片组、充足的内存和存储空间，以及大容量 5,000mAh 电池等均衡配置。

Acer Sospiro A70: Dual Display 5G Smartphone Revealed

Last July, Acer unveiled the Sospiro A15, a dual-display smartphone featuring an auxiliary screen on the back. Now, Acer's Latin America (LATAM) division has announced another smartphone with a similar design. Unlike the A15, which was a 4G LTE model, the A70 supports 5G connectivity. Here is everything you need to know about the Sospiro A70.

Acer Sospiro A70 Specifications and Features

The Acer Sospiro A70 is equipped with a 6.67-inch HD+ IPS display, featuring a resolution of 720 x 1600 pixels, a 120Hz refresh rate, and a teardrop notch design. The auxiliary rear display measures 1.88 inches with a resolution of 240 x 284 pixels and utilizes TFT technology. This small screen allows users to preview themselves using the main rear camera for selfies, making the dual-display design useful for photography.

The camera specifications are as follows:

  • Rear Camera: 64 Megapixels (AI sensor, includes LED flash)
  • Front Camera: 16 Megapixels (includes dedicated flash)

The rear display helps users compose selfies using the high-resolution main camera instead of the front sensor.

This smartphone runs on Android 16 and is powered by the Unisoc T8200 chipset, supporting 5G. It offers 8GB of RAM and 256GB of internal storage, boasting a total advertised memory capacity of 20GB with virtual RAM support (up to an additional 12GB). Storage can be expanded up to 1TB using a microSDXC card.

It is fitted with a 5,000mAh battery and supports fast charging. Connectivity options include 5G, NFC, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.2, GPS, and a 3.5mm headphone jack. Security features include face recognition and a side fingerprint sensor. The device measures 165.95 x 77.1 x 8.35mm, weighs 210g, and provides IP64 dust and water resistance ratings.

Acer Sospiro A70 Pricing and Availability

Acer has not yet announced the price for the Sospiro A70. The phone appears to be targeted at the Latin American market, with local product pages listing three color options: red, black, and white.

Summary

The Acer Sospiro A70 is a new smartphone that builds upon the dual-screen design featuring an auxiliary rear display while adding 5G connectivity. It offers unique functionality allowing users to take selfies using the high-resolution rear camera. The device boasts solid specifications, including the Unisoc T8200 chipset, ample RAM and storage, and a large 5,000mAh battery.
