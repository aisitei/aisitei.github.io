Design

The K100 features a 6.83-inch bar-type display and is characterized by its extremely thin bezel. The display is encased in a 1.3mm metal frame, which provides a smooth and comfortable grip. The flat metal camera decor design ensures it does not protrude or interfere with the hand when held horizontally. It is available in three colors: Black, Snow Rock White, and Fluorescent Green.

Performance

The K100 is equipped with a powerful dual chipset system, featuring the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 flagship chip alongside support for LPDDR5X Ultra + UFS4.1 storage. The standard version introduces a dedicated D2 independent display chip for the first time, significantly reducing power consumption during gaming.

Display

This new device boasts a 6.83-inch 1.5K large bar-type display, supporting a high refresh rate of 165Hz, full brightness DC dimming, circular polarization, peak brightness of 3500nits, and Xiaomi Qing Shan Eye Care 4.0.
