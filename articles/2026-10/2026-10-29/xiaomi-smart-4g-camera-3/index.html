샤오미 스마트 4G 카메라 3 출시

샤오미가 중국에서 Smart 4G Camera 3를 발표했으며, 현재 샤오미 유핀(Xiaomi Youpin)에서 크라우드펀딩을 진행하고 있습니다. 이 카메라는 일반적인 가정용 보안 카메라와 달리, 내장된 SIM 카드와 무료 모바일 데이터 덕분에 Wi-Fi 연결 없이도 작동합니다.

이 카메라는 China Telecom 및 China Unicom의 SIM 카드를 함께 제공합니다. 샤오미는 추가 요금이나 숨겨진 데이터 요금이 없다고 밝혀, 차고, 상점, 여유 공간 등 라우터가 없는 장소에 설치하고도 원격으로 실시간 피드를 확인할 수 있습니다.

다만 한 가지 제약이 있습니다. 무료 데이터는 Xiaomi Home 앱을 통해서만 작동하며 다른 기기에서는 사용할 수 없습니다. 이는 내장 SIM 카드와 비디오 스트리밍을 위한 무료 데이터를 제공하는 Xiaomi Smart Outdoor Camera 4 4G와 동일합니다.

小米Smart 4G摄像头3发布

小米在中国宣布推出Smart 4G Camera 3。目前，该产品正在通过小米有品（Xiaomi Youpin）进行众筹销售。与传统的家用安全摄像头不同，这款摄像头内置了SIM卡并提供免费移动数据，因此即使没有Wi-Fi连接也能正常工作。

该摄像头同时支持China Telecom和China Unicom的SIM卡。小米强调，用户无需支付额外费用或隐藏的数据费，即可在车库、商店、空旷区域等没有路由器的地方安装设备，并远程查看实时视频流。

需要注意的是，免费数据只能通过Xiaomi Home应用使用，不能用于其他设备。这一点与提供内置SIM卡和免费视频流数据的Xiaomi Smart Outdoor Camera 4 4G保持一致。

Xiaomi Smart 4G Camera 3を発表

Xiaomiが中国でSmart 4G Camera 3を発表しました。現在、Xiaomi Youpinを通じてクラウドファンディングが行われています。このカメラは、一般的な家庭用セキュリティカメラとは異なり、内蔵SIMカードと無料のモバイルデータ通信を利用できるため、Wi-Fi接続なしでも動作します。

本製品にはChina TelecomおよびChina UnicomのSIMカードが付属します。Xiaomiによると、追加料金や隠れたデータ費用はかからず、ルーターがない場所（ガレージ、店舗、空きスペースなど）に設置しても、遠隔でリアルタイムフィードを確認することが可能です。

ただし、利用上の制約があります。無料データ通信はXiaomi Homeアプリ経由でのみ機能し、他のデバイスでは使用できません。これは、内蔵SIMカードとビデオストリーミング用の無料データを提供するXiaomi Smart Outdoor Camera 4 4Gと同様の仕様です。

Xiaomi Smart 4G Camera 3 Launched

Xiaomi has announced the Xiaomi Smart 4G Camera 3 in China, which is currently undergoing crowdfunding through Xiaomi Youpin. Unlike typical residential security cameras, this model operates without requiring a Wi-Fi connection, thanks to its built-in SIM card and free mobile data.

The camera comes bundled with SIM cards from both China Telecom and China Unicom. Xiaomi stated that there are no additional or hidden data charges, allowing users to monitor real-time feeds remotely even in locations without a router, such as garages, shops, or empty spaces.

However, there is one limitation: the free data only functions through the Xiaomi Home app and cannot be used with other devices. This feature mirrors the Xiaomi Smart Outdoor Camera 4 4G, which provides both an integrated SIM card and free data for video streaming.

Xiaomi Smart 4G Camera 3 Specifications

  • Resolution: 2880 x 1620 (5MP lens)
  • Aperture: f/1.6
  • Sensor: High-sensitivity sensor (enables color video capture even in very low light conditions)
  • Night Vision: 940nm infrared auxiliary lighting (supports night video recording when ambient light is minimal)
  • Rotation System: Dual motor pan-tilt system
  • Horizontal Rotation: 360°
  • Vertical Tilt: 109°
  • AI Detection Features: 5 types (including situations related to children and pets)
  • Voice Calling: Real-time two-way voice communication
  • Recording Functionality: Ultra-low power 24-hour recording, time-lapse shooting
  • Smart Home Integration: Connects with other smart home devices via Xiaomi HyperConnect
  • Remote Viewing: Allows remote feed checking from multiple devices, such as mobile phones.

For users who prefer a Wi-Fi connection, Xiaomi’s Smart Camera 4 supports 4K video and Wi-Fi 6, but it does not offer cellular connectivity.

Pricing and Availability

  • Official Launch Price: ¥269 (approximately $38)
  • Crowdfunding Start Date: November 4th
  • Crowdfunding Price: ¥199 (approximately $28)

Summary

The Xiaomi Smart 4G Camera 3 is an innovative home security camera capable of remote monitoring without Wi-Fi, utilizing a built-in SIM card and free mobile data. It features enhanced low-light performance with a 5MP lens, f/1.6 aperture, high-sensitivity sensor, and infrared lighting. The device boosts user convenience with comprehensive smart functions, including 360° rotation, 109° tilt, five types of AI detection, and two-way voice calling.
