新品发布：全画幅专业镜头

唯卓仕今日发布了其全画幅 Pro 系列的新成员——AF 35mm F1.4 Pro 镜头，售价为 3899 元。

兼容性与规格参数

  • 支持卡口：Sony FE、Nikon Z。
  • 镜片结构：共包含 11 组 16 片镜片。
  • 特殊镜片配置：包括 2 枚超高精度非球面镜片、3 枚超低色散镜片和 4 枚高折射率镜片。

核心性能与对焦系统

  • 马达系统：搭载唯卓仕自研的双重 HyperVCM 马达，实现至远至近焦点切换快至 100ms。
  • 焦距范围：最近对焦距离为 0.3m，最大放大率达到 0.18 倍。
  • 视频拍摄优化：官方指出该镜头在焦点转换时视角变换极小，呼吸效应微乎其微，能满足专业视频创作需求。

人机交互与操控设计

  • 自定义按键：支持自定义 Fn 按键设置。
  • 光圈控制：配备带刻度的独立光圈环，并搭载 Click 切换开关，可实现光圈有级/无级调节的快速切换。
  • 模式切换：设有 AF / MF 切换开关。

耐用性与扩展功能

  • 防护等级：采用高等级防尘防滴设计，前镜片配备防水防污镀膜。
  • 接口支持：内置 USB-C 固件升级接口。
  • 滤镜适配：滤镜口径为 φ77mm，可搭配顺滑莲花型遮光罩安装常规滤镜。

总结

唯卓仕AF 35mm F1.4 Pro镜头是一款面向专业用户的全画幅定焦镜头，其核心亮点在于结合了超高精度光学设计（16片/11组）和先进的机械结构。该镜头不仅提供了快速、流畅的对焦系统，还通过独立光圈环和自定义按键等物理控制，提升了视频及专业摄影场景下的操作便捷性和可靠性。

Overview

Viltrox today launched its new full-frame Pro series member, the AF 35mm F1.4 Pro lens. This lens supports both Sony FE and Nikon Z mounts and is priced at ¥3899.

Lens Specifications

  • Lens Construction: 16 elements in 11 groups
  • Special Lenses: 2 ultra-precision aspherical lenses, 3 extra-low dispersion lenses, and 4 high refractive index lenses
  • Motor: Viltrox self-developed Dual HyperVCM motor
  • Minimum Focusing Distance: 0.3m
  • Maximum Magnification: 0.18x
  • Filter Diameter: φ77mm

By incorporating Viltrox's proprietary Dual HyperVCM motor, the lens can transition from the farthest to the closest focus point in just 100ms.

Video Performance

Viltrox stated that the lens minimizes view angle changes and exhibits minimal breathing effect during focusing transitions. This ensures smooth focusing suitable for meeting professional video production demands.

Features and Design

  • Fn Button: Customizable
  • Aperture Ring: Independent aperture ring with visible markings, supporting both click-stop and continuous adjustment via a switch
  • AF/MF Switch: Included
  • Dustproof/Splashproof: Advanced dust and splashproof design
  • Front Lens: Water and smudge-resistant coating
  • Firmware Upgrade: Built-in USB-C interface
  • Hood: Soft lotus-shaped hood

Summary

The Viltrox AF 35mm F1.4 Pro is a full-frame lens supporting Sony FE and Nikon Z mounts, featuring advanced optical design and Viltrox's self-developed Dual HyperVCM motor for fast and accurate AF performance. It is particularly suited for professional video production due to its minimal breathing effect during focus transitions. Furthermore, it offers enhanced user convenience with customizable Fn buttons and an aperture ring supporting both click-stop and continuous adjustment.
