Vivo X Fold 6规格与功能

Vivo X Fold 6配备了一块8.02英寸 2K+ LTPO AMOLED内屏，分辨率达到2,504 x 2,312像素；其6.51英寸外屏则提供2,528 x 1,120像素的分辨率。两个屏幕均支持1-120Hz自适应刷新率、Dolby Vision以及高达5,000尼特的峰值亮度。

这款折叠手机搭载了3nm MediaTek Dimensity 9500 Super Edition芯片组，配备最大16GB LPDDR5X RAM和最大1TB UFS 4.1存储空间。系统运行基于Android 17的OriginOS 7 Fold，集成了多任务工作台（Multi-Task Workbench）、多分割屏幕（Multi-Split Screen）和AI跨应用拖拽（AI Cross-App Drag）等功能。其他软件特性包括AI会议助手、AI文件管理器、AI阅读模式和Jovi AI智能体。

在影像方面，X Fold 6配备了以下专业级摄像头系统：

  • 主摄像头： 2000万像素，支持OIS防抖和ZEISS T*镀膜。
  • 超广角摄像头： 5000万像素。
  • 潜望式长焦摄像头： 5000万像素，提供3倍光学变焦。

内屏和外屏均配备了2000万像素的自拍摄像头。该设备支持4K 60fps Dolby Vision录制以及100倍 ZEISS HyperZoom功能。

  • 主要规格列表：
  • 主摄：2000万像素，OIS，ZEISS T*镀膜
  • 超广角：5000万像素
  • 潜望长焦：5000万像素，3倍光学变焦
  • 自拍摄像头（内/外）：各2000万像素
  • 视频录制：4K 60fps Dolby Vision支持
  • 变焦功能：100倍 ZEISS HyperZoom

电池容量为6,900mAh，支持80W有线和40W无线快充。关于机身尺寸，Prism Black版本展开后厚度仅为4.4mm，重量为228g；Horizon Blue版本则为厚度4.8mm，重量235g。此外，该折叠手机还具备IP58、IP59、IPX8和IPX9等级别的防尘防水功能。

Introduction

Vivo has expanded its foldable lineup by launching the X Fold 6 globally, following its initial release in the Chinese market. The new foldable phone combines a large high-refresh rate display, a slim design, and upgraded imaging hardware. Furthermore, it offers productivity-focused software and AI features aimed at professional users.

Vivo X Fold 6 Specifications and Features

The Vivo X Fold 6 is equipped with an 8.02-inch 2K+ LTPO AMOLED inner display with a resolution of 2,504 x 2,312 pixels, complemented by a 6.51-inch cover screen offering a resolution of 2,528 x 1,120 pixels. Both panels support 1-120Hz adaptive refresh rates, Dolby Vision, and a peak brightness of up to 5,000 nits.

This foldable phone is powered by the 3nm MediaTek Dimensity 9500 Super Edition chipset, featuring up to 16GB of LPDDR5X RAM and up to 1TB of UFS 4.1 storage. It runs on OriginOS 7 Fold, based on Android 17, which includes features such as Multi-Task Workbench, Multi-Split Screen, and AI Cross-App Drag. Other software functions include AI Meeting Assistant, AI File Manager, AI Reader Mode, and the Jovi AI agent.

The X Fold 6 is outfitted with a 20MP main camera featuring OIS and ZEISS T* coating, a 50MP ultra-wide-angle camera, and a 50MP 3x periscope telephoto camera. Both the inner and cover screens are equipped with 20MP selfie cameras. The phone also supports features such as 4K 60fps Dolby Vision recording and 100x ZEISS HyperZoom.

  • Main Camera: 20MP, OIS, ZEISS T* Coating
  • Ultra-Wide Camera: 50MP
  • Periscope Telephoto Camera: 50MP, 3x Optical Zoom
  • Selfie Cameras (Inner/Cover): Both 20MP
  • Video Recording: 4K 60fps Dolby Vision recording
  • Zoom Function: 100x ZEISS HyperZoom

The device is powered by a 6,900mAh battery and supports 80W wired and 40W wireless charging. In the Prism Black version, the foldable phone boasts a thickness of only 4.4mm when unfolded and weighs 228g. The Horizon Blue version has a thickness of 4.8mm and weighs 235g. Additionally, it features IP58, IP59, IPX8, and IPX9 dust and water resistance ratings.

Pricing and Availability

Vivo announced the global launch of the X Fold 6 but has not yet disclosed international pricing or specific market release dates. The company stated that prices and availability will be announced according to individual markets. Global models are offered in Prism Black and Horizon Blue colors, though configurations may vary by region.

Summary

The Vivo X Fold 6 is a premium foldable smartphone featuring powerful performance, an innovative camera system, and enhanced productivity tools. Key highlights include the large high-refresh rate display, the MediaTek Dimensity 9500 Super Edition chipset, and a triple camera setup including a 20MP main camera with ZEISS T* coating. The device also offers robust durability ratings (IP58/IPX8) and impressive battery life, promising an excellent user experience in the global market.
