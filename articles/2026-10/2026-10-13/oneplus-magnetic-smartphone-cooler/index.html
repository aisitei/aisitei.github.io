OnePlus의 새로운 열전 냉각기인 "Magnetic Ice Card Mini Cooler"를 소개합니다. 이 제품은 스마트폰이 고부하 상태에서도 원활하게 작동하도록 설계된 초소형 방열판입니다. 199위안(약 30달러)에 출시되었으며, 이미 중국에서 구매할 수 있습니다. OnePlus는 이 제품을 OnePlus 16과 함께 공개했습니다.

주요 특징 및 디자인

본체는 폴리카보네이트와 알루미늄 합금으로 제작되었으며, 두께는 9.7mm(공기 흡입구 부분은 최대 12.3mm)입니다. 10N의 자기 부착력으로 단단히 고정되며, 내장 자석이 없는 대부분의 Android 스마트폰에서도 사용할 수 있도록 마그네틱 플레이트가 함께 제공됩니다.

두 가지 모드를 제공합니다. 조용한 저소음 설정(약 25dB)과 고성능 설정(약 35dB)으로, 두 모드 모두 일반적인 대화보다 조용합니다. 상태 표시등은 현재 모드를 보여주며(고성능은 파란색, 저소음은 녹색), 문제가 발생하면 빨간색으로 깜빡입니다.

전원은 USB-C 포트를 통해 공급되며, 최대 5V, 0.7A를 사용합니다. 별도의 케이블이나 콘센트 없이 스마트폰에서 직접 전원을 공급받거나, 보조 배터리 또는 어댑터에 연결할 수 있습니다. OnePlus는 10,000mAh 보조 배터리로 최고 설정에서 최대 8시간 동안 사용할 수 있다고 설명합니다. Oppo 및 OnePlus 스마트폰에서는 ColorOS X 고성능 모드와 연동되어 더 나은 결과를 제공합니다.

Introduction to the OnePlus Magnetic Ice Card Mini Cooler

Extended gaming sessions can cause smartphones to overheat, leading to performance degradation and frame drops—problems that are critical during key moments. To solve this issue, OnePlus has unveiled a small new accessory featuring powerful cooling capabilities.

Introducing the "Magnetic Ice Card Mini Cooler," a thermoelectric cooler from OnePlus. This ultra-compact heatsink is designed to ensure smartphones operate smoothly even under heavy load. It was launched at ¥199 (approximately $30) and is already available for purchase in China. The product was revealed alongside the OnePlus 16.

Key Features and Design

Its most notable advantage is its surprisingly small size. Measuring 70mm x 53.4mm, it is smaller than a standard credit card and weighs only about 50g—making it approximately 30% smaller and 18g lighter than OnePlus's previous Magnetic Ice Cooling Clip.

The unit is constructed from polycarbonate and aluminum alloy, with a thickness of 9.7mm (up to 12.3mm at the air intake). It attaches securely using 10N magnetic adhesion. Furthermore, it includes a magnetic plate so that it can be used on most Android smartphones that do not have built-in magnets.

Cooling Modes and Power

The cooler offers two operational modes: a quiet low noise setting (approximately 25dB) and a high performance setting (approximately 35dB). Both modes are reported to be quieter than normal conversation.

  • Status Indicator: The status light shows the current mode (blue for high performance, green for low noise), and flashes red if an issue occurs.

Power is supplied via a USB-C port, utilizing a maximum of 5V and 0.7A. Users can power it directly from their smartphone or connect it to a power bank or adapter without needing separate cables or wall outlets.

OnePlus states that the cooler can operate for up to 8 hours on a 10,000mAh power bank at the highest setting. For optimal results, it integrates with ColorOS X high performance mode found on Oppo and OnePlus smartphones.

Included Components

The product is released in a single color: Silver Wing Grey. The box includes a charging cable, strap, magnetic plate, protective film, and user manual.

Summary

The OnePlus Magnetic Ice Card Mini Cooler maximizes portability while delivering powerful cooling performance despite its compact size. Key features include preventing performance degradation during high-load tasks like gaming, and providing a magnetic plate for compatibility with various smartphones. The device enhances user convenience through quiet operational modes and flexible power supply options.
