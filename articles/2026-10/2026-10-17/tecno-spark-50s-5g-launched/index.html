Tecno has launched a new smartphone aimed at buyers in India looking for a feature-rich device for everyday use. This model, newly added to the Spark series, combines connectivity-focused features with a durable design. Furthermore, it provides multiple AI-powered tools and Google services to the budget market.

Tecno Spark 50s 5G Specifications and Features

The Tecno Spark 50s 5G is equipped with a 6.78-inch LCD display, offering HD+ resolution (720 x 1576 pixels), a 120Hz refresh rate, a 240Hz touch sampling rate, and up to 560 nits of brightness. The device runs on HiOS 16 based on Android 16 and utilizes the MediaTek Dimensity 6400 processor, built on a 6nm process. Memory options include 6GB or 8GB LPDDR4x RAM and 128GB UFS 2.2 storage, which can be expanded up to 2TB using a microSD card.

  • Display: 6.78-inch LCD, HD+ (720 x 1576 pixels), 120Hz refresh rate, 240Hz touch sampling rate, up to 560 nits brightness
  • Processor: MediaTek Dimensity 6400 (6nm process)
  • Operating System: HiOS 16 based on Android 16
  • Memory: 6GB or 8GB LPDDR4x RAM
  • Storage: 128GB UFS 2.2 (microSD card slot, expandable up to 2TB)

Camera and Battery

Photography is handled by a 50-megapixel rear camera featuring an f/1.85 aperture and LED flash, while the 8-megapixel front camera handles selfies and video calls. The device features a large 6,500mAh battery that supports 45W wired fast charging.

  • Rear Camera: 50MP, f/1.85 aperture, LED flash
  • Front Camera: 8MP
  • Battery: 6,500mAh, supports 45W wired fast charging

Connectivity and Durability

Connectivity options include Dual SIM 5G, Wi-Fi 5, Bluetooth 5.3, GPS, USB Type-C, and a 3.5mm headphone jack. A side-mounted fingerprint sensor provides biometric security. Tecno claims that the device can boost download speeds by up to 82% with UPS 3.0, which includes 4x4 MIMO antenna technology to increase UPI transaction speeds in crowded areas. Additionally, FreeLink enables voice calls, text message sharing, and image sharing between compatible devices over a distance of up to 1.5km without cellular or Wi-Fi coverage. The IP64 Pro rating and MIL-STD-810H certification offer protection against dust, splashes, and everyday physical stress.

  • Connectivity: Dual SIM 5G, Wi-Fi 5, Bluetooth 5.3, GPS, USB Type-C, 3.5mm headphone jack
  • Security: Side-mounted fingerprint sensor
  • Features: 4x4 MIMO antenna technology, UPS 3.0, FreeLink
  • Durability: IP64 Pro rating, MIL-STD-810H certification

The Tecno Spark 50s 5G is priced at ₹19,999 (approximately $225) for the 6GB+128GB model, and ₹22,999 (approximately $260) for the 8GB+128GB model. Color options include Burgundy Red and Breeze Blue. Sales are scheduled to begin on October 20, 2026, though reports regarding whether it will be sold via Amazon or offline retail stores are mixed.
