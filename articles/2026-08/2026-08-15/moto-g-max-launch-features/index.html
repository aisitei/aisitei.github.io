Introduction

Motorola has officially launched the Moto G Max in India, adding a new option to its smartphone lineup. The device combines a high-refresh-rate display, a large-capacity battery, and a Snapdragon chipset, while placing a strong emphasis on durability and everyday usability.

Moto G Max Specifications and Features

The Moto G Max features a 6.72-inch FHD+ display with a 120Hz refresh rate and peak brightness of up to 1,050 nits in HBM (High Brightness Mode). It also supports Water Touch 2.0 to improve touch responsiveness on wet screens and is protected by Corning Gorilla Glass Ceramic.

Internally, the device is powered by a Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 chipset, paired with LPDDR5X RAM and UFS 3.1 storage, supporting up to 24GB of additional virtual memory. The phone runs Android 16 based on Hello UX, with a commitment to two OS upgrades and three years of security updates.

The camera system includes the following specifications:

  • Rear Main Camera: 50MP Sony LYTIA 600
  • Rear Ultra-Wide Camera: 8MP
  • Front Selfie Camera: 32MP (for photos and video calls)

The device is equipped with stereo speakers supporting Dolby Atmos and retains a 3.5mm headphone jack for audio connectivity.

Powering the device is a 7,000mAh silicon-carbon battery that supports 30W charging. Additionally, the Moto G Max has achieved an IP64 rating and MIL-STD-810H certification, providing enhanced protection against dust, water, and harsh environmental conditions.

Moto G Max Pricing and Availability

Motorola has released the Moto G Max in two configurations: the 6GB+128GB model is priced at ₹27,999, and the 8GB+128GB model is priced at ₹29,999. The device will be available for sale on Flipkart starting August 20, with bank offers providing discounts of up to ₹1,000. It is available in three Pantone-selected colors: Stargazer, Alaskan Blue, and Malaga.

Summary

The Moto G Max provides a balanced performance package by combining a high-refresh-rate display, a Snapdragon chipset, and a massive 7,000mAh battery. Its robust build, certified by IP64 and MIL-STD-810H standards, ensures high durability for daily use. With competitive pricing and various color options, it is positioned as a strong contender in the Indian market.
