Design and Durability

Chinese smartphone brand CUBOT has unveiled the KINGKONG Dura, a rugged smartphone featuring a retro-inspired swappable battery design.

The KINGKONG Dura boasts high durability with IP68 and IP69K water and dust resistance ratings while meeting MIL-STD-810H military standards. These features make it optimized for heavy-duty work and outdoor activities, and the device also includes a built-in camping light.

Battery

The KINGKONG Dura comes with a dual-battery set and a dedicated charging dock, allowing users to "use one battery while the other is charging." This setup provides significant convenience during real-time operations, travel, or outdoor activities.
