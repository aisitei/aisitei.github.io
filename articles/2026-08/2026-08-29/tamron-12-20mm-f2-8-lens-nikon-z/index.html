Tamron 12-20mm F/2.8 Di III VXD Lens

Tamron announced the 12-20mm F/2.8 Di III VXD wide-angle zoom lens for full-frame mirrorless cameras, available for both Sony E mount and Nikon Z mount (Model number A084). The version for the Sony E mount is already released, while the Nikon Z mount version officially started sales yesterday with a price of 12,690 yuan.

Optical Performance

The lens's optical system incorporates aspherical lenses and special glass materials, including XGM (Extra-low Dispersion Glass Molding) lenses. This design effectively suppresses distortion and chromatic aberration at the edges of the frame, correcting spherical coma aberration—which often appears easily in edge areas during astrophotography—to deliver high image sharpness from the center to the corners.

Key Specifications

  • Lens construction: 17 elements in 12 groups
  • Minimum focusing distance (Wide): 0.18m
  • Minimum focusing distance (Telephoto): 0.28m
  • Weight (Nikon Z mount version): 585g

Review

The Tamron 12-20mm F/2.8 Di III VXD lens is a wide-angle zoom lens designed for full-frame mirrorless cameras utilizing the Sony E and Nikon Z mounts. Through advanced optical design, including XGM lenses, it effectively suppresses distortion and aberrations while providing excellent image sharpness from edge to center. It is particularly expected to deliver high-quality images in ultra-wide shooting environments, such as astrophotography.

Summary

The Tamron 12-20mm F/2.8 Di III VXD is a wide-angle zoom lens for full-frame mirrorless cameras (Sony E and Nikon Z mounts). Its advanced optical design utilizes XGM lenses to effectively minimize distortion and chromatic aberration across the entire frame. The lens promises exceptional image sharpness, making it highly suitable for demanding ultra-wide applications like astrophotography.
