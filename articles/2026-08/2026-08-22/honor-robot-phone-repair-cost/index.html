Camera and Gimbal

The Honor Robot Phone features the industry's first titanium alloy gimbal architecture. Utilizing aerospace-grade titanium alloy, this architecture reduces volume by 65% compared to mainstream gimbals. Additionally, it is the first in the industry to feature a titanium alloy gimbal motor with a maximum rotation speed of 360° per second. The gimbal is equipped with a 200MP 1/1.28-inch F1.6 23mm sensor.

Summary

The Honor Robot Phone launched with two configurations, starting at 9,999 RMB for the 12GB+512GB model and 12,999 RMB for the 16GB+1TB model. It distinguishes itself with an industry-first titanium alloy gimbal architecture and motor that reduces size while enabling high rotation speeds. A key highlight of the device is the integration of a large 200MP sensor within its unique gimbal system.
