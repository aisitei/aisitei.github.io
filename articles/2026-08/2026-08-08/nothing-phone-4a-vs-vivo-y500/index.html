由于材质与功能丰富的屏幕，Nothing Phone (4a) 提供更高级的质感；而 Vivo Y500 在耐用性方面具有优势。对于注重娱乐体验和屏幕质量的用户，Nothing 的体验会显得更加精致。

Design and Display

The Nothing Phone (4a) follows the brand's signature transparency-inspired design, featuring glass on both the front and back with Gorilla Glass 7i protection. It offers a premium look with its iconic rear notification LED and holds an IP64 rating for dust and splash resistance.

The Vivo Y500 adopts a more traditional design using plastic for the frame and back but significantly enhances durability with IP68/IP69 ratings and MIL-STD-810H certification. While Nothing targets users seeking a sophisticated visual identity, Vivo is geared toward those prioritizing ruggedness and outdoor durability.

Regarding display technology, the Nothing Phone (4a) features a 6.78-inch AMOLED panel supporting 10-bit color, HDR10+, Ultra HDR images, and a 120Hz refresh rate. The Vivo Y500 counters with a larger 6.83-inch AMOLED panel that offers high peak brightness and slightly higher resolution. While both are high-quality, Nothing provides more advanced HDR standards for multimedia enthusiasts.

The Nothing Phone (4a) feels more premium due to its materials and feature-rich display, whereas the Vivo Y500 excels in physical durability. Users prioritizing entertainment and screen quality may find the Nothing experience more sophisticated.

Battery and Performance

There is a clear distinction in performance between the two devices. The Nothing Phone (4a) is powered by a 4nm Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 chipset, UFS 3.1 storage, and up to 12GB of RAM, ensuring smooth performance for high-end gaming, multitasking, and long-term software use.

The Vivo Y500 utilizes the Unisoc T7300 processor and UFS 2.2 storage, making it suitable for daily apps, social media, streaming, and casual gaming. While both devices run Android 16, Nothing offers a competitive edge in longevity by promising three major Android updates.

Battery life is the primary strength of the Vivo Y500. Its massive 8100mAh silicon-carbon battery provides exceptional endurance, outperforming most phones in its segment, and supports 44W charging with bypass charging features. The Nothing Phone (4a) features a 5080mAh (Global) or 5400mAh (India) battery with faster 50W charging.

  • Nothing Phone (4a): Snapdragon 7s Gen 4, UFS 3.1, up to 12GB RAM, 50W fast charging
  • Vivo Y500: Unisoc T7300, UFS 2.2, 8100mAh high-capacity battery, 44W charging with bypass functionality

The Nothing Phone (4a) provides superior overall performance and longer software support. Conversely, the Vivo Y500 is an excellent choice for users who prioritize massive battery life over raw processing power.

Main and Auxiliary Cameras

The Nothing Phone (4a) holds a significant advantage in photography with its versatile triple camera system.

  • Nothing Phone (4a): 50MP Main (with OIS), 50MP Periscope Telephoto (3.5x optical zoom), 8MP Ultra-wide
  • Vivo Y500: 50MP main and secondary sensor configuration, 1080p video recording

Nothing supports 4K video recording, Dolby Vision HDR, Gyro-EIS, and OIS. While the Vivo Y500's primary camera performs well in daylight, its utility is limited by the lack of dedicated telephoto and ultra-wide lenses.

Both smartphones feature a 32MP front camera suitable for social media and video calls. While selfie quality is comparable, Nothing provides superior image processing and HDR support in complex lighting environments.

Price and Value Proposition

The Nothing Phone (4a) sits in the premium mid-range category at approximately $450 (₹32,000). It offers a premium AMOLED display, Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 processor, triple camera system with periscope zoom, fast UFS 3.1 storage, and guaranteed software updates.

The Vivo Y500 is significantly more affordable at approximately $250 (₹22,000). Despite the lower price point, it provides an AMOLED display, a massive 8100mAh battery, stereo speakers, and robust IP68/IP69 protection.

The price difference reflects different priorities: Nothing focuses on flagship-level features and longevity, while Vivo emphasizes battery life and cost-effectiveness. Both devices offer great value within their respective segments but target different consumer bases.

Summary

The Nothing Phone (4a) is a high-performance premium mid-range device featuring superior processing power, a versatile camera system, and long-term software support. In contrast, the Vivo Y500 is a practical, value-oriented choice optimized for extreme battery life and rugged durability.
