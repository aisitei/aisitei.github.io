성능

성능 면에서 신제품은 Snapdragon 8 Elite 모바일 플랫폼을 탑재했으며, 해당 칩셋의 CPU 클럭은 4.74GHz에 달합니다. 또한, RedMagic이 자체 개발한 게이밍 칩인 레드 코어 R4를 탑재하여 터치 최적화, 광효과 제어, 렌더링 가속, 초해상도 및 프레임 보간 기능을 제공합니다.

냉각

냉각 시스템은 업계 유일의 '풍수(Wind & Water) 듀얼 쿨링' 방식을 채택했습니다. 주요 특징은 다음과 같습니다.

  • 능동형 냉각 팬(Yufeng 4.0): 최대 24,000rpm 회전 속도
  • 업계 최초 관통형 풍도 설계 및 IPX8 등급 방수 냉각 팬 (소음 30dB 미만)
  • 새롭게 업그레이드된 맥동 수냉 엔진: 독자적인 액체 유로 설계, AI 서버와 동일한 불화액(Fluorinated liquid), 전용 압력 마이크로 펌프, 낙하 방지 및 누수 방지 설계 적용

배터리

배터리 성능에 대해 RedMagic 11S Pro는 RedMagic 역사상 가장 큰 용량인 8,000mAh '우마왕(Bull King) 배터리 3.0'을 탑재했습니다. Pro+ 모델은 120W 매직 플래시 고속충전과 80W 무선충전을 지원합니다.

Display

The RedMagic 11S Pro series was released in May this year and features a flat back design. The new models in this series come equipped with the following display specifications:

  • 6.85-inch 1.5K Wukong Screen 2.0 (Notchless)
  • Supports 144Hz refresh rate
  • 95.3% screen ratio
  • Built-in 16-megapixel front camera

RedMagic collaborated with BOE to incorporate X10 under-screen lighting materials, which reduced power consumption by 10% and increased lifespan by 30%. Additionally, all products in the lineup feature 3D ultrasonic fingerprint recognition.

Performance

In terms of performance, the new device is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite mobile platform, featuring a CPU clock speed of 4.74GHz. Furthermore, it includes the Red Core R4, a gaming chip independently developed by RedMagic, which provides touch optimization, visual effects control, rendering acceleration, super-resolution, and frame interpolation.

Cooling

The cooling system adopts the industry-exclusive "Wind & Water" dual cooling method. Key features include:

  • Active cooling fan (Yufeng 4.0): Rotation speed up to 24,000rpm
  • Industry's first penetration wind path design and IPX8-rated waterproof cooling fan (noise level under 30dB)
  • Newly upgraded pulsating liquid cooling engine: Features a unique liquid circuit design, fluorinated liquid similar to that used in AI servers, a dedicated pressure micro-pump, and fall/leak protection designs.

Battery

Regarding battery performance, the RedMagic 11S Pro is equipped with the "Bull King Battery 3.0," which features an 8,000mAh capacity—the largest in RedMagic history. The Pro+ model supports 120W Magic Flash fast charging and 80W wireless charging.

Overall Evaluation

The RedMagic 11S Pro series is a high-performance device that combines the powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite processor and a self-developed gaming chip with innovative liquid and wind cooling designs. Specifically, its large battery capacity and fast charging capabilities provide specifications optimized for a premium gaming environment.

Summary

The RedMagic 11S Pro series features high-end hardware including the Snapdragon 8 Elite processor, a custom gaming chip, and an advanced "Wind & Water" dual cooling system. With its massive 8,000mAh battery and innovative display technology, it is designed to provide a top-tier experience for mobile gamers.
