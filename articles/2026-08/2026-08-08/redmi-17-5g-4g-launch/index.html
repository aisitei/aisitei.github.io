Redmi 17 5G, Redmi 17 4G 출시 및 사양 안내

Redmi 17 5G, Redmi 17 4G 상세 사양 및 기능

두 모델은 핵심 프로세서에서 차이를 보입니다. Redmi 17 5G는 4nm 공정 기반의 Snapdragon 4 Gen 5 칩셋을 탑재했으며, LPDDR4X RAM과 UFS 2.2 스토리지를 채택했습니다. 또한 5G 연결성, NFC, Bluetooth 5.1 기능을 지원합니다.

반면 Redmi 17 4G는 Helio G91-Ultra 칩셋을 탑재했으며, LPDDR4x RAM과 eMMC 5.1 스토리지를 사용하고 Bluetooth 5.4를 지원합니다.

이러한 프로세서 차이를 제외하면 두 스마트폰은 거의 동일한 하드웨어를 공유합니다.

  • 디스플레이: 6.9인치 HD+ LCD, 120Hz 가변 주사율
  • 밝기 및 보호: 최대 825니트 고휘도 모드, Corning Gorilla Glass 7i 적용
  • 인증: TÜV Rheinland 저청색광(Low Blue Light), 플리커 프리, 생체 리듬 친화적 시청 인증

배터리와 충전 성능은 두 기기 모두 동일하게 설계되었습니다.

  • 배터리 용량: 7,500mAh
  • 충전 속도: 45W 고속 충전 및 22.5W 유선 역충전 지원

카메라 시스템은 다음과 같습니다.

  • 후면 카메라: 50-megapixel AI dual rear camera

기타 주요 기능 및 사양은 다음과 같습니다.

  • 소프트웨어: HyperOS 3 기반 Android 16 탑재
  • 오디오: 최대 300% 볼륨 증폭을 지원하는 dual stereo speakers

  • 연결성: dual SIM 지원, microSD 카드 슬롯 제공
  • 메모리 확장: 최대 12GB RAM 확장 가능

Redmi 17 5G, Redmi 17 4G 가격 및 출시 정보

Redmi 17 5G는 이미 말레이시아에서 판매를 시작했으며, 4GB+128GB 모델은 MYR 769(약 $190), 4GB+256GB 모델은 MYR 869(약 $210)에 책정되었습니다. 글로벌 시장에서는 $249부터 시작하며, 6GB+128GB 및 6GB+256GB 옵션도 포함됩니다. 색상은 Black, Deep Blue, Vivid Orange 세 가지로 제공됩니다.

Redmi 17 5G 与 Redmi 17 4G 发布及规格说明

Redmi 近期在中国发布了搭载 Unisoc T8300 芯片的 Redmi 17 5G。随后，官方通过意外的公告推出了全球型号 Redmi 17 5G 和 Redmi 17 4G，这两款机型与中国国内版在硬件配置上存在显著差异。以下是面向全球市场的 Redmi 17 5G 及 Redmi 17 4G 的主要规格。

Redmi 17 5G 与 Redmi 17 4G 详细规格及功能

两款机型在核心处理器上有所不同。Redmi 17 5G 搭载基于 4nm 工程的 Snapdragon 4 Gen 5 芯片，采用 LPDDR4X RAM 和 UFS 2.2 存储，并支持 5G 连接、NFC 以及 Bluetooth 5.1 功能。

相比之下，Redmi 17 4G 则搭载 Helio G91-Ultra 芯片，使用 LPDDR4x RAM 和 eMMC 5.1 存储，并支持 Bluetooth 5.4。

除处理器差异外，两款智能手机在其他硬件上基本一致。

  • 显示屏：6.9英寸 HD+ LCD，120Hz 可变刷新率
  • 亮度与保护：最高 825尼特高亮度模式，采用 Corning Gorilla Glass 7i
  • 认证：TÜV Rheinland 低蓝光、无频闪、符合生物节律观看认证

电池和充电性能方面，两款设备的设计相同。

  • 电池容量：7,500mAh
  • 充电速度：支持 45W 快充及 22.5W 有线反向充电

摄像头系统如下：

  • 后置摄像头：50-megapixel AI 双摄
  • 前置摄像头：8-megapixel 前置镜头

其他主要功能及规格包括：

  • 软件：搭载基于 HyperOS 3 的 Android 16
  • 音频：支持最高 300% 音量放大的双立体声扬声器
  • 耐用性与便捷性：IP64 等级防尘防水、侧边指纹识别扫描仪、3.5mm 耳机孔
  • 连接性：支持双卡双待，提供 microSD 卡槽
  • 内存扩展：最高可扩展至 12GB RAM

Redmi 17 5G 与 Redmi 17 4G 价格及发布信息

Redmi 17 5G 已在马来西亚开始销售，4GB+128GB 型号定价为 MYR 769（约 $190），4GB+256GB 型号定价为 MYR 869（约 $210）。全球市场起售价为 $249，包含 6GB+128GB 及 6GB+256GB 选项。提供 Black、Deep Blue 和 Vivid Orange 三种颜色。

Redmi 17 4G 在全球市场提供 4GB+128GB、4GB+256GB、6GB+128GB 及 6GB+256GB 配置，可选 White、Red 和 Green 三种颜色。Xiaomi 目前尚未确定具体的地区发布日期，但两款型号都将很快在更多市场上市。

总结

Redmi 17 系列针对全球市场，根据处理器规格区分出 5G 和 4G 两个版本，提供大容量电池和高刷新率屏幕等实惠配置。凭借 HyperOS 3 系统及丰富的实用功能，该系列将成为追求高性价比用户的理想选择。

Redmi 17 5G and Redmi 17 4G Launch and Specifications

Redmi recently unveiled the Redmi 17 5G featuring the Unisoc T8300 chipset in China. Following this, an unexpected announcement introduced the global models: the Redmi 17 5G and the Redmi 17 4G. Both of these global models feature hardware specifications that differ significantly from their Chinese counterparts. Below are the key specifications for the globally released Redmi 17 5G and Redmi 17 4G.

Detailed Specifications and Features of Redmi 17 5G and Redmi 17 4G

The two models differ primarily in their core processors. The Redmi 17 5G is equipped with a Snapdragon 4 Gen 5 chipset based on a 4nm process, utilizing LPDDR4X RAM and UFS 2.2 storage. It also supports 5G connectivity, NFC, and Bluetooth 5.1.

In contrast, the Redmi 17 4G features the Helio G91-Ultra chipset, utilizes LPDDR4x RAM and eMMC 5.1 storage, and supports Bluetooth 5.4.

Aside from these processor differences, both smartphones share nearly identical hardware components:

  • Display: 6.9-inch HD+ LCD with a 120Hz variable refresh rate
  • Brightness and Protection: Up to 825 nits peak brightness and Corning Gorilla Glass 7i protection
  • Certifications: TÜV Rheinland Low Blue Light, Flicker Free, and Circadian Rhythm friendly viewing certifications

The battery and charging performance are designed identically for both devices:

  • Battery Capacity: 7,500mAh
  • Charging Speed: 45W fast charging and 22.5W wired reverse charging support

The camera systems are as follows:

  • Rear Camera: 50-megapixel AI dual rear camera
  • Front Camera: 8-megapixel front-facing camera

Other key features and specifications include:

  • Software: Powered by HyperOS 3 based on Android 16
  • Audio: Dual stereo speakers supporting up to a 300% volume boost
  • Durability and Convenience: IP64 dust and water resistance, side-mounted fingerprint scanner, and a 3.5mm headphone jack
  • Connectivity: Dual SIM support and a microSD card slot
  • Memory Expansion: Expandable up to 12GB RAM

Redmi 17 5G and Redmi 17 4G Pricing and Availability

The Redmi 17 5G has already begun sales in Malaysia, with the 4GB+128GB model priced at MYR 769 (approx. $190) and the 4GB+256GB model at MYR 869 (approx. $210). In the global market, prices start from $249, including 6GB+128GB and 6GB+256GB options. It is available in three colors: Black, Deep Blue, and Vivid Orange.

The Redmi 17 4G will be released in the global market with configurations of 4GB+128GB, 4GB+256GB, 6GB+128GB, and 6GB+256GB. It is available in White, Red, and Green. While Xiaomi has not yet confirmed specific regional release dates, both models are expected to launch in more markets soon.

Summary

The Redmi 17 series targets the global market by offering two distinct models—5G and 4G—based on their respective processor specifications. Both devices feature a massive 7,500mAh battery, high refresh rate displays, and the latest HyperOS 3 software, making them attractive options for value-oriented consumers.
