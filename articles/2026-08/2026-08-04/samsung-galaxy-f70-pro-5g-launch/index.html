Galaxy F70 Pro 5G 주요 사양 및 기능

Galaxy F70 Pro 5G는 Corning Gorilla Glass Victus+ 보호 기능이 적용된 120Hz 주사율의 6.7인치 Full HD+ Super AMOLED 디스플레이를 탑재했습니다.

소프트웨어 측면에서는 Android 16 및 One UI 8.5를 기반으로 하며, Samsung은 6회의 Android OS 업그레이드와 6년간의 보안 업데이트를 약속했습니다. 또한 Google과 협력한 Circle to Search, AI 음성 전사(Transcription), Direct Voicemail, 그리고 Samsung Knox 보안 기능 등 다양한 Galaxy AI 기능을 포함합니다.

기기의 핵심 성능은 Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 프로세서와 Adreno 710 GPU의 조합으로 구성됩니다. Samsung은 LPDDR5X 메모리를 강조하고 있으나, 실제 판매 모델에는 LPDDR5 RAM이 탑재되어 있어 해당 칩셋에서 LPDDR5와 LPDDR5X 간의 성능 차이는 크지 않을 것으로 예상됩니다.

메모리는 최대 8GB RAM과 256GB 저장 공간을 제공하며, 하이브리드 microSD 카드 슬롯을 지원합니다. 6000mAh 배터리는 긴 사용 시간을 보장하며 고속 충전을 지원합니다. 연결성 측면에서는 Dual SIM 5G, Wi-Fi ac, Bluetooth, USB Type-C, OTG 및 Samsung Wallet를 통한 NFC 기능을 제공합니다.

카메라 앱은 Night, Portrait, Pro, Pro Video, Hyperlapse, Slow Motion, Super Slow Motion, Single Take 등 다양한 촬영 모드를 지원하며, 동영상 촬영 시 Samsung의 No Shake Cam 기능을 통해 안정적인 4K 영상을 기록할 수 있습니다.

  • 후면 메인 카메라: 50MP (OIS 지원)
  • 후면 초광각 카메라: 5MP
  • 전면 셀피 카메라: 12MP (HDR 지원)
  • 지원 촬영 모드: Night, Portrait, Pro, Pro Video, Hyperlapse, Slow Motion, Super Slow Motion, Single Take 등

Samsung has unveiled the Galaxy F70 Pro 5G, a new device targeting the mid-range market. This model offers a harmonious blend of hardware, software support, and AI-driven features. Key Specifications and Features

The Galaxy F70 Pro 5G features a 6.7-inch Full HD+ Super AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate, protected by Corning Gorilla Glass Victus+.

On the software front, the device runs on Android 16 and One UI 8.5. Samsung has committed to providing six Android OS upgrades and six years of security updates. It also includes various Galaxy AI features in collaboration with Google, such as Circle to Search, AI Transcription, Direct Voicemail, and Samsung Knox security.

The core performance is powered by the Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 processor paired with the Adreno 710 GPU. While Samsung highlights the use of LPDDR5X memory, the actual retail models are equipped with LPDDR5 RAM; however, the performance difference between LPDDR5 and LPDDR5X on this specific chipset is expected to be minimal.

The device provides up to 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage, supporting a hybrid microSD card slot. A 6000mAh battery ensures long usage times and supports fast charging. Connectivity options include Dual SIM 5G, Wi-Fi ac, Bluetooth, USB Type-C, OTG, and NFC functionality via Samsung Wallet.

Detailed Camera Specifications

The rear camera system of the Galaxy F70 Pro 5G consists of a 50MP main camera with OIS and a 5MP ultra-wide camera. The front features a 12MP HDR selfie camera.

The camera app supports various modes, including Night, Portrait, Pro, Pro Video, Hyperlapse, Slow Motion, Super Slow Motion, and Single Take. Additionally, the device utilizes Samsung's No Shake Cam technology to record stable 4K videos.

  • Rear Main Camera: 50MP (OIS supported)
  • Rear Ultra-Wide Camera: 5MP
  • Front Selfie Camera: 12MP (HDR supported)
  • Supported Shooting Modes: Night, Portrait, Pro, Pro Video, Hyperlapse, Slow Motion, Super Slow Motion, Single Take
  • Video Specifications: No Shake Cam technology, 4K resolution support

Physical Dimensions and Weight

  • Dimensions: 162.4 x 78.2 x 8.1mm
  • Weight: 202g

Pricing and Availability

The Galaxy F70 Pro 5G is available in three configurations: 6GB+128GB, 8GB+128GB, and 8GB+256GB. While the original prices are Rs 25,999, Rs 29,999, and Rs 34,999 respectively, they can be purchased at promotional prices of Rs 23,999, Rs 27,999, and Rs 32,999. The device is available in two color options: Aura Green and Alpha Black.

Summary

The Galaxy F70 Pro 5G is a compelling mid-range smartphone that combines powerful hardware with extensive software support and integrated AI features. With its large 6000mAh battery and stable camera performance, it serves as an excellent choice for users seeking practicality and longevity.
