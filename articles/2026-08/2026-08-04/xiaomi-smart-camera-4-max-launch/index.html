디자인 및 하드웨어 사양

Xiaomi Smart Camera 4 Max AI Zoom Edition은 크라우드펀딩 당시와 동일한 799위안(약 $120)에 판매됩니다. 이 제품은 실버 그레이 마감과 패브릭 소재의 베이스를 특징으로 하며, 업그레이드된 스피커 시스템이 내장되어 있습니다.

성능 측면에서 3T 쿼드코어 칩을 탑재하여 Xiaomi C700 대비 3배 향상된 연산 성능으로 더욱 빠른 AI 처리를 지원합니다. 추가 기능으로는 정확도가 향상된 인물 인식, 제스처 기반 양방향 통화, 듀얼 밴드 Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.3, 최대 256GB microSD 카드 지원이 포함됩니다. 또한 데이터 전송 암호화 및 개인정보 보호를 위해 EAL5+ 인증을 받은 Mi Home 보안 칩을 탑재했습니다.

이 제품은 두 개의 카메라 시스템과 강력한 모터 구동 성능을 결합하여 고도화된 감시 기능을 제공합니다.

  • 야간 모드: 10개의 적외선 보조 조명을 활용한 저조도 환경 내 풀컬러 야간 시인성 확보
  • 모션 및 트래킹: 듀얼 모터 짐벌을 통해 수평 360도, 수직 180도 회전 지원 (Xiaomi C700 대비 30% 빠른 추적 속도로 신속한 대상 추적 가능)

AI 기반 스마트 기능

전용 촬영 모드를 통해 사용자는 단순한 홈 보안을 넘어 일상의 순간들을 기록할 수 있습니다.

Xiaomi Smart Camera 4 Max AI Zoom Edition 发布

今年初通过众筹发布的 Xiaomi 最新智能家居摄像头已在中国正式开售。新推出的 Xiaomi Smart Camera 4 Max AI Zoom Edition 配备双摄像头、基于 AI 的监控功能以及高性能处理芯片，被认为是目前功能最丰富的室内安防摄像头之一。

设计与硬件规格

Xiaomi Smart Camera 4 Max AI Zoom Edition 的售价与众筹时一致，为 799 元人民币（约 $120）。该产品采用银灰色饰面和织物底座，并内置了升级后的扬声器系统。

在性能方面，它搭载了 3T 四核芯片，相比 Xiaomi C700 的运算能力提升了 3 倍，支持更快速的 AI 处理。其他功能包括：准确性更高的行人识别、基于手势的双向通话、双频 Wi-Fi 6、Bluetooth 5.3 以及支持最大 256GB 的 microSD 卡。此外，为了确保数据传输加密和隐私保护，它还配备了获得 EAL5+ 认证的 Mi Home 安全芯片。

摄像头详细参数

该产品结合了双摄像头系统和强大的电机驱动性能，提供高级监控功能。

  • 望远镜头：1200万像素，f/1.6 光圈，6P 镜头
  • 超广角镜头：800万像素，f/1.6 光圈，6P 镜头
  • 录制与缩放功能：支持 4K 录制、3x 无损变焦（lossless zoom）、12x 混合变焦、自动对焦（autofocus）
  • 夜间模式：利用 10 个红外辅助灯，在低照度环境下确保全彩夜视
  • 运动与追踪：通过双电机云台支持水平 360 度、垂直 180 度旋转（比 Xiaomi C700 的追踪速度快 30%，可快速追踪目标）

基于 AI 的智能功能

最引人注目的功能之一是专为监测儿童、老人和宠物设计的 Xiaomi 首款 AI Care 大语言模型（LLM）。该系统可以识别学习时间、跌倒、长时间无活动以及宠物的行为，并每天自动生成监控报告，同时提供基于文本的视频搜索功能。

通过专用拍摄模式，用户不仅可以进行简单的家庭安防，还能记录日常生活中的瞬间。

总评

Xiaomi Smart Camera 4 Max AI Zoom Edition 是一款结合了强大硬件和先进 AI 技术的高端室内安防摄像头。特别是通过双摄像头系统和专用 LLM 实现的智能监控功能，为家庭成员和宠物的安全提供了创新性的解决方案。

总结

Xiaomi Smart Camera 4 Max AI Zoom Edition 正式上市，凭借高性能芯片、双摄像头系统及 4K 录制能力提供卓越的硬件性能。其核心亮点在于集成首款针对居家场景的 AI Care 大语言模型，能够自动生成监控报告并支持文本搜索。该产品是兼顾强大硬件与智能化监测的高端安防选择。

Xiaomi Smart Camera 4 Max AI Zoom Edition Released

The latest smart home camera from Xiaomi, which was first revealed through crowdfunding earlier this year, has officially launched in China. The new Xiaomi Smart Camera 4 Max AI Zoom Edition is equipped with dual cameras, AI-powered monitoring features, and a high-performance processing chip, making it one of the most feature-rich indoor security cameras currently available.

Design and Hardware Specifications

The Xiaomi Smart Camera 4 Max AI Zoom Edition is priced at 799 RMB (approximately $120), matching its original crowdfunding price. The device features a silver gray finish with a fabric base and includes an upgraded speaker system.

In terms of performance, it is powered by a 3T quad-core chip that provides three times the processing power compared to the Xiaomi C700, enabling significantly faster AI processing. Additional features include improved person recognition, gesture-based two-way calling, dual-band Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.3, and support for microSD cards up to 256GB. To ensure data encryption and privacy protection, it is equipped with a Mi Home security chip featuring EAL5+ certification.

Camera Specifications

This product combines two camera systems with powerful motor-driven performance to provide advanced surveillance capabilities.

  • Telephoto Camera: 12MP, f/1.6 aperture, 6P lens
  • Ultra-wide Camera: 8MP, f/1.6 aperture, 6P lens
  • Recording and Zoom: Supports 4K recording, 3x lossless zoom, 12x hybrid zoom, and autofocus
  • Night Mode: Provides full-color visibility in low-light environments using 10 infrared auxiliary lights
  • Motion and Tracking: Dual motor gimbal supports 360-degree horizontal and 180-degree vertical rotation (enabling tracking speeds 30% faster than the Xiaomi C700)

AI-Powered Smart Features

One of the most notable features is Xiaomi's first AI Care Large Language Model (LLM), specifically designed for monitoring children, the elderly, and pets. This system can recognize study time, falls, prolonged periods of inactivity, and pet behaviors, while automatically generating daily monitoring reports and providing text-based video search functionality.

Through dedicated filming modes, users can go beyond basic home security to record various moments in their daily lives.

Summary

The Xiaomi Smart Camera 4 Max AI Zoom Edition is a premium indoor security camera that combines high-performance hardware with advanced AI capabilities. Its dual-camera system and integrated LLM provide sophisticated monitoring solutions for families and pets, while the powerful processing chip ensures rapid response and enhanced data security.
