카메라 사양 상세

Vivo S2는 고성능 촬영을 위해 강력한 카메라 시스템을 탑재했습니다. 특히 전면과 후면 모두에서 4K 비디오 녹화를 지원하여 콘텐츠 제작에 최적화되어 있습니다.

  • 후면 메인 카메라: 50MP Sony IMX852
  • 전면 카메라: 32MP
  • 동영상 촬영: 전/후면 모두 4K 지원
  • 주요 기능: Aura Light Portrait, Dual View Video(전/후면 동시 촬영)

성능 및 배터리

Vivo S2는 MediaTek Dimensity 7360-Turbo 칩셋을 탑재했으며, AnTuTu 점수 약 970,000점을 달성하는 성능을 보여줍니다. 또한 7,050mAh의 대용량 배터리를 탑재하여 장시간 게임, 비디오 스트리밍 및 일상적인 사용 환경에서 안정적인 구동이 가능합니다.

소프트웨어 및 AI 기능

해당 스마트폰은 OriginOS 6를 기반으로 작동하며 다양한 AI 기반 기능을 도입했습니다. 주요 소프트웨어 특징은 다음과 같습니다.

  • AI 관련 기능: AI Creation, AI Transcript Assist, Vivo Office Kit
  • Circle to Search 2.0: 간단한 제스처로 화면 내 콘텐츠 검색
  • Shake and Share: 모바일 데이터에 의존하지 않고 호환 기기 간 사진 및 4K 비디오를 빠르게 전송

发布计划

Vivo S2 将于 8 月 6 日正式发布，届时将公布准确的价格及完整的规格参数。

Vivo S2 是一款结合了精致设计、大容量电池以及强劲 MediaTek Dimensity 7360-Turbo 芯片的高性能智能手机。通过支持前后 4K 拍摄以及搭载多种 AI 功能的 OriginOS 6 系统，该机在提升内容创作能力的同时也显著增强了用户体验。

Vivo S2 Information Overview

While Vivo has been teasing the upcoming Vivo S2 in the Indian market over the past few days without disclosing specific details, the brand today revealed several key specifications along with available color options. Below is a summary of all information released to date.

Design and Display Specifications

The Vivo S2 features an "Iconic Camera Design" and boasts a slim profile of 7.99mm. The device includes the following display specifications:

  • 6.83-inch 3D curved AMOLED display
  • 1.5K resolution and 120Hz refresh rate
  • Up to 3,000 nits peak brightness

The device is available in three color options: Silk White, Sapphire Blue, and Regal Bronze.

Detailed Camera Specifications

The Vivo S2 is equipped with a powerful camera system designed for high-performance photography. Notably, it supports 4K video recording on both the front and rear cameras, making it optimized for content creators.

  • Rear Main Camera: 50MP Sony IMX852
  • Front Camera: 32MP
  • Video Recording: 4K support for both front and rear
  • Key Features: Aura Light Portrait, Dual View Video (simultaneous front and rear recording)

Performance and Battery

The Vivo S2 is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 7360-Turbo chipset, achieving an AnTuTu score of approximately 970,000. Additionally, it features a large 7,050mAh battery to provide stable performance for long gaming sessions, video streaming, and daily usage.

Software and AI Features

The smartphone runs on OriginOS 6 and incorporates various AI-driven functions. Key software features include:

  • AI Features: AI Creation, AI Transcript Assist, Vivo Office Kit
  • Circle to Search 2.0: Search for content on the screen with simple gestures
  • Shake and Share: Quickly transfer photos and 4K videos between compatible devices without relying on mobile data

Release Schedule

The Vivo S2 is scheduled for an official launch on August 6, at which point the exact pricing and full specifications will be revealed.

Summary

The Vivo S2 combines a sleek design with a high-capacity battery and the powerful MediaTek Dimensity 7360-Turbo chipset. It is positioned as a high-performance device that enhances content creation through dual 4K recording capabilities and advanced AI features on OriginOS 6.
