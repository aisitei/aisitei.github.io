Design and Display

The Redmi Note 17 Pro is a durability-focused model featuring IP68/IP69K dust and water resistance, high-pressure wash protection, and excellent drop resistance. The front is reinforced with Gorilla Glass Victus 2, providing a practical choice for users who prioritize device longevity.

The Nothing Phone (4a) pursues a more sophisticated aesthetic with a glass back, Gorilla Glass 7i protection, and a unique rear notification LED. While its IP64 rating is sufficient for everyday splashes, it does not match the robust protection levels of the Redmi. If you need a device that can withstand harsh environments, the Redmi is preferable; if you value style and modern aesthetics, Nothing is more appealing.

The Redmi Note 17 Pro takes the lead in durability and protection, while the Nothing Phone (4a) provides a more refined multimedia display. Users focused on entertainment may prefer Nothing, whereas those prioritizing long-term reliability will find the Redmi to be the safer choice.

Display Quality

Both devices feature large AMOLED panels with a 120Hz refresh rate for smooth scrolling. The Redmi ensures high outdoor visibility with a peak brightness of 3,500 nits, 3,840Hz PWM dimming, and Gorilla Glass Victus 2.

Nothing offers a richer visual experience with an AMOLED panel supporting 1 billion colors, HDR10+, and Ultra HDR, providing a peak brightness of 4,500 nits when playing HDR content. While both displays are sharp, Nothing's color accuracy and HDR capabilities create a more premium viewing environment.

Specifications and Battery

The Redmi Note 17 Pro is equipped with the Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 processor running Android 16 and HyperOS 3, providing stable performance for daily tasks, casual gaming, and multitasking. In contrast, the Nothing Phone (4a) features a newer Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 and Adreno 810 GPU, offering significantly stronger CPU and graphics performance, along with a commitment to three major Android updates for long-term software value.

Redmi holds a dominant advantage in battery capacity:

  • Redmi Note 17 Pro: 9,000mAh silicon-carbon battery, 67W wired charging, and 22.5W reverse charging
  • Nothing Phone (4a): 5,080mAh (Global) or 5,400mAh (India) battery, 50W fast charging, and 7.5W reverse charging
Redmi provides significantly longer battery life, making it ideal for heavy users and travelers.

While the Nothing Phone (4a) is a performance-oriented device with a faster processor and superior long-term software support, the Redmi Note 17 Pro competes through its overwhelming battery endurance. The choice depends on whether performance or longevity is more important to the user.

Camera System

The camera hardware is a primary point of differentiation between the two devices. The Redmi Note 17 Pro includes a 50MP main camera and an auxiliary sensor for depth information, which is suitable for everyday photography but limited in versatility.

Nothing Phone (4a) features a much more powerful triple camera system:

  • Main: 50MP (with OIS)
  • Telephoto: 50MP Periscope telephoto camera (3.5x optical zoom)
  • Ultra-wide: 8MP
Additionally, it supports 4K video recording with Dolby Vision HDR, gyro-EIS, and OIS, providing much higher flexibility for both photos and videos.

Nothing also holds the advantage in front cameras. Compared to Redmi's 8MP camera, Nothing features a 32MP high-resolution front camera that captures more detail in selfies and offers improved video quality through advanced image processing.

Price and Value

The Redmi Note 17 Pro is priced at approximately $235 (₹22,690), placing it in the affordable mid-range category. At this price point, it delivers practical benefits such as a premium AMOLED display, excellent durability, and a massive battery.

The Nothing Phone (4a) sits at a significantly higher price of about $450 (₹32,000). However, this higher cost includes the faster Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 processor, a superior triple camera system, extended software support, and an optimized software experience.

Redmi provides solid hardware value for users who prioritize battery life and durability. Nothing justifies its premium price for those seeking better photography, a polished software experience, and overall performance. If advanced features are essential rather than optional, the higher cost of the Nothing model is justified.

Summary

The Redmi Note 17 Pro is the ideal choice for users seeking maximum practicality, durability, and exceptional battery life at a more affordable price point. In contrast, the Nothing Phone (4a) targets users who prioritize high-end performance, advanced camera capabilities, and a premium software experience.
