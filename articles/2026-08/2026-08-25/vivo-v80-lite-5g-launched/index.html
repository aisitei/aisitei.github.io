Vivo V80 Lite 5G 사양 및 기능

Introduction

The Vivo V80 Lite 5G has officially launched in Pakistan. This model combines a curved display, a high-capacity battery, a mid-range chipset, a 50MP dual camera setup, and an attractive design. Detailed information regarding the V80 Lite is provided below.

Vivo V80 Lite 5G Specifications and Features

The Vivo V80 Lite 5G features an eye-catching design with a curved AMOLED display on the front and a horizontal camera bar on the rear.

  • Main Camera: 50MP Sony IMX852 sensor
  • Bokeh Camera: 2MP
  • Flash: Ring LED Flash
  • Front Camera: 32MP

The device is equipped with a 6.83-inch 1.5K AMOLED display, providing a 120Hz refresh rate, 449ppi density, and a peak brightness of up to 3,000 nits. It runs on OriginOS 6 based on Android 16 and includes symmetrical stereo speakers and an in-display fingerprint sensor.

Performance is driven by the 4nm MediaTek Dimensity 7360-Turbo processor. The phone features 8GB LPDDR4X RAM and either 128GB or 256GB of UFS 3.1 storage, with support for up to 8GB of extended RAM.

It is powered by a 7,050mAh battery that supports 44W charging. Other features include bypass charging, Bluetooth 5.4, dual-band Wi-Fi, and USB 2.0 Type-C. For durability, the device holds IP68 and IP69 ratings, five MIL-STD-810H certifications, and has passed drop tests on seven different surfaces.

Vivo V80 Lite 5G Pricing and Availability

In Pakistan, the 8GB+128GB version is priced at PKR 124,999 (approximately $450), while the 8GB+256GB model is priced at PKR 145,999 (approximately $525). It is available in Sapphire Blue and Silk White.

The Vivo V80 Lite 5G is essentially a rebranded version of the Vivo S2 sold in India. Furthermore, Vivo offers a separate V80 Lite 5G with a 10,000mAh battery for Southeast Asian markets such as Malaysia, and Bhutan will soon release an S2-based V80 Lite.

Summary

The Vivo V80 Lite 5G is a compelling mid-range smartphone featuring a curved AMOLED display, a powerful MediaTek Dimensity 7360-Turbo chipset, and a 50MP dual camera system. With its large 7,050mAh battery, fast charging capabilities, and robust IP68/IP69 durability ratings, it is well-suited for daily use.
