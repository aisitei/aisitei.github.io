Introduction

Honor has launched the Play 20A smartphone in international markets. Aimed at the budget and mid-range segments, this new device features a dual screen on its rear panel.

Display and Camera

The smartphone's dual screen can be used for taking selfies and displaying information such as time, incoming calls, music, and photos. The device is equipped with a 6.9-inch IPS display featuring a teardrop notch and supports a 90Hz refresh rate. The front camera is 5 megapixels.

  • Display: 6.9-inch IPS with teardrop notch
  • Refresh Rate: 90Hz
  • Front Camera: 5MP
  • Rear Camera: 13MP single camera

Performance and Battery

The smartphone is powered by the MediaTek Helio G81 chipset, featuring 4GB of RAM and storage options of 64GB or 128GB. It also supports microSD card expansion. The device comes with a large 6000mAh battery and supports 15W charging.

  • Processor: MediaTek Helio G81
  • RAM: 4GB
  • Storage: 64GB/128GB (supports microSD expansion)
  • Battery: 6000mAh
  • Charging: 15W

Additional Features

The device supports side-mounted fingerprint recognition. The official price and release date have not yet been announced.

Summary

The Honor Play 20A is a budget-friendly smartphone characterized by its unique rear dual screen for selfies and notifications. It combines the MediaTek Helio G81 chipset with a high-capacity 6000mAh battery, targeting users looking for practicality and long battery life.
