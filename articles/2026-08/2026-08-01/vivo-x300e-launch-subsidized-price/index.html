Design and Camera

The vivo X300E features an integrated cold diamond glass platform and a large rounded R corner design. The rear camera system consists of a triple-camera setup (50MP + 50MP + 8MP). It supports Zeiss imaging technology, providing various capabilities such as telephoto stage, portrait, and landscape modes.

Performance and Display

The vivo X300E is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 flagship chipset and features a 144Hz Zeiss Master Color display. It is equipped with a 7200mAh battery and utilizes an LPDDR5X+UFS 4.1 combination to support 12GB of variable memory expansion.
