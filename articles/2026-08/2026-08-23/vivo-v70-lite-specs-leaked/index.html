Introduction

According to reports from IT Home on August 22, GSMArena has unveiled the vivo V70 Lite 4G smartphone. The device is powered by the UNISOC T7300 processor and is scheduled for an upcoming release.

Display and Camera

The device is available in two color options: Blue and White, and it features an IP69 rating for enhanced durability. The front of the device is equipped with a 6.83-inch AMOLED panel featuring a resolution of 1260x2800 and a 120Hz refresh rate. For photography, it includes a 32MP selfie camera on the front and a dual rear camera system consisting of a 50MP main sensor and a 2MP depth sensor.

Performance and Battery

The vivo V70 Lite 4G is powered by the UNISOC T7300 chipset, paired with 8GB of LPDDR4X RAM and storage options of either 128GB or 256GB (UFS 2.2). It features a massive 8100mAh battery that supports 44W wired charging. The device runs on OriginOS 6, which is based on Android 16.

Summary

The vivo V70 Lite 4G is an entry-level smartphone featuring the UNISOC T7300 processor and IP69 water and dust resistance. It combines a high-refresh-rate AMOLED display with a large 8100mAh battery to provide long usage times for users.
