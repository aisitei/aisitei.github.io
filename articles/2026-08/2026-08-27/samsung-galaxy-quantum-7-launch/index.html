介绍

Samsung Galaxy Quantum 7 规格与功能

Samsung Galaxy Quantum 7 配备了 6.7英寸 Super AMOLED Plus 显示屏，支持 Full HD+ 分辨率和 120Hz 刷新率。该手机由 Exynos 1680 芯片组驱动，拥有 8GB RAM 和 128GB 存储空间。它搭载了 5,000mAh 电池，可使用长达两天，并支持快充功能。

相机系统

该智能手机配备了三摄后置摄像头系统用于拍照。

  • 主传感器：5000万像素，支持 OIS
  • 超广角摄像头：1200万像素
  • 微距镜头：500万像素

前置配备了支持 Super HDR 的 1200万像素自拍相机。该设备通过增强的图像处理和更大的 1.0μm 像素，提升了低光照环境下的拍照性能。

软件与安全

Galaxy Quantum 7 运行基于 Android 的 One UI 8.5，并集成了三星的 Awesome Intelligence 功能。这些功能包括 Circle to Search、AI橡皮擦、Best Face 和编辑建议。此外，它还具备 Knox Vault 安全技术、IP68 防水防尘等级和 USB Type-C 端口，支持最多 6 次 Android OS 升级和 6 年的安全更新。

价格与上市信息

Galaxy Quantum 7 在韩国售价为 717,200 韩元（约合 520 美元），提供 Awesome Gray、Awesome Ice Blue 和 Awesome Lilac 三种颜色选择。Lilac 颜色将仅在 SKT T Direct Shop 独家销售。与前代 Galaxy Quantum 系列设备一样，预计这款智能手机将仅限于韩国市场。

总结

Galaxy Quantum 7 本质上是 Galaxy A57 5G 的品牌重塑版本，除运营商的品牌和功能外，规格与原机相似。该型号重点提升了安全性和相机性能，并提供了长期的软件支持，是一款专为韩国市场定制的运营商专属智能手机。

Introduction

Samsung Electronics has launched a new Galaxy Quantum series smartphone in the Korean market. This model features upgraded hardware and several AI-centric functions. The Galaxy Quantum 7 is positioned as a carrier-exclusive model focusing on enhanced security, camera improvements, and long-term software support. Here are the detailed specifications for the Quantum 7.

Samsung Galaxy Quantum 7 Specifications and Features

The Samsung Galaxy Quantum 7 is equipped with a 6.7-inch Super AMOLED Plus display, supporting Full HD+ resolution and a 120Hz refresh rate. It runs on the Exynos 1680 chipset and features 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage. With a 5,000mAh battery, it offers up to two days of usage and supports fast charging.

Camera System

The smartphone is equipped with a triple rear camera setup for photography:

  • Main Sensor: 50MP, OIS support
  • Ultra-wide Camera: 12MP
  • Macro Lens: 5MP

The front features a 12MP selfie camera supporting Super HDR. This device improves low-light photography performance through enhanced image processing and larger 1.0μm pixels.

Software and Security

The Galaxy Quantum 7 runs on One UI 8.5, based on Android, and includes Samsung's Awesome Intelligence features. These functions include Circle to Search, AI Eraser, Best Face, and editing suggestions. Furthermore, it boasts Knox Vault security, IP68 water and dust resistance, and a USB Type-C port. It supports up to six generations of Android OS upgrades and 6 years of security updates.

Pricing and Availability

The Galaxy Quantum 7 is priced at 717,200 KRW (approximately $520) in Korea and is available in Awesome Gray, Awesome Ice Blue, and Awesome Lilac color options. The Lilac color option is exclusively sold at the SK Telecom T Direct Shop. Similar to previous Galaxy Quantum series devices, this smartphone is expected to be limited to the Korean market.

Review

The Galaxy Quantum 7 essentially appears to be a rebranded version of the Galaxy A57 5G, possessing similar specifications aside from carrier branding and features. This model emphasizes security, camera enhancements, and long-term software support, making it a carrier-exclusive smartphone tailored for the Korean market.

Summary

The Samsung Galaxy Quantum 7 is a new, carrier-exclusive device launched in Korea, featuring the Exynos 1680 chipset and a 6.7-inch Super AMOLED Plus display with a 120Hz refresh rate. Key highlights include a triple rear camera setup (50MP main sensor with OIS) and advanced AI features like Circle to Search. The phone emphasizes long-term support, offering up to six OS upgrades and robust security via Knox Vault.
