Samsung의 전사적 AI 통합 이니셔티브

Samsung은 기업 운영 전반에 인공지능(AI)을 통합하여 일상적인 업무 효율을 높이기 위한 전사적 AI 프로젝트를 추진합니다.

2026년 6월 9일에 발표된 "AI Transformation"(AX) 프로그램은 새로운 AI 도구 도입, 직원 교육 프로그램 운영, 그리고 Samsung Group 전체에 걸친 전담 AI 팀 구성을 포함합니다. 이 계획은 생산성을 높이고 기업의 모든 운영 영역에서 AI 활용을 장려하기 위해 설계되었습니다.

도구 도입 및 활용 범위

Samsung은 이번 프로젝트의 일환으로 직원들에게 다음과 같은 외부 생성형 AI 서비스에 대한 접근 권한을 제공할 계획입니다.

  • Google Gemini
  • ChatGPT
  • Claude

이러한 도구들은 계열사 전반에서 활용될 예정이며, 소프트웨어 개발 및 마케팅부터 제조 및 비즈니스 운영에 이르기까지 다양한 업무를 지원할 것으로 기대됩니다.

대규모 교육 및 리더십 강화

교육은 이번 이니셔티브의 핵심 요소 중 하나로, Samsung은 2026년 말까지 약 280,000명의 전체 직원에게 AI 교육을 제공하는 것을 목표로 하고 있습니다.

또한 경영진부터 변화를 시작하며, 이번 달에 약 50명의 그룹 회장단이 Samsung 인재개발원에서 2일간의 AX Bootcamp에 참여합니다. 약 2,300명의 고위 임원들은 별도의 3일 교육 세션에 참여하며, 첫 번째 세션은 8월 중순까지 완료될 예정입니다.

AX 비전 및 조직 개편

Samsung은 리더십 교육 프로그램 기간 동안 그룹 전체의 AX 비전을 공개하고, AI가 각 사업 분야에 어떻게 적용될지 구체화할 계획입니다. 또한 각 계열사 내에 전담 AI 부서를 신설하여 기술 도입을 감독하고 내부 전문성을 강화할 예정입니다.

이러한 움직임은 워크플로우 자동화와 의사결정 개선을 위해 AI의 중요성이 커지고 있는 기술 산업의 흐름을 반영합니다. 이는 Samsung이 제품 개발을 넘어 기업 운영 방식 자체에 AI를 내재화하기 위해 추진하는 가장 대규모의 노력 중 하나입니다.

총평

Samsung은 'AI Transformation(AX)'을 통해 단순한 제품 혁신을 넘어 조직 운영 전반에 AI를 통합하는 강력한 실행 의지를 보여주고 있습니다. 2026년까지 방대한 규모의 교육과 전용 부서 신설을 병행함으로써, 기술 생태계에서 생산성을 극대화하고 경쟁 우위를 확보하려는 전략적 행보로 평가됩니다.

Samsung's Enterprise-wide AI Integration Initiative

Samsung is launching a company-wide AI project to integrate artificial intelligence into its overall operations to enhance daily work efficiency.

The "AI Transformation" (AX) program, announced on June 9, 2026, includes the introduction of new AI tools, the operation of employee training programs, and the formation of dedicated AI teams across the entire Samsung Group. This initiative is designed to boost productivity and encourage the adoption of AI across all operational areas of the company.

Tool Adoption and Scope

As part of this project, Samsung plans to provide employees with access to the following external generative AI services:

  • Google Gemini
  • ChatGPT
  • Claude

These tools are expected to be utilized across all affiliates, supporting a wide range of tasks ranging from software development and marketing to manufacturing and business operations.

Large-scale Training and Leadership Strengthening

Education is a core component of this initiative, with Samsung aiming to provide AI training to approximately 280,000 employees by the end of 2026.

The transformation begins at the leadership level; this month, about 50 group chairpersons will participate in a two-day AX Bootcamp at the Samsung Talent Development Center. Additionally, approximately 2,300 senior executives will attend separate three-day training sessions, with the first session scheduled to be completed by mid-August.

AX Vision and Organizational Restructuring

During the leadership training period, Samsung plans to unveil its group-wide AX vision and define how AI will be applied across various business sectors. Furthermore, dedicated AI departments will be established within each affiliate to oversee technology adoption and strengthen internal expertise.

These moves reflect a growing trend in the tech industry where the importance of AI for workflow automation and improved decision-making is increasing. This represents one of Samsung's most extensive efforts to internalize AI into its corporate operations, moving beyond mere product development.

Summary

Through the "AI Transformation (AX)" initiative, Samsung is demonstrating a strong commitment to integrating AI across its entire organizational structure rather than just focusing on product innovation. By combining large-scale employee training with the creation of dedicated departments, the company aims to maximize productivity and secure a competitive advantage in the global technology ecosystem.

Samsungの全社的なAI統合イニシアチブ

Samsungは、日常業務の効率を向上させるため、企業運営の全域に人工知能（AI）を統合する全社的なAIプロジェクトを推進します。

2026年6月9日に発表された「AI Transformation（AX）」プログラムには、新しいAIツールの導入、従業員向けトレーニングプログラムの実施、およびSamsungグループ全体における専任AIチームの編成が含まれます。この計画は、生産性を高め、企業のあらゆる運営領域においてAIの活用を促進するために設計されています。

ツールの導入と活用範囲

Samsungは本プロジェクトの一環として、従業員に対して以下の外部生成AIサービスへのアクセス権を提供することを計画しています。

  • Google Gemini
  • ChatGPT
  • Claude

これらのツールはグループ各社で活用される予定であり、ソフトウェア開発やマーケティングから製造、ビジネス運営に至るまで、多岐にわたる業務をサポートすることが期待されています。

大規模な教育とリーダーシップの強化

教育はこのイニシアチブの核心要素の一つであり、Samsungは2026年末までに全従業員約280,000人に対してAIトレーニングを提供することを目指しています。

また、経営陣から変革を開始しており、今月にはグループ会長団のうち約50名がSamsung人材開発センターにて2日間のAX Bootcampに参加します。さらに、約2,300名のシニアエグゼクティブは個別の3日間のトレーニングセッションに参加し、その第1回セッションは8月中旬までに完了する予定です。

AXビジョンと組織再編

Samsungはリーダーシップ教育プログラムの期間中にグループ全体のAXビジョンを公開し、各事業分野においてAIがどのように適用されるかを具体化する計画です。また、各子会社内に専任のAI部門を新設し、技術導入の監督と内部専門性の強化を図ります。

こうした動きは、ワークフローの自動化や意思決定の改善に向けてAIの重要性が高まっている技術業界の潮流を反映しています。これはSamsungが製品開発を超え、企業運営のあり方そのものにAIを内製化するために推進する、最大規模の取り組みの一つです。

総評

Samsungは「AI Transformation（AX）」を通じて、単なる製品革新にとどまらず、組織運営全体にAIを統合する強力な実行意欲を示しています。2026年までに大規模な教育と専用部署の設置を並行して進めることで、技術エコシステムにおける生産性を最大化し、競争優位性を確保しようとする戦略的な動きと評価されます。

まとめ

Samsungは「AI Transformation（AX）」プログラムを通じて、2026年までに全従業員への大規模な教育や専任部門の設置を行い、企業運営全体にAIを統合する方針です。Google GeminiやChatGPTなどの主要ツールを導入し、製品開発のみならず組織運営の効率化と競争力の強化を目指す戦略的な動きを見せています。

Samsung 的全公司 AI 集成计划

Samsung's Enterprise-wide AI Integration Initiative

Samsung is launching a company-wide initiative to integrate artificial intelligence (AI) into its operations to enhance daily work efficiency.

The "AI Transformation" (AX) program, announced on June 9, 2026, includes the introduction of new AI tools, the implementation of employee training programs, and the formation of dedicated AI teams across the entire Samsung Group. This plan is designed to boost productivity and encourage the adoption of AI across all operational areas of the company.

Tool Adoption and Scope

As part of this project, Samsung plans to provide employees with access to several external generative AI services:

  • Google Gemini
  • ChatGPT
  • Claude

These tools are expected to be utilized across all affiliates, supporting a wide range of tasks including software development and marketing, as well as manufacturing and business operations.

Large-scale Training and Leadership Strengthening

Education is a core component of this initiative, with Samsung aiming to provide AI training to approximately 280,000 employees by the end of 2026.

The transformation begins at the leadership level; this month, about 50 group chairpersons will participate in a two-day AX Bootcamp at the Samsung Talent Development Center. Additionally, approximately 2,300 senior executives will attend separate three-day training sessions, with the first session scheduled to be completed by mid-August.

AX Vision and Organizational Restructuring

During the leadership training period, Samsung plans to unveil its group-wide AX vision and define how AI will be applied across various business sectors. Furthermore, dedicated AI departments will be established within each affiliate to oversee technology adoption and strengthen internal expertise.

These moves reflect a growing trend in the tech industry where the importance of AI for workflow automation and improved decision-making is increasing. This represents one of Samsung's most extensive efforts to internalize AI into its corporate operations, moving beyond mere product development.

Summary

Samsung is launching an "AI Transformation" (AX) program to integrate artificial intelligence across its entire organizational structure by 2026. The initiative includes providing access to major tools like Google Gemini and ChatGPT, training over 280,000 employees, and establishing dedicated internal AI departments. This strategic move aims to enhance productivity and secure a competitive advantage in the global technology ecosystem.
