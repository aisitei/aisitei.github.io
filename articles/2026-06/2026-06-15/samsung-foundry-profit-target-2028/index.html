Samsung전자 한진완: 파운드리 사업 내년에도 수익성 확보 어려워 2028년 흑자 전환 목표

2026-06-15

경영 전망

한국 선구보의 보도에 따르면, Samsung전자 DS 부문 파운드리 사업 책임자인 한진만(Han Jin-man)은 회사의 파운드리 사업이 내년에 흑자로 전환될 가능성이 낮다고 밝혔습니다. 이에 따라 경영 실적 반등을 위한 목표 시점을 2028년으로 설정하는 것이 더욱 현실적이라고 언급했습니다.

한진만 책임자는 온·오프라인 동시 진행된 관리 브리핑에서 직원들에게 "내년에 파운드리 사업의 수익성을 확보하는 것은 현재로서는 쉽지 않은 과제"라고 설명했습니다.

올해와 내년 지속적인 적자가 예상되는 구체적인 원인으로는 다음과 같은 요소들이 포함됩니다.

  • 특별 성과급으로 인한 비용 지출
  • 파운드리 사업의 모바일 고객 의존도 탈피 지연
  • 기술 준비 부족
  • 저수익 주문 과다
  • 성숙 공정 및 레거시 노드 전략의 취약점

사업 전략 및 인력 관리

한진만 책임자는 직원 급여와 관련하여 현재 가장 시급한 과제는 사업 경쟁력을 회복하는 것이라고 강조했습니다. 다만, 해당 부문에서 실질적인 성과가 나타나기 시작하면 경영진이 임직원의 처우와 복지 개선을 위해 최선을 다할 것이며, 이를 통해 직원들이 실질적인 보람을 느낄 수 있도록 할 것이라고 덧붙였습니다.

또한, Samsung의 8인치 파운드리 사업에 대해서도 언급했습니다. 해당 사업은 현재 수익을 유지하고 있으나 점점 치열해지는 경쟁에 직면해 있습니다.

그는 "시장이 점점 혼잡해지고 있으며 완전히 가격 중심적으로 변하고 있다"고 설명하며, 이에 따라 Samsung은 8인치 파운드리 공장의 운영 규모를 점진적으로 축소할 계획이라고 밝혔습니다.

핵심 기술 및 파트너십

현재 Samsung 파운드리 사업은 인공지능(AI), 차량용 반도체, 첨단 패키징 및 공정 기술 분야에서 고부가가치 수주를 확보함으로써 시장 주도권을 다시 확보하기 위해 노력하고 있습니다.

지난해 Tesla와 체결한 22조 원 규모의 차세대 AI 칩 생산 계약에 이어, Samsung은 현재 NVIDIA 플랫폼 기반의 Groq 언어 처리 장치(LPU) 및 자율주행 플랫폼용 차량용 시스템 반도체 주문을 수행하고 있습니다.

총평

晶圆代工业务盈利目标

Samsung电子 DS 部门晶圆代工业务负责人韩真晚（Han Jin-man）表示，由于当前面临多种挑战，预计明年很难实现扭亏为盈。公司目前已将 2028 年作为实现业绩反转的更现实目标。

导致经营困难的主要原因

韩真晚指出，今年和明年晶圆代工业务可能持续亏损，主要受以下因素影响：

  • 特殊绩效奖金带来的费用开支
  • 摆脱对移动端客户依赖的进展迟缓
  • 技术准备不足
  • 低利润订单过多
  • 在成熟与传统制程节点上的策略短板

业务调整与市场策略

针对不同领域的布局，Samsung 采取了不同的应对措施：

  • 8 英寸晶圆代工： 该业务目前虽仍盈利，但面临严重的价格驱动竞争，因此计划逐步缩减运营规模。
  • 高价值领域扩张： 致力于在人工智能、汽车芯片以及先进封装/制程技术领域获取更多高价值订单以夺回市场主动权。

核心合作与关键合同

Samsung 目前正通过以下重要项目强化竞争力：

  • 与特斯拉（Tesla）签署的 22 万亿韩元（约合 980.98 亿元人民币）下一代 AI 芯片生产合同。
  • 基于 NVIDIA 平台的 Groq 语言处理单元（LPU）。
  • 用于自动驾驶平台的车用系统级半导体订单。

总结

Samsung 晶圆代工业务因技术准备不足和低利润订单过多等因素，预计需到 2028 年才能实现业绩反转。目前公司正通过缩减竞争激烈的 8 英寸晶圆业务，并转向 AI 和汽车芯片等高价值领域来重塑竞争力。

経営見通し

韓国の先駆報（Sunkyubo）の報道によると、Samsung電子DS部門のファウンドリ事業責任者であるHan Jin-man氏は、同社のファウンドリ事業が来年度に黒字転換する可能性は低いとの見解を示しました。これに伴い、経営実績の回復に向けた目標時期を2028年に設定することがより現実的であると述べています。

Han Jin-man氏は、オン・オフラインの両方で行われた管理ブリーフィングにおいて、従業員に対し「来年度にファウンドリ事業の収益性を確保することは、現時点では容易な課題ではない」と説明しました。

今年および来年にわたり継続的な赤子が予想される具体的な要因には、以下の要素が含まれています。

  • 特別成果報酬によるコスト支出
  • ファウンドリ事業におけるモバイル顧客への依存からの脱却の遅れ
  • 技術準備の不足
  • 低収益な受注の過多
  • 成熟工程およびレガシーノード戦略の脆弱性

事業戦略および人員管理

Han Jin-man氏は従業員の給与に関して、現在最も緊急な課題は事業競争力の回復であると強調しました。ただし、当該部門で実質的な成果が出始めれば、経営陣は従業員の待遇や福利厚生の改善に最善を尽くし、従業員が実質的なやりがいを感じられるよう取り組むと付け加えました。

また、Samsungの8インチファウンドリ事業についても言及しました。この事業は現在収益を維持しているものの、激化する競争に直面しています。

同氏は「市場はますます混雑しており、完全に価格重視へと変化している」と説明し、これに伴いSamsungは8インチファウンドリ工場の運営規模を段階的に縮小する計画であることを明らかにしました。

核心技術およびパートナーシップ

現在、Samsungのファウンドリ事業は、人工知能（AI）、車載半導体、先端パッケージングおよび工程技術の分野で高付加価値な受注を獲得することで、市場の主導権を奪還しようと取り組んでいます。

昨年、Teslaと締結した22兆ウォン規模の次世代AIチップ生産契約に続き、Samsungは現在、NVIDIAプラットフォームベースのGroq言語処理装置（LPU）および自動運転プラットフォーム向けの車載システム半導体の受注を遂行しています。

まとめ

Samsungのファウンドリ事業は、低収益なレガシー工程やモバイル依存からの脱却に課題を抱えており、黒字転換に向けた現実的な目標を2028年に設定しています。今後は、AIや車載半導体といった高付加価値分野への注力と、競争の激しい汎用市場からの戦略的撤退を通じて、事業競争力の回復を目指します。

Business Outlook

According to a report by Hankyung, Han Jin-man, head of Samsung Electronics' DS Foundry division, stated that it is unlikely for the foundry business to turn a profit next year. Consequently, he noted that setting 2028 as the target date for a recovery in management performance is more realistic.

During an internal briefing conducted both online and offline, Han explained to employees that "securing profitability for the foundry business next year is currently not an easy task."

The specific factors contributing to the expected continued losses through this year and next include:

  • Expenditure on special performance bonuses
  • Delayed diversification away from mobile customers in the foundry business
  • Insufficient technical preparation
  • Excessive volume of low-profit orders
  • Weaknesses in mature process and legacy node strategies

Business Strategy and Workforce Management

Regarding employee salaries, Han emphasized that the most urgent task is restoring business competitiveness. However, he added that once substantial results begin to appear in this sector, management will do its best to improve the treatment and welfare of employees so they can feel a sense of practical accomplishment.

He also addressed Samsung's 8-inch foundry business. While this segment is currently maintaining profitability, it faces increasingly intense competition.

He explained that "the market is becoming more crowded and is shifting toward being entirely price-centered," and as a result, Samsung plans to gradually reduce the operating scale of its 8-inch foundry plants.

Core Technologies and Partnerships

Currently, Samsung's foundry business is striving to regain market leadership by securing high-value orders in fields such as artificial intelligence (AI), automotive semiconductors, advanced packaging, and process technologies.

Following a 22 trillion KRW contract signed with Tesla last year for the production of next-generation AI chips, Samsung is currently fulfilling orders for Groq Language Processing Units (LPUs) based on the NVIDIA platform and automotive system semiconductors for autonomous driving platforms.

Summary

Samsung's foundry division faces a challenging road to profitability, with a realistic turnaround target set for 2028 due to high costs and market pressures. The company plans to scale back its 8-inch production while pivoting toward high-value segments like AI and automotive semiconductors. Strategic partnerships with major players like Tesla and Groq are central to Samsung's strategy to regain leadership in the semiconductor market.

Foundry Business Outlook

According to reports from Korea's Seon-gu-bo, Han Jin-man, the head of Samsung Electronics' DS division foundry business, stated that it is unlikely for the company's foundry business to turn a profit next year. Consequently, the management has set 2028 as a more realistic target for achieving a performance turnaround.

During an internal briefing, Han explained that securing profitability in the coming year is currently a difficult task due to several specific factors:

  • Expenditures related to special performance bonuses
  • Delayed progress in diversifying away from mobile customer dependency
  • Insufficient technical preparation
  • An overabundance of low-margin orders
  • Strategic weaknesses in mature processes and legacy nodes

Business Strategy and Management

Regarding employee compensation, Han emphasized that the most urgent task is restoring business competitiveness. He added that once tangible results are achieved, management will do its best to improve employee benefits and welfare so that staff can feel a sense of practical reward.

Samsung is also adjusting its strategy for different market segments:

  • 8-inch Foundry: While this business remains profitable, it faces increasingly intense competition driven by price. Consequently, Samsung plans to gradually scale down the operation size of 8-inch foundry plants.
  • High-Value Expansion: The company is focusing on securing high-value orders in AI, automotive semiconductors, and advanced packaging/process technologies to regain market leadership.

Key Technologies and Partnerships

Samsung is currently working to reclaim its market position by securing high-value contracts in several key areas:

  • A production contract with Tesla for next-generation AI chips valued at approximately 22 trillion KRW.
  • Groq Language Processing Units (LPU) based on the NVIDIA platform.
  • System-on-chip (SoC) orders for autonomous driving platforms.

Summary

Samsung's foundry business is targeting 2028 for a profit turnaround due to high costs, low-margin orders, and gaps in technical preparation. The company plans to scale back its 8-inch wafer production while aggressively expanding into high-value sectors such as AI and automotive semiconductors.
