Coolpad Cool 80 사양 및 특징

  • 메모리: 6GB 물리 RAM (8GB 가상 RAM 확장 지원)

  • 디자인: iPhone에서 영감을 받은 후면 스타일과 독특한 카메라 배치

현재 제품은 JD.com에 등록되어 있으며, 배터리 사양을 제외한 대부분의 정보가 공개되었습니다. 다만 JD.com 리스팅에 기재된 4,000mAh 배터리와 10W 충전은 현재 시장의 다른 스마트폰들과 비교했을 때 다소 낮게 느껴지므로 정확한 수치 확인이 필요합니다.

Coolpad Cool 80 Launch News

Coolpad has unveiled the Coolpad Cool 80, a new budget-friendly smartphone aimed at the Chinese market. Targeting entry-level buyers, the device combines a familiar design with a proven chipset and a high refresh rate display. This release is viewed as a strategic move to strengthen the brand's position in the low-end smartphone segment.

Coolpad Cool 80 Specifications and Features

The Coolpad Cool 80 features a high refresh rate display to enhance visual satisfaction.

  • Display: 6.7-inch IPS LCD, HD+ resolution, 90Hz refresh rate
  • Dimensions: 168.5 x 76.3 x 8.2mm

Internal performance is powered by the MediaTek Helio G99 processor, which has proven its stability in various budget and mid-range devices over the past few years.

  • Memory: 6GB physical RAM (supports 8GB virtual RAM expansion)
  • Storage: 128GB internal memory (includes microSD card slot)

Regarding camera and design, the device features the following:

  • Rear Camera: 13MP
  • Front Camera: 5MP
  • Design: A rear style inspired by the iPhone with a unique camera layout

The product is currently listed on JD.com, and most information has been disclosed except for specific battery details. However, since the 4,000mAh battery and 10W charging listed on JD.com seem somewhat low compared to other smartphones in the current market, these figures may require further verification.

Coolpad Cool 80 Price and Availability

The Coolpad Cool 80 is released as a single model with the following sales information:

  • Price: 819 Yuan (approximately $120)
  • Color Options: Rock Sand Black, Dune Orange, Gauze White

There is currently no confirmation regarding an international release for the Coolpad Cool 80. As the manufacturer has not announced any plans for global markets, it appears to be available only through Chinese channels at this time.

Summary

The Coolpad Cool 80 is a typical budget-friendly smartphone offering a high refresh rate display and expandable memory at an affordable price point. While it features a stylish design and practical specifications, further confirmation on battery capacity and charging speeds is needed to fully assess its capabilities.
