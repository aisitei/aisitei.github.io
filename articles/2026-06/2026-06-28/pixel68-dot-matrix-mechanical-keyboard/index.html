Product Introduction

AJAZZ recently launched the Pixel68, a 3-mode mechanical keyboard. This model features a 65% layout designed to emphasize "deskterior" aesthetics. The back of the device is equipped with a 10.6-inch pixel dot screen consisting of 260 LEDs (52x5), allowing users to customize various display effects.

Specifications and Features

The Pixel68 features an ABS plastic body with an integrated knob located in the upper right corner. The internal structure utilizes a Gasket mount system, includes five layers of filling material, and features RGB LED backlighting.

  • Connectivity: Wired USB-C and Wireless 2.4GHz
  • Polling Rate: Supports 8kHz for both modes
  • Advanced Features: Equipped with advanced key functions including SOCD, MT, and TGL
  • Battery Capacity: 10,000mAh built-in battery

Options and Pricing

AJAZZ offers the Pixel68 in two different switch versions: "Side Engraved Black-White Peach" and "Glass Low-Ice Sea Elf."

  • Side Engraved Black-White Peach: 399 RMB
  • Glass Low-Ice Sea Elf: 449 RMB

Summary

The AJAZZ Pixel68 is a 65% mechanical keyboard that combines high-performance features like an 8kHz polling rate and SOCD support with a unique 10.6-inch pixel dot screen for customized desk aesthetics. With a massive 10,000mAh battery and two distinct switch options, it caters to both performance-oriented users and those focused on customization.
