사진 촬영을 위해 X80 Pro Max는 OIS와 CIPA 6.0 등급 안정화 시스템을 갖춘 500만 화소 후면 카메라를 포함하며, 셀카는 800만 화소 전면 카메라로 촬영합니다.

이 스마트폰은 Android 16 기반의 MagicOS 10.0을 실행하며, 백그라운드 메모리 관리를 위한 Hummingbird Architecture 2.0으로 구동됩니다. 칩셋은 Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 플랫폼입니다. 기타 기능으로는 통화를 위한 AI 그립 감지, NFC, 적외선 리모컨, 스테레오 듀얼 스피커가 있습니다.

The Honor X80 Pro Max has launched in China. This device features a massive 11,000mAh battery—the largest ever found in a smartphone—and a display boasting an impressive 10,000nit brightness. As previously noted, the Honor X80 Pro Max is a mid-range smartphone that focuses heavily on battery life and display brightness.

Honor X80 Pro Max Specifications

The Honor X80 Pro Max features a 6.8-inch screen with 1.3mm bezels. It supports a 1.5K resolution (1280x2788) and a 120Hz refresh rate, while its 10,000nit peak brightness is an industry first.

Additionally, the 11,000mAh battery supports 90W wired fast charging and 27W reverse wired charging. Honor announced that this smartphone achieved a Guinness World Record for continuous smartphone live streaming, lasting 26 hours, 8 minutes, and 34 seconds.

For photography, the X80 Pro Max includes a 5MP rear camera equipped with OIS and a CIPA 6.0 stabilization system, while selfies are captured via an 8MP front camera.

The device runs MagicOS 10.0 based on Android 16, powered by Hummingbird Architecture 2.0 for background memory management. The chipset is the Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 platform. Other features include AI grip detection for calls, NFC, an infrared remote, and stereo dual speakers.
