Product Overview

According to IT Home, vivo has officially launched the Y6a model on its official website. The device is equipped with a Qualcomm Snapdragon 4 Gen2 chipset, 8GB of RAM, and 256GB of storage, priced at 1,999 RMB.

Design and Display

The device is available in three colors and features a thickness of 8.39mm. It also boasts "6-minute IP69 / 24-hour IP68" water resistance capabilities.

The front features a 6.75-inch LCD punch-hole display with a resolution of 1570x720 and a 120Hz refresh rate, reaching a peak brightness of 1,200 nits.

Camera

  • Front: 8MP selfie camera
  • Rear: 50MP main camera

  • Processor: Qualcomm Snapdragon 4 Gen2
  • Memory: 8GB LPDDR4X RAM
  • Storage: 256GB UFS 3.1
  • Battery: 7200mAh (supports 44W wired charging)

The device runs on the OriginOS 6 system based on Android 16.

Summary

The vivo Y6a is a budget-friendly smartphone featuring a Qualcomm Snapdragon 4 Gen2 processor, a massive 7200mAh battery, and robust water resistance. Running on OriginOS 6 (Android 16), it provides solid specifications for its price point.
