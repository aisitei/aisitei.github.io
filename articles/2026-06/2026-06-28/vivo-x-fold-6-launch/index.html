Vivo X Fold 6 Launch

Vivo has launched the Vivo X Fold 6, a new book-style foldable smartphone in China. This model marks a significant milestone as it is the brand's first foldable to feature a MediaTek chipset, departing from the Snapdragon processors used in previous generations. Additionally, it features the largest battery capacity ever in a Vivo foldable and introduces a 200MP rear camera system for the first time in its foldable lineup.

Vivo X Fold 6 Specifications and Key Features

The inner display is an 8.02-inch AMOLED foldable display with a resolution of 2504 x 2312, supporting LTPO variable refresh rates from 1Hz to 120Hz. It features Samsung M14 materials, peak brightness of up to 5,000 nits, 1,920Hz PWM dimming, and UTG protection.

The outer display measures 6.51 inches with a resolution of 2528 x 1120, featuring an LTPO refresh rate from 1Hz to 120Hz and a BOE Q11 panel. This screen also supports peak brightness of up to 5,000 nits and is protected by 2nd-generation Armour Glass. Both the inner and outer displays are equipped with 20MP selfie cameras.

The rear camera system features the following specifications:

  • Main Camera: 200MP Samsung HPB sensor (OIS supported)
  • Ultra-wide Camera: 50MP
  • Telephoto Camera: 50MP Sony LYTIA 602 Periscope telephoto camera (equipped with VCS technology and OIS)
  • Accessory Support: Compatible with Vivo Zeiss Teleconverter G2

In terms of performance, the device is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 9500 Super Edition chipset and a Vivo V3+ image chip, running on LPDDR5X Ultra RAM and UFS 4.1 storage. The battery is a 7,000mAh Blue Ocean battery utilizing 3rd-generation solid-state technology, supporting 80W wired charging, 40W wireless charging, and 5W reverse charging. The software is OriginOS 6 Fold based on Android 16.

Other key specifications include:

  • Durability and Connectivity: IP58 and IP59 ratings, Wi-Fi 7, USB 3.2 Gen 1, NFC, and IR blaster
  • Hardware Configuration: X-axis linear motor, stereo speakers, and a side fingerprint scanner
  • Dimensions and Weight: 4.4mm to 4.8mm thickness when unfolded; 9.4mm to 9.9mm when folded; weight between 228g and 235g

Vivo X Fold 6 Pricing and Availability

The Vivo X Fold 6 is available in various configurations:

  • 12GB+256GB: CNY 7,999 (~$1,175)
  • 12GB+512GB: CNY 8,999 (~$1,320)
  • 16GB+512GB: CNY 9,999 (~$1,470)
  • 16GB+1TB: CNY 10,999 (~$1,615)
  • Black Gold Edition (16GB+1TB): CNY 11,299 (~$1,660)

Users can choose from colors including Blue Cave, Salt Lake, Extreme Night, and Black Gold Edition. Information regarding a global release has not been disclosed yet.

Summary

The Vivo X Fold 6 pushes the boundaries of foldable devices by combining a MediaTek Dimensity 9500 Super Edition chipset with a massive 7,000mAh solid-state battery. It offers a premium photography experience through its 200MP main camera and dedicated image chip while maintaining high durability and a sleek design.
