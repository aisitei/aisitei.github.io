성능 및 기능

또한 3축 기계식 짐벌 안정화 기술을 통해 보행, 팔로잉, 장면 전환 시에도 흔들림 없는 영상을 제공하며, 스마트 팔로잉(Smart Follow) 8.0 기술이 탑재되어 전 초점 영역 추적, 다인 추적 및 피사체 추적 모드를 지원합니다. 특히 최대 12배 줌 상태에서도 안정적인 추적이 가능합니다. 기기 내에서 실시간으로 피부 보정, 미백 수준, 피부톤의 온도를 조절할 수 있으며, 6종의 필름 스타일이 내장되어 더욱 스타일리시한 영상을 연출할 수 있습니다.

소프트웨어 및 확장성

내부 사양으로는 103GB의 저장 공간을 내장하여 별도의 메모리 카드 없이도 녹화가 가능하며, USB 3.1 프로토콜을 지원해 유선 전송 속도가 최대 800MB/s에 달합니다. 배터리는 최대 210분간 지속되며 고속충전을 지원합니다. 또한 Wi-Fi 라이브 스트리밍, 온라인 회의, 웹캠 모드 및 인체 내 타임코드 등 전문적인 기능을 갖추고 있습니다. 필라이트, 미용 소프트 라이트 렌즈, ND 필터 키트, 원격 컨트롤러 등 다양한 액세서리를 선택하여 생태계를 확장할 수 있습니다.

Product Overview

According to IT news, DJI has officially announced the Osmo Pocket 4P, a pocket cinematic camera featuring dual main cameras. The new device is equipped with a 1-inch wide-angle main camera that supports 17-bit dynamic range and D-Log 2 color. Additionally, it includes a 60mm medium telephoto lens capable of capturing slow-motion footage at up to 4K/240 FPS. The product starts at 3,799 RMB and is available for immediate pre-order.

Camera Specifications

The Osmo Pocket 4P provides two primary lens systems to deliver powerful optical performance.

  • Wide Main Camera: 20mm equivalent focal length, f/2.0 aperture, and 17-bit dynamic range powered by LOFIC technology
  • Medium Telephoto Lens: 60mm focal length, f/1.8 aperture, supporting 3x optical zoom and up to 12x magnification

Performance and Features

This camera supports the new D-Log 2 color mode and 10-bit color depth, allowing it to maintain richer details in both highlight and shadow areas. It supports "cinematic" ultra-high-definition slow motion at up to 4K/240 FPS. In video mode, users can also utilize slow shutter speeds to capture light trails or motion blur.

The device features a 3-axis mechanical gimbal stabilization system to provide steady footage during walking, following, and scene transitions. It is equipped with Smart Follow 8.0 technology, which supports full-focus area tracking, multi-person tracking, and subject tracking modes. Notably, stable tracking is maintained even at up to 12x zoom. The device allows for real-time adjustments to skin retouching, whitening levels, and skin tone temperature, while six built-in film styles allow users to create more stylish visuals.

Software and Expandability

When connected to the DJI Mimo app, the Osmo Pocket 4P offers a wider range of beauty features. Users can perform post-production and color correction through pre-set editing presets and color recipes. The product is available in two colors: Black and White.

Regarding internal specifications, it features 103GB of built-in storage, allowing for recording without an external memory card. It supports the USB 3.1 protocol, reaching wired transmission speeds of up to 800MB/s. The battery lasts up to 210 minutes and supports fast charging. Furthermore, it includes professional features such as Wi-Fi live streaming, online meeting mode, webcam mode, and internal timecode. The ecosystem can be expanded with various accessories, including FlyLight, soft light lenses, ND filter kits, and remote controllers.
