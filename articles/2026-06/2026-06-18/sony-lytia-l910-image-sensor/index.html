Sony 첫 LOFIC 이미지 센서 LYTIA L910 출시: 1/1.28인치, 50MP

2026-06-18

신제품 발표

Sony Semiconductor Shanghai가 차세대 이미지 센서 LYTIA L910을 발표했습니다. 이는 LYTIA 시리즈 중 처음으로 LOFIC 기술이 적용된 센서로, 저전력 환경에서도 100dB의 높은 다이내믹 레인지를 구현할 수 있는 것이 특징입니다.

LOFIC 기술 설명

LOFIC(lateral overflow integration capacitors)은 차세대 HDR 촬영 기술로, 단일 픽셀 내에 선형 및 로그 방식의 두 가지 전하 수용 방식을 통합하여 CMOS 센서의 다이내믹 레인지를 향상시킵니다. 이를 통해 스마트폰이 명암비가 큰 장면에서도 더욱 풍부한 디테일을 담은 사진을 제공할 수 있습니다.

주요 사양

  • 해상도: 50MP
  • 픽셀 크기: 1.22μm
  • 센서 크기: 1/1.28인치

핵심 기능 및 장점

  • 포화 전하량(Full Well) 확대: 전하 수용 능력을 높여 하이라이트 영역의 전환을 더욱 자연스럽게 표현합니다.
  • 넓은 다이내믹 레인지: 하이라이트 과노출과 암부 노출 부족을 줄이며, 강한 조명 아래에서도 뛰어난 촬영 성능을 제공합니다.
  • 낮은 읽기 노이즈: 저조도 환경에서 세밀한 디테일을 표현하며, 화면을 더욱 깨끗하고 노이즈가 적게 구현합니다.
  • 저전력 성능: 4K 60fps HDR 비디오를 지속적으로 촬영할 수 있으며, 안정적이고 매끄러운 구동과 긴 지속성을 보장합니다.

기술적 특징 및 상세 사양

Sony의 공식 설명에 따르면, LYTIA L910은 광전 다이오드의 과잉 전하를 축적할 수 있는 LOFIC 구조를 채택하여 기존 제품 대비 포화 전하량(Full Well)을 더욱 확대했습니다. 동시에 'Triple Conversion Gain-HDR(TCG-HDR)' 기술을 적용하여, 단 한 번의 노출로 얻은 전하를 세 가지 다른 변환 효율로 읽어냄으로써 밝은 영역의 과노출 현상과 암부 및 중간 톤 영역의 노이즈를 효과적으로 억제하고 고화질 이미지를 구현합니다.

또한, 전하를 전압으로 변환하는 효율을 높이는 UHCG(Ultra High Conversion Gain) 회로 기술을 통해 무작위 노이즈 발생률을 기존 제품 대비 약 30% 낮추었으며, 이를 통해 저조도 환경에서의 화질을 크게 개선했습니다.

촬영 성능 및 기대 효과

LYTIA L910은 단 한 번의 노출로 100dB의 다이내믹 레인지를 실현할 수 있어, 저조도 환경에서 노이즈로 인한 입자감을 억제할 뿐만 아니라 명암 대비가 큰 환경에서도 부드러운 계층 표현을 가능하게 합니다. 또한 멀티 노출 과정이 필요 없으므로 동적인 물체의 블러 현상을 방지하며, 노출 시간을 늘려 촬영 신호의 신호 대 잡음비(SNR)를 높이고 조명 플리커(Flicker) 현상을 억제합니다.

양산 및 출시 일정

Sony Semiconductor 공식 홈페이지에 따르면, LYTIA L910은 2026년 여름부터 양산 및 출하가 시작될 예정입니다.

총평

新一代图像传感器 LYTIA L910 发布

Sony半导体上海公司发布了新一代图像传感器 LYTIA L910。这是 LYTIA 系列首款采用 LOFIC（lateral overflow integration capacitors）技术的产品，旨在低功耗状态下实现 100dB 的高动态范围拍摄。LOFIC 技术通过在单像素中整合线性与对数两种电荷容纳方式，显著提升 CMOS 传感器的动态范围。

核心规格与性能亮点

LYTIA L910 在硬件参数和功能表现上具有以下特点：

  • 基础规格： 50MP 分辨率、1.22μm 像素尺寸、1/1.28 英寸传感器尺寸。
  • 高动态范围： 单次曝光即可实现 100dB 动态范围，有效减少高光过曝与暗部欠曝。
  • 低读出噪声： 通过 UHCG（Ultra High Conversion Gain）电路技术，使随机噪点发生率较传统产品降低约 30%。
  • 高效能表现： 支持 4K 60fps HDR 视频持续录制，确保在高性能拍摄时保持冷静流畅。

核心技术原理

LYTIA L910 集成了多种关键技术以优化成像效果：

  • LOFIC 结构： 通过可积累光电二极管溢出电荷，显著扩大饱和电荷量，使高光过渡更自然。
  • TCG-HDR 技术： 采用 Triple Conversion Gain-HDR 技术，通过三种不同转换效率读取单次曝光的电荷，抑制暗部至中间调区域的噪点。
  • 优化成像体验： 无需多次曝光即可实现高画质，有效避免动态物体的模糊并抑制照明频闪。

量产计划

根据 Sony 官方信息，LYTIA L910 预计将于 2026 年夏季开始量产出货。

总结

Sony 推出的 LYTIA L910 是首款搭载 LOFIC 技术的新一代图像传感器，具备 50MP 分辨率和 1/1.28 英寸尺寸。该产品通过 TCG-HDR 和 UHCG 等核心技术实现 100dB 的高动态范围及低噪点表现，预计将于 2026 年夏季量产。

新製品の発表

Sony Semiconductor Shanghaiは、次世代イメージセンサー「LYTIA L910」を発表しました。これはLYTIAシリーズで初めてLOFIC技術を搭載したセンサーであり、低電力環境下でも100dBの高いダイナミックレンジを実現できるのが特徴です。

LOFIC技術の解説

LOFIC（lateral overflow integration capacitors）は次世代HDR撮影技術であり、単一ピクセル内に線形およびログ方式の2種類の電荷受容方式を統合することで、CMOSセンサーのダイナミックレンジを向上させます。これにより、スマートフォンにおいて明暗差の激しいシーンでも、より豊かなディテールを含んだ写真を提供することが可能になります。

主要スペック

  • 解像度: 50MP
  • ピクセルサイズ: 1.22μm
  • センサーサイズ: 1/1.28インチ

主な機能とメリット

  • フルウェル（Full Well）の拡大：電荷受容能力を高めることで、ハイライト領域の階調をより自然に表現します。
  • 広いダイナミックレンジ：ハイライトの白飛びやシャドウの露出不足を抑え、強い照明下でも優れた撮影性能を提供します。
  • 低い読み出しノイズ：低照度環境で細部を鮮明に描き出し、よりクリアでノイズの少ない映像を実現します。
  • 低電力性能：4K 60fps HDRビデオの継続的な撮影が可能であり、安定した動作と長時間の駆動を保証します。

技術的特徴および詳細仕様

Sonyの公式説明によると、LYTIA L910は光電ダイオードの過剰電荷を蓄積できるLOFIC構造を採用しており、従来製品と比較してフルウェル（Full Well）容量を拡大しています。同時に「Triple Conversion Gain-HDR（TCG-HDR）」技術を適用することで、一度の露光で得られた電荷を3種類の異なる変換効率で読み取り、明るい領域の白飛びや暗部・中間トーンのノイズを効果的に抑制し、高画質な画像を実現します。

また、電荷を電圧に変換する効率を高めるUHCG（Ultra High Conversion Gain）回路技術により、ランダムノイズの発生率を従来製品と比較して約30%低減しており、これにより低照度環境での画質を大幅に改善しています。

撮影性能と期待される効果

LYTIA L910は単一の露光で100dBのダイナミックレンジを実現できるため、低照度環境におけるノイズによる粒状感を抑えるだけでなく、明暗差の激しい環境でも滑らかな階調表現を可能にします。さらに、マルチエクスポージャーの工程を必要としないため、動く物体のブレを防ぎ、露光時間を延ばすことで撮影信号のSN比（SNR）を高め、照明のフリッカー現象も抑制します。

量産およびリリーススケジュール

Sony Semiconductorの公式サイトによると、LYTIA L910は2026年夏から量産および出荷が開始される予定です。

New Product Announcement

Sony Semiconductor Shanghai has unveiled the LYTIA L910, a next-generation image sensor. As the first in the LYTIA series to incorporate LOFIC technology, it is designed to achieve a high dynamic range of 100dB even in low-power environments.

LOFIC Technology Overview

LOFIC (lateral overflow integration capacitors) is a next-generation HDR imaging technology that integrates two types of charge storage—linear and logarithmic—within a single pixel to enhance the dynamic range of CMOS sensors. This allows smartphones to capture images with richer detail in scenes featuring high contrast.

Key Specifications

  • Resolution: 50MP
  • Pixel Size: 1.22μm
  • Sensor Size: 1/1.28-inch

Core Features and Benefits

  • Expanded Full Well Capacity: Increases charge storage capacity to provide more natural transitions in highlight areas.
  • Wide Dynamic Range: Reduces overexposure in highlights and underexposure in shadows, providing superior performance under intense lighting.
  • Low Read Noise: Captures fine details in low-light environments, resulting in cleaner images with less noise.
  • Low Power Performance: Supports continuous 4K 60fps HDR video recording, ensuring stable operation and extended endurance.

Technical Features and Detailed Specifications

According to Sony's official description, the LYTIA L910 adopts a LOFIC structure capable of accumulating excess charge in photodiodes, significantly expanding the Full Well capacity compared to previous products. Simultaneously, it utilizes "Triple Conversion Gain-HDR" (TCG-HDR) technology, which reads charges at three different conversion efficiencies from a single exposure. This effectively suppresses overexposure in bright areas and reduces noise in shadow and mid-tone regions to produce high-quality images.

Furthermore, the UHCG (Ultra High Conversion Gain) circuit technology improves the efficiency of converting charge into voltage, reducing random noise by approximately 30% compared to previous models. This significantly enhances image quality in low-light environments.

Capture Performance and Expected Results

The LYTIA L910 achieves a dynamic range of 100dB with a single exposure, which not only suppresses noise grain in low-light conditions but also enables smooth gradations in high-contrast scenes. Additionally, because it does not require multi-exposure processing, it prevents motion blur on moving objects and improves the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) by extending exposure time, while also suppressing lighting flicker.

Mass Production and Release Schedule

According to the official Sony Semiconductor website, mass production and shipping of the LYTIA L910 are scheduled to begin in the summer of 2026.

Summary

The LYTIA L910 is a next-generation image sensor that utilizes LOFIC and TCG-HDR technologies to achieve a 100dB dynamic range with a single exposure. It offers significant improvements in low-light noise reduction and high-contrast detail while maintaining low power consumption for high-frame-rate video. Mass production of the sensor is expected to begin in the summer of 2026.

LYTIA L910 通过多种关键技术提升影像质量：

  • 扩大饱和电荷量： 利用 LOFIC 结构提高电荷容纳能力，使高光过渡更加自然。
  • 宽动态范围： 有效减少高光过曝与暗部欠曝，增强强光下的成像表现。
  • 低读出噪声： 通过 UHCG（Ultra High Conversion Gain）电路技术，将随机噪点发生率降低约 30%，提升低照度环境的画质。
  • 低功耗表现： 支持 4K 60fps HDR 视频持续录制，确保拍摄过程稳定且流畅。

此外，该传感器采用 Triple Conversion Gain-HDR（TCG-HDR）技术，通过三种不同转换效率读取单次曝光的电荷，在抑制高亮区域过曝的同时，减少暗部至中间调区域的噪点。由于无需多次曝光，该技术还能有效避免动态物体的模糊并抑制照明频闪。

LOFIC（lateral overflow integration capacitors）は次世代のHDR撮影技術です。単一ピクセル内に線形およびログの2種類の電荷受容方式を統合することで、CMOSセンサーのダイナミックレンジを向上させます。これにより、スマートフォンにおいて明暗差の激しいシーンでも、より豊かなディテールを含んだ写真を捉えることが可能になります。

New Product Announcement

Sony Semiconductor Shanghai has unveiled the LYTIA L910, a next-generation image sensor. As the first in the LYTIA series to incorporate LOFIC technology, it is designed to achieve a high dynamic range of 100dB even in low-power environments.

LOFIC Technology Overview

LOFIC (lateral overflow integration capacitors) is an advanced HDR imaging technology that integrates two types of charge storage—linear and logarithmic—within a single pixel to enhance the dynamic range of CMOS sensors. This allows smartphones to capture images with richer detail in scenes featuring high contrast between light and shadow.

Key Specifications

  • Resolution: 50MP
  • Pixel Size: 1.22μm
  • Sensor Size: 1/1.28-inch

Core Features and Benefits

  • Expanded Full Well Capacity: Increases charge storage capacity to provide more natural transitions in highlight areas.
  • Wide Dynamic Range: Reduces overexposure in highlights and underexposure in shadows, providing superior performance under intense lighting.
  • Low Read Noise: Captures fine details in low-light environments, resulting in cleaner images with less noise.
  • Low Power Performance: Supports continuous 4K 60fps HDR video recording, ensuring stable operation and extended endurance.

Technical Features and Detailed Specifications

According to Sony's official description, the LYTIA L910 adopts a LOFIC structure capable of accumulating excess charge in photodiodes, significantly expanding its Full Well capacity compared to previous products. Simultaneously, it incorporates "Triple Conversion Gain-HDR (TCG-HDR)" technology; by reading charges from a single exposure at three different conversion efficiencies, it effectively suppresses overexposure in bright areas and noise in shadow and mid-tone regions to produce high-quality images.

Furthermore, the UHCG (Ultra High Conversion Gain) circuit technology improves the efficiency of converting charge into voltage, reducing random noise by approximately 30% compared to previous products. This significantly improves image quality in low-light environments. Because the LYTIA L910 achieves a 100dB dynamic range in a single exposure, it eliminates the need for multi-exposure processing, which prevents motion blur of moving objects and suppresses lighting flicker while improving the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) by increasing exposure time.

Production and Release Schedule

According to Sony Semiconductor's official website, mass production and shipping of the LYTIA L910 are scheduled to begin in Summer 2026.

The LYTIA L910 is a new 50MP image sensor from Sony that introduces LOFIC and TCG-HDR technologies to achieve a high dynamic range of 100dB. It features significantly reduced noise through UHCG circuitry and supports stable 4K 60fps HDR video recording in low-power environments. Mass production for the sensor is expected to begin in the summer of 2026.
