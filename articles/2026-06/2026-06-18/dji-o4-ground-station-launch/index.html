제품 개요

DJI는 산업용 드론 애플리케이션을 위한 DJI O4 Ground Station을 발표했습니다.

이 제품은 고정형 원거리 전송 강화 시스템으로, 전문 드론이 신호 간섭, 장애물 차단 또는 장거리 작업 시 겪는 통신 신뢰성 문제를 해결하기 위해 설계되었습니다. 해당 제품의 시작 가격은 14,999 위안이며, 현재 DJI 산업용 인증 채널을 통해 구매가 가능합니다.

전송 및 신호 성능

DJI O4 Ground Station은 내장 및 외장 안테나가 결합된 12개 안테나 전방향 배열을 통합하여 수평 및 수직 듀얼 폴라리제이션을 지원하며, 어떤 비행 방향에서도 안정적인 고이득 신호 포착이 가능합니다. 고성능 RF 구성 요소는 신호 투과력과 커버리지를 크게 향상시키는 동시에 신호 대 잡음비(SNR)와 수신 감도를 최적화하여, 전자기 간섭이 심한 환경에서도 미세한 신호를 복구할 수 있습니다.

또한 이 장치는 sub-2GHz, 2.4GHz, 5.2GHz, 5.8GHz 등 여러 주파수 대역의 지능형 자동 전환을 지원하며, 간섭이 감지될 경우 사용 가능한 주파수로 원활하게 전환되어 비디오 전송의 연속성을 보장합니다.

위치 정보 및 정밀도

O4 Ground Station은 고이득 RTK 모듈을 탑재하여 전 세계 5대 위성 시스템의 19개 주파수를 추적하며, 도심 빌딩 숲이나 울창한 숲과 같은 복잡한 환경에서도 지속적으로 센티미터급 위치 정보를 제공합니다.

작동 모드

이 장치는 두 가지 주요 작동 모드를 제공합니다. 게이트웨이(Gateway) 모드에서 O4 Ground Station은 이더넷 또는 DJI 셀룰러 암호화 동글 2를 통해 DJI FlightHub 2 클라우드 플랫폼에 직접 연결되며, 특정 기지국에 종속되지 않고 배치될 수 있습니다. DJI Dock 3와 결합 시 작업 범위가 최대 30km까지 확장됩니다. 이 모드는 FlightHub 2의 가상 콕핏 기능을 지원하여 운영자가 원격으로 비행 제어권을 확보하고 필요시 미리 설정된 경로를 수행할 수 있게 합니다. 또한 신호 지도 기능은 비행 데이터를 기반으로 신호 약세 지역을 분석 및 시각화하여 운영자가 지상국 배치 위치를 정확하게 계획하도록 돕습니다.

중계(Relay) 모드에서 O4 Ground Station은 셀룰러 네트워크가 없는 외딴 지역에서 신호 중계 노드로 설치되어 드론의 비디오 신호를 컨트롤러나 기지국으로 전달합니다. Matrice 400과 결합 시 중계 전송 거리는 최대 40km에 달하며, Dock 3, Matrice 400, Matrice 4E, Matrice 4T, Matrice 4D, Matrice 4TD 및 FlyCart 100 등 다양한 기종과 호환됩니다.

내구성 및 전력

환경 적응성 측면에서 DJI O4 Ground Station은 IP67 등급의 방진·방수 성능을 갖추었으며, -40°C에서 55°C 사이의 온도 범위에서 안정적으로 작동합니다. 유지보수가 필요 없는 설계와 이상 상황 시 자동 복구 기능을 지원하며, 지능형 저전력 설계를 통해 드론이 연결되지 않을 때 자동으로 절전 모드로 진입하여 대기 전력을 7W로 낮춥니다.

또한 태양광 발전 솔루션을 지원하여 상시 무인 운영 환경에 적합합니다. 추가로 GB/T 28181 프로토콜을 준수하는 네트워크 카메라를 연결하여 제3자 카메라의 실시간 영상을 DJI FlightHub 2 플랫폼으로 통합할 수 있으며, ESDK 2.0 기반의 RID 및 ADS-B 수신기를 연결하여 주변 드론이나 유인 항공기의 실시간 상황을 파악할 수 있습니다.

상세 사양

产品概述

DJI 推出了面向行业级无人机应用的 DJI O4 地面站。这是一款固定式远距离图传增强系统，旨在解决专业无人机在信号干扰、障碍物遮挡或长距离作业场景下的通信可靠性问题。该产品的起售价为 14999 元。

核心技术特性

DJI O4 地面站通过高性能硬件提升信号稳定性：

  • 天线系统： 集成由内置与外置天线构成的 12 天线全向阵列，支持水平与垂直双极化。
  • 频段切换： 支持 sub-2GHz、2.4GHz、5.2GHz、5.8GHz 等多个频段的智能自动切换，确保在干扰环境下传输流畅。
  • 高精度定位： 搭载高增益 RTK 模块，支持全球五大卫星系统的 19 个频段，可在复杂环境中提供厘米级定位信息。

工作模式与兼容性

该设备提供两种主要工作模式以适应不同场景：

  • 网关模式： 通过以太网或 DJI 蜂窝加密狗 2 直连 DJI 司空 2 云平台。配合 DJI Dock 3 使用时，可将作业覆盖范围扩展至 30 公里，并支持虚拟座舱功能和信号地图功能。
  • 中继模式： 在无蜂窝网络的偏远地区作为信号中继节点。搭配 Matrice 400 使用时，中继图传距离可达 40 公里。该模式兼容 Matrice 400、Matrice 4E、Matrice 4T、Matrice 4D、Matrice 4TD 及 FlyCart 100 等机型。

环境适应性与扩展能力

针对严苛的工业环境，DJI O4 地面站具备以下特性：

  • 防护性能： 支持 IP67 级防尘防水，工作温度范围为 -40°C 至 55°C。
  • 低功耗设计： 无无人机接入时进入休眠模式，待命功耗仅为 7 瓦，并支持太阳能供电方案。
  • 第三方集成： 支持符合 GB/T 28181 协议的网络摄像机以及基于 ESDK 2.0 开发的 RID 与 ADS-B 接收机。

详细技术参数

以下为 DJI O4 地面站的核心硬件与性能指标：

  • 定位精度： 网络 RTK 标定下水平可达 1.0 厘米（RMS），垂直 3.0 厘米（RMS）。
  • 图传距离： 在无干扰、无遮挡情况下，与 Matrice 400 的最大距离为 40 公里；在有干扰的情况下，弱干扰环境可达 18 至 40 公里。
  • 供电规格： 支持 PoE 供电及 9V/12V PD 充电，内置电池可在断电情况下提供约 30-45 分钟的续航。
  • 物理参数： 重量为 2.68 千克，尺寸为 170 x 170 x 352 mm，具备 IK07 抗冲击性能。

总结

DJI O4 地面站是一款专为工业级无人机设计的远距离图传增强系统，通过多频段切换、高增益 RTK 定位和双模式（网关/中继）设计，显著提升了复杂环境下的通信可靠性。该设备具备极高的环境耐受性和第三方设备集成能力，是专业远程作业场景的强力辅助工具。

伝送および信号性能

DJI O4 Ground Stationは、内蔵および外装アンテナを組み合わせた12個のアンテナ全指向性アレイを統合し、水平および垂直のデュアルポーラリゼーションをサポートします。これにより、あらゆる飛行方向において安定した高利得信号の捕捉が可能となります。高性能なRFコンポーネントは、信号の透過性とカバレッジを大幅に向上させると同時に、信号対雑音比（SNR）と受信感度を最適化し、電磁干渉の激しい環境でも微弱な信号を復元します。

また、この装置はsub-2GHz、2.4GHz、5.2GHz、5.8GHzなどの複数の周波数帯域におけるインテリジェントな自動切替をサポートしており、干渉を検知した際に利用可能な周波数へスムーズに切り替わることで、ビデオ伝送の継続性を保証します。

位置情報と精度

O4 Ground Stationは高利得RTKモジュールを搭載し、世界5つの主要な衛星システムから19の周波数を追跡します。これにより、都市部のビル群やうっそうとした森のような複雑な環境でも、継続的にセンチメートル級の位置情報を提供します。

動作モード

この装置は2つの主要な動作モードを提供します。 「ゲートウェイ（Gateway）モード」では、O4 Ground StationがイーサネットまたはDJIセルラー暗号化ドングル2を介してDJI FlightHub 2クラウドプラットフォームに直接接続され、特定の基地局に依存せずに配置できます。DJI Dock 3と組み合わせることで、作業範囲は最大30kmまで拡張されます。このモードはFlightHub 2のバーチャルコックピット機能をサポートし、運用者が遠隔で飛行制御権を確保し、必要に応じて事前設定された経路を実行できるようにします。また、信号マップ機能により、飛行データに基づいた信号の弱いエリアを分析・可視化し、地上局の配置場所を正確に計画するのに役立ちます。

「中継（Relay）モード」では、セルラーネットワークのない人里離れた場所で信号中継ノードとして設置され、ドローンのビデオ信号をコントローラーや基地局へ伝送します。Matrice 400と組み合わせた場合、中継伝送距離は最大40kmに達し、Dock 3、Matrice 400、Matrice 4E、Matrice 4T、Matrice 4D、Matrice 4TD、およびFlyCart 100など、多様な機材との互換性を備えています。

耐久性と電力

環境適応性において、DJI O4 Ground StationはIP67等級の防塵・防水性能を備え、-40°Cから55°Cの温度範囲で安定して動作します。メンテナンスフリーの設計と異常時の自動復旧機能を備え、インテリジェントな低電力設計により、ドローンが接続されていない際に自動的に省電力モードに入り、待機電力を7Wまで抑えます。

さらに、太陽光発電ソリューションをサポートしているため、常時無人運用環境に適しています。加えて、GB/T 28181プロトコルに準拠するネットワークカメラを接続することで、サードパーティ製カメラのリアルタイム映像をDJI FlightHub 2プラットフォームに統合でき、ESDK 2.0ベースのRIDおよびADS-B受信機を接続して周辺のドローンや有人航空機の状況をリアルタイムで把握することも可能です。

Product Overview

DJI has announced the DJI O4 Ground Station, designed specifically for industrial drone applications.

This product is a fixed long-range transmission enhancement system engineered to solve communication reliability issues caused by signal interference, physical obstacles, or long-distance operations. The starting price is 14,999 RMB, and it is currently available through DJI's authorized industrial channels.

Transmission and Signal Performance

The DJI O4 Ground Station integrates a 12-antenna omnidirectional array combining internal and external antennas to support horizontal and vertical dual polarization. This ensures stable high-gain signal capture regardless of the flight direction. High-performance RF components significantly improve penetration and coverage while optimizing Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) and reception sensitivity, allowing for the recovery of faint signals even in environments with heavy electromagnetic interference.

Additionally, the device supports intelligent automatic switching across multiple frequency bands to ensure continuous video transmission by automatically switching to available frequencies when interference is detected:

  • sub-2GHz
  • 2.4GHz
  • 5.2GHz
  • 5.8GHz

Positioning Information and Precision

Equipped with a high-gain RTK module, the O4 Ground Station tracks 19 frequencies from five global satellite systems. This provides consistent centimeter-level positioning even in complex environments such as urban "concrete jungles" or dense forests.

Operating Modes

The device offers two primary operating modes:

In Gateway mode, the O4 Ground Station connects directly to the DJI FlightHub 2 cloud platform via Ethernet or a DJI Cellular Encryption Dongle 2, independent of specific base stations. When paired with DJI Dock 3, it extends the operational range up to 30km. This mode supports Virtual Cockpit features on FlightHub 2, allowing operators to maintain remote flight control and execute pre-set paths. Furthermore, a Signal Map feature analyzes and visualizes weak signal areas based on flight data to help operators plan ground station placement accurately.

In Relay mode, the O4 Ground Station acts as a relay node in remote areas without cellular coverage, transmitting drone video signals to controllers or base stations. When paired with the Matrice 400, it achieves a relay transmission distance of up to 40km. It is compatible with various models including:

  • DJI Dock 3
  • Matrice 400
  • Matrice 4E
  • Matrice 4T
  • Matrice 4D
  • Matrice 4TD
  • FlyCart 100

Durability and Power

Regarding environmental adaptability, the DJI O4 Ground Station features an IP67 rating for dust and water resistance and operates reliably in temperatures ranging from -40°C to 55°C. It is designed for maintenance-free operation with auto-recovery functions. An intelligent low-power design reduces standby power to 7W when no drone is connected.

The system also supports solar power solutions, making it suitable for continuous unmanned operations. Additionally, it can integrate third-party cameras via the GB/T 28181 protocol to feed real-time footage into the DJI FlightHub 2 platform. It can also connect to RID and ADS-B receivers based on ESDK 2.0 to provide real-time awareness of nearby drones or manned aircraft.

The DJI O4 Ground Station is a robust, industrial-grade communication system designed to ensure reliable long-range drone transmission and centimeter-level positioning in challenging environments. It features advanced multi-band switching, dual operating modes (Gateway and Relay), and high durability with an IP67 rating. The system is engineered for large-scale operations, offering seamless integration with DJI's ecosystem and third-party hardware.

動作モード

この装置は2つの主要な動作モードを提供します。「ゲートウェイ（Gateway）」モードでは、O4 Ground StationはEthernetまたはDJIセルラー暗号化ドングル2を介してDJI FlightHub 2クラウドプラットフォームに直接接続され、特定の基地局に依存せずに配置できます。DJI Dock 3と組み合わせることで、作業範囲は最大30kmまで拡張されます。このモードはFlightHub 2のバーチャルコックピット機能をサポートし、運用者が遠隔で飛行制御権を確保し、必要に応じて事前設定された経路を実行できるようにします。また、信号地図機能により、飛行データに基づいた信号の弱いエリアを分析・可視化し、運用者が地上局の配置場所を正確に計画するのを支援します。

「中継（Relay）」モードでは、O4 Ground Stationはセルラーネットワークのない遠隔地において通信の中継ノードとして設置され、ドローンのビデオ信号をコントローラーや基地局へ転送します。Matrice 400と組み合わせた場合、中継伝送距離は最大40kmに達します。

Product Overview

DJI has introduced the DJI O4 Ground Station, specifically designed for industrial drone applications. This fixed long-range transmission enhancement system is engineered to solve communication reliability issues caused by signal interference, physical obstacles, or long-distance operations. The product starts at a price of 14,999 RMB and is available through authorized DJI industrial channels.

Transmission and Signal Performance

The DJI O4 Ground Station integrates an omnidirectional array of 12 antennas (combining internal and external units) to support horizontal and vertical dual polarization. This configuration ensures stable, high-gain signal capture regardless of the flight direction. High-performance RF components significantly improve signal penetration and coverage while optimizing Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) and reception sensitivity, allowing for the recovery of faint signals in environments with heavy electromagnetic interference.

Additionally, the device supports intelligent automatic switching across multiple frequency bands to ensure continuous video transmission even when interference is detected:

  • sub-2GHz
  • 2.4GHz
  • 5.2GHz
  • 5.8GHz

Positioning Information and Precision

Equipped with a high-gain RTK module, the O4 Ground Station tracks 19 frequencies from five major global satellite systems. This allows it to provide consistent centimeter-level positioning information even in complex environments such as urban "concrete jungles" or dense forests.

Operating Modes

The device offers two primary operating modes tailored for different mission requirements:

Gateway Mode: In this mode, the O4 Ground Station connects directly to the DJI FlightHub 2 cloud platform via Ethernet or a DJI Cellular Encryption Dongle 2. It can be deployed without being dependent on specific base stations. When integrated with DJI Dock 3, the operational range is extended up to 30km. This mode supports Virtual Cockpit features for remote flight control and pre-set routes. Furthermore, a Signal Map feature analyzes and visualizes weak signal areas based on flight data, helping operators accurately plan ground station placement.

Relay Mode: In this mode, the O4 Ground Station serves as a relay node in remote areas lacking cellular networks, transmitting drone video signals to controllers or base stations. When paired with the Matrice 400, the transmission distance can reach up to 40km. It is compatible with various models including:

  • DJI Dock 3
  • Matrice 400
  • Matrice 4E
  • Matrice 4T
  • Matrice 4D
  • Matrice 4TD
  • FlyCart 100

Durability and Power

The DJI O4 Ground Station is built for extreme environments with an IP67 rating for dust and water resistance, operating reliably in temperatures ranging from -40°C to 55°C. It features a maintenance-free design with automatic recovery functions and an intelligent low-power design that reduces standby power to 7W when no drone is connected.

The system also supports solar power solutions for continuous unmanned operation. Furthermore, it can integrate third-party camera footage into the DJI FlightHub 2 platform via GB/T 28181 protocol network cameras. It also supports ESDK 2.0 based RID and ADS-B receivers to provide real-time awareness of nearby drones or manned aircraft.

Summary

The DJI O4 Ground Station is a robust industrial solution designed to ensure reliable long-range communication for professional drone operations through advanced antenna arrays and multi-band switching. It offers versatile Gateway and Relay modes to provide extensive coverage in both urban and remote environments while maintaining centimeter-level precision via RTK technology.
