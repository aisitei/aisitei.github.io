Motorola Edge 70 Pro+가 실용적이고 편안한 느낌이라면, OnePlus 15R은 외관과 질감 면에서 더욱 프리미엄한 인상을 줍니다. 디스플레이 품질만 놓고 본다면 더 높은 주사율과 폭넓은 HDR 지원을 갖춘 OnePlus 15R이 근소하게 앞섭니다.

  • Motor1000 Edge 70 Pro+: 6500mAh 배터리, 90W 유선 충전, 15W 무선 충전 및 역충전 지원

Design and Display

The Motorola Edge 70 Pro+ focuses on comfort and practicality through its eco-leather back, Gorilla Glass 7i protection layer, and IP68/IP69 certifications. The textured rear panel enhances grip and prevents fingerprints, providing a user-friendly feel for daily use. Additionally, Motorola has secured extra durability for outdoor enthusiasts with MIL-STD-810H certification.

The OnePlus 15R takes a more premium approach by utilizing an aluminum frame and glass materials. This model also features IP68/IP69K protection ratings and supports eSIM on specific variants. The overall design utilizes high-quality materials to achieve a flagship-oriented aesthetic.

Display Quality

Motorola provides a 6.78-inch AMOLED display supporting a 144Hz refresh rate, HDR10+, and a high peak brightness of 5200 nits. The colors are vivid and vibrant, providing an excellent media consumption experience.

The OnePlus 15R responds with a larger 6.83-inch AMOLED panel featuring a 165Hz refresh rate, Dolby Vision, HDR10+, HDR Vivid support, and Ultra HDR compatibility. While both displays boast high quality, the OnePlus offers a slightly richer visual experience in gaming and HDR content.

While the Motorola Edge 70 Pro+ offers a practical and comfortable feel, the OnePlus 15R provides a more premium impression in terms of aesthetics and texture. Regarding display quality alone, the OnePlus 15R takes a slight lead with its higher refresh rate and broader HDR support.

Specifications and Battery

The performance gap between the two devices is clearly evident in their specifications. The Motorola Edge 70 Pro+ is equipped with a MediaTek Dimensity 8500 Extreme chipset, 12GB of RAM, and UFS 4.1 storage. It delivers smooth daily performance, seamless multitasking, and strong gaming capabilities.

The OnePlus 15R features the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5, one of the most powerful Android processors currently available. Combined with UFS 4.1 storage and a capacity of up to 512GB, it provides flagship-level speeds. It excels in high-end gaming, video editing, and AI-driven tasks, making it more attractive to users who prioritize long-term performance headroom.

The battery and charging features are as follows:

While the OnePlus 15R holds an advantage in raw performance and battery capacity, Motorola provides a more complete charging package including wireless charging. Therefore, the choice may depend on whether the user prioritizes "performance" or "convenience."

Main and Auxiliary Cameras

Motorola targets camera enthusiasts with a triple 50MP camera configuration. The combination of a 50MP main sensor, a 50MP ultra-wide camera, and a 50MP periscope telephoto lens with 3.5x optical zoom creates a very versatile shooting system. Furthermore, Pantone-certified color accuracy and skin tone representation enhance image consistency.

The OnePlus 15R uses a simpler dual-camera setup consisting of a 50MP main sensor and an 8MP ultra-wide camera. While the primary camera performs strongly, zoom versatility is limited due to the lack of a dedicated telephoto lens. Additionally, the ultra-wide sensor specifications are lower than those on the Motorola.

The selfie camera specifications are as follows:

Overall, the Motorola wins in terms of the camera package. The dedicated telephoto lens, high-resolution ultra-wide camera, and superior selfie hardware are significant advantages for users who prioritize photography.

Price and Value

The Motorola Edge 70 Pro+ is priced at approximately $570, while the OnePlus 15R is priced at approximately $650. This price difference positions the OnePlus in a more premium segment.

Motorola offers a flagship-grade display, a versatile triple camera system, wireless charging support, advanced durability features, and a large battery within this price range. The overall configuration is very aggressive, including many features typically found only in high-end devices.

The OnePlus 15R justifies its higher price with the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 processor, a larger battery, premium build materials, and advanced display technologies. It is well worth the extra cost for users who primarily perform high-end gaming or performance-heavy tasks.

In terms of overall value, the Motorola Edge 70 Pro+ offers more hardware. The OnePlus 15R justifies its premium only if top-tier performance is the user's highest priority.

Overall Review

The Motorola Edge 70 Pro+ is a balanced model that provides a versatile flagship experience based on a powerful camera system, wireless charging support, and excellent value for money. In contrast, the OnePlus 15R is optimized for power users seeking high-end gaming and extreme performance through its Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 processor and large battery capacity.
