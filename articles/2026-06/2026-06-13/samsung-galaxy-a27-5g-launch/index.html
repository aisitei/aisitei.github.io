Samsung Galaxy A27 5G Specifications and Features

The Galaxy A27 5G features a 6.7-inch FHD+ Super AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate and up to 800 nits of brightness. It is powered by the Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 chipset, offering options for 6GB or 8GB of LPDDR5x RAM and 128GB or 256GB of UFS 3.1 storage. A hybrid SIM slot allows users to expand storage via a microSD card up to 2TB.

The camera specifications are as follows:

  • Rear Triple Camera:
    • 5MP main sensor (with OIS support)
    • 5MP ultra-wide camera
    • 2MP macro camera
  • Front Camera: 12MP selfie camera
  • Video Recording: Supports 4K at 30fps

Samsung equipped the Galaxy A27 5G with a 5,000mAh battery and 25W charging support. It comes with Android 16 and One UI 8.5 pre-installed, and Samsung has stated that the phone will receive six years of OS and security updates until 2032.

Other features include:

  • IP64 dust and water resistance
  • Gorilla Glass Victus+
  • Side fingerprint scanner
  • Dual SIM 5G support
  • Wi-Fi ac
  • Bluetooth 5.1
  • USB Type-C
  • Dimensions: 162.4 x 78.2 x 7.8mm
  • Weight: 200g
