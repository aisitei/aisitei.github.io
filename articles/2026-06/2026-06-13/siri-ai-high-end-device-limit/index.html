Apple Siri AI 최신 기기 한정: iPhone 15 Pro 이상 및 Apple Silicon 탑재 Mac 등 온디바이스 AI 모델 문턱 높아져

2026-06-13

신규 운영체제 발표

IT홈의 보도에 따르면, Apple은 6월 9일 WWDC 26에서 차세대 운영체제인 iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27 Golden Gate, watchOS 27 및 visionOS 27을 공식 발표했습니다.

이번 시스템 업데이트의 핵심은 Apple Intelligence가 깊이 통합된 새로운 Siri AI로, 대대적인 업그레이드가 이루어졌습니다. 다만, Apple이 공개한 호음성 정보에 따르면 인공지능으로 재구축된 이 음성 비서는 최신 기기에서만 사용할 수 있습니다.

기기별 Siri AI 지원 범위

iPhone 라인업의 경우, iOS 27 운영체제 자체는 iPhone 11 및 그 이후 모델까지 하위 호환되지만, Siri AI의 전체 기능은 iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, 그리고 iPhone 16 및 이후 출시되는 기종에만 제공됩니다. 즉, iPhone 15 기본 모델 및 이전 Plus 모델은 해당 AI 기능을 경험할 수 없습니다.

iPad의 경우, Siri AI는 M1 또는 그 이상의 칩이 탑재된 iPad 모델과 A17 Pro 칩을 탑재한 iPad mini에서 지원됩니다.

Mac 및 Apple Watch 호환성

Mac 제품군의 경우, macOS 27은 Intel 칩 기반 Mac에 대한 지원을 완전히 중단했습니다. M1 칩 이상의 Apple silicon이 탑재된 Mac 기기에서만 새 시스템 실행과 Siri AI 기능 지원이 가능합니다.

Apple Watch의 호환성은 상대적으로 엄격하게 제한됩니다. watchOS 27은 Apple Watch Series 9 이상, Apple Watch Ultra 2 이상 및 SE 3를 지원하지만, 이 기기들의 Siri AI 기능은 iOS 27 AI 기능을 지원하는 iPhone과 함께 사용할 때만 활성화됩니다.

Apple Vision Pro의 경우 모든 모델이 Siri AI를 지원합니다.

고급 기능 및 하드웨어 요구사항

Siri AI를 지원하는 기기 범위 내에서도 일부 고급 기능은 더 높은 하드웨어 문턱이 존재합니다. Apple은 사용자가 Siri의 말하기 속도, 감정 표현 및 톤을 개인화할 수 있도록 가장 강력한 온디바이스(On-device) AI 모델을 도입했습니다.

해당 고성능 기능은 다음 기기에서만 지원됩니다:

  • iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air
  • M4 칩 및 최소 12GB 통합 메모리 탑재 iPad
  • M3 칩 및 최소 12GB 통합 메모리 탑재 Mac
  • M5 칩 탑재 Apple Vision Pro

iPhone 17 기본 모델은 통합 메모리가 8GB에 불과하여 이 고성능 모델의 최소 요구 사양을 충족하지 못해 제외되었습니다.

총평

Apple은 iOS 27을 통해 Siri AI를 대폭 강화했으나, 기능의 고도화에 따라 기기별로 엄격한 하드웨어 요구 사양을 구분하여 적용했습니다. 특히 고급 온디바이스 AI 기능을 활용하기 위해서는 높은 메모리 용량과 최신 프로세서가 필수적으로 요구되는 구조입니다.

新系统发布

Apple 在 WWDC 26 上正式发布了多款新一代操作系统，涵盖其全产品线：

  • iOS 27
  • iPadOS 27
  • macOS 27 Golden Gate
  • watchOS 27
  • visionOS 27

Siri AI 功能与设备兼容性

作为本次更新的核心，深度集成 Apple Intelligence 的 Siri AI 对不同设备的硬件支持要求如下：

  • iPhone 系列：iOS 27 系统支持 iPhone 11 及更新机型；但 Siri AI 全功能仅限 iPhone 15 Pro、iPhone 15 Pro Max 以及 iPhone 16 及后续机型。
  • iPad 系列：Siri AI 要求配备 M1 或更新芯片的 iPad，以及搭载 A17 Pro 芯片的 iPad mini。
  • Mac 系列：macOS 27 已停止支持 Intel 芯片，仅限搭载 M1 及更新版本 Apple silicon 的 Mac 机型。
  • Apple Watch 系列：支持 Series 9 或更新机型、Ultra 2 或更新型号以及 SE 3（需配合兼容的 iPhone 使用）。
  • Apple Vision Pro：所有型号均支持 Siri AI。

高阶功能与硬件门槛

针对涉及语音语速、情感表达等个性化调节的高端端侧 AI 模型，Apple 设置了更高的硬性要求：

  • iPhone 17 Pro、iPhone 17 Pro Max、iPhone Air
  • 配备 M4 芯片且统一内存至少 12GB 的 iPad
  • 配备 M3 芯片且统一内存至少 12GB 的 Mac
  • 搭载 M5 芯片的 Apple Vision Pro

由于 iPhone 17 标准版仅配备 8GB 统一内存，无法满足上述高阶模型的要求。

总结

Apple 在 WWDC 26 上推出了全新的操作系统系列并引入了重构后的 Siri AI。为了平衡性能需求，Apple 为 Siri AI 功能设置了三层硬件门槛：基础系统兼容、基础功能支持以及针对高性能设备的高端模型支持。

The core of this system update is a major overhaul of Siri AI, which is deeply integrated with Apple Intelligence. However, according to information released by Apple, this AI-reconstructed voice assistant is only available on the latest devices.

Device-Specific Siri AI Support

Regarding the iPhone lineup, while the iOS 27 operating system itself supports models from the iPhone 11 onwards, the full suite of Siri AI features is exclusive to the iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, and the iPhone 16 series and subsequent models. This means that the base iPhone 15 model and previous Plus models will not have access to these specific AI capabilities.

In the Mac lineup, macOS 27 has completely discontinued support for Intel-based Macs. The new system and Siri AI features are only available on Mac devices equipped with Apple silicon (M1 chip or higher).

Compatibility for Apple Watch is more strictly limited. watchOS 27 supports Apple Watch Series 9 and later, Apple Watch Ultra 2 and later, and the SE 3. However, these devices' Siri AI features are only activated when paired with an iPhone that supports iOS 27 AI features.

All models of the Apple Vision Pro support Siri AI.

Advanced Features and Hardware Requirements

Even within the range of devices supporting Siri AI, certain advanced features have higher hardware thresholds. Apple introduced a powerful on-device AI model that allows users to personalize Siri's speaking speed, emotional expression, and tone.

These high-performance features are only supported on the following devices:

  • iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air
  • iPad equipped with M4 chip and at least 12GB of unified memory
  • Mac equipped with M3 chip and at least 12GB of unified memory
  • Apple Vision Pro equipped with the M5 chip

The base iPhone 17 model was excluded from these high-performance features because its unified memory is only 8GB, failing to meet the minimum requirements for these advanced capabilities.

Apple has significantly enhanced Siri AI with iOS 27 but implemented strict hardware tiers to gate specific functionalities. High-end on-device features, such as personalized voice characteristics, require premium processors and a minimum of 12GB of unified memory.

New Operating Systems Announced

According to reports from IT Home, Apple officially announced its next-generation operating systems at WWDC 26 on June 9. The new lineup includes iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27 Golden Gate, watchOS 27, and visionOS 27.

The core of this update is the overhaul of Siri AI, which has been deeply integrated with Apple Intelligence. However, according to information released by Apple, this reconstructed voice assistant is only available on the latest devices.

Siri AI Support Across Devices

For the iPhone lineup, while the iOS 27 operating system itself maintains backward compatibility for models from the iPhone 11 onwards, the full suite of Siri AI features is exclusive to the iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, and the iPhone 16 series and subsequent models. Consequently, the base iPhone 15 model and previous Plus models will not have access to these specific AI capabilities.

For iPads, Siri AI is supported on models equipped with the M1 chip or higher, as well as the iPad mini featuring the A17 Pro chip.

Mac and Apple Watch Compatibility

In the Mac lineup, macOS 27 has completely discontinued support for Intel-based Macs. The new system and Siri AI features are only available on Mac devices equipped with Apple silicon (M1 chip or higher).

Compatibility for Apple Watch is more strictly limited. watchOS 27 supports Apple Watch Series 9 and later, Apple Watch Ultra 2 and later, and the SE 3. However, these devices' Siri AI features are only activated when paired with an iPhone that supports iOS 27.

All models of the Apple Vision Pro support Siri AI.

Advanced Features and Hardware Thresholds

Even within the range of devices that support Siri AI, certain advanced features require higher hardware thresholds. Apple has introduced powerful on-device AI models to allow users to personalize Siri's speaking speed, emotional expression, and tone.

These high-performance features are only supported on the following devices:

  • iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air
  • iPad equipped with an M4 chip and at least 12GB of unified memory
  • Mac equipped with an M3 chip and at least 12GB of unified memory
  • Apple Vision Pro equipped with the M5 chip

The base iPhone 17 model was excluded from these high-performance features because it only features 8GB of unified memory, failing to meet the minimum requirements for the advanced models.

Summary

Apple has introduced a suite of new operating systems featuring a significantly upgraded Siri AI integrated with Apple Intelligence. While basic OS support is broad, full and advanced AI capabilities are strictly gated by hardware specifications, specifically requiring high-performance chips and sufficient memory for on-device processing.
