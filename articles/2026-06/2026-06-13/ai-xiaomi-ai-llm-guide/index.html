Xiaomi의 AI 여정과 LLM 경쟁 참여

Xiaomi는 그동안 저렴한 스마트폰과 스마트 홈 기기로 잘 알려져 왔으나, 지난 1년 반 동안 조용히 세계에서 가장 야심 찬 AI 플레이어 중 하나로 변모해 왔습니다.

거대 언어 모델(LLM)부터 음성 복제, 자율형 휴대폰 에이전트에 이르기까지 Xiaomi는 매우 빠르게 움직이고 있습니다. 다음은 Xiaomi가 거대한 AI 및 LLM 경쟁에서 어느 위치에 와 있는지에 대한 모든 내용입니다.

MiMo-7B: LLM 레이스의 시작

Xiaomi의 AI 이야기는 2025년 4월, 첫 번째 오픈 소스 대형 언어 모델인 MiMo-7B를 출시하며 본격적으로 시작되었습니다. "MiMo"는 Xiaomi Model(MiandMo)을 의미합니다. 초기부터 주목할 점은 Xiaomi가 단순한 채팅보다는 추론과 코딩에 집중했다는 것입니다.

MiMo-7B는 70억 개의 파라미터를 가졌음에도 불구하고 뛰어난 성능을 보여주었습니다.

  • MATH-500 같은 수학 벤치마크에서 강화 학습 버전이 95.8%를 기록했습니다.
  • AIME 2024 및 2025 수학 경진대회에서 OpenAI의 o1-mini와 Alibaba의 Qwen-32B-Preview를 능가했습니다.

이 모델은 2000억 개의 추론 토큰으로 구성된 특별히 선별된 데이터셋과 세 단계의 학습 과정(총 25조 토큰)을 통해 훈련되었습니다. MiMo-7B는 MIT 라이선스로 공개되었으며 Hugging Face에서 이용 가능합니다.

개발팀은 DeepSeek 출신의 Luo Fuli가 이끌었습니다.

MiMo-V2-Flash 및 MiMo-V2-Pro

2025년 12월, Xiaomi는 Mixture-of-Experts(MoE) 설계를 통해 대부분의 가중치를 "비활성" 상태로 유지하면서도 높은 성능을 내는 3,090억 파라미터 모델인 MiMo-V2-Flash를 발표했습니다.

MiMo-V2-Flash의 특징은 다음과 같습니다.

  • 추론 벤치마크에서 오픈 소스 모델 중 상위 2위를 차지했습니다.
  • 소프트웨어 엔지니어링 테스트(SWE-Bench Verified)에서 GPT-5 및 Claude 4.5 Sonnet과 대등한 성능을 보였습니다.
  • 초당 150개의 토큰을 생성하며, 추론 비용은 Claude의 약 2.5% 수준으로 매우 경제적입니다.

MiMo-V2-Flash는 또한 모델이 한 번에 여러 토큰을 생성하고 검증할 수 있게 하는 Multi-Token Prediction(MTP) 기술을 도입했습니다.

2026년 3월에는 더욱 야심 찬 모델인 MiMo-V2-Pro가 등장했습니다. 이 모델은 총 1조 개 이상의 파라미터를 보유하며, 한 번에 수백 페이지 분량의 소설을 처리할 수 있는 100만 토큰의 컨텍스트 창을 지원합니다. 특히 복잡한 계획과 실행이 필요한 "에이전트(agentic)" 작업을 위해 설계되었습니다.

MiMo-V2-Pro와 함께 멀티모달 버전인 MiMo-V2-Omni와 텍스트 음성 변환용 MiMo-V2-TTS가 동시 출시되었습니다.

MiMo-V2.5 및 특화 모델군

2026년 4월 말, Xiaomi는 V2 제품군의 장점을 통합한 MiMo-V2.5-Pro를 발표했습니다. 이 모델은 텍스트, 이미지, 오디오, 비디오를 모두 처리할 수 있는 1.02조 파라미터 규모의 모델입니다.

V2.5-Pro는 출시 당시 Artificial Analysis 벤치마크에서 에이전트 기능 부문 세계 최고의 오픈 소스 모델로 선정되었습니다. 또한, Xiaomi는 100만 토큰 컨텍스트 창 사용에 대한 추가 비용을 제거하여 개발자 친화적인 환경을 제공했습니다.

또한 다양한 목적을 위한 특화 모델들도 출시되었습니다.

  • MiMo Code: 긴 프로젝트에서도 문맥을 유지하는 지속적 메모리 시스템을 갖춘 터미널 기반 AI 코딩 에이전트입니다.
  • MiMo-VL 및 MiMo-VL-Miloco-7B: 시각 언어 모델로, 특히 Miloco는 가정 환경을 이해하도록 설계되어 일상적인 제스처와 가사 활동을 인식합니다.
  • MiDashengLM-7B 및 MiMo-Audio: 단순 음성 인식을 넘어 음악, 주변 소리, 화자의 감정까지 파악하는 오음(audio) AI 모델입니다.

OmniVoice 및 음성 기술 파이프라인

2026년 5월에 공개된 OmniVoice는 Xiaomi AI Lab의 차세대 Kaldi 팀에서 개발한 TTS 모델입니다.

  • 646개 언어를 지원하며, 데이터가 부족한 저자원 언어도 포함합니다.
  • 단 몇 초의 샘플만으로 목소리를 복제하는 제로샷(zero-shot) 음성 복제 기능을 제공하며, 여러 언어에 걸쳐 원본 목소리의 특징을 유지하면서 자연스러운 음성을 생성합니다.

OmniVoice는 단일 트랜스포머 구조를 통해 매우 빠른 추론 속도를 자랑하며, 다양한 발음 교정 도구를 포함합니다. MiMo-V2.5-TTS와 ASR 시스템은 이들과 결합하여 개발자들이 별도의 도구 연결 없이도 엔드포인트 음성 기반 제품을 구축할 수 있게 합니다.

miclaw는 현재 Xiaomi 17 시리즈를 지원하는 비공개 베타 단계에 있으며, HyperOS 4에서 시스템 수준으로 통합될 예정입니다.

Xiaomi's AI Journey and LLM Competition

While Xiaomi has traditionally been known for its affordable smartphones and smart home devices, the company has quietly transformed into one of the world's most ambitious AI players over the past 1.5 years.

From Large Language Models (LLMs) to voice cloning and autonomous mobile agents, Xiaomi is moving at a rapid pace. Here is everything regarding Xiaomi's position in the massive AI and LLM competition.

MiMo-7B: The Start of the LLM Race

Xiaomi's AI narrative began in earnest in April 2025 with the release of its first open-source large language model, MiMo-7B. "MiMo" stands for Xiaomi Model (MiandMo). From the beginning, it was notable that Xiaomi focused on reasoning and coding rather than simple chatting.

Despite having only 7 billion parameters, MiMo-7B demonstrated exceptional performance.

  • The reinforced learning version scored 95.8% on math benchmarks such as MATH-500.
  • It outperformed OpenAI's o1-mini and Alibaba's Qwen-32B-Preview in the AIME 2024 and 2025 math competitions.

The model was trained using a specially curated dataset of 200 billion reasoning tokens through a three-stage training process (totaling 25 trillion tokens). MiMo-7B is released under the MIT license and is available on Hugging Face.

The development team was led by Luo Fuli, formerly of DeepSeek.

MiMo-V2-Flash and MiMo-V2-Pro

In December 2025, Xiaomi announced MiMo-V2-Flash, a 309-billion parameter model utilizing a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) design to maintain high performance while keeping most weights "inactive."

Key features of MiMo-V2-Flash include:

  • Ranked second among open-source models in reasoning benchmarks.
  • Demonstrated performance comparable to GPT-5 and Claude 4.5 Sonnet in software engineering tests (SWE-Bench Verified).
  • Generates 150 tokens per second with inference costs at approximately 2.5% of Claude's level, making it highly economical.

MiMo-V2-Flash also introduced Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) technology, allowing the model to generate and verify multiple tokens simultaneously.

In March 2026, the even more ambitious MiMo-V2-Pro was released. This model features over 1 trillion parameters and supports a 1 million token context window capable of processing hundreds of pages of text at once. It is specifically designed for "agentic" tasks requiring complex planning and execution.

Launched alongside MiMo-V2-Pro were the multimodal version, MiMo-V2-Omni, and the text-to-speech model, MiMo-V2-TTS.

MiMo-V2.5 and Specialized Model Families

In late April 2026, Xiaomi released MiMo-V2.5-Pro, which integrates the advantages of the V2 series. This is a 1.02 trillion parameter model capable of processing text, images, audio, and video.

At launch, V2.5-Pro was selected as the world's best open-source model for agent capabilities in the Artificial Analysis benchmark. Additionally, Xiaomi provided a developer-friendly environment by removing extra costs for using the 1 million token context window.

Several specialized models were also released for specific purposes:

  • MiMo Code: A terminal-based AI coding agent with a continuous memory system to maintain context in long projects.
  • MiMo-VL and MiMo-VL-Miloco-7B: Vision language models; specifically, Miloco is designed to understand home environments and recognize daily gestures and household activities.
  • MiDashengLM-7B and MiMo-Audio: Audio AI models that go beyond simple speech recognition to identify music, ambient sounds, and the speaker's emotions.

OmniVoice and Voice Technology Pipeline

Released in May 2026, OmniVoice is a TTS model developed by the next-generation Kaldi team at Xiaomi AI Lab.

  • Supports 646 languages, including low-resource languages with limited data.
  • Provides zero-shot voice cloning, capable of cloning a voice from just a few seconds of samples while maintaining original characteristics across multiple languages.

OmniVoice boasts very fast inference speeds through a single transformer architecture and includes various pronunciation correction tools. Combined with MiMo-V2.5-TTS and ASR systems, these allow developers to build endpoint voice-based products without connecting separate tools.

Consumer Services: Xiao AI and HyperAI

Xiaomi's core experience for general users is divided into two main areas.

Xiao AI (小爱) is a long-standing voice assistant operating on smartphones, speakers, and wearable devices. It was upgraded to "Super Xiao AI" with HyperOS 2, featuring improved context memory and image generation capabilities.

HyperAI is a suite of features introduced starting with the Xiaomi 15 series, including real-time translation, AI writing assistance, smart voice recognition (recording summaries), and AI photo editing. It also integrates Google Gemini as a backend in certain applications.

The Future Core: miclaw

The most advanced part of Xiaomi's AI strategy is miclaw, announced in March 2026. This is not a simple chatbot but an autonomous AI agent based on MiMo.

miclaw interprets user intent to perform multi-step tasks such as executing apps, navigating interfaces, and filling out forms. Through a "reasoning-execution loop," it verifies the process until a result is achieved, while personal information is processed locally to enhance security.

miclaw is currently in a private beta phase supporting the Xiaomi 17 series and is scheduled for system-level integration in HyperOS 4.

Investment Scale and Strategic Goals

In March 2026, CEO Lei Jun announced an investment of at least $8.7 billion in the AI field over the next three years. This is additional funding beyond the existing R&D budget; annual R&D spending for 2026 is expected to reach approximately 40 billion yuan (roughly $5.7 billion).

Xiaomi aims for a "grand convergence" by integrating its own chips, OS, and proprietary AI models into a single device. It has already demonstrated technical competitiveness by capturing a significant portion of OpenRouter traffic.

Conclusion

Xiaomi is evolving rapidly from a hardware manufacturer into a company building a complete AI stack, ranging from internal models to agent systems. Through massive investment and an open-source strategy, the company is establishing its own ecosystem and is expected to establish itself as a true AI platform company through the integration of miclaw and HyperOS 4.

Summary

Xiaomi has rapidly transitioned from a hardware-focused brand to a major player in the AI space by developing a comprehensive suite of models, including the MiMo series and the OmniVoice system. The company is investing billions into "grand convergence," integrating proprietary chips, OS, and autonomous agents like miclaw into its ecosystem. This strategy positions Xiaomi as a full-stack AI platform provider rather than just a consumer electronics manufacturer.

XiaomiのAIへの歩みとLLM競争への参戦

Xiaomiはこれまで、手頃な価格のスマートフォンやスマートホーム機器で広く知られてきましたが、過去1年半の間に、世界で最も野心的なAIプレーヤーの一人へと静かに変貌を遂げてきました。

大規模言語モデル（LLM）から音声複製、自律型スマートフォンエージェントに至るまで、Xiaomiは非常に迅速な動きを見せています。以下は、Xiaomiが巨大なAIおよびLLM競争においてどのような位置にいるかを示す詳細です。

MiMo-7B：LLMレースの始まり

XiaomiのAIへの歩みは、2025年4月に最初のオープンソース大規模言語モデルであるMiMo-7Bをリリースしたことで本格的に始まりました。「MiMo」はXiaomi Model（MiandMo）を意味します。初期段階から注目すべき点は、Xiaomiが単なるチャットよりも推論とコーディングに焦点を当てたことです。

MiMo-7Bは70億のパラメータを持ちながらも、優れた性能を示しました。

  • MATH-500などの数学ベンチマークにおいて、強化学習バージョンが95.8%を記録。
  • AIME 2024および2025の数学競技において、OpenAIのo1-miniやAlibabaのQwen-32B-Previewを上回る性能を達成。

このモデルは、特別に選定された2,000億の推論トークンを含むデータセットと、3段階の学習プロセス（計25兆トークン）を通じてトレーニングされました。MiMo-7BはMITライセンスで公開されており、Hugging Faceで利用可能です。

開発チームはDeepSeek出身のLuo Fuliによって率いられました。

MiMo-V2-FlashおよびMiMo-V2-Pro

2025年12月、XiaomiはMixture-of-Experts（MoE）設計を採用し、ほとんどの重みを「非アクティブ」な状態に保ちながらも高い性能を発揮する3,090億パラメータモデルであるMiMo-V2-Flashを発表しました。

MiMo-V2-Flashの特徴は以下の通りです。

  • 推論ベンチマークにおいて、オープンソースモデルの中で上位2位を獲得。
  • ソフトウェアエンジニアリングテスト（SWE-Bench Verified）において、GPT-5およびClaude 4.5 Sonnetに匹敵する性能を実証。
  • 毎秒150トークンを生成し、推論コストはClaudeの約2.5%という非常に経済的な水準を実現。

MiMo-V2-Flashはさらに、モデルが一度に複数のトークンを生成・検証できるようにするMulti-Token Prediction（MTP）技術を採用しています。

2026年3月には、より野心的なモデルであるMiMo-V2-Proが登場しました。このモデルは計10億以上のパラメータを持ち、一度に数百ページ分の小説を処理できる100万トークンのコンテキストウィンドウをサポートします。特に、複雑な計画と実行を必要とする「エージェント（agentic）」のタスク向けに設計されています。

MiMo-V2-Proと同時に、マルチモーダル版のMiMo-V2-Omniおよびテキストから音声への変換用MiMo-V2-TTSもリリースされました。

MiMo-V2.5および特化型モデル群

2026年4月末、XiaomiはV2シリーズの利点を統合したMiMo-V2.5-Proを発表しました。このモデルはテキスト、画像、オーディオ、ビデオのすべてを処理可能な1.02兆パラメータ規模のモデルです。

V2.5-Proはリリース当時、Artificial Analysisベンチマークにおいてエージェント機能部門で世界最高のオープンソースモデルに選ばれました。また、Xiaomiは100万トークンのコンテキストウィンドウの使用に関する追加費用を撤廃し、開発者フレンドリーな環境を提供しました。

さらに、さまざまな目的のための特化型モデルもリリースされました。

  • MiMo Code：長いプロジェクトでも文脈を維持する持続的なメモリシステムを備えた、ターミナルベースのAIコーディングエージェント。
  • MiMo-VLおよびMiMo-VL-Miloco-7B：視覚言語モデル（VLM）であり、特にMilocoは家庭環境を理解するように設計され、日常的なジェスチャーや家事活動を認識します。
  • MiDashengLM-7BおよびMiMo-Audio：単なる音声認識を超え、音楽、周囲の音、話者の感情まで把握するオーディオAIモデル。

OmniVoiceと音声技術パイプライン

2026年5月に公開されたOmniVoiceは、Xiaomi AI Labの次世代Kaldiチームによって開発されたTTSモデルです。

  • 646の言語をサポートしており、データが不足している低リソース言語も含まれます。
  • わずか数秒のサンプルだけで声を複製するゼロショット（zero-shot）音声複製機能を提供し、複数の言語にわたって元の声の特徴を維持しながら自然な音声を生成します。

OmniVoiceは単一のトランスフォーマー構造を通じて非常に高速な推論速度を誇り、さまざまな発音補正ツールを含んでいます。MiMo-V2.5-TTSとASRシステムはこれらと統合され、開発者が個別のツール接続なしにエンドポイント音声ベースの製品を構築できるようにします。

Xiaomi's AI Journey and LLM Competition

While Xiaomi has long been recognized for its affordable smartphones and smart home devices, it has quietly transformed into one of the world's most ambitious AI players over the past year and a half.

From Large Language Models (LLMs) to voice cloning and autonomous mobile agents, Xiaomi is moving at an accelerated pace. Here is everything regarding Xiaomi's position in the massive AI and LLM competition.

MiMo-7B: The Start of the LLM Race

Xiaomi's AI narrative began in earnest in April 2025 with the release of its first open-source large language model, MiMo-7B. "MiMo" stands for Xiaomi Model (MiandMo). From the beginning, it was notable that Xiaomi focused on reasoning and coding rather than simple chatting.

Despite having only 7 billion parameters, MiMo-7B demonstrated exceptional performance.

  • The reinforced learning version scored 95.8% on math benchmarks such as MATH-500.
  • It outperformed OpenAI's o1-mini and Alibaba's Qwen-32B-Preview in the AIME 2024 and 2025 math competitions.

The model was trained using a specially curated dataset of 200 billion reasoning tokens through a three-stage training process (totaling 25 trillion tokens). MiMo-7B is released under the MIT license and is available on Hugging Face.

The development team was led by Luo Fuli, who previously worked at DeepSeek.

MiMo-V2-Flash and MiMo-V2-Pro

In December 2025, Xiaomi unveiled MiMo-V2-Flash, a 309 billion parameter model. Utilizing a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) design, it maintains high performance while keeping most weights "inactive."

Key features of MiMo-V2-Flash include:

  • Ranked second among open-source models in reasoning benchmarks.
  • Demonstrated performance comparable to GPT-5 and Claude 4.5 Sonnet in software engineering tests (SWE-Bench Verified).
  • Generates 150 tokens per second, making it highly economical with inference costs at approximately 2.5% of Claude's levels.

MiMo-V2-Flash also introduced Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) technology, allowing the model to generate and verify multiple tokens simultaneously.

In March 2026, an even more ambitious model, MiMo-V2-Pro, was released. This model features over 1 trillion parameters and supports a 1 million token context window capable of processing hundreds of pages of text at once. It is specifically designed for "agentic" tasks requiring complex planning and execution.

Alongside MiMo-V2-Pro, the multimodal version MiMo-V2-Omni and the text-to-speech model MiMo-V2-TTS were released simultaneously.

MiMo-V2.5 and Specialized Model Families

In late April 2026, Xiaomi launched MiMo-V2.5-Pro, which integrates the advantages of the V2 series. This is a 1.02 trillion parameter model capable of processing text, images, audio, and video.

At launch, V2.5-Pro was selected as the world's best open-source model for agent capabilities in the Artificial Analysis benchmark. Additionally, Xiaomi provided a developer-friendly environment by removing extra costs for using the 1 million token context window.

Furthermore, several specialized models were released for specific purposes:

  • MiMo Code: A terminal-based AI coding agent with a continuous memory system to maintain context in long projects.
  • MiMo-VL and MiMo-VL-Miloco-7B: Vision language models; specifically, Miloco is designed to understand home environments and recognize everyday gestures and household activities.
  • MiDashengLM-7B and MiMo-Audio: Audio AI models that go beyond simple speech recognition to identify music, ambient sounds, and the speaker's emotions.

OmniVoice and Voice Technology Pipeline

Released in May 2026, OmniVoice is a TTS model developed by the next-generation Kaldi team at Xiaomi AI Lab.

  • Supports 646 languages, including low-resource languages with limited data.
  • Features zero-shot voice cloning, capable of cloning a voice with only a few seconds of samples while maintaining original characteristics across multiple languages.

OmniVoice boasts very fast inference speeds through a single transformer structure and includes various pronunciation correction tools. Combined with MiMo-V2.5-TTS and ASR systems, it allows developers to build endpoint voice-based products without connecting separate tools.

Consumer Services: Xiao AI and HyperAI

Xiaomi's core experience for general users is divided into two main areas.

Xiao AI (小爱) is a long-standing voice assistant operating on smartphones, speakers, and wearable devices. It was upgraded to "Super Xiao AI" with HyperOS 2, featuring improved context memory and image generation capabilities.

HyperAI is a suite of features introduced starting with the Xiaomi 15 series, including real-time translation, AI writing assistance, smart voice recognition (recording summaries), and AI photo editing. It also integrates Google Gemini as a backend for certain functions.

The Future Core: miclaw

The most advanced part of Xiaomi's AI strategy is miclaw, announced in March 2026. This is not a simple chatbot but an autonomous AI agent based on MiMo.

miclaw interprets user intent to perform multi-step tasks such as executing apps, navigating interfaces, and filling out forms. Through a "reasoning-execution loop," it verifies the process until a result is achieved, while personal information is processed locally to enhance security.

miclaw is currently in a private beta stage supporting the Xiaomi 17 series and is scheduled to be integrated at the system level in HyperOS 4.

Investment Scale and Strategic Goals

In March 2026, CEO Lei Jun announced that Xiaomi would invest at least $8.7 billion in the AI field over the next three years. This is an additional investment beyond the existing R&D budget; annual R&D spending for 2026 is expected to reach approximately 40 billion yuan (about $5.7 billion).

Xiaomi aims for a "grand convergence" by integrating its own chips, OS, and proprietary AI models into a single device. It has already demonstrated technical competitiveness by capturing a significant portion of OpenRouter traffic.

Overall Assessment

Xiaomi is evolving rapidly from a hardware manufacturer into a full-stack AI company, encompassing everything from internal models to agent systems. Through massive investment and an open-source strategy, the company is building its own ecosystem and is expected to establish itself as a true AI platform provider through the integration of miclaw and HyperOS 4.

Summary

Xiaomi has rapidly transitioned from a hardware-focused brand into a major player in the AI space by developing a comprehensive suite of models, including the MiMo series and specialized tools for coding and vision. With significant investments and a focus on "grand convergence," the company aims to integrate proprietary chips, OS, and autonomous agents like miclaw into its ecosystem. This strategy positions Xiaomi as a full-stack AI platform provider rather than just a device manufacturer.

Xiaomi 的 AI 之旅在2025年4月随着首个开源大语言模型 MiMo-7B 的发布正式开启。 "MiMo" 代表 Xiaomi Model（MiandMo）。从初期就可以看出，Xiaomi 专注于推理和编程，而非简单的聊天。

尽管只有70亿个参数，MiMo-7B仍展现出了卓越的性能。

  • 在 MATH-500 等数学基准测试中，强化学习版本达到了95.8%。
  • 在 AIME 2024 和 2025 数学竞赛中，其表现超越了 OpenAI 的 o1-mini 以及阿里巴巴的 Qwen-32B-Preview。

该模型通过包含2000亿个推理 token 的特别筛选数据集以及三个阶段的训练过程（总计25万亿 token）进行训练。MiMo-7B 以 MIT 许可证发布，可在 Hugging Face 上获取。

开发团队由来自 DeepSeek 的 Luo Fuli 领导。

MiMo-V2-Flash 与 MiMo-V2-Pro

2025年12月，Xiaomi 发布了 MiMo-V2-Flash。该模型采用 Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) 设计，在保持大部分权重处于“非活跃”状态的同时实现高性能，拥有3090亿个参数。

MiMo-V2-Flash 的特点包括：

  • 在推理基准测试中位列开源模型前二。
  • 在软件工程测试（SWE-Bench Verified）中表现与 GPT-5 及 Claude 4.5 Sonnet 持平。
  • 每秒生成150个 token，且推理成本仅为 Claude 的约2.5%，极具经济性。

MiMo-V2-Flash 还引入了 Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) 技术，允许模型一次生成并验证多个 token。

2026年3月，更具野心的 MiMo-V2-Pro 问世。该模型拥有超过1万亿个参数，支持100万 token 的上下文窗口，能够处理长达数百页的小说。它特别为需要复杂计划和执行的“代理（agentic）”任务而设计。

与 MiMo-V2-Pro 同时推出的还有多模态版本 MiMo-V2-Omni 以及用于文本转语音的 MiMo-V2-TTS。

MiMo-V2.5 及特化模型系列

2026年4月下旬，Xiaomi 发布了整合了 V2 系列优势的 MiMo-V2.5-Pro。这是一个拥有1.02万亿参数的模型，能够处理文本、图像、音频和视频。

MiMo-V2.5-Pro 在发布时被 Artificial Analysis 基准测试评选为代理功能领域的全球最佳开源模型。此外，Xiaomi 还取消了使用 100 万 token 上下文窗口的额外费用，提供了对开发者友好的环境。

此外，针对不同用途的特化模型也相继推出：

  • MiMo Code：具备持续记忆系统的终端基础 AI 编码代理，可在长项目中保持上下文。
  • MiMo-VL 及 MiMo-VL-Miloco-7B：视觉语言模型，其中 Miloco 特别设计用于理解家庭环境，能够识别日常手势和家务活动。
  • MiDashengLM-7B 及 MiMo-Audio：超越简单语音识别的音频 AI 模型，可识别音乐、环境音及说话者的情感。

OmniVoice 与语音技术管线

2026年5月发布的 OmniVoice 是由 Xiaomi AI Lab 的下一代 Kaldi 团队开发的 TTS 模型。

  • 支持 646 种语言，包括数据稀缺的低资源语言。
  • 提供零样本（zero-shot）语音克隆功能，仅需几秒采样即可克隆声音，并在跨语言生成时保持原声特征。

OmniVoice 通过单一 Transformer 结构实现极快的推理速度，并包含多种发音纠正工具。MiMo-V2.5-TTS 和 ASR 系统与这些工具结合，让开发者无需连接额外工具即可构建基于端点的语音产品。

消费级服务：Xiao AI 与 HyperAI

面向普通用户的 Xiaomi 核心体验分为两个部分。

Xiao AI（小爱）是在智能手机、扬声器和可穿戴设备上运行的长期语音助手。随着 HyperOS 2 的推出，它升级为“Super Xiao AI”，具备更好的上下文记忆和图像生成功能。

HyperAI 是从 Xiaomi 15 系列开始引入的功能集，包括实时翻译、AI 写作支持、智能语音识别（录音摘要）、AI 照片编辑等，并集成了 Google Gemini 作为后端。

未来的核心：miclaw

Xiaomi AI 战略最前沿的部分是 2026年3月发布的 miclaw。它不是简单的聊天机器人，而是基于 MiMo 的自主 AI 代理。

miclaw 能够解析用户的意图，并自动执行应用启动、界面导航、表单填写等多步骤任务。通过“推理-执行循环”，它会持续确认过程直到产生结果。此外，个人信息在本地处理，增强了安全性。

miclaw 目前处于支持 Xiaomi 17 系列的非公开测试阶段，并计划在 HyperOS 4 中作为系统级功能整合。

投资规模与战略目标

2026年3月，CEO Lei Jun 宣布在未来 3 年内向 AI 领域投资至少 87 亿美元。这是在现有研发预算之外的额外投资，预计 2026 年的年度研发支出将达到约 400 亿人民币（约 57 亿美元）。

Xiaomi 目标是实现包括自有芯片、操作系统以及独立 AI 模型在内的“大融合（grand convergence）”。目前，它已通过占据 OpenRouter 大量流量证明了其技术竞争力。

总结

Xiaomi 正迅速从硬件制造商转型为拥有完整 AI 技术栈的平台企业。通过大规模投资、开源策略以及 MiMo 系列模型和 miclaw 代理系统的开发，Xiaomi 正在构建一个涵盖底层模型到端侧应用的全面 AI 生态系统。

MiMo-7B：LLMレースの始まり

XiaomiのAIの物語は、2025年4月に最初のオープンソース大規模言語モデルであるMiMo-7Bをリリースしたことで本格的に始まりました。「MiMo」はXiaomi Model（MiandMo）を意味します。初期段階から注目すべき点は、Xiaomiが単純なチャットよりも推論とコーディングに焦点を当てたことです。

MiMo-7Bはわずか70億のパラメータを持ちながらも、優れた性能を示しました。

  • MATH-500などの数学ベンチマークにおいて、強化学習バージョンが95.8%を記録。
  • AIME 2024および2025の数学競技において、OpenAIのo1-miniおよびAlibabaのQwen-32B-Previewを上回る結果を達成。

このモデルは、特別に選別された2,000億の推論トークンで構成されるデータセットと、3段階の学習プロセス（計25兆トークン）を通じてトレーニングされました。MiMo-7BはMITライセンスで公開されており、Hugging Faceで利用可能です。

MiMo-V2-FlashおよびMiMo-V2-Pro

MiMo-V2-Flashの特徴は以下の通りです。

  • 推論ベンチマークにおいて、オープンソースモデルの中で第2位を獲得。
  • ソフトウェアエンジニアリングテスト（SWE-Bench Verified）において、GPT-5およびClaude 4.5 Sonnetに匹敵する性能を実証。
  • 毎秒150トークンを生成し、推論コストはClaudeの約2.5%のレベルと非常に経済的。

MiMo-V2-Flashはまた、モデルが一度に複数のトークンを生成・検証できるようにするMulti-Token Prediction（MTP）技術も導入しています。

2026年3月には、さらに野心的なモデルであるMiMo-V2-Proが登場しました。このモデルは合計1兆以上のパラメータを持ち、一度に数百ページ分の小説を処理できる100万トークンのコンテキストウィンドウをサポートします。特に、複雑な計画と実行を必要とする「エージェント（agentic）」のタスク向けに設計されています。

MiMo-V2-Proとともに、マルチモーダル版のMiMo-V2-Omniおよびテキストから音声への変換用MiMo-V2-TTSも同時にリリースされました。

MiMo-V2.5および特化モデル群

V2.5-Proはリリース当時、Artificial Analysisベンチマークにおいてエージェント機能部門で世界最高のオープンソースモデルに選ばれました。また、Xiaomiは100万トークンのコンテキストウィンドウの使用に関する追加費用を撤廃し、開発者フレンドリーな環境を提供しました。

さらに、さまざまな目的のための特化型モデルもリリースされました。

  • MiMo Code：長いプロジェクトでも文脈を維持する継続的なメモリシステムを備えた、ターミナルベースのAIコーディングエージェント。
  • MiMo-VLおよびMiMo-VL-Miloco-7B：視覚言語モデルで、特にMilocoは家庭環境を理解するように設計されており、日常的なジェスチャーや家事活動を認識します。
  • MiDashengLM-7BおよびMiMo-Audio：単なる音声認識を超え、音楽、周囲の音、話者の感情まで把握するオーディオAIモデル。

  • わずか数秒のサンプルだけで声を複製するゼロショット（zero-shot）音声複製機能を提供し、複数の言語にわたって元の声の特徴を維持しながら自然な音声を生成。

OmniVoiceは単一のトランスフォーマー構造を通じて非常に高速な推論速度を誇り、さまざまな発音補正ツールを含んでいます。MiMo-V2.5-TTSとASRシステムはこれらと組み合わされることで、開発者が個別のツール接続なしにエンドポイント音声ベースの製品を構築できるようにします。

HyperAIはXiaomi 15シリーズから適用された機能セットで、リアルタイム翻訳、AIライティング支援、スマート音声認識（録音の要約）、AI写真編集などを含み、バックエンドにGoogle Geminiを統合することもあります。

Xiaomi's AI Journey and LLM Competition

While Xiaomi has traditionally been known for its affordable smartphones and smart home devices, the company has quietly transformed into one of the world's most ambitious AI players over the past 1.5 years.

From Large Language Models (LLMs) to voice cloning and autonomous mobile agents, Xiaomi is moving at a rapid pace. Here is everything regarding Xiaomi's position in the massive AI and LLM competition.

MiMo-7B: The Start of the LLM Race

Xiaomi's AI narrative began in earnest in April 2025 with the release of its first open-source large language model, MiMo-7B. "MiMo" stands for Xiaomi Model (MiandMo). From the beginning, it was notable that Xiaomi focused on reasoning and coding rather than simple chatting.

Despite having only 7 billion parameters, MiMo-7B demonstrated exceptional performance.

  • The reinforced learning version scored 95.8% on math benchmarks such as MATH-500.
  • It outperformed OpenAI's o1-mini and Alibaba's Qwen-32B-Preview in the AIME 2024 and 2025 math competitions.

The model was trained using a specially curated dataset of 200 billion reasoning tokens through a three-stage training process (totaling 25 trillion tokens). MiMo-7B is released under the MIT license and is available on Hugging Face.

The development team was led by Luo Fuli, formerly of DeepSeek.

MiMo-V2-Flash and MiMo-V2-Pro

In December 2025, Xiaomi announced MiMo-V2-Flash, a 309-billion parameter model utilizing a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) design to maintain high performance while keeping most weights "inactive."

Key features of MiMo-V2-Flash include:

  • Ranked second among open-source models in reasoning benchmarks.
  • Demonstrated performance comparable to GPT-5 and Claude 4.5 Sonnet in software engineering tests (SWE-Bench Verified).
  • Generates 150 tokens per second with inference costs at approximately 2.5% of Claude's level, making it highly economical.

MiMo-V2-Flash also introduced Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) technology, allowing the model to generate and verify multiple tokens simultaneously.

In March 2026, an even more ambitious model, MiMo-V2-Pro, was released. This model features over 1 trillion parameters and supports a 1 million token context window capable of processing hundreds of pages of text at once. It is specifically designed for "agentic" tasks requiring complex planning and execution.

Alongside MiMo-V2-Pro, the multimodal version MiMo-V2-Omni and the text-to-speech model MiMo-V2-TTS were released simultaneously.

MiMo-V2.5 and Specialized Model Family

In late April 2026, Xiaomi released MiMo-V2.5-Pro, which integrates the advantages of the V2 series. This is a 1.02 trillion parameter model capable of processing text, images, audio, and video.

At launch, V2.5-Pro was selected as the world's best open-source model in the agent functionality category by Artificial Analysis benchmarks. Additionally, Xiaomi provided a developer-friendly environment by removing extra costs for using the 1 million token context window.

A suite of specialized models was also released for specific purposes:

  • MiMo Code: A terminal-based AI coding agent with a continuous memory system to maintain context in long projects.
  • MiMo-VL and MiMo-VL-Miloco-7B: Vision language models; specifically, Miloco is designed to understand home environments and recognize daily gestures and household activities.
  • MiDashengLM-7B and MiMo-Audio: Audio AI models that go beyond simple speech recognition to identify music, ambient sounds, and the speaker's emotions.

OmniVoice and Voice Technology Pipeline

Released in May 2026, OmniVoice is a TTS model developed by the next-generation Kaldi team at Xiaomi AI Lab.

  • Supports 646 languages, including low-resource languages with limited data.
  • Features zero-shot voice cloning, capable of cloning a voice with only a few seconds of samples while maintaining original characteristics across multiple languages.

OmniVoice boasts very fast inference speeds through a single transformer architecture and includes various pronunciation correction tools. When combined with MiMo-V2.5-TTS and ASR systems, it allows developers to build endpoint voice-based products without needing separate tool integrations.

Consumer Services: Xiao AI and HyperAI

Xiaomi's core experience for general users is divided into two main areas.

Xiao AI (小爱) is a long-standing voice assistant operating on smartphones, speakers, and wearables. It was upgraded to "Super Xiao AI" with HyperOS 2, featuring improved context memory and image generation capabilities.

HyperAI is a suite of features introduced starting with the Xiaomi 15 series, including real-time translation, AI writing assistance, smart voice recognition (recording summaries), and AI photo editing. It also integrates Google Gemini as a backend for certain functions.

The Future Core: miclaw

The most advanced part of Xiaomi's AI strategy is miclaw, announced in March 2026. Rather than a simple chatbot, it is an autonomous AI agent based on the MiMo architecture.

miclaw interprets user intent to perform multi-step tasks such as executing apps, navigating interfaces, and filling out forms. Through a "reasoning-execution loop," it verifies steps until a result is achieved, while processing personal information locally to enhance security.

miclaw is currently in private beta for the Xiaomi 17 series and is scheduled to be integrated at the system level in HyperOS 4.

Investment Scale and Strategic Goals

In March 2026, CEO Lei Jun announced an investment of at least $8.7 billion in the AI field over the next three years. This is in addition to existing R&D budgets; annual R&D spending for 2026 is expected to reach approximately 40 billion yuan (roughly $5.7 billion).

Xiaomi aims for a "grand convergence," integrating proprietary chips, OS, and independent AI models into a single device. The company has already demonstrated its technical competitiveness by capturing a significant portion of OpenRouter traffic.

Overall Assessment

Xiaomi is evolving rapidly from a hardware manufacturer into a full-stack AI provider, encompassing everything from internal models to agent systems. Through massive investment and an open-source strategy, the company is building a unique ecosystem. With the integration of miclaw and HyperOS 4, Xiaomi is positioned to establish itself as a true AI platform company.

Summary

Xiaomi has rapidly transitioned from a hardware-focused manufacturer to a major player in the AI space by developing its own "MiMo" LLM series and specialized models for coding, vision, and audio. The company's strategy involves a "grand convergence" of proprietary chips, OS, and autonomous agents like miclaw to create a comprehensive AI ecosystem. Backed by significant investment and an open-source approach, Xiaomi is building a robust infrastructure to compete at the highest levels of AI technology.
