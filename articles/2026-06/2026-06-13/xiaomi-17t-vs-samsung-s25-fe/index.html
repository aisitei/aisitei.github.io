Xiaomi 17T vs Samsung Galaxy S25 FE 비교: 디스플레이, 카메라, 배터리 및 성능

2026-06-13

디자인 및 디스플레이

Xiaomi 17T와 Samsung Galaxy S25 FE는 초고가 플래그십 가격대에 진입하지 않으면서도 프리미엄급 성능을 원하는 소비자들을 타겟으로 합니다. Xiaomi는 강력한 하드웨어와 카메라 업그레이드에 집중하는 반면, Samsung은 장기적인 소프트웨어 지원과 생태계의 이점을 강조합니다.

두 기기는 유사한 프리미엄 세그먼트를 겨냥함에도 디자인 접근 방식에서 차이를 보입니다. Xiaomi는 전·후면에 Gorilla Glass 7i 보호 기능을 적용하고 강화된 플라스틱 프레임을 사용한 반면, Samsung은 Gorilla Glass Victus+와 알루미늄 프레임의 조합을 선택하여 보다 고급스러운 마감을 제공합니다. 두 기기 모두 IP68 등급의 방수 및 방진 기능을 갖춰 일상적인 내구성을 확보했습니다.

Samsung의 구조는 금속 프레임과 유리 소재의 조합 덕분에 더욱 플래그십에 가까운 느낌을 줍니다. 반면 Xiaomi는 현대적인 Leica 브랜드 카메라 구성과 세련되고 성능 중심적인 디자인 언어로 이를 보완합니다. 프리미엄 소재를 중시하는 사용자는 Samsung을, 실용성을 우선하는 사용자는 Xiaomi를 매력적으로 느낄 수 있습니다.

Xiaomi 17T는 6.59인치 AMOLED 패널을 탑재하여 1268 x 2756의 고해상도, 120Hz 주사율, Dolby Vision 지원, 3840Hz PWM 디밍, 그리고 최대 3500니트의 밝기를 제공합니다. 이에 맞선 Samsung은 더 큰 6.7인치 Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X 디스플레이를 탑재했으며, 가변 주사율 기술과 HDR10+를 지원합니다.

Xiaomi는 특히 야외에서 훨씬 더 선명하고 밝은 시각적 경험을 제공하며, Samsung의 LTPO 기술은 일상적인 사용 환경에서 전력 효율을 높이는 데 도움을 줍니다. 결과적으로 Samsung Galaxy S25 FE가 더 고급스러운 소재를 제공하지만, Xiaomi 17T는 높은 해상도, 우수한 밝기, Dolby Vision 지원 및 향상된 시력 보호 기능으로 디스플레이 성능에서 우위를 점합니다.

성능 및 배터리

Xiaomi 17T는 MediaTek Dimensity 8500 Ultra 프로세서와 12GB RAM, UFS 4.1 스토리지를 탑재했습니다. Samsung Galaxy S25 FE는 Exynos 2400 칩셋과 8GB RAM, UFS 4.0 스토리지를 채택했습니다.

Samsung의 Exynos 2400은 전반적으로 더 강력한 성능을 발휘하며, 특히 그래픽 집약적인 작업과 지속적인 플래그십 수준의 성능 유지에서 강점을 보입니다. 또한 Samsung의 광범위한 소프트웨어 최적화와 7년간의 Android 업데이트 지원의 혜택을 받습니다. 반면 Xiaomi는 기본적으로 더 많은 RAM과 더 빠른 스토리지를 제공하여 멀티태스킹과 앱 로딩 속도에서 이점이 있습니다.

배터리 성능은 Xiaomi의 주요 강점입니다. 글로벌 모델 기준, Xiaomi는 6500mAh 배터리와 67W 유선 충전을 지원하는 반면, Samsung은 4900mAh 배터리에 45W 유선 충전 및 15W 무선 충전을 제공합니다.

Samsung의 무선 충전과 DeX 지원은 편의성을 더해주지만, Xiaomi의 훨씬 큰 배터리 용량과 빠른 충전 속도는 헤비 유저에게 더욱 실용적입니다. 일상적인 사용에서 두 기기 간의 지속 시간 차이는 뚜렷하게 나타날 것으로 보입니다.

Samsung은 강력한 플래그십급 처리 능력과 업계 최고 수준의 소프트웨어 지원을 제공합니다. 반면 Xiaomi는 훨씬 뛰어난 배터리 수명, 빠른 충전 속도, 더 많은 RAM 및 빠른 스토리지를 통해 대응합니다. 대부분의 구매자에게 Xiaomi는 더욱 균형 잡힌 하드웨어 패키지를 제공하는 선택지가 될 것입니다.

Design and Display

The Xiaomi 17T and Samsung Galaxy S25 FE target consumers seeking premium performance without reaching the ultra-high flagship price point. While Xiaomi focuses on robust hardware and camera upgrades, Samsung emphasizes long-term software support and ecosystem advantages.

Despite targeting similar premium segments, the two devices differ in their design approaches. Xiaomi utilizes Gorilla Glass 7i protection on both front and back with a reinforced plastic frame, while Samsung opts for a combination of Gorilla Glass Victus+ and an aluminum frame to provide a more premium finish. Both devices feature IP68-rated water and dust resistance to ensure everyday durability.

Samsung's construction offers a feel closer to a flagship due to the metal and glass combination, whereas Xiaomi compensates with its Leica-branded camera configuration and a modern, performance-oriented design language. Users prioritizing premium materials may find Samsung more appealing, while those prioritizing practicality might prefer the Xiaomi.

The Xiaomi 17T features a 6.59-inch AMOLED panel with high resolution (1268 x 2756), a 120Hz refresh rate, Dolby Vision support, 3840Hz PWM dimming, and a peak brightness of up to 3500 nits. In contrast, the Samsung Galaxy S25 FE features a larger 6.7-inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X display with variable refresh rate technology and HDR10+ support.

Xiaomi provides a significantly brighter and clearer visual experience, particularly outdoors, while Samsung's LTPO technology helps improve power efficiency during daily use. Ultimately, while the Samsung Galaxy S25 FE offers more premium materials, the Xiaomi 17T takes the lead in display performance with its higher resolution, superior brightness, Dolby Vision support, and enhanced eye protection features.

Performance and Battery

The Xiaomi 17T is equipped with a MediaTek Dimensity 8500 Ultra processor, 12GB of RAM, and UFS 4.1 storage. The Samsung Galaxy S25 FE utilizes an Exynos 2400 chipset with 8GB of RAM and UFS 4.0 storage.

Samsung's Exynos 2400 delivers stronger overall performance, particularly in graphics-intensive tasks and maintaining consistent flagship-level performance. Additionally, it benefits from Samsung's extensive software optimization and a commitment to seven years of Android updates. Conversely, Xiaomi provides more RAM and faster storage out of the box, offering advantages in multitasking and app loading speeds.

Battery performance is a major strength for Xiaomi. Global models feature:

  • Xiaomi: 6500mAh battery with 67W wired charging
  • Samsung: 4900mAh battery with 45W wired charging and 15W wireless charging

While Samsung's wireless charging and DeX support add convenience, Xiaomi's significantly larger battery capacity and faster charging speeds are more practical for heavy users. A noticeable difference in endurance between the two devices is expected during daily use.

Samsung provides powerful flagship-level processing and industry-leading software support, while Xiaomi counters with superior battery life, faster charging, and higher RAM/storage specifications. For most buyers, Xiaomi offers a more balanced hardware package.

In terms of camera hardware, the Xiaomi 17T holds a significant advantage.

Main and Auxiliary Lens Configuration:

    • Main Sensor: 50MP
    • Telephoto: 50MP Periscope Telephoto (5x Optical Zoom)
    • Ultra-wide: 12MP
  • Samsung Galaxy S25 FE:
    • Main Sensor: 50MP

    • Ultra-wide: 12MP

Xiaomi's periscope telephoto camera provides a clear advantage for long-distance shots. Furthermore, the Leica tuning adds a unique character to photos, creating a more premium and cinematic feel. Samsung's image processing maintains consistent performance within the Android ecosystem, providing reliable colors and strong video performance.

Front Camera Specifications:

  • Xiaomi 17T: 32MP front camera (4K recording supported)
  • Samsung Galaxy S25 FE: 12MP selfie camera (4K video supported)

Xiaomi's high-resolution sensor offers more detail and flexibility, especially for social media creators and video calls. While Samsung's selfie camera provides reliable performance, it falls behind Xiaomi in terms of specifications.

While Samsung offers excellent processing and impressive 8K video recording capabilities, Xiaomi provides a more capable photography package thanks to its superior telephoto system, stronger selfie camera, Leica enhancements, and overall hardware diversity.

Price and Value

The Xiaomi 17T is priced at approximately ₹60,000, while the Samsung Galaxy S25 FE is priced at approximately ₹50,000. Despite the higher price point, Xiaomi offers several hardware upgrades including a high-resolution display, larger battery capacity, faster charging, more RAM/faster storage, and a significantly more powerful telephoto camera.

Samsung strengthens its immediate value proposition through a lower price point. Buyers can enjoy additional benefits such as seven years of Android updates, wireless charging, Samsung DeX, and a premium aluminum frame design. These factors are particularly appealing to users planning to keep their device for several years.

When evaluating pure hardware performance, Xiaomi justifies its premium price with tangible upgrades that impact daily use. Conversely, Samsung focuses on ecosystem benefits and long-term software support rather than maximizing specifications. The Galaxy S25 FE offers strong value at a lower price point, while the Xiaomi 17T justifies its cost through superior hardware.

The Xiaomi 17T distinguishes itself with a Leica-based camera system, a 5x periscope zoom lens, a brighter and clearer display, larger battery capacity, faster charging speeds, and a 12GB RAM configuration. These upgrades target users who prioritize photography, multimedia consumption, and long battery life.

The Samsung Galaxy S25 FE distinguishes itself through seven years of Android updates, wireless charging, Samsung DeX, a premium aluminum structure, and the mature Samsung ecosystem. The Xiaomi 17T is a more comprehensive smartphone for users seeking powerful hardware, superior display performance, long battery life, fast charging, and camera versatility. The Samsung Galaxy S25 FE remains an excellent alternative due to its lower price, strong software commitment, wireless charging, and ecosystem benefits. For most power users, the Xiaomi 17T emerges as the more complete package.

Summary

The Xiaomi 17T is the ideal choice for power users who prioritize high-end hardware, superior camera capabilities (including a periscope lens), and fast charging. In contrast, the Samsung Galaxy S25 FE offers a compelling value proposition for those seeking a reliable ecosystem, long-term software support, and premium build quality at a lower price point.

デザインとディスプレイ

Xiaomi 17TとSamsung Galaxy S25 FEは、超高額なフラッグシップ価格帯には入らないものの、プレミアムな性能を求めるユーザーをターゲットにしています。Xiaomiが強力なハードウェアとカメラのアップグレードに注力する一方で、Samsungは長期的なソフトウェアサポートとエコシステムの利点を強調しています。

両デバイスとも同様のプレミアムセグメントを狙っていますが、デザインへのアプローチには違いがあります。Xiaomiは前面および背面にGorilla Glass 7iの保護機能を適用し、強化されたプラスチックフレームを採用しました。対するSamsungは、Gorilla Glass Victus+とアルミニウムフレームの組み合わせを選択し、より高級感のある仕上げを提供しています。両デバイスともIP68等級の防水・防塵機能を備えており、日常的な耐久性を確保しています。

Samsungの構造は、金属フレームとガラス素材の組み合わせにより、よりフラッグシップに近い質感を与えています。一方、XiaomiはモダンなLeicaブランドのカメラ構成と、洗練された性能重視のデザイン言語でこれを補っています。プレミアムな素材を重視するユーザーにはSamsungが、実用性を優先するユーザーにはXiaomiが魅力的に映るでしょう。

Design and Display

The Xiaomi 17T and Samsung Galaxy S25 FE target consumers seeking premium performance without reaching the ultra-high flagship price point. While Xiaomi focuses on robust hardware and camera upgrades, Samsung emphasizes long-term software support and ecosystem benefits.

Despite targeting similar premium segments, the two devices differ in their design approaches. Xiaomi utilizes Gorilla Glass 7i protection on both front and back with a reinforced plastic frame, while Samsung opts for a combination of Gorilla Glass Victus+ and an aluminum frame to provide a more premium finish. Both devices feature IP68-rated water and dust resistance to ensure everyday durability.

Samsung's construction offers a feel closer to a flagship thanks to the metal frame and glass materials. In contrast, Xiaomi compensates with its modern Leica-branded camera configuration and a sleek, performance-oriented design language. Users prioritizing premium materials may find Samsung more appealing, while those prioritizing practicality might prefer the Xiaomi.

The Xiaomi 17T features a 6.59-inch AMOLED panel offering high resolution (1268 x 2756), a 120Hz refresh rate, Dolby Vision support, 3840Hz PWM dimming, and a peak brightness of up to 3,500 nits. In response, Samsung features a larger 6.7-inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X display with variable refresh rate technology and HDR10+ support.

Xiaomi provides a significantly brighter and clearer visual experience, especially outdoors, while Samsung's LTPO technology helps improve power efficiency in daily usage. Ultimately, while the Samsung Galaxy S25 FE offers more premium materials, the Xiaomi 17T takes the lead in display performance with higher resolution, superior brightness, Dolby Vision support, and enhanced eye protection features.

Conversely, Xiaomi provides more RAM and faster storage out of the box, offering advantages in multitasking and app loading speeds.

Samsung provides powerful flagship-level processing and industry-leading software support. Meanwhile, Xiaomi counters with superior battery life, faster charging speeds, more RAM, and faster storage. For most buyers, the Xiaomi offers a more balanced hardware package.
