미국 내 DJI와 Insta360의 법적 분쟁

중국의 두 거대 카메라 브랜드가 미국에서 서로를 상대로 소송을 제기했습니다. DJI는 2026년 6월 10일경, Insta360을 상대로 두 건의 소송을 제기했습니다. 소송의 대상은 Insta360이 최근 출시한 Luna 시리즈 짐벌 카메라로, DJI는 이 제품들이 시각적 및 기술적으로 자사의 Osmo Pocket 라인과 지나치게 유사하다고 주장하고 있습니다. 두 소송 모두 미국 텍사스 동부 지역 법원에 제출되었습니다.

디자인 특허 관련 쟁점

첫 번째 소송은 디자인 특허에 관한 것입니다. DJI는 Luna 카메라가 Osmo Pocket 3와 매우 흡사한 외관을 가지고 있다고 주장하며, 다음과 같은 요소들이 소비자에게 혼동을 줄 수 있다고 강조합니다.

  • 슬림한 핸드헬드 바디
  • 짐벌 암과 연결되는 독특한 목(neck) 구조
  • 회전하는 화면
  • 측면 포트 및 하단 인터페이스

기술적 기능 및 실용 특허 관련 쟁점

두 번째 소송은 더 깊은 기술적 문제를 다루며, 카메라 내부의 실제 기술을 포함하는 네 가지 실용 특허를 겨냥합니다. DJI는 Insta360이 다음의 핵심 기술들을 인지한 상태에서 "노골적으로 복제"했다고 주장하며 고의적 침해에 따른 강화된 손해 배상을 요구하고 있습니다.

  • 짐벌의 'lock mode'와 'follow mode' 간 전환 방식
  • 피사체 추적 시스템(subject tracking system)
  • 디스플레이와 짐벌의 실시간 통합 기술

소송 배경 및 향후 전망

DJI는 보상책으로 미국 내 Luna 제품 판매 금지, 최소한의 합리적인 로열티 지급, 그리고 잠력적으로는 이익 전체를 몰수하는 수준의 손해 배상을 요구하고 있습니다. 이러한 갈등은 갑자기 발생한 것이 아니며, 2026년 초부터 DJI는 Insta360에 합류한 전직 엔지니어들이 개발한 기술과 관련된 특허 소유권을 중국에서 주장해 왔습니다.

Insta360은 이에 강력하게 대응할 것으로 예상됩니다. 그들은 이미 포켓 짐벌 및 360도 영상 촬영 분야의 자사 지식재산권(IP)에 대한 반소 가능성을 포함하여 강력한 방어 의사를 밝힌 상태입니다. 이번 소송 결과는 향후 두 회사의 제품 개발 및 시장 전략에 큰 영향을 미칠 것이며, 이미지 기술 산업 내 교차 국경 IP 분쟁에 있어 중요한 선례를 남길 것으로 보입니다.

총평

DJI와 Insta360은 Luna 시리즈의 디자인과 핵심 짐벌 제어 기술을 둘러싸고 미국에서 치열한 법적 공방을 벌이고 있습니다. 이번 소송은 단순한 외형 유사성을 넘어 피사체 추적 및 모드 전환 등 핵심 알고리즘에 대한 특허 침해를 포함하고 있어, 향후 두 브랜드의 시장 점유율과 제품 개발 방향에 결정적인 영향을 미칠 것으로 예상됩니다.

Legal Dispute Between DJI and Insta360 in the U.S.

Two Chinese camera giants have initiated legal action against each other in the United States. On June 10, 2026, DJI filed two lawsuits against Insta360 regarding its recently released Luna series gimbal cameras. DJI claims that these products are visually and technically too similar to its own Osmo Pocket line. Both lawsuits were filed in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Texas.

Design Patent Issues

The first lawsuit pertains to design patents. DJI argues that the Luna camera possesses an appearance very similar to the Osmo Pocket 3, highlighting several elements that could cause consumer confusion:

  • Slim handheld body
  • Unique "neck" structure connecting to the gimbal arm
  • Rotating screen
  • Side ports and bottom interface

Technical Features and Utility Patents

The second lawsuit addresses deeper technical issues, targeting four utility patents involving internal camera technologies. DJI claims that Insta360 "blatantly replicated" these core technologies while being aware of them, and is seeking enhanced damages for intentional infringement:

  • Switching mechanism between the gimbal's 'lock mode' and 'follow mode'
  • Subject tracking system
  • Real-time integration technology between the display and the gimbal

Background and Outlook

As compensation, DJI is seeking a ban on Luna product sales in the U.S., payment of reasonable royalties, and potentially the forfeiture of all profits from these products. This conflict did not emerge suddenly; since early 2026, DJI has been asserting patent ownership over technologies developed by former engineers who joined Insta360 in China.

Insta360 is expected to respond strongly. They have already expressed a firm intent to defend their position, including the possibility of filing counter-suits regarding their own intellectual property (IP) in the fields of pocket gimbals and 360-degree video recording. The outcome of this lawsuit will significantly impact both companies' product development and market strategies, while setting an important precedent for cross-border IP disputes within the imaging technology industry.

Summary

DJI and Insta360 are engaged in a fierce legal battle in the U.S. over the design and core gimbal control technologies of the Luna series. The lawsuit covers both aesthetic similarities and critical technical infringements, such as subject tracking and mode switching algorithms. This case is expected to have a significant impact on market share and future product development for both brands.

Legal Dispute Between DJI and Insta360 in the U.S.

Two Chinese camera giants have entered into a legal battle in the United States. On June 10, 2026, DJI filed two lawsuits against Insta360 regarding its recently released Luna series gimbal cameras. DJI claims that these products are visually and technically too similar to its own Osmo Pocket line. Both lawsuits were filed in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Texas.

Design Patent Issues

The first lawsuit pertains to design patents. DJI argues that the Luna camera possesses an appearance very similar to the Osmo Pocket 3, highlighting several elements that could cause consumer confusion:

  • Slim handheld body
  • Unique "neck" structure connecting to the gimbal arm
  • Rotating screen
  • Side ports and bottom interface

Technical Features and Utility Patents

The second lawsuit addresses deeper technical issues, targeting four utility patents involving internal camera technologies. DJI claims that Insta360 "blatantly replicated" these core technologies while being aware of them, and is seeking enhanced damages for intentional infringement:

  • Switching mechanism between the gimbal's 'lock mode' and 'follow mode'
  • Subject tracking system
  • Real-time integration technology between the display and the gimbal

Background and Outlook

As compensation, DJI is seeking a ban on Luna product sales in the U.S., payment of reasonable royalties, and potentially the forfeiture of all profits from these products. This conflict did not emerge suddenly; since early 2026, DJI has been asserting patent ownership over technologies developed by former engineers who joined Insta360 in China.

Insta360 is expected to respond strongly. They have already expressed a firm intent to defend their position, including the possibility of filing counter-suits regarding their own intellectual property (IP) in the fields of pocket gimbals and 360-degree video recording. The outcome of this lawsuit will significantly impact both companies' product development and market strategies, while setting an important precedent for cross-border IP disputes within the imaging technology industry.

Summary

DJI and Insta360 are engaged in a fierce legal battle in the U.S. over the design and core gimbal control technologies of the Luna series. The lawsuit covers both aesthetic similarities and critical technical infringements, such as subject tracking and mode switching algorithms. This case is expected to have a significant impact on market share and future product development for both brands.
