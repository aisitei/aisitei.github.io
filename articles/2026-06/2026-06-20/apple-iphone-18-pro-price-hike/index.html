Apple iPhone 18 Pro 시작 가격 18.2% 인상 우려

2026-06-20

가격 인상 전망

월스트리트 저널은 어제(6월 18일) 게시물을 통해 Apple iPhone 18 Pro의 시작 가격이 약 200달러 상승하여, 기존 1,099달러에서 1,299달러로 인상될 것으로 예측했습니다. 이는 약 18.2%의 인상 폭이며, 상황에 따라 최대 300달러(약 27.3%)가 인상된 1,399달러까지 올라갈 가능성도 배제할 수 없습니다.

Apple의 입장

Apple은 AI 열풍으로 인한 메모리 칩의 심각한 공급 부족과 가격 폭등으로 인해 가격 인상이 "피할 수 없는" 상황이라고 밝혔습니다. 그동안 Apple은 비용 상승을 자체적으로 흡수해 왔으나, 현재의 상황이 더 이상 "지속 가능하지 않다"는 입장입니다. 다만 구체적인 시기나 범위, 영향을 받는 제품에 대해서는 밝히지 않았습니다.

가격 상승 원인

월스트리트 저널은 iPhone 18 Pro의 가격 상승을 초래하는 주요 원인으로 AI 열풍에 따른 DRAM 및 NAND 플래시 메모리 칩의 공급 부족과 가격 급등을 지목했습니다. 조사 기관 TechInsights는 이 두 종류의 칩 가격이 올해 가을, 작년보다 약 4배가량 상승할 것으로 예측했습니다.

비용 분석

해당 매체는 구체적인 비용 분석을 통해 다음과 같은 수치를 제시했습니다.

  • iPhone 17 Pro: DRAM 약 39달러, 256GB 플래시 메모리 약 13달러
  • iPhone 18 Pro: DRAM 약 145달러(약 271.79% 상승), 플래시 메모리 약 51달러(약 292.31% 상승)

기타 부품 및 조립 비용을 약 530달러로 계산할 경우, iPhone 18 Pro의 총 자재 비용은 582달러(iPhone 17 Pro 기준)에서 726달러로 약 25% 증가하게 됩니다.

최종 예상 가격

Apple이 iPhone 17 Pro와 동일한 47%의 매출 총이익률을 유지하려면 제품 가격을 1,371달러로 책정해야 합니다. 그러나 Apple은 통상적으로 정형화된 숫자로 가격을 결정하므로, 가장 가능성 높은 판매가는 1,299달러(수익률 44%)입니다.

공급망 분석가 밍치궈는 새로운 카메라 시스템의 비용이 이전 세대보다 약 50% 높을 수 있다고 언급했습니다. 이 요소를 포함할 경우, 시작 가격은 1,399달러까지 추가로 상승할 가능성이 있습니다.

총평

AI 열풍으로 인한 DRAM 및 NAND 플래시 메모리 칩의 공급 부족과 비용 급증이 iPhone 18 Pro의 가격 인상을 강력하게 압박하고 있습니다. 핵심 부품의 원가가 크게 상승함에 따라, Apple은 수익성 유지를 위해 기존 모델보다 높은 수준의 판매가를 책정할 가능성이 매우 높습니다.

iPhone 18 Pro 价格上涨预测

据华尔街日报报道，受AI浪潮引发的存储芯片短缺及价格飙升影响，iPhone 18 Pro 的起步售价预计将大幅上调。初步预估涨幅为200美元（从1099美元增至1299美元），甚至可能上涨300美元，达到1399美元。

核心原因：AI驱动的芯片成本飙升

Apple首席执行官蒂姆·库克表示，由于AI热潮导致存储芯片严重短缺且价格暴涨，提价已变得“不可避免”。

  • DRAM内存与NAND闪存因AI需求导致供应紧张。
  • TechInsights预测，这两类核心芯片的价格在今年秋季将比去年上涨约四倍。

成本构成对比分析

由于关键零部件成本大幅攀升，iPhone 18 Pro 的总物料成本预计将从 iPhone 17 Pro 的 582 美元增加到 726 美元，涨幅达 25%。

  • DRAM内存：成本可能从约 39 美元飙升至 145 美元（涨幅 271.79%）。
  • NAND闪存：成本可能从约 13 美元上涨至 51 美元（涨幅 292.31%）。
  • 其他部件与组装成本：约为 530 美元。
  • 额外因素：若计入新相机系统可能增加的 50% 成本，起售价或进一步推高至 1399 美元。

总结

由于AI浪潮导致DRAM和NAND闪存芯片价格大幅飙升，iPhone 18 Pro 的生产成本显著增加。为维持利润率，预计其起步价将从1099美元上调至1299美元或1399美元。

価格上昇の見通し

ウォル街新聞（WSJ）は昨日（6月18日）の投稿において、Apple iPhone 18 Proの開始価格が約200ドル上昇し、従来の1,099ドルから1,299ドルになると予測しました。これは約18.2%の引き上げにあたりますが、状況によっては最大300ドル（約27.3%）の上昇により、1,399ドルに達する可能性も排除できません。

Appleの立場

Appleは、AIブームに伴うメモリチップの深刻な供給不足と価格高騰により、価格の引き上げが「避けられない」状況にあると明らかにしました。これまでAppleはコストの上昇を自社で吸収してきましたが、現在の状況はもはや「持続可能ではない」との見解です。ただし、具体的な時期や範囲、影響を受ける製品については明かしていません。

価格上昇の要因

ウォル街新聞は、iPhone 18 Proの価格上昇を招く主な要因として、AIブームによるDRAMおよびNANDフラッシュメモリチップの供給不足と価格の急騰を挙げています。調査機関のTechInsightsは、これら2種類のチップ価格が今年の秋、前年比で約4倍に上昇すると予測しています。

コスト分析

同メディアは具体的なコスト分析を通じて、以下の数値を提示しました。

  • iPhone 17 Pro: DRAM 約39ドル、256GB フラッシュメモリ 約13ドル
  • iPhone 18 Pro: DRAM 約145ドル（約271.79%上昇）、フラッシュメモリ 約51ドル（約292.31%上昇）

その他の部品および組み立て費用を約530ドルと計算した場合、iPhone 18 Proの総材料費は582ドル（iPhone 17 Pro基準）から726ドルへと約25%増加することになります。

最終予想価格

AppleがiPhone 17 Proと同等の47%の売上総利益率を維持する場合、製品価格は1,371ドルに設定される必要があります。しかし、Appleは通常、整った数字で価格を決定するため、最も可能性の高い販売価格は1,299ドル（利益率44%）です。

サプライチェーンアナリストのミンチー・クォ（Ming-Chi Kuo）は、新しいカメラシステムのコストが前世代よりも約50%高くなる可能性があると指摘しています。この要素を含める場合、開始価格は1,399ドルまでさらに上昇する可能性があります。

総評

AIブームによるDRAMおよびNANDフラッシュメモリチップの供給不足とコスト急増が、iPhone 18 Proの価格引き上げを強く後押ししています。主要部品の原価が大幅に上昇しているため、Appleは収益性を維持するために、従来モデルよりも高い販売価格を設定する可能性が非常に高いです。

まとめ

AIブームに伴うメモリチップ（DRAM、NANDフラッシュ）の深刻な供給不足とコスト高騰により、iPhone 18 Proの価格は大幅に上昇する見通しです。Appleは収益性を維持するため、従来モデルよりも高い販売価格を設定する可能性が非常に高い状況にあります。

Price Hike Forecast

The Wall Street Journal reported on June 18 that the starting price of the Apple iPhone 18 Pro is expected to rise by approximately $200, increasing from $1,099 to $1,299. This represents an increase of about 18.2%. Depending on specific circumstances, the price could potentially reach as high as $1,399, which would be a hike of up to $300 (approximately 27.3%).

Apple's Stance

Apple stated that price increases are "inevitable" due to severe shortages and skyrocketing costs of memory chips driven by the AI boom. While Apple has previously absorbed rising costs internally, they indicated that the current situation is no longer "sustainable." However, they have not disclosed specific dates, ranges, or which products will be affected.

Causes of Price Increase

The Wall Street Journal identified the shortage and price spikes of DRAM and NAND flash memory chips—driven by the AI craze—as the primary factors behind the iPhone 18 Pro's price hike. Research firm TechInsights predicts that the prices for these two types of chips will increase approximately fourfold this fall compared to last year.

Cost Analysis

The publication provided a detailed cost analysis with the following figures:

  • iPhone 17 Pro: DRAM approx. $39, 256GB Flash memory approx. $13
  • iPhone 18 Pro: DRAM approx. $145 (an increase of about 271.79%), Flash memory approx. $51 (an increase of about 292.31%)

If other parts and assembly costs are calculated at approximately $530, the total material cost for the iPhone 18 Pro would rise from $582 (based on the iPhone 17 Pro) to $726, an increase of about 25%.

Final Expected Price

To maintain a gross profit margin of 47%, identical to the iPhone 17 Pro, Apple would need to set the product price at $1,371. However, since Apple typically sets prices using standardized figures, the most likely retail price is $1,299 (a 44% margin).

Supply chain analyst Ming-Chi Kuo noted that the cost of the new camera system could be about 50% higher than the previous generation. If this factor is included, the starting price could rise further to $1,399.

Overall Outlook

The shortage and surging costs of DRAM and NAND flash memory chips caused by the AI boom are putting significant pressure on the pricing of the iPhone 18 Pro. As the cost of core components rises sharply, it is highly likely that Apple will set a higher retail price than previous models to maintain its profit margins.

Summary

The iPhone 18 Pro is expected to see a significant price hike due to the soaring costs and shortages of DRAM and NAND flash memory chips driven by the AI boom. While $1,299 is the most likely starting price, additional factors like advanced camera systems could push the cost even higher to $1,399.

AIブームに起因するDRAMおよびNANDフラッシュメモリチップの供給不足とコスト急増が、iPhone 18 Proの価格引き上げを強く後押ししています。主要部品の原価が大幅に上昇しているため、Appleは収益性を維持するために、従来モデルよりも高い販売価格を設定する可能性が非常に高い状況です。

まとめ

AIブームによるメモリチップ（DRAM、NANDフラッシュ）の深刻な供給不足とコスト高騰により、iPhone 18 Proの製造コストが大幅に上昇しています。これに伴い、Appleは収益性を維持するため、販売価格を従来の1,099ドルから1,299ドル、あるいはカメラシステムのコストを含めて1,399ドルまで引き上げる見通しです。

Price Hike Forecast

The Wall Street Journal reported that the starting price of the Apple iPhone 18 Pro is expected to rise by approximately $200, increasing from $1,099 to $1,299. This represents an increase of about 18.2%. Depending on specific circumstances, the price could potentially reach as high as $1,399, which would be a hike of up to $300 (approximately 27.3%).

Apple's Stance

Apple has stated that price increases are "inevitable" due to the severe shortage and skyrocketing costs of memory chips driven by the AI boom. While Apple has previously absorbed rising costs internally, they indicated that the current situation is no longer "sustainable." However, they have not disclosed specific dates, ranges, or which products will be affected.

Causes of Price Increase

The Wall Street Journal identified the shortage and price spikes of DRAM and NAND flash memory chips—driven by the AI craze—as the primary factors behind the iPhone 18 Pro's price hike. Research firm TechInsights predicts that the prices for these two types of chips will increase approximately fourfold this fall compared to last year.

Cost Analysis

The publication provided a detailed cost analysis with the following figures:

  • iPhone 17 Pro: DRAM approx. $39, 256GB Flash memory approx. $13
  • iPhone 18 Pro: DRAM approx. $145 (an increase of about 271.79%), Flash memory approx. $51 (an increase of about 292.31%)

If other parts and assembly costs are calculated at approximately $530, the total material cost for the iPhone 18 Pro would rise from $582 (based on the iPhone 17 Pro) to $726, an increase of about 25%.

Final Expected Price

To maintain a gross profit margin of 47%, identical to the iPhone 17 Pro, Apple would need to set the product price at $1,371. However, since Apple typically sets prices using standardized figures, the most likely retail price is $1,299 (a 44% margin).

Supply chain analyst Ming-Chi Kuo noted that the cost of the new camera system could be about 50% higher than the previous generation. If this factor is included, the starting price could rise further to $1,399.

Summary

The iPhone 18 Pro is expected to see a significant price hike, potentially reaching $1,299 or $1,399, due to the soaring costs of DRAM and NAND flash memory chips driven by the AI boom. These rising component costs make it difficult for Apple to maintain current profit margins without increasing the retail price. Additional factors, such as more expensive camera systems, may push the final price toward the higher end of the estimate.
