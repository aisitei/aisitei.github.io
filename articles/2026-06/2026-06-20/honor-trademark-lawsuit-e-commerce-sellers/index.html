소송 사유 및 배경

Honor 측은 해당 휴대폰 케이스 제조사들이 상품 제목과 상세 페이지에서 "Honor"라는 단어 또는 "Honor" 로고를 과도하게 사용하고 있다고 주장합니다. Honor는 이 두 가지가 자사의 등록 상표임을 강조하며, 이러한 판매자의 행위가 '브랜드 편승(Brand Riding)'에 해당하여 상표권을 침해한다고 판단하고 있습니다.

상표권 침해 유형 정의

공개된 자료에 따르면 '악의적인 상표 편승'은 운영자가 주관적 악의를 가지고 타인의 유명 상표와 동일하거나 유사한 표식을 등록·사용하거나, 기업명 및 포장 디자인 등을 활용하여 대중에게 혼동을 주고 부당하게 이익을 취하는 행위를 의미합니다. 이러한 행위는 상표권 침해뿐만 아니라 부정경쟁행위에 해당할 수 있습니다.

주요 위반 유형은 다음과 같습니다:

  • 유사 상표 등록: 타 브랜드의 인지도가 높음을 알면서도 유사한 상표를 등록하고, 이를 동일하거나 유사한 상품에 사용하는 경우
  • 상호 및 명칭 강조 사용: 타인의 등록 상표 중 핵심 문구를 자사의 기업명으로 사용하거나 홍보 시 강조하여 소비자가 관련 관계가 있는 것으로 오인하게 하는 경우
  • 허위 연관 광고: 공식 홈페이지, 포장 또는 광고에서 자사 제품을 원본 브랜드의 '업그레이드 제품'이나 '고급 라인'으로 표방하며 소비자를 오도하는 경우
  • 액세서리 품목의 과도한 사용: 휴대폰 케이스 등 액세서리 판매 시 필수적인 설명 범위를 벗어나 메인 이미지나 제목에 브랜드명을 대량 노출하면서 '비공식', '호환용' 등의 문구를 명시하지 않아 출처를 혼동하게 하는 경우
  • 선점적 상표 등록 및 사용: 타인이 먼저 사용 중인 영향력 있는 표식을 알면서도 이를 가로채 등록하고, 해당 상표를 이용해 기존 사용자에게 소송을 제기하거나 시장에 혼란을 주는 경우

恶意攀附商标的判定标准

根据相关规定，以下行为被视为恶意攀附商标及不正当竞争：

  • 注册近似商标：在明知他人品牌具有较高知名度的情况下，注册与其高度相似的商标。
  • 突出使用字号 / 名称：将他人的注册商标核心文字作为企业字号，并在宣传中过度强调。
  • 虚假关联宣传：在广告或包装中宣称产品是原品牌的“升级产品”或“高档系列”。
  • 配件类商品的过度使用：在手机壳等配件销售中，未明确标注“非官方”或“兼容”字样，而在标题、主图等位置大量使用品牌词。
  • 抢注在先使用标识：明知他人在先使用的标识而抢先注册并利用进行市场混淆。

总结

Honor正采取强硬法律手段打击涉及400多家手机壳电商的商标侵权行为，旨在严厉打击通过“品牌攀附”获取不正当利益的行为。相关案例表明，配件商若在宣传中过度使用品牌词且未明确标注兼容关系，将面临较高的法律风险。

商標権侵害の定義

公開された資料によると、「悪質な商標便乗」とは、運営者が主観的な悪意を持って他者の有名商標と同一または類似する標識を登録・使用したり、企業名やパッケージデザインなどを利用して大衆に混乱を与え、不当な利益を得る行為を指します。これらの行為は商標権侵害だけでなく、不正競争行為にも該当する可能性があります。

主な違反類型は以下の通りです：

  • 類似商標の登録：他ブランドの知名度が高いことを知りながら、類似した商標を登録し、それを同一または類似の製品に使用する場合。
  • 商号および名称の強調使用：他人の登録商標のうち核心的な文句を自社の企業名として使用したり、宣伝時に強調して消費者が関連関係があるものと誤認させる場合。
  • 虚偽の関連広告：公式サイト、パッケージ、または広告において、自社製品を純正ブランドの「アップグレード製品」や「高級ライン」として標榜し、消費者を誤導する場合。
  • アクセサリー項目の過度な使用：スマートフォンケースなどのアクセサリー販売時、必須の説明範囲を超えてメイン画像やタイトルにブランド名を大量に露出させ、「非公式」や「互換用」といった文言を明記せずに出所を混同させる場合。
  • 先占的な商標登録および使用：他人が先行して使用している影響力のある標識を認識しながら、それを横取りして登録し、当該商標を利用して既存のユーザーに訴訟を起こしたり市場に混乱を与える場合。

Honor's Trademark Infringement Lawsuit

According to a report by IT Home, Honor has recently launched a series of large-scale trademark infringement lawsuits against numerous e-commerce sellers of mobile phone cases. The scope of these legal actions is significant, with over 400 companies identified as targets in the litigation.

Grounds and Background for Litigation

Honor claims that these phone case manufacturers are excessively using the "Honor" name or logo in product titles and on detailed description pages. Honor emphasizes that both the word and the logo are its registered trademarks, asserting that such actions by sellers constitute "Brand Riding," which constitutes a violation of their trademark rights.

Seller Response and Settlement Attempts

A phone case seller in Shenzhen (referred to by the pseudonym Mr. Lin) argued that using the term "Honor" was intended solely to specify model compatibility and did not constitute trademark infringement. The hearing for this case concluded on June 15, with a verdict expected at least one month later. Mr. Lin expressed concern over his chances of winning the case.

Prior to the trial, Honor's legal counsel attempted to reach a settlement with Mr. Lin, reducing the claim amount from 380,000 yuan to 65,000 yuan. However, Mr. Lin declined the offer, stating that the amount was still too high.

Types of Trademark Infringement

According to public records, "malicious brand riding" refers to actions where operators, with subjective intent, register or use marks identical or similar to famous trademarks, or utilize corporate names and packaging designs to mislead the public for unfair gain. Such acts can be classified as both trademark infringement and unfair competition.

The primary types of violations include:

  • Registration of Similar Trademarks: Registering marks similar to those of other brands despite knowing their high recognition, and using them for identical or similar products.
  • Emphasis on Names and Titles: Using key phrases from another's registered trademark as a company name or highlighting them in promotions to mislead consumers into believing there is an official connection.
  • False Association Advertising: Misleading consumers by claiming one's own products are "upgraded" versions or "premium lines" of the original brand on official websites, packaging, or advertisements.
  • Excessive Use in Accessory Items: When selling accessories like phone cases, exceeding the scope of necessary descriptions by exposing the brand name extensively in main images or titles without clearly stating terms such as "unofficial" or "compatible," thereby confusing the source.
  • Preemptive Trademark Registration and Usage: Knowingly hijacking and registering influential marks used by others to file lawsuits against existing users or create market confusion.

Analysis

Honor is demonstrating a strong commitment to protecting its brand value and intellectual property through these large-scale trademark infringement lawsuits. These legal actions are specifically aimed at clarifying the boundary between "compatibility labeling" and "brand riding," which frequently occurs in the accessories market.

Summary

Honor has initiated massive litigation against over 400 phone case sellers for "Brand Riding" and trademark infringement involving the excessive use of its name and logo. The primary types of violations include:

  • Registering similar trademarks: Registering marks similar to a well-known brand's identity while knowing the original brand's popularity, then using them for identical or similar products.
  • Prominent use of names and titles: Using core phrases from another party's registered trademark as one's own company name or highlighting them in promotions to mislead consumers into believing there is a formal relationship.
  • False association advertising: Misleading consumers by labeling products as "upgraded" or "premium" versions of the original brand on official websites, packaging, or advertisements.
  • Excessive use in accessory categories: Exceeding the necessary scope of description when selling accessories like phone cases by exposing the brand name excessively in main images or titles without clearly stating they are "unofficial" or "compatible."
  • Preemptive trademark registration and usage: Knowingly taking over and registering influential marks used by others to file lawsuits against existing users or create market confusion.

Analysis

Honor is demonstrating a strong commitment to protecting its brand value and intellectual property rights through these large-scale lawsuits. These legal actions are specifically aimed at clarifying the boundary between "compatibility labeling" and "brand riding," which frequently occurs in the mobile accessory market.

Summary

Honor has initiated widespread litigation against over 400 phone case sellers for "Brand Riding" by using its trademarked name and logos excessively. The company is taking a firm legal stance to distinguish between legitimate compatibility information and unauthorized brand exploitation in the accessories market.
