The collection is centered around the theme of "Summer Aesthetics" and features premium specifications such as Kunlun Glass and Red Maple original color lenses. The series is endorsed by the group Teens in Times (TNT). Prices start from 2,999 RMB, with official sales beginning on June 5 at 10:08 AM.

Design

The Huawei nova 16 Pro adopts a trendy and aesthetic design. The rear of the device features a highly recognizable layout where four cameras, a flash, and sensors are integrated into two circular Deco modules arranged horizontally in a three-dimensional configuration. This is an upgrade from the previous "Dazzling Twin" design, creating a unique visual balance that resembles two large eyes.

Below the camera module, only the "HUAWEI nova" logo is displayed, with minimal decorative elements to maintain a clean look. The rear utilizes AG Velvet Glass to provide a premium matte texture that feels smooth during daily use and effectively resists fingerprints or smudges.

Color Options

The Huawei nova 16 Pro features a sophisticated color lineup:

  • Sky Blue: Inspired by clear summer skies and blue oceans, it combines a low-saturation matte base with transparent ice blue tones to evoke the texture of waves.
  • Fantastic Mother of Pearl: Inspired by the natural iridescent colors of deep-sea mother-of-pearl, it features a gradient of pink and blue that creates rich layers under various lighting conditions.
  • Sky White: Combines a milky white glow with warm sky blue tones for a sophisticated atmosphere, featuring a matte texture that complements any style.
  • Starry Black: A minimal black color paired with a premium sandblasted glass texture to provide understated elegance.

Display and Build

The sides of the device feature a 7.1mm "aircraft-grade aluminum alloy mid-frame." The frame color is designed to match the back for visual unity, offering the durability and premium feel characteristic of metal materials.

The front features a 6.84-inch OLED dynamic clear vision protection display. The screen bezels are designed to be very narrow and uniform, providing a strong visual impact when the device is powered on.

Additionally, Kunlun Glass is applied to the display surface along with a long-term nano coating process, improving drop resistance by 10 times compared to previous models to withstand everyday impacts.

Features and Specifications

  • Right side: Volume keys and power button
  • Left side: No buttons
  • Top: Infrared sensor
  • Bottom: Speaker holes, USB-C port, microphone, and SIM card slot
  • Frame: Antenna lines positioned around the perimeter

Overall Review

The Huawei nova 16 series inherits a young and trendy design DNA, successfully balancing aesthetic value with practicality through the combination of sophisticated colors and durable materials. Its enhanced durability and stylish appearance make it an attractive choice for younger users who prioritize both style and functionality.

Summary

The Huawei nova 16 series features a "Summer Aesthetics" design, utilizing AG Velvet Glass and a unique dual-circle camera module to appeal to trend-conscious users. The device is built with an aircraft-grade aluminum frame and Kunlun Glass for enhanced durability while offering a high-quality 6.84-inch OLED display. It combines premium materials with a sophisticated color palette, starting at a price of 2,999 RMB.
