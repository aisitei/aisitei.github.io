Reno 16 시리즈 출시 소식

Oppo는 태국과 말레이시아 등 여러 시장에서 Reno 16 시리즈를 공개하며 Reno 16 Pro 5G, Reno 16 5G, 그리고 Reno 16 F 5G라는 세 가지 새로운 스마트폰을 선보였습니다. 이 모델들은 동일한 디자인 언어와 소프트웨어 경험을 공유하지만, 카메라, 성능, 배터리 용량 및 연결 기능의 조합에 따라 각기 다른 가격대를 공략합니다.

이번 신규 라인업은 전 시리즈에 걸쳐 Snap Key, Oppo Bubble 지원, 그리고 대용량 배터리와 같은 기능을 도입했습니다.

Reno 16 시리즈 사양 및 주요 기능

라인업의 최상위 모델인 Reno 16 Pro 5G는 6.32인치 AMOLED 디스플레이를 탑재했으며, 이는 2640 x 1216 픽셀의 1.5K 해상도, 지원되는 게임에서 최대 144Hz의 주사율, 10비트 컬러, 그리고 최대 1,800니트의 고휘도 모드를 제공합니다. 이 모델은 MediaTek Dimensity 8550 Super 칩셋과 LPDDR5X RAM, UFS 3.1 스토리지를 기반으로 구동됩니다.

배터리는 6,000mAh 용량으로 80W 충전을 지원합니다. 카메라 시스템은 다음과 같습니다.

  • 메인 카메라: 200-megapixel (2-axis OIS 지원)
  • 초광각 카메라: 50-megapixel
  • 망원 카메라: 50-megapixel 페리스코프 망원 카메라 (3.5x 광학 줌, OIS 지원)
  • 전면 셀피 카메라: 50-megapixel 오토포커스(autofocus)

표준 모델인 Reno 16 5G는 Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 칩셋을 탑재했으며 LPDDR5X RAM과 UFS 3.1 스토리지를 결합했습니다. 이 기기는 6.32인치 AMOLED 패널(1.5K 해상도, 120Hz 주사율, 1,800니트 HBM 밝기)을 채택했으며, 6,700mAh 배터리와 80W 충전 기능을 갖췄습니다.

후면 카메라 시스템은 다음과 같습니다.

  • 메인 카메라: 50-megapixel (2-axis OIS 지원)
  • 초광각 카메라: 50-megapixel
  • 망원 카메라: 50-megapixel 페리스코프 망원 카메라 (3.5x 광학 줌, OIS 지원)
  • 전면 셀피 카메라: 50-megapixel 오토포커스(autofocus)

Reno 16 F 5G는 MediaTek Dimensity 7300-Energy 칩셋과 LPDDR4X RAM, UFS 3.1 스토리지를 탑재했습니다. 이 모델은 더 큰 6.57인치 Crystal Guard AMOLED 디스플레이를 채택했으며, FHD+ 해상도와 120Hz 주사율, 최대 1,400니트의 HBM 밝기를 지원합니다.

이 기기는 라인업 중 가장 큰 7,000mAh 배터리를 탑재하면서도 80W 충전 기능을 유지합니다. 카메라 구성은 다음과 같습니다.

  • 메인 카메라: 50-megapixel (2-axis OIS 지원)
  • 초광각 카메라: 8-megapixel
  • 망원 카메라: 50-megapixel 망원 카메라 (3.5x 광학 줌, OIS 지원)
  • 전면 셀피 카메라: 50-megapixel 오토포커스(autofocus)

Reno 16 시리즈 전체는 ColorOS 16 기반의 Android 16을 탑재했으며, 디스플레이 내장 지문 인식 스캐너, 스테레오 스피커, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.4, NFC, IR 블래스터 기능을 포함합니다. 또한 알루미늄 프레임과 Crystal Guard 보호 기술이 적용되었으며, IP68, IP69, 및 IP69K 등급의 방수 및 방진을 지원합니다. 표준 및 Pro 모델은 eSIM을 포함한 듀얼 SIM 5G를 지원합니다.

Oppo는 이번 Reno 16 시리즈를 통해 기존에 Find X 시리즈에만 제한적으로 제공하던 AI 기반 Snap Key 기능을 Reno 라인업에 처음으로 도입했습니다. 사용자 맞춤형 사이드 버튼을 통해 복잡한 메뉴 이동 없이도 특정 기능과 AI 기능을 즉시 실행할 수 있습니다.

또한, 호환되는 ColorOS 스마트폰과 함께 사용할 수 있는 소형 마그네틱 스마트 디스플레이 액세서리인 Oppo Bubble Mag E-Badge를 출시했습니다. 이 액세서리는 휴대폰 뒷면에 자석으로 부착되어 후면 카메라의 실시간 미리보기를 보여줌으로써 셀피나 단체 사진 촬영 시 구도를 잡는 것을 훨씬 용이하게 만듭니다.

이 액세서리는 최대 10미터 거리에서 원격 카메라 셔터로도 작동하며, 1.73인치 AMOLED 디스플레이를 통해 사용자 지정 이미지, 애니메이션, 짧은 영상을 표시할 수 있습니다. 무게는 27.5g, 두께는 7mm이며 550mAh 배터리를 탑재하고 IP54 등급의 방수 및 방진 기능을 갖추어 촬영 도구이자 패션 액세서리로도 활용 가능합니다.

Reno 16 Series Launch News

Oppo has unveiled the Reno 16 series in several markets, including Thailand and Malaysia, introducing three new smartphones: the Reno 16 Pro 5G, Reno 16 5G, and Reno 16 F 5G. While these models share a consistent design language and software experience, they are positioned at different price points based on their specific combinations of camera systems, performance, battery capacity, and connectivity features.

The new lineup introduces several key features across the entire series, including Snap Key support, Oppo Bubble integration, and high-capacity batteries.

Reno 16 Series Specifications and Key Features

The flagship model of the lineup, the Reno 16 Pro 5G, features a 6.32-inch AMOLED display with a 1.5K resolution (2640 x 1216), supporting up to a 144Hz refresh rate, 10-bit color, and a high brightness mode of up to 1,800 nits. This model is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 8550 Super chipset, paired with LPDDR5X RAM and UFS 3.1 storage.

The device is equipped with a 6,000mAh battery supporting 80W charging. The camera system includes:

  • Main Camera: 200-megapixel (with 2-axis OIS support)
  • Ultra-wide Camera: 50-megapixel
  • Telephoto Camera: 50-megapixel periscope telephoto (3.5x optical zoom, OIS support)
  • Front Selfie Camera: 50-megapixel with autofocus

The standard Reno 16 5G is equipped with the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset combined with LPDDR5X RAM and UFS 3.1 storage. It features a 6.32-inch AMOLED panel (1.5K resolution, 120Hz refresh rate, and 1,800 nits HBM brightness) and a 6,700mAh battery with 80W charging capabilities.

The rear camera system for this model consists of:

  • Main Camera: 50-megapixel (with 2-axis OIS support)
  • Ultra-wide Camera: 50-megapixel
  • Telephoto Camera: 50-megapixel periscope telephoto (3.5x optical zoom, OIS support)
  • Front Selfie Camera: 50-megapixel with autofocus

The Reno 16 F 5G features the MediaTek Dimensity 7300-Energy chipset with LPDDR4X RAM and UFS 3.1 storage. This model adopts a larger 6.57-inch Crystal Guard AMOLED display, supporting FHD+ resolution, a 120Hz refresh rate, and up to 1,400 nits HBM brightness.

Despite its position in the lineup, this device features a massive 7,000mAh battery while maintaining 80W charging. The camera configuration is as follows:

  • Main Camera: 50-megapixel (with 2-axis OIS support)
  • Ultra-wide Camera: 8-megapixel
  • Telephoto Camera: 50-megapixel telephoto (3.5x optical zoom, OIS support)
  • Front Selfie Camera: 50-megapixel with autofocus

The entire Reno 16 series runs on Android 16 based on ColorOS 16 and includes an in-display fingerprint scanner, stereo speakers, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.4, NFC, and an IR blaster. The devices are constructed with aluminum frames and Crystal Guard protection, supporting IP68, IP69, and IP69K ratings for water and dust resistance. Both the standard and Pro models support Dual SIM 5G, including eSIM.

With the Reno 16 series, Oppo has introduced the AI-powered Snap Key feature—previously exclusive to the Find X series—to the Reno lineup. This customizable side button allows users to immediately execute specific functions and AI features without navigating through complex menus.

Additionally, Oppo launched the "Oppo Bubble Mag E-Badge," a small magnetic smart display accessory compatible with ColorOS smartphones. Attached magnetically to the back of the phone, it provides a real-time preview of the rear camera, making it significantly easier to frame selfies or group photos. This accessory functions as a remote camera shutter at distances up to 10 meters and features a 1.73-inch AMOLED display capable of displaying custom images, animations, and short videos. It weighs 27.5g, is 7mm thick, contains a 550mAh battery, and carries an IP54 rating for water and dust resistance.

Reno 16 Series Pricing and Availability

In the Thai market, the Reno 16 Pro 5G (12GB + 512GB) is priced at 26,999 THB (approximately $810). It is available in Starlight Black and Pop White.

The Reno 16 5G starts at 19,999 THB (approximately $600) for the 8GB + 256GB model, while the 12GB + 256GB model is priced at 22,999 THB (approximately $690). Available colors include Pop White, Twilight Violet, and Dream Purple.

The Reno 16 F 5G is available in two versions: the 8GB + 128GB version for 15,999 THB (approximately $480) and the 8GB + 256GB model for 16,999 THB (approximately $510). Color options include Pop White, Dream Purple, and Twilight Violet.

Summary

The Oppo Reno 16 series offers a versatile lineup that caters to various user needs through high-performance processors, massive battery capacities, and tiered camera specifications. By introducing the AI-powered Snap Key and the unique Oppo Bubble Mag E-Badge accessory, Oppo focuses on providing an innovative user experience across both software and hardware.

Reno 16 Series Launch News

Oppo has unveiled the Reno 16 series in several markets, including Thailand and Malaysia, introducing three new smartphones: the Reno 16 Pro 5G, Reno 16 5G, and Reno 16 F 5G. While these models share a consistent design language and software experience, they are positioned at different price points based on their specific combinations of camera systems, performance, battery capacity, and connectivity features.

The new lineup introduces several key features across the entire series, including Snap Key support, Oppo Bubble integration, and high-capacity batteries.

Reno 16 Series Specifications and Key Features

The flagship model of the lineup, the Reno 16 Pro 5G, features a 6.32-inch AMOLED display with a 1.5K resolution (2640 x 1216), supporting up to a 144Hz refresh rate, 10-bit color, and a high brightness mode of up to 1,800 nits. This model is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 8550 Super chipset, paired with LPDDR5X RAM and UFS 3.1 storage.

The device is equipped with a 6,000mAh battery supporting 80W charging. The camera system includes:

  • Main Camera: 200-megapixel (with 2-axis OIS support)
  • Ultra-wide Camera: 50-megapixel
  • Telephoto Camera: 50-megapixel periscope telephoto (3.5x optical zoom, OIS support)
  • Front Selfie Camera: 50-megapixel with autofocus

The standard Reno 16 5G is equipped with the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset combined with LPDDR5X RAM and UFS 3.1 storage. It features a 6.32-inch AMOLED panel (1.5K resolution, 120Hz refresh rate, and 1,800 nits HBM brightness) and a 6,700mAh battery with 80W charging capabilities.

The rear camera system for this model consists of:

  • Main Camera: 50-megapixel (with 2-axis OIS support)
  • Ultra-wide Camera: 50-megapixel
  • Telephoto Camera: 50-megapixel periscope telephoto (3.5x optical zoom, OIS support)
  • Front Selfie Camera: 50-megapixel with autofocus

The Reno 16 F 5G features the MediaTek Dimensity 7300-Energy chipset with LPDDR4X RAM and UFS 3.1 storage. This model adopts a larger 6.57-inch Crystal Guard AMOLED display, supporting FHD+ resolution, a 120Hz refresh rate, and up to 1,400 nits HBM brightness.

Despite its position in the lineup, this device features a massive 7,000mAh battery while maintaining 80W charging. The camera configuration is as follows:

  • Main Camera: 50-megapixel (with 2-axis OIS support)
  • Ultra-wide Camera: 8-megapixel
  • Telephoto Camera: 50-megapixel telephoto (3.5x optical zoom, OIS support)
  • Front Selfie Camera: 50-megapixel with autofocus

The entire Reno 16 series runs on ColorOS 16 based on Android 16. It includes an in-display fingerprint scanner, stereo speakers, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.4, NFC, and an IR blaster. The devices are built with aluminum frames and Crystal Guard protection, supporting IP68, IP69, and IP69K ratings. The standard and Pro models also support dual SIM 5G, including eSIM.

Oppo has introduced the AI-powered Snap Key feature to the Reno lineup for the first time, a feature previously limited to the Find X series. A customizable side button allows users to instantly launch specific functions and AI features without navigating through complex menus.

Additionally, Oppo launched the Oppo Bubble Mag E-Badge, a small magnetic smart display accessory compatible with ColorOS smartphones. Attached to the back of the phone via magnets, it displays a real-time preview of the rear camera, making it easier to frame selfies or group photos. This accessory acts as a remote camera shutter at distances up to 10 meters and features a 1.73-inch AMOLED display capable of showing custom images, animations, and short videos. It weighs 27.5g, is 7mm thick, has a 550mAh battery, and carries an IP54 rating.

The Reno 16 5G starts at 19,999 THB (approx. $600) for the 8GB + 256GB model, with a 12GB + 256GB model priced at 22,999 THB (approx. $690). It is available in Pop White, Twilight Violet, and Dream Purple.

The Reno 16 F 5G is priced at 15,999 THB (approx. $480) for the 8GB + 128GB version and 16,999 THB (approx. $510) for the 8GB + 256GB model. It comes in Pop White, Dream Purple, and Twilight Violet.
