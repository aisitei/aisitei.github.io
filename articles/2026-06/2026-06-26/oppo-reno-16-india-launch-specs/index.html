Oppo Reno 16 系列相机规格与功能

  • 5000万像素主摄

  • 5000万像素超广角镜头

Oppo Reno 16 和 Reno 16c 在超广角、主摄和变焦镜头上均支持 4K 60fps HDR 视频录制。此外，该手机引入了 Natural Tone Imaging 技术，旨在各种肤色和光照条件下保持准确的肤色。其他功能还包括 4K 自动水平校正视频录制以及为更平滑的变焦切换而设计的 Zoom Free Video。

Oppo Reno 16 系列还引入了 AI Remix Collage，允许用户在不依赖第三方编辑应用的情况下，将照片、Live Photos 和视频合并到单一的拼贴画中。其他软件功能包括提供 9 种相机风格的 Pop Cam、支持人物、宠物及物体的增强型剪裁动画 Popout 2.0，以及可同时录制前置和后置镜头的 Dual-View Video 2.0。

  • 电池容量：550mAh
  • 防护等级：IP54
  • 功能：支持作为蓝牙遥控器使用（范围达 10 米），并支持定制宠物、照片、Live 图像及短视频。

Oppo Reno 16 系列将于 7 月 2 日在印度首发。关于价格和可用性的详细信息预计将在发布活动中公布。

Oppo Reno 16 Series: AI-Powered Camera and New Accessories

The Oppo Reno 16 series, including the Reno 16 and Reno 16c, is scheduled to launch in India on July 2, with a primary focus on AI-driven photography and content creation. Alongside upgraded camera hardware, Oppo has introduced "Oppo Bubble," a new smart accessory, along with various AI editing tools designed to simplify photo and video production.

Oppo Reno 16 Series Camera Specifications and Features

The Oppo Reno 16 features a high-performance camera setup including:

  • 50MP main camera
  • 50MP ultra-wide selfie camera with a 100-degree field of view
  • 50MP ultra-wide camera
  • 50MP 3.5x portrait telephoto camera with an 85mm equivalent focal length for enhanced subject separation and natural portraits

Both the Reno 16 and Reno 16c support 4K 60fps HDR video recording across the ultra-wide, main, and telephoto lenses. The series also introduces Natural Tone Imaging, which aims to maintain accurate skin tones under various lighting conditions and across different skin tones. Other features include 4K auto-leveling for video recording and Zoom Free Video for smoother zoom transitions.

The Reno 16 series incorporates AI Remix Collage, allowing users to combine photos, Live Photos, and videos into a single collage without relying on third-party editing apps. Additional software features include Pop Cam, which offers nine different camera styles; Popout 2.0, providing enhanced cut-out animations for people, pets, and objects; and Dual-View Video 2.0, which enables simultaneous recording from both front and rear cameras.

Oppo also unveiled its first smart camera companion, the Oppo Bubble. Weighing 27.5g, it attaches magnetically to the Reno 16 series and features a 1.73-inch AMOLED display that mirrors the rear camera preview. It functions as a Bluetooth remote with a range of up to 10 meters, is equipped with a 550mAh battery, holds an IP54 rating, and supports customization for pets, photos, live images, and short videos.

The Oppo Reno 16 series will debut in India on July 2. Detailed information regarding pricing and availability is expected to be announced during the launch event.

Summary

The Oppo Reno 16 series focuses on AI-driven content creation through a high-resolution quad-camera system and advanced tools like Natural Tone Imaging and AI Remix Collage. The addition of the "Oppo Bubble" smart accessory provides a unique hardware companion to enhance the mobile photography experience.
