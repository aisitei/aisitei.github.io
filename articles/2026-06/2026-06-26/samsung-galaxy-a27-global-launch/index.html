  • Memory: Up to 8GB RAM
  • Storage: Up to 256GB internal (expandable up to 2TB via microSD card)

The Galaxy A27 5G ships with Android 16 and Samsung One UI 8.5.   • Main Rear Camera: 50MP, OIS support, f/1.8 aperture
  • Ultra-Wide Rear Camera: 8MP, f/2.2 aperture
  • Macro Rear Camera: 2MP, f/2.4 aperture
