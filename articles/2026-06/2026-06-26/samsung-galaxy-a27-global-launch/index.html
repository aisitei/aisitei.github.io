Samsung Galaxy A27 5G 参数

该机型配备了 6.7 英寸 FHD+ Super AMOLED Infinity-O 显示屏，支持 120Hz 刷新率和冲孔摄像头设计。屏幕分辨率为 1080 x 2340，最高亮度可达 1400 nit，并采用 Corning Gorilla Glass Victus+ 进行防护。

前置镜头为 12MP 自拍照摄像头，配备 f/2.2 光圈。

该设备搭载 5000mAh 大电池，支持 25W 快充。在连接性方面，支持混合双卡 SIM、5G、Bluetooth 5.3 以及 NFC。

Introduction

Samsung has unveiled its latest mid-range 5G smartphone, the Galaxy A27 5G, to a global audience. Following its initial debut in the Czech Republic earlier this month, the device serves as the successor to the Galaxy A26 5G.

Samsung Galaxy A27 5G Specifications

The device features a 6.7-inch FHD+ Super AMOLED Infinity-O display with a 120Hz refresh rate and a punch-hole camera cutout. It provides a resolution of 1080 x 2340 and a peak brightness of 1400 nits, all protected by Corning Gorilla Glass Victus+.

The Galaxy A27 5G is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 mobile platform based on a 4nm process. The hardware specifications include:

  • Octa-core chipset (4x Cortex-A78 at 2.4GHz and 4x Cortex-A55 at 1.8GHz)
  • Adreno 710 GPU
  • Up to 8GB RAM
  • Up to 256GB internal storage
  • microSD card slot supporting up to 2TB expansion

The device runs on Android 16 with Samsung One UI 8.5. Samsung has committed to providing up to six generations of Android OS and One UI updates, along with six years of security updates from the initial global launch date. The phone also includes Samsung Knox and Knox Vault for hardware-based protection of sensitive data.

The camera system features a triple rear setup:

  • 50MP Main camera (OIS, f/1.8)
  • 8MP Ultra-wide camera (f/2.2)
  • 2MP Macro camera (f/2.4)
  • 12MP front selfie camera (f/2.2)

The device is equipped with a 5000mAh battery supporting 25W fast charging. Connectivity features include Hybrid Dual SIM, 5G, Bluetooth 5.3, and NFC. Additional hardware features include stereo speakers, a side-mounted fingerprint scanner, and an IP64 rating for dust and water resistance. The physical dimensions are:

  • Size: 162.4 x 78.2 x 7.8mm
  • Weight: 200g

Price and Availability

The Samsung Galaxy A27 5G starts at $349.99 or €349. It will be available in Black, Blue, Light Green, and Light Pink. The device is scheduled to launch in select markets starting July 3.

Summary

The Samsung Galaxy A27 5G is a compelling mid-range 5G smartphone featuring a 120Hz Super AMOLED display, the Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 processor, and an OIS-equipped triple camera system. With a commitment to six years of software and security updates, it offers significant long-term value starting at $349.99.
