샤오미 17T 시리즈 발표 개요

IT之家에 따르면, Xiaomi는 새로운 Xiaomi 17T 시리즈 스마트폰을 공식 발표했습니다. 이 시리즈는 Xiaomi 17T Pro와 Xiaomi 17T 두 모델로 구성되며, '올라운드 이미징 플래그십'으로 포지셔닝되었습니다.

두 모델은 이미 해외 시장에 출시되었으며, 유럽 시장의 경우 17T는 749유로(현재 환율 약 5,918위안)부터 시작하고, 17T Pro는 899유로(현재 환율 약 7,103위안)부터 판매됩니다.

디자인 및 디스플레이

Pro 버전은 더 큰 본체 크기를 자랑하며, 두 모델의 화면 사양에 차이가 있습니다. Pro는 6.83인치, 일반 모델(17T)은 6.59인치의 대화면을 탑재했습니다.

  • Xiaomi 17T Pro:
  • 디스플레이 크기: 6.83인치
  • 패널 사양: 12-bit OLED 패널, 해상도 1280p+, 최대 밝기 3500니트
  • 주요 업그레이드: 주사율이 120Hz에서 144Hz로 향상되었으며, 초암 모드에서는 최소 1니터까지 밝기를 낮출 수 있습니다.

두 모델 모두 Corning Gorilla Glass 7i를 적용하여 내구성을 높였습니다.

성능 및 배터리

배터리 용량과 프로세서 사양에서 차이를 보입니다.

  • 배터리:
  • 17T: 6500mAh 실리콘 카본 배터리 탑재
  • 17T Pro: 배터리 용량이 7000mAh로 증가

두 모델 모두 MediaTek Dimensity 칩셋을 탑재하고 12GB 메모리를 갖추고 있습니다.

  • 프로세서 사양:
  • Xiaomi 17T: Dimensity 8500-Ultra 탑재
  • Xiaomi 17T Pro: Dimensity 9500 탑재

충전 기능 면에서는 Pro 버전이 우위를 점합니다.

  • Pro 버전: 144Hz 주사율, 무선 충전 및 더 빠른 100W 유선 충전을 지원합니다.

카메라 시스템

두 모델 모두 라이카(라이카) Summilux 렌즈(1G+6P)와 광학 손떨림 보정(OIS) 기능을 탑재했습니다. 주요 차이점은 메인 카메라 센서에 있습니다.

  • 공통 스펙:
  • 메인 카메라 화소수: 5000만 화소
  • 영상 기능: 새로운 4K 60fps 비디오 모드 지원, HDR10+ 및 Log 옵션 지원.

센서 비교 (핵심 차이점):

  • Xiaomi 17T Pro 메인 카메라:
  • 센서 모델/크기: OmniVision Light Fusion 950, 1/1.31인치
  • 픽셀 크기: 1.2μm
  • Xiaomi 17T 메인 카메라:
  • 센서 모델/크기: Light Fusion 800, 1/1.55인치
  • 픽셀 크기: 1.0μm

나머지 초광각 및 전면 카메라는 두 모델에서 동일하게 구성되었습니다.

연결성 및 기타 기능

오디오 및 통신:

  • 스피커: 돌비 애트모스를 지원하는 스테레오 스피커를 탑재했으며, Hi-Res Audio와 Hi-Res Audio Wireless 인증을 받았습니다.
  • 단점: 3.5mm 이어폰 잭은 양 모델 모두 제거되었습니다.
  • 연결성: 두 모델 모두 듀얼 SIM을 지원하며, 일부 지역에서는 eSIM 옵션도 제공됩니다. 또한 Xiaomi 오프라인 통신 기능을 지원합니다.

무선 연결 규격:

  • Xiaomi 17T Pro: Wi-Fi 7 및 Dual-Bluetooth가 적용된 블루투스 6을 지원합니다.
  • Xiaomi 17T: Wi-Fi 6E 및 블루투스 6.0을 지원하지만, 듀얼 블루투스 연결은 지원하지 않습니다.

두 모델 모두 NFC 기능을 지원하며, 색상은 다음과 같습니다.

  • Xiaomi 17T Pro: 블랙, 딥 블루, 딥 퍼플 세 가지 색상 제공
  • Xiaomi 17T: 블랙, 블루, 퍼플, 오팔 화이트 네 가지 색상 제공

총평

Xiaomi 17T 시리즈는 '올라운드 이미징 플래그십'이라는 목표 아래 강력한 성능과 카메라 스펙을 갖춘 모델입니다. 특히 Pro 버전은 더 큰 배터리 용량(7000mAh), 향상된 주사율, 그리고 최신 Dimensity 9500 프로세서를 탑재하여 하이엔드 사용자에게 매력적입니다. 메인 센서의 업그레이드는 사진 촬영 경험을 크게 개선했을 것으로 기대됩니다.

发布信息与定价

Xiaomi 将于 6 月 8 日正式发布定位为“全能影像旗舰”的 Xiaomi 17T 系列，包含 Xiaomi 17T Pro 和 Xiaomi 17T 两款机型。

  • Xiaomi 17T：欧洲市场起售价为 749 欧元。
  • Xiaomi 17T Pro：欧洲市场起售价为 899 欧元。

核心硬件与电池

两款机型均配备 12GB 内存，并搭载 MediaTek Dimensity 芯片组，但在具体型号和电池容量上有所不同：

  • Xiaomi 17T：搭载 Dimensity 8500-Ultra 芯片，配备 6500mAh 硅碳电池。
  • Xiaomi 17T Pro：搭载 Dimensity 9500 芯片，配备 7000mAh 大容量电池。

屏幕与影像配置

两款手机均采用康宁大猩猩玻璃 7i 保护，并支持 Leica Summilux 镜头、光学防抖以及多种视频模式（4K 60fps, HDR10+, Log）。

  • Xiaomi 17T Pro：配备 6.83 英寸 12-bit OLED 面板，分辨率为 1280p+，峰值亮度达 3500 尼特，支持 144Hz 刷新率。
  • Xiaomi 17T：配备 6.59 英寸屏幕，支持 120Hz 刷新率。
  • 主摄传感器：Pro 版采用 1/1.31 英寸 OmniVision Light Fusion 950（单像素 1.2μm）；标准版 17T 采用 1/1.55 英寸 Light Fusion 800（单像素 1.0μm）。

连接规格与配色

两款手机均支持双 SIM、NFC 及杜比全景声，但均取消了 3.5mm 耳机接口。

  • Xiaomi 17T Pro：支持 Wi-Fi 7 和带 Dual-Bluetooth 的蓝牙 6；提供黑色、深蓝色和深紫色。
  • Xiaomi 17T：支持 Wi-Fi 6E 和蓝牙 6.0（不支持双蓝牙连接）；提供黑色、蓝色、紫色和蛋白石白色。

总结

Xiaomi 将于 6 月 8 日发布定位为“全能影像旗舰”的 17T 系列，两款机型均搭载高容量硅碳电池与 MediaTek Dimensity 芯片。其中 Pro 版本在屏幕尺寸、刷新率、电池容量及主摄传感器规格上显著优于标准版，并支持 Wi-Fi 7 等更高级的连接技术。

Xiaomi 17T Series Overview

According to IT Home, Xiaomi has officially unveiled the new Xiaomi 17T series. The lineup consists of two models, the Xiaomi 17T Pro and the Xiaomi 17T, which are positioned as "all-around imaging flagships."

Both models have already been released in international markets. In Europe, the Xiaomi 17T starts at €749 (approximately 5,918 RMB), while the Xiaomi 17T Pro starts at €899 (approximately 7,103 RMB).

Design and Display

The Pro version features a larger body size, and there are notable differences in display specifications between the two models. The Pro model features a 6.83-inch screen, while the standard 17T model features a 6.59-inch large display.

  • Xiaomi 17T Pro:
  • Display Size: 6.83 inches
  • Panel Specifications: 12-bit OLED panel, 1280p+ resolution, and a peak brightness of 3,500 nits
  • Key Upgrades: The refresh rate has been increased from 120Hz to 144Hz, and the ultra-dim mode can lower the brightness to as low as 1 nit.

Both models utilize Corning Gorilla Glass 7i to enhance durability.

Performance and Battery

The two models differ in battery capacity and processor specifications.

  • Battery:
  • Xiaomi 17T: Equipped with a 6,500mAh silicon-carbon battery
  • Xiaomi 17T Pro: Features an increased battery capacity of 7,000mAh

Both models are equipped with MediaTek Dimensity chipsets and 12GB of memory.

  • Processor Specifications:
  • Xiaomi 17T: Powered by the Dimensity 8500-Ultra
  • Xiaomi 17T Pro: Powered by the Dimensity 9500

The Pro version holds an advantage in charging capabilities.

  • Pro Version: Supports a 144Hz refresh rate, wireless charging, and faster 100W wired charging.

Camera System

Both models are equipped with Leica Summilux lenses (1G+6P) and Optical Image Stabilization (OIS). The primary difference lies in the main camera sensor.

  • Common Specifications:
  • Main Camera Resolution: 50 megapixels
  • Video Features: Supports a new 4K 60fps video mode, HDR10+, and Log options.

Sensor Comparison (Key Differences):

  • Xiaomi 17T Pro Main Camera:
  • Sensor Model/Size: OmniVision Light Fusion 950, 1/1.31-inch
  • Pixel Size: 1.2μm
  • Xiaomi 17T Main Camera:
  • Sensor Model/Size: Light Fusion 800, 1/1.55-inch
  • Pixel Size: 1.0μm

The remaining ultra-wide and front cameras are identical across both models.

Connectivity and Other Features

Audio and Communication:

  • Speakers: Both models feature stereo speakers supporting Dolby Atmos and have received Hi-Res Audio and Hi-Res Audio Wireless certifications.
  • Drawback: The 3.5mm headphone jack has been removed from both models.
  • Connectivity: Both models support Dual SIM, with eSIM options available in certain regions. They also support Xiaomi's offline communication features.

Wireless Connection Standards:

  • Xiaomi 17T Pro: Supports Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6.0 with Dual-Bluetooth.
  • Xiaomi 17T: Supports Wi-Fi 6E and Bluetooth 6.0, but does not support Dual-Bluetooth connectivity.

Both models support NFC functionality and are available in the following colors:

  • Xiaomi 17T Pro: Black, Deep Blue, and Deep Purple.
  • Xiaomi 17T: Black, Blue, Purple, and Opal White.

Summary

The Xiaomi 17T series is positioned as an "all-around imaging flagship," offering high performance and premium camera specifications for both models. The Pro version specifically targets high-end users by offering a larger battery (7,000mAh), a higher refresh rate, and the powerful Dimensity 9500 processor. Both devices leverage Leica optics to provide an enhanced photography experience.

Xiaomi 17T Series Overview

According to IT Home, Xiaomi has officially unveiled the new Xiaomi 17T series. The lineup consists of two models—the Xiaomi 17T Pro and the Xiaomi 17T—positioned as "all-around imaging flagships."

Both models have been released in international markets. In Europe, the 17T starts at €749 (approximately 5,918 yuan), while the 17T Pro starts at €899 (approximately 7,103 yuan).

Design and Display

The Pro version features a larger body size, with differences in display specifications between the two models. The Pro model features a 6.83-inch display, while the standard 17T model features a 6.59-inch large screen.

  • Xiaomi 17T Pro:
  • Display Size: 6.83 inches
  • Panel Specs: 12-bit OLED panel, 1280p+ resolution, and peak brightness of 3,500 nits
  • Key Upgrades: Refresh rate increased from 120Hz to 144Hz; ultra-low light mode can reduce brightness to as low as 1 nit.

Both models utilize Corning Gorilla Glass 7i to enhance durability.

Performance and Battery

The two models differ in battery capacity and processor specifications.

  • Battery:
  • 17T: Equipped with a 6,500mAh silicon-carbon battery
  • 17T Pro: Features an increased capacity of 7,000mAh

Both models are equipped with MediaTek Dimensity chipsets and feature 12GB of memory.

  • Processor Specifications:
  • Xiaomi 17T: Powered by the Dimensity 8500-Ultra
  • Xiaomi 17T Pro: Powered by the Dimensity 9500

The Pro version holds an advantage in charging capabilities.

  • Pro Version: Supports a 144Hz refresh rate, wireless charging, and faster 100W wired charging.

Camera System

Both models are equipped with Leica Summilux lenses (1G+6P) and Optical Image Stabilization (OIS). The primary difference lies in the main camera sensor.

  • Common Specs:
  • Main Camera Resolution: 50MP
  • Video Features: Supports new 4K 60fps video mode, HDR10+, and Log options.

Sensor Comparison (Key Differences):

  • Xiaomi 17T Pro Main Camera:
  • Sensor Model/Size: OmniVision Light Fusion 950, 1/1.31-inch
  • Pixel Size: 1.2μm
  • Xiaomi 17T Main Camera:
  • Sensor Model/Size: Light Fusion 800, 1/1.55-inch
  • Pixel Size: 1.0μm

The remaining ultra-wide and front cameras are identical across both models.

Connectivity and Other Features

Audio and Communication:

  • Speakers: Equipped with stereo speakers supporting Dolby Atmos, with Hi-Res Audio and Hi-Res Audio Wireless certifications.
  • Drawback: The 3.5mm headphone jack has been removed from both models.
  • Connectivity: Both models support Dual SIM, with eSIM options available in certain regions.

Summary

Xiaomi has launched the 17T and 17T Pro as "all-around imaging flagships" featuring Leica Summilux lenses and high-resolution displays. The Pro model distinguishes itself with a larger screen, higher refresh rate, more powerful Dimensity 9500 chip, and a larger battery capacity.
