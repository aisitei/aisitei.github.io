Full-Scale Patent War Between DJI and Insta360

The rivalry between DJI and Insta360 has escalated into a full-scale patent lawsuit in the United States. Just days after Insta360 released its new Luna Ultra, DJI filed a lawsuit, which was met with an immediate counter-suit from Insta360. This exchange serves as a clear demonstration of how intense the competition has become in the pocket gimbal and vlogging camera markets.

Insta360 Luna Ultra Specifications

On June 10, 2026, Insta360 unveiled the Luna Ultra, a premium dual-lens gimbal camera co-developed with Leica. The model features the following key capabilities:

  • 8K video recording support
  • 4K 120fps slow-motion recording
  • Detachable OLED touchscreen
  • Advanced stabilization technology

Mutual Legal Battles

On the day of the Luna Ultra's launch, DJI filed two lawsuits against Insta360 in an East Texas court. DJI claims that the Luna series infringes on design and utility patents related to its Osmo Pocket lineup, seeking a sales ban and damages within the United States.

Insta360 responded immediately. On June 12, Insta360 filed a counter-suit alleging that DJI infringed upon five of its utility patents. These patents include gimbal stabilization, direction control, smooth camera stabilization, telemetry overlay, and panorama video stabilization. According to Insta366, these technologies are featured in numerous DJI products, including the Ronin/RS gimbals, Osmo Mobile, and Osmo 360 cameras.

Insta360's Official Stance and Market Reaction

Insta366 founder JK Liu issued a strong statement: "While Insta366 prefers to let its products speak for themselves, we do not shy away from legal battles when faced with challenges. We are committed to protecting our innovation and will take firm action against intellectual property infringements." He emphasized that the Luna Ultra was not an imitation but was born through years of independent R&D since 2020, based on previous products such as the ONE R, Link series, and Flow gimbal. Furthermore, JK Liu noted that DJI's same-day lawsuit reflects "competitive fear regarding a highly competitive product."

Insta366 is firmly rejecting DJI's claims. Priced at approximately $770, the Luna Ultra saw high sales volume in the Amazon US camcorder category on its first day of release. Insta366 expects this product to become a new and innovative choice in the stabilized vlogging space through its dual-lens and modular design.

This dispute follows previous patent conflicts between the two companies in China. As these proceedings take place in Texas—a major hub for intellectual property disputes—the outcome could significantly impact future product availability and fundamentally change how both brands approach innovation.

Summary

DJI and Insta360 are engaged in a heated legal battle over core technologies like gimbal stabilization and camera control following the launch of the Luna Ultra. The outcome of this lawsuit in Texas could significantly impact product availability and define the competitive landscape of the pocket gimbal market for years to come.

DJI与Insta360的全面专利战

DJI与Insta360之间的竞争已演变为美国境内的正式专利诉讼。在Insta360推出新款Luna Ultra仅几天后，DJI便提起诉讼，而Insta360随即采取了反诉措施。这充分展示了目前口袋云台及Vlog相机市场中两家公司之间极其激烈的竞争态势。

Insta360 Luna Ultra 产品规格

  • 支持8K视频录制
  • 支持4K 120fps慢动作录制
  • 可拆卸式OLED触摸屏
  • 采用高级稳定技术

针锋相对的法律交锋

在Luna Ultra发布当天，DJI在德克萨斯州东部法院对Insta360提起了两项诉讼。DJI声称Luna系列侵犯了与Osmo Pocket产品线相关的设计及实用专利，并要求禁止其在美国销售并支付损害赔偿。

Insta360随即做出回应。6月12日，Insta360提起反诉，指称DJI侵犯了其5项实用专利。这些专利包括云台稳定、方向控制、平滑相机稳定、遥测叠加以及全景视频稳定等技术。据Insta360称，这些技术广泛应用于包括Osmo Pocket系列在内的Ronin/RS云台、Osmo Mobile以及Osmo 360相机等众多DJI产品中。

Insta360创始人JK Liu发表了强硬声明：“Insta360更倾向于让产品自己说话，但在面临挑战时，我们不会畏惧法律斗争。我们致力于保护创新，并对侵犯知识产权的行为采取果断措施。”他强调Luna Ultra并非模仿他人，而是基于ONE R、Link系列和Flow云台等以往产品，经过多年自主研发而成。此外，JK Liu提到DJI在发布当日提起的诉讼反映了“对具有竞争力的产品的竞争性恐惧”。

Insta360坚决反驳DJI的指控。定价约为$770的Luna Ultra在上市首日于亚马逊美国站的摄像机类别中录得高销量，取得了成功的开端。Insta366预计该产品凭借双镜头和模块化设计，将成为稳定Vlog领域的一个创新选择。

此次争端延续了双方此前在中国经历的专利纠纷。在作为知识产权诉讼中心的德克萨斯州进行的此次诉讼结果，可能会影响未来产品的可用性，并可能导致两家品牌在追求创新的方式上发生重大变化。

总结

DJI与Insta360因Luna Ultra的推出在美国展开了激烈的专利诉讼战。双方针对云台稳定及视频技术等核心领域互相指控，此结果将对未来口袋云台市场的竞争格局和产品供应产生重大影响。
