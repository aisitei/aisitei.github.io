일본 정부, Rapidus에 추가 투자 결정

IT之家 보도에 따르면, 일본 경제산업대신 아카사와 료세(赤澤亮正)는 기자회견에서 일본 정부가 첨단 반도체 국산화를 목표로 하는 Rapidus사에 1,500억 엔을 추가 출자한다고 밝혔습니다. (참고: 현재 환율 기준 약 63.63억 위안입니다.)

아카사와 신장은 이번 투자가 정부가 성장을 추진하는 핵심 프로젝트이며, 국가 이익을 위해 반드시 성공시켜야 한다고 강조했습니다.

투자금 사용 목적 및 지분 구조

이번에 확보된 자금은 다음과 같은 용도로 사용될 예정입니다:

  • 2 나노 공정 제품의 양산 장비 투자
  • 성능이 더 높은 1.4 나노 공정 연구 개발(R&D)

새로 취득하는 지분은 의결권이 없도록 설정되었으며, 정부가 보유한 의결권 비율은 11.5%로 유지됩니다. 다만, 만약 경영 악화 등의 상황이 발생할 경우, 무의결권 주식이 의결권 주식으로 전환되어 정부의 의결권 비율을 약 60%까지 높일 수 있는 구조입니다.

Rapidus 설립 배경 및 주요 참여사

Rapidus는 일본의 첨단 반도체 국산화를 목표로 하는 회사입니다. 이 회사는 다음과 같은 8개 대형 일본 기업들이 2022년에 합작하여 설립했습니다:

  • Toyota
  • Sony
  • Softbank
  • Kioxia (铠侠)
  • NEC (日本電装)
  • Hitachi (日本電気)
  • Nippon Telegraph and Telephone (日本電信電話)
  • Mitsubishi UFJ Bank

자금 조달 현황 및 향후 계획

Rapidus는 이번 추가 출자 이전에 이미 일본 정부와 그 산하 기관으로부터 여러 차례 자금 지원을 받아왔습니다. 주요 자금 지원 내역은 다음과 같습니다:

  • 2022년 11월: 일본 정부, Rapidus에 700억 엔 보조금 제공
  • 2024년 10월: 일본 정부, 현물 출자 방식으로 직접 지분 투자 계획
  • 2025년 2월: 일본 정부, 하반기 동안 1,000억 엔 출자 계획
  • 2025년 6월: Rapidus가 같은 해에 최대 8,025억 엔의 추가 지원을 받았으며, 동시에 정부 기관이 1,000억 엔을 출자하는 방안도 논의되었습니다.

Rapidus는 막대한 자금 수요를 가지고 있습니다. 추산에 따르면, 2 나노 칩 시제품 제작에는 약 2조 엔, 양산에는 약 3조 엔 규모의 자금이 필요합니다. 현재(2025년 3월 말 기준) 민간 출자액은 73억 엔에 불과하여 정부 지원에 크게 의존하고 있습니다. 회사는 2027년 양산을 목표로 삼고 있으며, 대규모 자금 확보와 더불어 Fujifilm, Honda 등 잠재 고객사로부터의 수주 및 투자를 적극적으로 유치할 계획입니다.

총평

일본 정부는 국가 안보 차원에서 첨단 반도체 기술을 확보하기 위해 Rapidus에 막대한 규모로 자금을 지속 지원하고 있습니다. 이번 추가 출자는 2나노 공정 양산 및 고성능 1.4나노 공정 개발이라는 핵심 목표 달성을 위한 필수적인 단계입니다. 다만, 회사 운영의 성공은 정부 자금 외에도 대규모 민간 투자 유치와 실제 고객사로부터의 안정적인 수주 확보에 달려있습니다.

政府追加投资计划

日本政府宣布向旨在实现尖端半导体国产化的 Rapidus 公司追加 1500 亿日元（约合 63.63 亿元人民币）的资金支持。此项投资被视为国家利益的关键项目，旨在确保公司在先进制程领域的成功。

  • 资金用途：用于量产 2 纳米制程产品的设备投资，以及研发性能更高的 1.4 纳米制程。
  • 股权安排：新获得的股份初始为无表决权股份（比例约 11.5%），若出现经营恶化等情况，可转换为有表决权的股份，使政府持股的表决权比例提升至约 6 成。

公司背景与合作伙伴

Rapidus 是由多家日本大型企业于 2022 年合资成立的半导体公司，旨在开发 2 纳米制程技术并实现先进半导体国产化。其核心股东包括：

  • Toyota、Sony、SoftBank、Kioxia、Denso、NEC、NTT 以及 MUFG Bank 等 8 家大型企业。

资金现状与量产挑战

尽管获得了政府的多轮支持，Rapidus 目前仍面临巨大的资金缺口。为了在 2027 年实现量产目标，公司正积极寻求潜在客户的订单与投资。

  • 2 纳米芯片试制成本：约 2 万亿日元。
  • 2 纳米芯片量产成本：约 3 万亿日元。
  • 目前民间出资：截至 2025 年 3 月底仅为 73 亿日元。
  • 潜在客户：Fujifilm、Honda 等。

总结

日本政府通过追加 1500 亿日元的投资，旨在支持 Rapidus 在 2 纳米及 1.4 纳米制程上的技术突破与国产化进程。尽管面对巨大的量产成本和资金缺口，该项目仍是日本强化半导体核心竞争力的关键战略举措。

日本政府、Rapidusへ追加投資を決定

IT之家の報道によると、日本の経済産業大臣である赤澤亮正氏は記者会見において、先端半導体の国産化を目指すRapidus社に対し、1,500億円の追加出資を行うことを明らかにしました。（現在の為替レートで約63.63億元に相当します。）

赤澤大臣は、この投資が政府が推進する核心的なプロジェクトであり、国家の利益のために必ず成功させなければならないと強調しました。

投資金の使途および持分構造

今回確保された資金は、以下の用途に使用される予定です：

  • 2ナノ（nm）プロセス製品の量産設備への投資
  • より高性能な1.4ナノ（nm）プロセスの研究開発（R&D）

新しく取得する株式は議決権のないものとして設定され、政府が保有する議決権比率は11.5%のまま維持されます。ただし、経営悪化などの事態が発生した場合には、無議決権株が議決権のある株式に転換され、政府の議決権比率を約60%まで引き上げられる構造となっています。

  • 2022年11月：日本政府がRapidussに700億円の補助金を提供

  • 2025年2月：日本政府が下半期中に1,000億円を出資する計画
  • 2025年6月：Rapidussは同年に最大8,025億円の追加支援を受け、同時に政府機関による1,000億円の出資案も議論された。

Japanese Government Decides on Additional Investment for Rapidus

According to a report by IT之家, Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry Ryose Akasawa announced during a press conference that the Japanese government will invest an additional 150 billion yen in Rapidus, a company aimed at localizing advanced semiconductor production. (This is approximately 6.363 billion yuan based on current exchange rates).

Minister Akasawa emphasized that this investment is a core project for promoting national growth and must succeed for the sake of national interests.

Purpose of Investment and Share Structure

The newly secured funds will be utilized for the following purposes:

  • Investment in mass production equipment for 2nm process products
  • Research and development (R&D) for high-performance 1.4nm processes

The newly acquired shares are structured to be non-voting, maintaining the government's voting share at 11.5%. However, in the event of deteriorating management or other adverse conditions, these non-voting shares can be converted into voting shares, which could increase the government's voting stake to approximately 60%.

Rapidus Background and Key Partners

Rapidus is a company established with the goal of localizing advanced semiconductor production in Japan. The company was founded in 2022 as a joint venture between the following eight major Japanese corporations:

  • Toyota
  • Sony
  • Softbank
  • Kioxia
  • NEC
  • Hitachi
  • Nippon Telegraph and Telephone
  • Mitsubishi UFJ Bank

Funding Status and Future Plans

Prior to this additional investment, Rapidus has received several rounds of funding from the Japanese government and its affiliated agencies. The key funding details are as follows:

  • November 2022: The Japanese government provided a 70 billion yen subsidy to Rapidus.
  • October 2024: Plans for direct equity investment by the Japanese government through in-kind contributions.
  • February 2025: A plan for the government to invest 100 billion yen during the second half of the year.
  • June 2025: Rapidus received up to 802.5 billion yen in additional support, while discussions were held regarding a 100 billion yen investment from government agencies.

Rapidus faces massive capital requirements. Estimates suggest that approximately 2 trillion yen is needed for the production of 2nm chip prototypes and about 3 trillion yen for mass production. As of late March 2025, private investment stands at only 7.3 billion yen, leaving the company heavily dependent on government support. The company aims for mass production by 2027 and plans to actively secure orders and investments from potential customers such as Fujifilm and Honda.

Overall Outlook

The Japanese government is providing substantial funding to Rapidus to secure advanced semiconductor technology for national security purposes. This additional investment is a critical step toward achieving the core goals of 2nm process mass production and high-performance 1.4nm process development. However, the company's ultimate success will depend on securing large-scale private investments and stable orders from actual customers beyond government funding.

Summary

The Japanese government has committed an additional 150 billion yen to Rapidus to accelerate its development of advanced 2nm and 1.4nm semiconductor technologies. While this investment is a vital move for national security, the company's long-term success hinges on transitioning from heavy government reliance toward securing private investments and stable orders from major customers.

为了确保 Rapidus 能迅速做出经营决策，新获得的股份初期设定为无表决权股份。

  • 初始持股比例：政府持有的无表决权股份比例控制在 11.5%。
  • 特殊触发机制：若出现经营恶化等情况，无表决权的股份将转换为有表决权的股份，届时政府的表决权比例将提升至约 6 成。

公司背景与资金支持历程

Rapidus 是由丰田、Sony、SoftBank、Kioxia、日本电装、日本电气、日本电信电话和三菱 UFJ 银行等 8 家大型企业于 2022 年合资成立的半导体公司。

  • 前期支持：自 2022 年以来，Rapidus 已获得多轮政府及相关机构的支持，包括 700 亿日元的初期补贴以及后续多次计划中的出资。

资金缺口与生产目标

由于先进半导体制造成本极高，Rapidus 目前高度依赖政府支持，并面临巨大的融资压力。

  • 2 纳米芯片试制需求：约 2 万亿日元。
  • 2 纳米芯片量产需求：约 3 万亿日元。
  • 当前现状：截至 2025 年 3 月底，其民间出资仅为 73 亿日元。

总结

日本政府通过追加 1500 亿日元的资金支持，旨在推动 Rapidus 在 2 纳米及 1.4 纳米制程上的技术突破与量产能力。尽管获得大量政府注资，但面对巨大的生产成本压力，Rapidus 未来仍需通过争取更多客户订单和外部投资来填补巨额的资金缺口。

日本政府、Rapidsへ追加投資を決定

IT之家の報道によると、日本の経済産業大臣である赤澤亮正氏は記者会見において、先端半導体の国産化を目指すRapidus社に対し、1,500億円の追加出資を行うことを明らかにしました。この投資は、政府が推進する成長に向けた核心的なプロジェクトであり、国家の利益のために必ず成功させなければならないと強調されました。

投資金の使途および持分構造

今回確保された資金は、以下の用途に使用される予定です：

  • 2ナノ（nm）プロセス製品の量産設備への投資
  • より高性能な1.4ナノ（nm）プロセスの研究開発（R&D）

新しく取得する株式は、当初は議決権のないものとして設定され、政府が保有する議決権比率は11.5%のまま維持されます。ただし、経営悪化などの事態が発生した場合には、無議決権株が議決権のある株式に転換され、政府の議決権比率を約60%まで引き上げられる構造となっています。

Rapidusの設立背景と主要参画企業

Rapidsは日本の先端半導体の国産化を目指す企業です。同社は2022年に以下の8つの大手日本企業によって合弁設立されました：

  • Toyota
  • Sony
  • Softbank
  • Kioxia
  • NEC
  • Hitachi
  • Nippon Telegraph and Telephone (NTT)
  • Mitsubishi UFJ Bank

資金調達の現状と今後の計画

Rapidsは今回の追加出資の前に、すでに日本政府およびその傘下の機関から複数回の資金援助を受けています。主な支援内容は以下の通りです：

  • 2022年11月：日本政府がRapidsに700億円の補助金を提供
  • 2024年10月：日本政府による現物出資方式での直接出資計画
  • 2025年2月：日本政府が下半期中に1,000億円を出資する計画
  • 2025年6月：Rapidsは同年に最大8,025億円の追加支援を受け、同時に政府機関による1,000億円の出資案も議論された。

Rapidsは膨大な資金需要を抱えています。推計によると、2ナノチップの試作には約2兆円、量産には約3兆円規模の資金が必要とされています。現在（2025年3月末時点）の民間出資額はわずか73億円にとどまっており、政府の支援に大きく依存しています。同社は2027年の量産を目標としており、大規模な資金確保とともに、FujifilmやHondaなどの潜在的な顧客からの受注および投資を積極的に獲得する計画です。

まとめ

日本政府は国家安全保障の観点から先端半導体技術の確保を目指し、Rapidsに対して1,500億円の追加出資を決定しました。この資金は2ナノおよび1.4ナノプロセスの開発と量産に向けた重要なステップとなります。今後の成功には、政府による強力な支援に加え、民間投資の誘致と実需に基づく安定した受注確保が不可欠な要素となります。

Japanese Government Decides on Additional Investment for Rapidus

According to a report by IT之家, Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry Ryose Akasawa announced during a press conference that the Japanese government will provide an additional 150 billion yen in investment to Rapidus. The company aims to localize advanced semiconductor production. (This amount is approximately 6.363 billion yuan based on current exchange rates).

Minister Akasawa emphasized that this investment is a core project for promoting national growth and must succeed for the sake of national interests.

Purpose of Investment and Share Structure

The newly secured funds are intended for the following purposes:

  • Investment in mass production equipment for 2nm process products
  • Research and development (R&D) for high-performance 1.4nm processes

The newly acquired shares are structured as non-voting initially, maintaining the government's voting share at 11.5%. However, in the event of management deterioration or other adverse conditions, these non-voting shares can be converted into voting shares, which would increase the government's voting stake to approximately 60%.

Rapidus Background and Key Partners

Rapidus is a company established with the goal of localizing advanced semiconductor production in Japan. It was founded in 2022 as a joint venture between eight major Japanese corporations:

  • Toyota
  • Sony
  • Softbank
  • Kioxia
  • NEC
  • Hitachi
  • Nippon Telegraph and Telephone (NTT)
  • Mitsubishi UFJ Bank

Funding Status and Future Plans

Prior to this additional investment, Rapidus has received several rounds of funding from the Japanese government and its affiliated agencies. The key support details are as follows:

  • November 2022: The Japanese government provided 70 billion yen in subsidies to Rapidus.
  • October 2024: A plan for direct investment via in-kind contributions by the Japanese government.
  • February 2025: A plan for the government to invest 100 billion yen during the second half of the year.
  • June 2025: Rapidus received up to 802.5 billion yen in additional support, while a proposal for a 100 billion yen investment from government agencies was also discussed.

Rapidus faces massive capital requirements. Estimates suggest that approximately 2 trillion yen is needed for the prototyping of 2nm chips and about 3 trillion yen for mass production. As of the end of March 2025, private investment stands at only 7.3 billion yen, leaving the company heavily dependent on government support. The company aims to begin mass production in 2027 and is actively seeking orders and investments from potential customers such as Fujifilm and Honda.

Summary

The Japanese government is providing significant additional funding to Rapidus to secure advanced semiconductor technologies, specifically targeting the development of 2nm and 1.4nm processes. While the project faces a substantial capital gap for mass production, it remains a critical strategic move for Japan to strengthen its national competitiveness in the semiconductor industry.
