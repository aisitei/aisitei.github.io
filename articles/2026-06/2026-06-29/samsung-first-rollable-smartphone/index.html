롤러블 스마트폰 개발 가속화

출시 계획 및 예상 사양

한국 매체인 Maeil Business의 보도에 따르면, Samsung Display는 이 기술을 강력하게 추진하고 있습니다. 목표는 2028년 상반기 내에 첫 번째 상용 롤러블 폰을 출시하는 것이며, 이는 Galaxy S28 시리즈와 함께 또는 그 일부로 출시될 가능성이 높습니다. 해당 기기는 Galaxy Z Slide라는 명칭으로 불릴 것으로 예상됩니다.

  • 10인치 크기의 16:9 디스플레이
  • 약 440 PPI의 고해상도
  • 2030년 두 번째 모델 출시 가능성

시장 경쟁과 전략적 배경

이러한 행보는 Samsung에 매우 전략적인 선택입니다. 현재 폴더블 시장을 주도하고는 있지만, Omdia에 따르면 폴더블 디스플레이 패널 점유율이 2025년 4분기 약 41.8%에서 2026년 1분기 27%로 눈에 띄게 하락했습니다. 경쟁이 치열해지는 상황에서, 롤러블 폰은 혁신의 아이콘으로 다시 주목받고 기술적 장벽을 높이는 수단이 될 수 있습니다.

기술적 도전 과제

롤러블 스크린은 폴더블보다 훨씬 까다로운 기술입니다. 가장 큰 과제는 디스플레이가 울거나 구겨지거나 고르지 않게 되지 않으면서 수백, 수천 번의 롤링을 안정적으로 수행하게 만드는 것입니다. 롤러와 내부 레이어, 지지 구조 등 모든 요소가 매우 정밀해야 하며 동시에 일상적인 사용에 적합하도록 얇고 가벼워야 합니다.

연구 개발의 역사

Samsung은 수년간 이 기술을 실험해 왔습니다. CES 2023에서 폴딩과 슬라이딩을 결합한 Flex Hybrid를 선보였으며, 같은 해 SID Display Week에서는 49mm의 작은 화면을 254mm 이상으로 확장하는 Rollable Flex 프로토타입을 공개하여 큰 주목을 받았습니다.

미래 전망

기술이 성공적으로 구현된다면, 롤러블 폰은 게임 체인저가 될 수 있습니다. 주머니에 들어갈 만큼 작으면서도 동영상 시청이나 멀티태스킹을 위해 태블릿급 경험을 제공하며, 현재 폴더블 기기에서 발생하는 눈에 띄는 주름 문제도 해결할 수 있기 때문입니다. 만약 Samsung이 고가의 가격을 정당화할 수 있는 프리미엄 품질을 구현해낸다면, 이는 스마트폰 디자인의 다음 큰 진화가 될 것입니다.

총평

Samsung은 경쟁 심화에 대응하고 기술적 우위를 확보하기 위해 롤러블 디스플레이 기술 개발에 박차를 가하고 있습니다. Galaxy Z Slide는 휴대성과 대화면이라는 두 마리 토끼를 잡으면서 기존 폴더블의 한계인 주름 문제를 해결할 핵심 혁신으로 기대됩니다.

Accelerating Rollable Smartphone Development

Samsung is accelerating the development of rollable smartphones that can smoothly expand from a compact size to a tablet-like scale whenever needed.

Launch Plans and Expected Specifications

According to reports from the Korean outlet Maeil Business, Samsung Display is aggressively pushing this technology. The goal is to launch the first commercial rollable phone in the first half of 2028, which is likely to be released as part of or alongside the Galaxy S28 series. The device is expected to be named the "Galaxy Z Slide."

  • 10-inch 16:9 display
  • High resolution of approximately 440 PPI
  • Potential for a second model in 2030

Market Competition and Strategic Context

This move is highly strategic for Samsung. While it currently leads the foldable market, Omdia reports that its share of the foldable display panel market dropped significantly from approximately 41.8% in Q4 2025 to 27% in Q1 2026. Amid intensifying competition, rollable phones can serve as a symbol of innovation and a means to establish higher technical barriers.

Technical Challenges

Rollable screens are much more technically demanding than foldables. The primary challenge lies in ensuring the display remains stable through thousands of rolls without wrinkling, buckling, or becoming uneven. Every component—including the rollers, internal layers, and support structures—must be extremely precise while remaining thin and light enough for everyday use.

R&D History

Samsung has been experimenting with this technology for years. At CES 2023, it showcased the Flex Hybrid, which combines folding and sliding mechanisms. Later that year at SID Display Week, it unveiled a Rollable Flex prototype that expanded from a small 49mm screen to over 254mm, garnering significant attention.

Future Outlook

If successfully implemented, rollable phones could be a game-changer. They offer the ability to fit in a pocket while providing a tablet-like experience for video consumption and multitasking, while also solving the noticeable crease issues found in current foldable devices. If Samsung can deliver premium quality that justifies a high price point, it will mark the next major evolution in smartphone design.

Overall Assessment

Samsung is pushing rollable display technology to counter growing competition and secure a technical advantage. The Galaxy Z Slide is expected to be a key innovation that balances portability with a large screen while resolving the crease issues inherent in existing foldable devices.

Summary

Samsung is accelerating the development of "Galaxy Z Slide" rollable phones, aiming for a commercial launch in the first half of 2028. This technology aims to provide a tablet-like experience in a compact form factor while overcoming the crease issues common in current foldables. The move is a strategic effort to maintain market leadership and establish high technical barriers against competitors.

ロール型スマートフォンの開発加速

Samsungは、必要な時にコンパクトなサイズからタブレットに近いサイズへとスムーズに拡張する「ロール型（Rollable）」スマートフォンの開発を加速させています。

リリース計画と予想スペック

韓国のメディア「Maeil Business」の報道によると、Samsung Displayはこの技術を強力に推進しています。目標は2028年上半期までに最初の商用ロール型スマートフォンを発売することであり、これはGalaxy S28シリーズと同時、あるいはその一部としてリリースされる可能性が高いです。このデバイスは「Galaxy Z Slide」という名称で呼ばれると予想されています。

  • 10インチの16:9ディスプレイ
  • 約440 PPIの高解像度
  • 2030年に第2モデルを発売する可能性

市場競争と戦略的背景

この動きはSamsungにとって非常に戦略的な選択です。現在、折りたたみ（Foldable）市場を主導しているものの、Omdiaのデータによると、折りたたみディスプレイパネルのシェアは2025年第4四半期の約41.8%から2026年第1四半期には27%へと顕著に低下しています。競争が激化する中で、ロール型スマートフォンは革新の象徴として再び注目を集め、技術的な参入障壁を高める手段となり得ます。

技術的な課題

ロール型スクリーンは、折りたたみ式よりもはるかに高度な技術を要します。最大の課題は、ディスプレイが波打ったり、しわができたり、不均一になったりすることなく、数百から数千回の巻き取りを安定して行えるようにすることです。ローラー、内部レイヤー、支持構造など、すべての要素が非常に精密であると同時に、日常的な使用に適した薄さと軽さを両立させる必要があります。

研究開発の歴史

Samsungはこの技術を長年にわたり試験してきました。CES 2023では、折りたたみとスライドを組み合わせた「Flex Hybrid」を発表し、同年同じSID Display Weekでは、49mmの小さな画面を254mm以上に拡張する「Rollable Flex」のプロトタイプを公開して大きな注目を集めました。

未来の展望

技術が成功裏に実装されれば、ロール型スマートフォンはゲームチェンジャーとなる可能性があります。ポケットに入るほど小さく保ちながら、動画視聴やマルチタスクのためにタブレット級の体験を提供でき、現在の折りたたみデバイスで見られる顕著な「しわ」の問題も解決できるためです。もしSamsungが高価格を正当化できるプレミアムな品質を実現できれば、これはスマートフォンデザインにおける次の大きな進化となるでしょう。

総評

Samsungは競争の激化に対応し、技術的優位性を確保するためにロール型ディスプレイ技術の開発に拍車をかけています。Galaxy Z Slideは、携帯性と大画面の両立を実現しつつ、従来の折りたたみデバイスの限界であった「しわ」の問題を解決する核心的な革新として期待されています。

まとめ

Samsungは競合他社への対抗と技術的優位性の確保のため、ロール型ディスプレイ技術の開発を加速させています。2028年までに登場が期待される「Galaxy Z Slide」は、折りたたみ式の課題である「しわ」を解消しつつ、携帯性と大画面を両立する革新的なデバイスとなる可能性があります。

加速卷轴式智能手机开发

Samsung 正在加速研发“卷轴式”（Rollable）智能手机，该设备能够根据需求从紧凑尺寸平滑扩展至接近平板电脑的尺寸。

发布计划与预期规格

据韩国媒体 Maeil Business 报道，Samsung Display 正在大力推动这一技术。目标是在 2028 年上半年推出首款商用卷轴式手机，该产品极有可能作为 Galaxy S28 系列的一部分或与其同步发布。预计该设备将被命名为 Galaxy Z Slide。

  • 10 英寸的 16:9 显示屏
  • 约 440 PPI 的高分辨率
  • 可能在 2030 年推出第二款型号

市场竞争与战略背景

这一举动是 Samsung 的战略性选择。尽管目前处于折叠式市场的领先地位，但根据 Omdia 的数据，折叠式显示面板的份额从 2025 年第四季度的约 41.8% 下降到 2026 年第一季期的 27%。在竞争日益激烈的环境下，卷轴式手机可以作为创新的象征重新获得关注，并成为提高技术门槛的手段。

技术挑战

卷轴式屏幕的技术难度远高于折叠式。最大的挑战在于确保显示屏在经历数百甚至数千次滚动时，不会出现起皱、褶皱或不平整的情况。滚轮、内部层以及支撑结构等所有元素都必须极其精密，同时还要保证足够轻薄以满足日常使用需求。

研发历史

Samsung 已对该技术进行了多年的实验。在 CES 2023 上，公司展示了结合折叠与滑动功能的 Flex Hybrid；同年在 SID Display Week 上，其展示的能将 49mm 小屏幕扩展至 254mm 以上的 Rollable Flex 原型机获得了广泛关注。

未来展望

如果技术能够成功实现，卷轴式手机将成为改变游戏规则的产品。因为它既能保持放入口袋的紧凑尺寸，又能为观看视频或多任务处理提供平板级的体验，同时还能解决当前折叠设备明显的折痕问题。如果 Samsung 能打造出足以支撑高昂价格的优质产品，这将成为智能手机设计的下一次重大进化。

总结

Samsung 正通过加速研发卷轴式显示技术来应对日益激烈的市场竞争并建立技术壁垒。Galaxy Z Slide 被视为解决折叠屏折痕问题、同时兼顾便携性与大尺寸体验的核心创新产品。若能成功量产，该技术将成为智能手机设计领域的重大突破。

Accelerating Rollable Smartphone Development

Samsung is accelerating the development of rollable smartphones that can smoothly expand from a compact size to a tablet-like scale whenever needed.

Launch Plans and Expected Specifications

According to reports from the Korean outlet Maeil Business, Samsung Display is aggressively pushing this technology. The goal is to launch the first commercial rollable phone in the first half of 2028, which is likely to be released as part of or alongside the Galaxy S28 series. The device is expected to be named the "Galaxy Z Slide."

  • 10-inch 16:9 display
  • High resolution of approximately 440 PPI
  • Potential for a second model in 2030

Market Competition and Strategic Context

This move is highly strategic for Samsung. While it currently leads the foldable market, Omdia reports that its share of the foldable display panel market dropped significantly from approximately 41.8% in Q4 2025 to 27% in Q1 2026. Amid intensifying competition, rollable phones can serve as a symbol of innovation and a means to establish higher technical barriers.

Technical Challenges

Rollable screens are much more technically demanding than foldables. The primary challenge lies in ensuring the display remains stable through thousands of rolls without wrinkling, buckling, or becoming uneven. Every component—including the rollers, internal layers, and support structures—must be extremely precise while remaining thin and light enough for everyday use.

R&D History

Samsung has been experimenting with this technology for years. At CES 2023, it showcased the Flex Hybrid, which combines folding and sliding mechanisms. Later that year at SID Display Week, it unveiled a Rollable Flex prototype that expanded from a small 49mm screen to over 254mm, garnering significant attention.

Future Outlook

If successfully implemented, rollable phones could be a game-changer. They offer the ability to fit in a pocket while providing a tablet-like experience for video consumption and multitasking, while also solving the noticeable crease issues found in current foldable devices. If Samsung can deliver premium quality that justifies a high price point, it will mark the next major evolution in smartphone design.

Overall Assessment

Samsung is pushing rollable display technology to counter growing competition and secure a technical advantage. The Galaxy Z Slide is expected to be a key innovation that balances portability with a large screen while resolving the crease issues inherent in existing foldable devices.

Summary

Samsung is accelerating the development of "Galaxy Z Slide" rollable phones, aiming for a commercial launch in the first half of 2028. This technology aims to provide a tablet-like experience in a compact form factor while overcoming the crease issues common in current foldables. The move is a strategic effort to maintain market leadership and establish high technical barriers against competitors.
