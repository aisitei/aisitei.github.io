AI 기업 Midjourney, 첫 하드웨어 '전신 초음파 스캐너' 출시: 60초 만에 3D 인체 지도 생성, 전직 Apple Vision Pro 엔지니어 팀이 이끌어

신제품 및 의료 부문 설립

이미지 생성 AI 도구로 잘 알려진 Midjourney는 현지 시간 6월 17일, 의료 부문을 신설하고 첫 번째 하드웨어 제품인 전신 초음파 스캐너 'Midjourney Scanner'(FBUCT 유형 장치)를 발표했습니다.

이 장치는 초음파와 물을 활용하여 약 60초 내에 인체 전체를 스캔할 수 있으며, 마이크로미터(μm) 수준의 정밀도를 가진 3차원 인체 이미지를 생성합니다. 이는 기존의 전신 MRI(자기공명영상) 기술보다 약 100배 빠른 속도이며, 전리 방사선이나 강한 자기장을 사용하지 않는다는 특징이 있습니다.

Midjourney의 CEO David Holz는 공식 블로그를 통해 이번 프로젝트를 "약간 이상하고 광기 어린 것처럼 보일 수 있지만, 매우 웅장하며 희망적인 프로젝트"라고 설명했습니다.

기술적 원리 및 사용자 경험

Midjourney는 이 프로젝트가 기존의 사업 방향과는 무관하지만, "우리가 어떻게 달라질 것인가"와 "우리는 무엇이 되고 싶은가"에 대한 고민 끝에 의료 부문 설립과 Scanner 출시를 결정했다고 밝혔습니다. 그들은 MRI처럼 강력하면서도 스파(Spa)에 온 것처럼 편안한 경험을 제공하는 것을 목표로 합니다.

사용자는 플랫폼 위에 서서 초당 2인치 속도로 몸을 물에 담그고, 이후 원형 구조물을 통과하게 됩니다. 이 구조물은 50만 개의 모래알 크기 사각형 단위로 구성되어 있으며, 각 단위는 초음파를 발사하고 신체에서 반사되는 에코를 기록합니다.

Midjourney는 이 원리를 돌고래의 에코 로케이션(반향 위치 측정)에 비유하며, 스캔을 받는 느낌이 "50만 마리의 작은 돌고래들에게 둘러싸인 것과 같다"고 묘사했습니다. 최종적으로 생성되는 결과물은 현재의 MRI 영상과 유사하지만 훨씬 빠른 속도로 제작된 정밀한 3차원 지도입니다.

파트너십 및 투자 규모

이 프로젝트는 휴대용 초음파 장치 제조사인 Butterfly Network의 협력으로 진행됩니다. Midjourney는 2025년 11월 Butterfly Network와 라이선스 계약을 체결하고 '온칩(on-chip) 초음파' 기술에 대한 독점 사용권을 확보했습니다.

계약 조건은 다음과 같습니다:

  • 선급금: 1,500만 달러
  • 연간 라이선스 비용: 1,000만 달러
  • 마일스톤 및 하드웨어 판매 수익 공유: 최대 900만 달러

Butterfly Network는 최근 AI를 활용해 초음파 워크플로우를 개선하는 Compass AI를 출시하는 등 기술력을 인정받고 있는 기업입니다.

향후 로드맵 및 비전

Midjourney Scanner 프로젝트는 Apple에서 Vision Pro 개발에 참여했던 Ahmad Abbas가 이끌고 있습니다. 향후 12개월 동안 Midjourney는 알고리즘 최적화, 스캐너 자체 연구, 그리고 2세대 하드웨어 설계에 집중할 계획입니다.

내년에는 샌프란시스코에 첫 번째 스파 스타일 체험 센터를 개설하고 FDA의 진단 기능 허가를 신청할 예정입니다. 2028년까지는 더 많은 도시로 확장하고 맞춤형 칩을 탑재한 3세대 기기를 출시하여 이미지 품질을 대폭 향상시키고, 표준 MRI 장비와 직접 경쟁하는 수준으로 성능을 끌어올릴 계획입니다.

Midjourney의 최종 목표는 2031년까지 전 세계에 5만 대의 Scanner를 배치하는 것입니다. 회사는 조기 영상 스크리닝이 충분히 이루어진다면 미래에 사망자의 30%와 의료비 지출의 50%를 절감할 수 있을 것이라고 기대하고 있습니다.

총평

Midjourney가 AI 이미지 생성 기술을 넘어 초음파 스캐닝이라는 혁신적인 의료 하드웨어 분야로 영역을 확장하며 파격적인 행보를 보여주고 있습니다. Butterfly Network와의 강력한 파트너십과 구체적인 로드맵은 이들이 단순한 소프트웨어 기업을 넘어 의료 진단 생태계를 변화시키려는 야심찬 비전을 실현하고자 함을 시사합니다.

Midjourney 成立医疗部门并推出首款硬件

Midjourney 近期宣布成立 Midjourney Medical 部门，并推出了其首款硬件产品——全身体扫描仪 "Midjourney Scanner"（属于 FBUCT 类设备）。该项目旨在提供一种比传统 MRI 更快速、更便捷的全身影像获取手段。

Midjourney Scanner 技术规格与特性

  • 扫描速度： 约 60 秒即可完成人体全身扫描。
  • 成像精度： 生成达亚毫米级别的三维人体图像。
  • 性能对比： 扫描速度约为传统核磁共振成像（MRI）技术的近百倍。
  • 安全性： 不涉及电离辐射或强磁场。
  • 核心原理： 利用由 50 万个微型单元组成的环形结构发射超声波，类似于海豚的回声定位技术。

合作伙伴与商业协议

该项目由手持超声设备制造商 Butterfly Network 提供支持。Midjourney 已获得其“片上超声”技术的独家使用权。

  • 预付款： 1500 万美元（约 1.02 亿元人民币）。
  • 年度许可费： 每年 1000 万美元（约 6774.2 万元人民币）。
  • 其他条款： 最高 900 万美元的里程碑付款及硬件销售的收入分成。

研发团队与未来路线图

该项目由曾参与 Apple Vision Pro 研发的 Ahmad Abbas 负责。Midjourney 将在接下来 12 个月内专注于算法优化、研究实验及第二代硬件设计。

  • 2025 年： 在旧金山开设首个体验中心，并向 FDA 申请诊断功能许可。
  • 2028 年： 推出采用定制芯片的第三代机器，旨在与标准 MRI 设备进行直接竞争。
  • 2031 年目标： 计划在全球部署 5 万台 Scanner，旨在通过早期影像筛查降低医疗支出并减少死亡人数。

总结

Midjourney 通过成立专门的医疗部门，推出基于超声技术的 Midjourney Scanner，试图在极速、高精度且无辐射的领域挑战传统 MRI 技术。公司计划在未来几年内通过技术迭代和全球扩张，建立大规模的早期影像筛查网络。

Midjourneyは、このプロジェクトが従来の事業方向とは異なるものの、「私たちがどのように変化するか」「私たちは何になりたいのか」という葛り合いの末、医療部門の設立とScannerのリリースを決定したと明らかにしました。彼らは、MRIのような強力な性能を持ちながらも、スパ（Spa）に来たような快適な体験を提供することを目指しています。

Midjourneyはこの原理をイルカのエコーロケーション（反響定位）に例え、スキャンを受ける感覚を「50万匹の小さなイルカに囲まれているようなもの」と表現しました。最終的に生成される結果は、現在のMRI画像と類似していますが、より高速なスピードで作成された精密な3次元マップとなります。

New Product and Medical Division Establishment

Midjourney, widely known for its image generation AI tools, established a new medical division on June 17 (local time) and announced its first hardware product, the "Midjourney Scanner" (an FBUCT-type device).

This device utilizes ultrasound and water to scan the entire human body in approximately 60 seconds, generating a three-dimensional image of the human body with micrometer (μm) precision. It is roughly 100 times faster than existing full-body MRI technology and features the advantage of not using ionizing radiation or strong magnetic fields.

Midjourney CEO David Holz described the project in an official blog post as "a bit strange and crazy, but very grand and hopeful."

Technical Principles and User Experience

Midjourney stated that while this project is unrelated to their existing business direction, they decided to establish a medical division and launch the Scanner after reflecting on "how we will change" and "what we want to become." They aim to provide an experience that is as powerful as an MRI but as comfortable as visiting a spa.

Users stand on a platform while their bodies are submerged in water at a speed of 2 inches per second, subsequently passing through a circular structure. This structure consists of units the size of 500,000 grains of sand; each unit emits ultrasound and records the echoes reflected from the body.

Midjourney compares this principle to dolphin echolocation, describing the feeling of undergoing a scan as being "like being surrounded by 500,000 tiny dolphins." The final output is a precise three-dimensional map similar to current MRI images but produced at much higher speeds.

Partnerships and Investment Scale

This project is carried out in collaboration with Butterfly Network, a manufacturer of portable ultrasound devices. In November 2025, Midjourney signed a licensing agreement with Butterfly Network to secure exclusive rights to "on-chip" ultrasound technology.

The terms of the agreement are as follows:

  • Upfront payment: $15 million
  • Annual license fee: $10 million
  • Milestones and hardware sales revenue sharing: Up to $9 million

Butterfly Network is a company recognized for its technological prowess, recently launching Compass AI to improve ultrasound workflows using AI.

Future Roadmap and Vision

The Midjourney Scanner project is led by Ahmad Abbas, who participated in the development of Apple's Vision Pro. Over the next 12 months, Midjourney plans to focus on algorithm optimization, scanner research, and the design of second-generation hardware.

Next year, they plan to open the first "spa-style" experience center in San Francisco and apply for FDA approval for diagnostic functions. By 2028, they plan to expand to more cities and release a third-generation device equipped with custom chips to significantly improve image quality, bringing its performance to a level that competes directly with standard MRI equipment.

Midjourney's ultimate goal is to deploy 50,000 Scanners worldwide by 2031. The company expects that if sufficient early imaging screening is conducted, it could reduce deaths by 30% and medical expenditures by 50% in the future.

Overall Review

Midjourney is making a bold move by expanding beyond AI image generation into the innovative field of ultrasound scanning hardware. The strong partnership with Butterfly Network and a specific roadmap suggest that they aim to go beyond being a software company to realize an ambitious vision of transforming the medical diagnostic ecosystem.

New Product and Medical Division Establishment

Midjourney, widely known for its generative AI tools, established a new medical division on June 17 (local time) and unveiled its first hardware product: the "Midjourney Scanner" (an FBUCT-type device).

The device utilizes ultrasound and water to scan the entire human body in approximately 60 seconds, generating a three-dimensional image of the human body with micrometer (μm) precision. This is reported to be about 100 times faster than existing full-body MRI technology and features the advantage of not using ionizing radiation or strong magnetic fields.

Midjourney CEO David Holz described the project on the official blog as "a bit strange and crazy looking, but a very grand and hopeful project."

Technical Principles and User Experience

Midjourney stated that while this project is unrelated to their existing business direction, they decided to establish the medical division and launch the Scanner after reflecting on "how we will change" and "what we want to become." They aim to provide an experience that is as powerful as an MRI but as comfortable as visiting a spa.

Users stand on a platform and move through water at a speed of 2 inches per second while passing through a circular structure. This structure consists of units the size of sand grains (500,000 in total), each of which emits ultrasound and records echoes reflected from the body.

Midjourney compared this principle to dolphin echolocation, describing the sensation of being scanned as "being surrounded by 500,000 tiny dolphins." The final output is a precise three-dimensional map similar to current MRI images but produced at much higher speeds.

Partnership and Investment Scale

This project is being conducted in collaboration with Butterfly Network, a manufacturer of portable ultrasound devices. In November 2025, Midjourney signed a licensing agreement with Butterfly Network to secure exclusive rights to "on-chip ultrasound" technology.

The terms of the contract are as follows:

  • Upfront payment: $15 million
  • Annual license fee: $10 million
  • Milestone and hardware sales revenue sharing: Up to $9 million

Butterfly Network is a company recognized for its technical prowess, recently launching Compass AI to improve ultrasound workflows using AI.

Future Roadmap and Vision

The Midjourney Scanner project is led by Ahmad Abbas, who participated in the development of Apple's Vision Pro. Over the next 12 months, Midjourney plans to focus on algorithm optimization, scanner research, and the design of second-generation hardware.

Next year, they plan to open the first "spa-style" experience center in San Francisco and apply for FDA approval for diagnostic functions. By 2028, they plan to expand to more cities and release a third-generation device equipped with custom chips to significantly improve image quality, aiming to reach performance levels that compete directly with standard MRI equipment.

Midjourney's ultimate goal is to deploy 50,000 Scanners worldwide by 2031. The company expects that if sufficient early screening is conducted, it could reduce the number of deaths by 30% and medical expenditures by 50% in the future.
