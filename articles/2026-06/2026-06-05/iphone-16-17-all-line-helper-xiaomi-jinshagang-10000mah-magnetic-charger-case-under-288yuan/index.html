Xiaomi Jinshajiang Power Bank Overview

The new "Jinshajiang" power bank from Xiaomi features a 10,000mAh capacity and supports up to 45W output. Scheduled to go on sale in late May, the device boasts an ultra-slim design of just 13.2mm. It supports 20W Max wireless charging and 45W Max wired charging, with a retail price of 369 RMB.

Key Technologies and Safety

This power bank is engineered to maximize safety in accordance with the latest national standards. Key features include:

  • Safety Certification: The structure is designed to prevent short-circuiting from puncture impacts, avoid explosions under pressure, and prevent self-ignition even at high temperatures.
  • New Standard Compliance: The product is manufactured according to the new 2026 national standard (GB47372-2026). While currently sold products may follow the older GB31241-2022 standard, brands like Xiaomi are already aligning production with the newer regulations.
  • Smart Management Features: The new national standards mandate that power banks must display real-time information such as battery voltage, temperature, and health status, while also including data storage and reading functions for abnormal information. While these "intelligent management" requirements are mandatory, features like screens or Bluetooth are not required to meet the regulations.
