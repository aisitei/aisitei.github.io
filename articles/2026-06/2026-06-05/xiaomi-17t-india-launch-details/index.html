Xiaomi 17T 주요 사양 및 특징

샤오미 17T는 사용자를 위한 최신 기술과 강력한 성능을 결합했습니다. 이 스마트폰은 다음과 같은 핵심 스펙을 자랑합니다:

  • 디스플레이: 6.59인치 AMOLED 패널 (해상도 2756 x 1268 픽셀)

카메라 시스템 상세 분석

Xiaomi 17T는 전문적인 사진 촬영 경험을 제공하는 트리플 카메라 설정을 갖추고 있으며, 특히 Leica의 색감 보정이 적용되었습니다.

  • 메인 카메라: 50메가픽셀 Light Fusion 800 센서 (OIS 탑재)
  • 망원 카메라: 50메가픽셀 페리스코프(Periscope) 망원 렌즈 (OIS 및 115mm 초점거리 제공)
  • 초광각 카메라: 12메가픽셀 초광각 렌즈
  • 전면 카메라: 디스플레이 커팅 아웃 내부에 위치한 32메가픽셀 셀피 카메라

이 모델은 강력한 배터리 성능과 사용자 편의 기능도 강화했습니다. 6,500mAh 용량의 실리콘-카본 배터리가 탑재되었으며, 충전 기술로는 다음과 같은 기능을 제공합니다:

  • HyperCharge 지원: 67W 고속 충전
  • PPS 충전: 50W PPS 충전 지원
  • 역방향 무선 충전: 22.5W 유선 역방향 충전 기능

기타 주요 하드웨어 및 소프트웨어 스펙은 다음과 같습니다:

  • 쿨링 시스템: 3D IceLoop 냉각 시스템 적용
  • 오디오: Dolby Atmos를 지원하는 듀얼 스테레오 스피커 탑재
  • 보안 기능: X-axis 리니어 모터와 인디스플레이 지문 인식 센서 내장
  • 내구성 및 OS: IP68 등급의 방진/방수 기능을 갖추었으며, HyperOS 3 기반 Android 16을 구동합니다.
  • 업그레이드 정책: 안드로이드 OS 업그레이드는 3년간, 보안 업데이트는 4년간 제공됩니다.

Xiaomi 17T India Launch Information

Last week, Xiaomi unveiled its flagship models, the Xiaomi 17T and 17T Pro, to the global market. Today, the brand held a dedicated launch event where it showcased both the Xiaomi 17T and the Xiaomi TV FX Mini LED series. This article focuses on all the essential information regarding the Indian launch of the Xiaomi 17T.

Xiaomi 17T Key Specifications and Features

The Xiaomi 17T combines cutting-edge technology with powerful performance for its users. The smartphone boasts the following core specifications:

  • Display: 6.59-inch AMOLED panel (Resolution: 2756 x 1268 pixels)
  • Refresh Rate and Brightness: Supports a variable refresh rate from 1Hz to 120Hz, providing peak brightness of up to 3,500 nits.
  • Touch Responsiveness: Features a 480Hz touch sampling rate and 3840Hz PWM dimming.
  • Processor and Memory: Powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 8500-Ultra processor, running with LPDDR5X RAM and UFS 4.1 storage.

Camera System Analysis

The Xiaomi 17T features a triple camera setup designed to provide a professional photography experience, featuring Leica color calibration.

  • Main Camera: 50MP Light Fusion 800 sensor (with OIS)
  • Telephoto Camera: 50MP Periscope telephoto lens (with OIS and 115mm focal length)
  • Ultra-wide Camera: 12MP ultra-wide lens
  • Front Camera: 32MP selfie camera located within a display cutout

This model also features enhanced battery performance and user convenience. It is equipped with a 6,500mAh silicon-carbon battery and supports the following charging technologies:

  • HyperCharge Support: 67W fast charging
  • PPS Charging: Supports 50W PPS charging
  • Reverse Charging: 22.5W wired reverse charging functionality

Other key hardware and software specifications include:

  • Cooling System: 3D IceLoop cooling system
  • Audio: Dual stereo speakers supporting Dolby Atmos
  • Security Features: X-axis linear motor and in-display fingerprint sensor
  • Durability and OS: IP68 dust/water resistance, running HyperOS 3 based on Android 16
  • Update Policy: Provides 3 years of Android OS updates and 4 years of security updates

Xiaomi 17T Pricing and Purchase Information

The following are the launch prices and sales channels for the Xiaomi 17T in the Indian market.

  • Price (12GB + 256GB): Rs 59,999 (~$625)
  • Price (12GB + 512GB): Rs 64,999 (~$670)

Users can choose from three color options: Violet, Blue, and Black. The smartphone will go on sale starting June 10 through Amazon India and Xiaomi India channels. As part of the launch promotion, a discount of Rs 5,000 is available for eligible credit card and EMI transactions.

Summary

The Xiaomi 17T delivers high-end performance with its large 6,500mAh battery and MediaTek Dimensity 8500-Ultra processor. Its Leica-calibrated triple camera system and IP68 durability ensure a premium experience, while the extended software update policy provides long-term reliability for users.
