현재 이 시스템은 다음과 같은 장비와 범위를 갖추고 있습니다:

The Three Gorges Corporation announced that this drone intelligent patrol management system was implemented primarily by the Power Production Department of the Three Gorges Land New Energy Headquarters. They established a standardized "1+1+1+5" management system (consisting of plans, connection methods, management rules, and five specialized manuals), resolving the issue of lacking unified management standards for existing drone patrols in new energy power plants.

The current system includes the following equipment and scope:

  • Drone Nests: 21 locations
  • Patrol Drones: 28 units

These facilities enable all-around patrol monitoring covering solar panels, wind turbines, collection lines, and substations.

Operational Efficiency and Process

Compared to traditional manual labor patrols, this system provides overwhelming efficiency gains:

  • Annual labor savings: Over 110,000 hours
  • Patrol efficiency increase: 13.2x
  • Pre-emptive fault detection rate: 63%

The entire system is built on a self-developed drone intelligent patrol platform that establishes a closed-loop patrol model. The operational procedures are as follows:

  • Establishment and approval of patrol plans
  • Issuance of work orders through the platform
  • Autonomous takeoff from drones' nests to perform patrols
  • Real-time transmission of patrol data and entry into storage
  • AI-powered fault identification and diagnosis
  • Completion of repairs, re-inspections, and record keeping according to established procedures

Safety Management and Compliance

The Three Gorges Corporation has established specialized control mechanisms that strictly reflect national drone flight management regulations. The system implements measures such as "double approval," "full reporting," and "real-time monitoring."

All management standards are aligned with the implementation guidelines of national drone flight laws, ensuring that all patrol missions are traceable, controllable, and operated in a closed-loop manner. This ensures that patrol operations are conducted safely and legally.

By implementing this system, the Three Gorges Corporation has secured capabilities for integrated drone management, systematic scheduling, intelligent diagnosis, and closed-loop fault handling. This serves as a significant milestone in the field of large-scale smart operation and maintenance (O&M) for new energy power plants, providing a practical standard model for patrolling such facilities both domestically and internationally.

Management Framework and Scope

The Three Gorges Corporation announced that this drone intelligent patrol management system was implemented primarily by the Power Production Department of the Three Gorges Land New Energy Headquarters. They established and applied a standardized "1+1+1+5" management system (consisting of plans, connection methods, management rules, and five specialized manuals) to resolve the previous lack of unified management standards for drone patrols in new energy power plants.

The current system includes the following equipment and scope:

  • Drone Nests: 21 locations
  • Patrol Drones: 28 units

These facilities enable comprehensive patrol monitoring of solar panels, wind turbines, collection lines, and substations.

Operational Efficiency and Process

Compared to traditional manual labor patrols, this system provides overwhelming improvements in efficiency:

  • Annual man-hour savings: Over 110,000 hours
  • Patrol efficiency increase: 13.2x improvement
  • Pre-emptive fault detection rate: 63%

The entire system is built upon a self-developed drone intelligent patrol platform to establish a closed-loop patrol model where all processes are interconnected. The operational procedures are as follows:

  • Establishment and approval of patrol plans
  • Issuance of work orders through the platform
  • Autonomous takeoff from the nest by drones to perform patrols
  • Real-time transmission of patrol data and input into storage
  • Fault identification and diagnosis using AI
  • Completion of fault repair, re-inspection, and record keeping according to set procedures

Safety Management and Compliance

The Three Gorges Corporation has established specialized control mechanisms that strictly reflect national regulations regarding drone flight management. The system implements measures such as "dual approval," "full reporting," and "real-time monitoring."

All management standards are aligned with the implementation guidelines of national drone flight regulations, ensuring that all patrol missions are traceable, controllable, and operated in a closed-loop manner. This ensures that patrol operations are conducted safely and legally.

Summary

The Three Gorges Corporation's drone intelligent patrol management system represents a fundamental shift in the O&M paradigm for new energy power plants by combining AI-driven processes with standardized management. By automating the cycle from inspection to diagnosis and repair, the system significantly enhances operational efficiency and safety for large-scale energy infrastructure.

管理体制の構築と範囲

三峡グループは公式発表を通じて、このシステムが「三峡陸上新エネルギー本部電力生産部」を主軸として構築されたことを明らかにしました。同組織は「1+1+1+5」（計画案、接続方法、管理規則、および5つの専門マニュアル）という標準化された管理制度を確立・適用することで、従来課題となっていた新エネルギー拠点のドローン巡回における統一的な管理基準の欠如を解決しました。

現在、このシステムは以下の設備と範囲を備えています：

  • ドローン基地（Drone Nest）：21箇所

  • 年間削減される人件費：11万時間以上
  • 巡回効率の向上率：13.2倍の増加
  • 設備欠陥の事前発見率：63%を達成

システム全体は、独自開発されたドローン型インテリジェント巡回プラットフォームに基づき、全工程が連結されたクローズドループ（Closed-Loop）巡回モデルを構築しています。運用手順は以下の通りです：

  • 巡回計画の策定および承認
  • プラットフォームを通じた作業命令の伝達
  • ドローンが基地から自律的に離陸し、巡回を実行
  • 巡回データのリアルタイム送信およびストレージへの入力
  • AIを活用した欠陥の識別および診断
  • 規定の手順に従った欠陥の修理、再検査、記録保管までを完了

安全管理と規制遵守

三峡グループは、国家が定めるドローン飛行管理に関する法規を厳格に反映し、特化した制御メカニズムを構築しました。このシステムでは、「二重承認」「全件報告」「リアルタイム監視」などの措置を実施しています。
