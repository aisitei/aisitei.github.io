Overview

According to a report by the tech outlet digitalcitizen on June 23, Samsung plans to launch three foldable smartphones in 2026, including the Galaxy Z Fold8. These devices are expected to be available in four colors with storage capacities starting from 256GB.

Color Lineup

The color configurations for Samsung's 2026 foldable lineup are as follows:

All three models—the Galaxy Z Flip8, Galaxy Z Fold8 (Wide Foldable), and Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra (successor to the Fold7)—will feature two base colors: Cream and Graphite. Additionally, each model will offer two exclusive colors.

Storage and Options

The following information regarding storage specifications has been provided:

  • Base capacity: 256GB

Summary

Samsung plans to release three foldable smartphones in 2026, including the Galaxy Z Fold8 and Flip8 series, featuring four color options and starting storage of 256GB. By allowing users to choose colors and storage capacities independently, Samsung aims to provide a wider range of customization for its upcoming foldable lineup.
