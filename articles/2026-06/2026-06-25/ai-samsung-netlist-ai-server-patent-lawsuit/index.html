Samsung, Netlist의 AI 서버 메모리 기술 관련 특허 소송 제기

2026-06-25

치열한 반도체 시장의 특허 전쟁

반도체 업계의 치열한 경쟁 속에서 특허 분쟁은 멈추지 않고 있습니다. Netlist Inc.는 최근 삼성전자를 상대로 소송을 제기했으며, 이는 급성장하는 AI 산업에 필수적인 고대역폭 메모리(HBM) 및 DDR5 제품을 겨냥하고 있습니다.

이번 소송은 미국 국제무역위원회(ITC)와 텍사스 동부 지역 법원에 각각 제기되었습니다. Netlist는 삼성의 첨단 메모리 기술이 자사의 두 가지 특허를 침해했다고 주장합니다.

소송 대상 및 핵심 기술

Netlist가 지적하는 구체적인 기술은 다음과 같습니다.

  • 특허 No. 12,646,537: TSV(through-silicon via)를 활용한 메모리 칩 적층 기술로, 전력 문제를 줄이면서 고밀도 메모리 패키지에 더 높은 성능을 담아내는 핵심 기술입니다.
  • 특허 No. 12,650,937: DDR5 메모리 모듈(RDIMMs 및 MRDIMMs 등)이 고속에서 안정적으로 작동하도록 돕는 Register Clock Driver(RCD) 관련 기술입니다.

이러한 기술들은 현재 AI 가속기와 고성능 서버가 매우 필요로 하는 핵심 구성 요소들입니다.

공급망에 미치는 영향

ITC 소송에는 삼성의 메모리를 사용하는 여러 주요 기업들이 포함되었습니다. 여기에는 Google의 TPUs, Nvidia의 Blackwell 및 Rubin GPU, 그리고 다양한 Supermicro 서버 등이 포함됩니다.

Netlist는 수입 금지 및 판매 중단 명령을 요구하고 있으며, 만약 이 요청이 받아들여질 경우 해당 제품들의 미국 시장 진입이 차단되어 AI 하드웨어 공급망에 심각한 혼란을 초래할 수 있습니다.

과거 분쟁 및 현재 상황

이번은 Netlist가 제기한 첫 소송이 아닙니다. 캘리포니아 소재의 메모리 모듈 및 스토리지 전문 기업인 Netlist는 2015년부터 삼성과 라이선스 계약을 체결했으나, 2020년경부터 관계가 악화되며 복잡한 법적 분쟁으로 이어졌습니다. 삼성은 이미 텍사스에서 두 차례의 대규모 배심원 판결(2023년 3억 300만 달러, 2024년 말 1억 1,800만 달러)을 통해 Netlist 메모리 특허 침해로 인한 패소 사례가 있습니다.

현재 AI 학습 및 추론 분야에서 HBM 수요가 폭증함에 따라 삼성의 메모리 사업은 매우 중요한 위치를 차지하고 있으며, Nvidia와 같은 기업들이 이를 대량으로 확보하려 노력 중입니다. Netlist의 CEO인 C.K. Hong은 이번 소송이 AI 서버 메모리 공간에서의 자사 혁신을 보호하기 위한 필수적인 조치라고 강조했습니다.

삼성 측은 제품이 법을 준수하고 있으며 강력하게 대응할 것이라는 입장을 밝혔습니다. 특히 ITC는 일반 법원보다 절차가 빠르게 진행되는 경향이 있어, 향후 몇 달 내에 실질적인 상업적 영향력을 가진 예비 판결이 나올 가능성이 있습니다.

총평

Netlist Inc.가 삼성전자의 HBM 및 DDR5 기술을 대상으로 특허 침해 소송을 제기하며 AI 하드웨어 공급망에 긴장감을 불어넣고 있습니다. 특히 ITC를 통한 수입 금지 명령이 현실화될 경우, Nvidia와 Google 등 주요 기업들의 제품 공급에 차질이 생길 수 있어 향후 법적 진행 상황이 주목됩니다.

Patent Warfare in the Intense Semiconductor Market

Amid intense competition within the semiconductor industry, patent disputes continue to escalate. Netlist Inc. recently filed a lawsuit against Samsung Electronics targeting High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) and DDR5 products, which are essential components for the rapidly growing AI industry.

The lawsuits were filed with both the U.S. International Trade Commission (ITC) and the Eastern District of Texas. Netlist claims that Samsung's advanced memory technologies infringe upon two of its specific patents.

Target Suits and Core Technologies

Netlist points to the following specific technologies in its claim:

  • Patent No. 12,646,537: Memory chip stacking technology utilizing TSV (through-silicon via) to achieve higher performance in high-density memory packages while reducing power issues.
  • Patent No. 12,650,937: Register Clock Driver (RCD) related technology that enables DDR5 memory modules (such as RDIMMs and MRDIMMs) to operate stably at high speeds.

These technologies are critical components currently in high demand for AI accelerators and high-performance servers.

Impact on the Supply Chain

The ITC lawsuit involves several major companies that utilize Samsung's memory, including Google's TPUs, Nvidia's Blackwell and Rubin GPUs, and various Supermicro servers.

Netlist is seeking import bans and sales halts. If these requests are granted, the entry of these products into the U.S. market could be blocked, potentially causing significant disruptions to the AI hardware supply chain.

Past Disputes and Current Status

This is not the first legal action initiated by Netlist. Based in California, Netlist—a company specializing in memory modules and storage—entered into a licensing agreement with Samsung in 2015. However, the relationship deteriorated around 2020, leading to complex legal disputes. Samsung has already lost two major jury trials in Texas due to Netlist patent infringements (awarded $303 million in 2023 and $118 million in late 2024).

As demand for HBM surges in the AI training and inference sectors, Samsung's memory business occupies a critical position, with companies like Nvidia striving to secure large quantities. Netlist CEO C.K. Hong emphasized that this lawsuit is a necessary measure to protect the company's innovations in the AI server memory space.

Samsung stated that its products comply with the law and that it will respond strongly to the allegations. Notably, because ITC proceedings tend to move faster than standard courts, preliminary rulings with substantial commercial impact could emerge within the coming months.

Summary

Netlist Inc.'s lawsuit against Samsung's HBM and DDR5 technologies has created significant tension in the AI hardware supply chain. If an ITC import ban is enacted, it could disrupt the distribution of products from major players like Nvidia and Google. The legal proceedings are being closely watched due to their potential impact on the global AI infrastructure market.

激烈半导体市场的专利战

在半导体行业激烈的竞争中，专利纠纷不断升级。Netlist Inc. 近期对 Samsung Electronics 发起诉讼，目标是 AI 产业急需的高带宽内存（HBM）及 DDR5 产品。此次诉讼分别在美国国际贸易委员会（ITC）和德克萨斯州东部地区法院提起。Netlist 声称 Samsung 的先进内存技术侵犯了其两项专利。

诉讼目标及核心技术

Netlist 指出的具体技术如下：

  • 专利号 12,646,537：利用 TSV（硅穿孔）技术的内存芯片堆叠技术，是降低功耗并提高高密度内存封装性能的关键技术。
  • 专利号 12,650,937：旨在帮助 DDR5 内存模块（如 RDIMMs 和 MRDIMMs 等）在高速状态下稳定运行的 Register Clock Driver (RCD) 相关技术。

这些技术是当前 AI 加速器和高性能服务器极其需要的核心组件。

对供应链的影响

在 ITC 诉讼中，涉及使用 Samsung 内存的多个主要企业。其中包括 Google 的 TPUs、Nvidia 的 Blackwell 和 Rubin GPU，以及各类 Supermicro 服务器等。

Netlist 要求采取进口禁令和停止销售命令。如果该请求获得批准，相关产品进入美国市场的路径将被阻断，可能导致 AI 硬件供应链出现严重混乱。

过往纠纷及当前情况

这并非 Netlist 发起的首次诉讼。总部位于加利福尼亚的内存模块及存储专业公司 Netlist 自 2015 年起与 Samsung 签订许可协议，但自 2020 年左右关系恶化，演变为复杂的法律纠纷。Samsung 此前在德克萨斯州已两次败诉于 Netlist 的内存专利侵权案（2023 年判决金额为 3.03 亿美元，2024 年末判决为 1.18 亿美元）。

随着 AI 训练和推理领域对 HBM 需求激增，Samsung 的内存业务占据重要地位，Nvidia 等企业正努力大量获取相关产品。Netlist 首席执行官 C.K. Hong 强调，此次诉讼是保护其在 AI 服务器内存领域的创新所采取的必要措施。

Samsung 表示其产品符合法律规定并会强力应对。由于 ITC 的程序通常比普通法院更快，未来几个月内可能会出现具有实质商业影响力的初步裁决。

总评

Netlist Inc. 对 Samsung 的 HBM 及 DDR5 技术提起专利侵权诉讼，引发了 AI 硬件供应链的紧张局势。特别是如果通过 ITC 执行进口禁令，可能会影响 Nvidia 和 Google 等主要企业的产品供应，后续法律进展值得密切关注。

总结

Netlist Inc. 因 HBM 和 DDR5 技术对 Samsung 发起专利诉讼，可能导致涉及 Google 和 Nvidia 的 AI 硬件供应链受到冲击。由于案件涉及 ITC 快速审理程序，未来数月内可能会产生影响美国市场的关键裁决。

If the ITC grants an import ban, it could severely disrupt the supply of products from major players like Nvidia and Google. The case highlights the critical legal risks surrounding core memory technologies essential for modern AI infrastructure.

激烈半导体市场的专利战

在半导体行业竞争日益激烈的背景下，专利纠纷仍在持续。Netlist Inc. 近期针对 Samsung Electronics 提起诉讼，目标直指对快速增长的 AI 产业至关重要的核心组件：高带宽内存（HBM）及 DDR5 产品。

此次诉讼分别在美国国际贸易委员会（ITC）和德克萨斯州东部地区法院提起。Netlist 指称 Samsung 的先进内存技术侵犯了其两项核心专利。

诉讼目标及核心技术

Netlist 指出的具体涉及技术如下：

  • 专利 No. 12,646,537：利用 TSV（硅穿孔）技术的内存芯片堆叠技术，是降低功耗的同时在高密度内存封装中实现更高性能的核心技术。
  • 专利 No. 12,650,937：与 Register Clock Driver（RCD）相关的技术，旨在帮助 DDR5 内存模块（如 RDIMMs 和 MRDIMMs 等）在高速运行下保持稳定。

这些技术是目前 AI 加速器和高性能服务器高度需求的核心组成部分。

对供应链的影响

此次 ITC 诉讼涉及多家使用 Samsung 内存的主要企业，其中包括 Google 的 TPU、Nvidia 的 Blackwell 及 Rubin GPU，以及各类 Supermicro 服务器等。

Netlist 要求采取进口禁令和停止销售的命令。如果这些要求获得批准，相关产品进入美国市场的路径将被阻断，可能导致 AI 硬件供应链出现严重混乱。

过往纠纷与现状

这并非 Netlist 发起的首次诉讼。总部位于加利福尼亚州的内存模块及存储专业公司 Netlist 自 2015 年起与 Samsung 签署了许可协议，但自 2020 年左右关系恶化，演变为复杂的法律纠纷。Samsung 此前在德克萨斯州已因侵犯 Netlist 内存专利而在两次大规模陪审团判决中败诉（分别获得 3.03 亿美元和 1.18 亿美元的赔偿）。

随着 AI 训练和推理领域对 HBM 需求激增，Samsung 的内存业务占据了极其重要的地位，Nvidia 等企业正努力获取大量供应。Netlist 首席执行官 C.K. Hong 强调，此次诉讼是保护公司在 AI 服务器内存领域的创新成果的必要措施。

Samsung 方面表示其产品符合法律规定，并将强力应对相关指控。值得注意的是，由于 ITC 的程序通常比普通法院更快，未来几个月内可能会出现具有实质性商业影响的初步裁决。

总结

Netlist Inc. 对 Samsung 的 HBM 及 DDR5 技术发起专利诉讼，引发了 AI 硬件供应链的高度紧张。若 ITC 最终下达进口禁令，将直接影响 Nvidia 和 Google 等核心企业的产品供应。由于相关技术在当前 AI 基建中地位极其重要，此案的法律进展正受到全球市场的密切关注。

激化する半導体市場の特許戦争

半導体業界における競争が激化する中、特許を巡る紛争も止むことなく続いています。Netlist Inc.は最近、Samsungを相手取って訴訟を提起しました。この訴訟は、急速に成長するAI産業において不可欠なコンポーネントである高帯域幅メモリ（HBM）およびDDR5製品をターゲットとしています。

今回の訴訟は、米国国際貿易委員会（ITC）およびテキサス州東部地方裁判所の両方で提起されました。Netlistは、Samsungの先端メモリ技術が自社の2つの特許を侵害していると主張しています。

訴訟対象および核心技術

Netlistが指摘する具体的な技術は以下の通りです。

  • 特許 No. 12,646,537：TSV（through-silicon via）を活用したメモリチップ積層技術。消費電力を抑えつつ、高密度メモリパッケージにおいてより高い性能を実現するための核心技術です。
  • 特許 No. 12,650,937：DDR5メモリモジュール（RDIMMsおよびMRDIMMsなど）が高速動作時に安定して動作することを可能にするRegister Clock Driver（RCD）関連の技術です。

これらの技術は、現在AIアクセラレータや高性能サーバーにおいて非常に需要が高い主要な構成要素です。

サプライチェーンへの影響

今回のITC訴訟には、Samsungのメモリを採用している複数の主要企業が含まれています。これらには、GoogleのTPU、NvidiaのBlackwellおよびRubin GPU、そして様々なSupermicroサーバーなどが含まれます。

Netlistは輸入禁止および販売停止命令を求めており、もしこの要求が認められた場合、該当製品の米国市場への参入が阻止され、AIハードウェアのサプライチェーンに深刻な混乱をもたらす可能性があります。

過去の紛争と現在の状況

今回の訴訟は、Netlistによる初めての法的措置ではありません。カリフォルニア州に拠点を置くメモリモジュールおよびストレージ専門企業であるNetlistは、2015年からSamsungとライセンス契約を締結していましたが、2020年頃から関係が悪化し、複雑な法的紛争へと発展しました。Samsungはすでにテキサス州において、Netlistの特許侵害による2度の大規模な陪審員判決（2023年に3億300万ドル、2024年末に1億1,800万ドル）で敗訴した経緯があります。

現在、AIの学習および推論分野においてHBMの需要が急増しており、Samsungのメモリ事業は極めて重要な位置を占めています。Nvidiaなどの企業も大量の確保に努めています。NetlistのCEOであるC.K. Hong氏は、今回の訴訟はAIサーバーメモリ空間における自社の革新を守るための不可欠な措置であると強調しました。

Samsung側は、製品は法を遵守しており、訴えに対して強力に対応する姿勢を示しています。特にITCの手続きは通常の裁判よりも迅速に進む傾向があるため、今後数ヶ月以内に実質的な商業的影響を伴う予備判決が出る可能性があります。

まとめ

Netlist Inc.によるSamsungのHBMおよびDDR5技術に対する特許侵害訴訟は、AIハードウェアのサプライチェーンに緊張をもたらしています。特にITCによる輸入禁止命令が現実となった場合、NvidiaやGoogleといった主要企業の製品供給に支障をきたす可能性があるため、今後の法的手続きの動向が注視されています。

Patent Warfare in the Intense Semiconductor Market

Amid intense competition within the semiconductor industry, patent disputes continue to escalate. Netlist Inc. recently filed a lawsuit against Samsung Electronics targeting High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) and DDR5 products, which are essential components for the rapidly growing AI industry.

The lawsuits were filed with both the U.S. International Trade Commission (ITC) and the Eastern District of Texas. Netlist claims that Samsung's advanced memory technologies infringe upon two of its specific patents.

Target Suits and Core Technologies

Netlist points to the following specific technologies in its claim:

  • Patent No. 12,646,537: Memory chip stacking technology utilizing TSV (through-silicon via) to achieve higher performance in high-density memory packages while reducing power issues.
  • Patent No. 12,650,937: Register Clock Driver (RCD) related technology that enables DDR5 memory modules (such as RDIMMs and MRDIMMs) to operate stably at high speeds.

These technologies are critical components currently in high demand for AI accelerators and high-performance servers.

Impact on the Supply Chain

The ITC lawsuit involves several major companies that utilize Samsung's memory, including Google's TPUs, Nvidia's Blackwell and Rubin GPUs, and various Supermicro servers.

Netlist is seeking import bans and sales halts. If these requests are granted, the entry of these products into the U.S. market could be blocked, potentially causing significant disruptions to the AI hardware supply chain.

Past Disputes and Current Status

This is not the first legal action initiated by Netlist. Based in California, Netlist—a company specializing in memory modules and storage—entered into a licensing agreement with Samsung in 2015, but the relationship deteriorated around 2020, leading to complex legal disputes. Samsung has already lost two major jury trials in Texas due to Netlist patent infringements:

  • $303 million (awarded in 2023)
  • $118 million (awarded in late 2024)

As demand for HBM surges in the AI training and inference sectors, Samsung's memory business occupies a critical position, with companies like Nvidia striving to secure large quantities. Netlist CEO C.K. Hong emphasized that this lawsuit is a necessary measure to protect the company's innovations in the AI server memory space.

Samsung stated that its products comply with the law and that it will respond strongly to the allegations. Notably, because ITC proceedings tend to move faster than standard courts, preliminary rulings with substantial commercial impact could emerge within the coming months.

Summary

Netlist Inc.'s lawsuit against Samsung's HBM and DDR5 technologies has created significant tension in the AI hardware supply chain involving major players like Nvidia and Google. If an ITC import ban is enacted, it could disrupt the distribution of critical components for AI accelerators and high-performance servers. The legal proceedings are being closely watched due to their potential impact on the global AI infrastructure market.
