Multi-Camera Collaboration Features

The DJI SDR Video Transmission 2 allows a single transmitter to connect to up to eight receivers, with each receiver supporting uplink control. This not only meets the high-definition monitoring demands of directors and camera assistants but also enables camera operators and focus pullers to remotely adjust parameters and control cameras. To meet the needs of using multiple cameras simultaneously in large-scale production environments, the multi-channel simultaneous transmission function supports up to five transmitters and 40 receivers operating concurrently.

Unified Transmitter/Receiver Design

The DJI SDR Video Transmission 2 adopts a unified transmitter/receiver design, overcoming the limitations of traditional video transmission equipment that required separate mounting points for transmitting and receiving units. Users no longer need to consider the ratio between the transmitter and receiver when purchasing or packaging gear. On set, users can switch the device's transmit/receive function via a physical dip switch on the main unit. The system automatically restarts and adapts, which lowers equipment idle time and increases asset utilization, allowing production teams to respond flexibly to shooting demands with less total equipment volume and more efficient cost management.

Auto Channel 2.0 Technology

The DJI SDR Video Transmission 2 supports the new Auto Channel 2.0 technology, optimized for multi-camera scenarios through enhanced smart scheduling and frequency scanning capabilities. Activating auto channel mode allows the DJI SDR Video Transmission 2 transmitter/receivers to recognize each other and intelligently schedule frequency bands, eliminating cumbersome manual planning and revolutionizing the multi-camera collaboration experience. Furthermore, even in complex environments like studios or large events with complicated signals, the device provides more accurate frequency scanning instructions and channel conflict diagnosis. This allows multiple transmit/receive groups to maintain stable and smooth video transmission simultaneously, regardless of environmental interference, allowing production teams to focus purely on the image.

Transmission Distance

DJI emphasized that thanks to the powerful anti-interference and penetration capabilities provided by its next-generation SDR technology, the DJI SDR Video Transmission 2 achieves a maximum video transmission distance of up to 4km—a 60% improvement over previous models (2.5km). This robust transmission capability provides great stability for long-distance wireless monitoring in highly challenging environments such as open stadiums, mountainous forests with obstacles, or during high-speed vehicle and first-person drone filming.

Image Quality and Latency

In terms of image quality, the DJI SDR Video Transmission 2 supports video transmission specifications of DCI 4K/60fps, 10-bit HDR, and H.265. The maximum encoding bitrate reaches 50Mbps, which is top-tier among comparable industry products, preserving rich detail on monitoring screens. The introduction of 10-bit HDR color sampling captures details in both highlights and shadows. Alongside significantly improved image quality, the transmission latency is low at 35 milliseconds.

The DJI SDR Video Transmission 2 transmitter/receiver features one HDMI video interface and two USB-C interfaces, meeting the demands of lightweight shooting scenarios.
