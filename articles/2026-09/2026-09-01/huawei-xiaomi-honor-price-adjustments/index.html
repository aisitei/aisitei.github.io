Market Context and Component Costs

Previous reports from IT之家 indicated that Qualcomm chip prices were expected to increase by double-digit percentages in September of this year, which could affect various technology sectors including smartphones and wearables. Furthermore, at the 2026 Mid-term Performance Media Conference Call held on the evening of August 18th, Xiaomi Group Chairman Li Weibing predicted that while memory price increases might ease in the second half of the year, upward trends could persist, keeping storage costs high.

Summary

Multiple smartphone brands, including Huawei, Honor, Xiaomi, and Redmi, implemented price increases starting September 1st. These adjustments are primarily attributed to anticipated rises in component costs, particularly related to Qualcomm chips and memory prices. The overall trend suggests that product pricing across the smartphone market is being driven upward by rising raw material expenses.
