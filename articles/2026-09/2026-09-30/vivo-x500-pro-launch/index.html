성능 및 카메라

vivo X500 Pro 시리즈는 Dimensity 9600 Pro 플래그십 칩을 세계 최초로 탑재하여 강력한 이미징 성능을 지원합니다. 이 신제품은 칼 자이스 초동적 메인 카메라를 탑재했으며, VCS 4.0, 칼 자이스 T* 코팅, 심층 흡수 블루 글래스가 새롭게 업그레이드되었습니다. 또한, 블루프린트 광위 900 센서를 세계 최초로 탑재하여 17EV의 다이내믹 레인지를 처음으로 구현했습니다.

이 제품은 칼 자이스 APO 슈퍼 망원 렌즈를 최초로 탑재했으며, 블루프린트 x Sony LYTIA 610 센서를 사용하여 85mm 황금 초점 거리를 지원합니다.

배터리

vivo X500 Pro는 6510mAh 배터리를 탑재했으며, 90W 유선 충전과 40W 무선 충전을 지원합니다.

Performance and Camera

The vivo X500 Pro series supports powerful imaging performance by featuring the Dimensity 9600 Pro flagship chip for the first time globally. This new product is equipped with a Zeiss T* main camera, which has been newly upgraded with VCS 4.0, Zeiss T* coating, and deep absorption blue glass. Furthermore, it incorporates the BluePrint 광위 900 sensor for the first time, achieving a dynamic range of 17EV.

The device also features the Zeiss APO super telephoto lens for the first time, utilizing the BluePrint x Sony LYTIA 610 sensor to support an 85mm golden focal length.

Display

The vivo X500 Pro series phone is available in four colors: Clear Day (晴天), Sunset (览霞), Night Shooting (摄夜), and Earth Echo (大地回声). It features a 6.36-inch screen, which incorporates Q11 emitting materials developed in collaboration with BOE. The display supports LTPO 8T with a 144Hz adaptive refresh rate, along with hardware-level low brightness of 1nit, adaptive dimming, Dolby Vision, HDR 10+, original polarization 2.0, and SGS low blue light certification.

Battery

The vivo X500 Pro is equipped with a 6510mAh battery and supports 90W wired charging and 40W wireless charging.

Summary

The vivo X500 Pro series boasts powerful performance by debuting the Dimensity 9600 Pro flagship chip globally. It offers an exceptional imaging experience through its Zeiss T* main camera and Zeiss APO super telephoto lens, achieving a dynamic range of 17EV for the first time. The device features a 6.36-inch LTPO 8T display with a 144Hz adaptive refresh rate, complemented by a large 6510mAh battery and high-speed charging capabilities.
