iQOO가 중국에서 많은 기대를 모았던 iQOO 16을 마침내 출시했습니다. 이 모델은 작년 iQOO 15의 성능 중심 후속작으로 포지셔닝됩니다. 새로운 플래그십은 Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 Extreme, iQOO 자체 개발 Q4 게이밍 칩, 8,400mAh 배터리, 8,500mm² VC 쿨링 시스템을 탑재했습니다. 또한 2K 165Hz Samsung M16 디스플레이를 채택하여 고성능 게이밍과 지속적인 성능 유지를 핵심 초점으로 삼았습니다. iQOO 16에 대한 자세한 내용은 아래에서 확인하세요.

iQOO 16：性能至上的旗舰智能手机发布

iQOO终于在中国市场发布了备受期待的iQOO 16。这款机型定位为去年iQOO 15的性能升级版，是一款以极致性能为核心的全新旗舰。它搭载了Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 Extreme处理器、iQOO自研的Q4游戏芯片、8,400mAh大容量电池以及8,500mm² VC散热系统。此外，iQOO 16还采用了2K 165Hz Samsung M16显示屏，将高性能游戏体验和持续稳定性能作为核心卖点。以下是关于iQOO 16的详细信息。

iQOO 16规格与功能

iQOO 16搭载的8400mAh电池采用了第五代硅碳负极技术，支持100W有线快充和40W无线充电。此外，它集成了iQOO独有的Q-flex控制技术，优化了触控控制管线的六个阶段，并在游戏引擎层面与触摸芯片及陀螺仪联动，从而大幅降低游戏延迟。

iQOO 16: Performance-Focused Flagship Launch

iQOO has finally launched the iQOO 16 in China, a model that generated significant anticipation. This new flagship is positioned as a performance successor to last year's iQOO 15. The device is equipped with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 Extreme, iQOO's proprietary Q4 gaming chip, an 8,400mAh battery, and an 8,500mm² VC cooling system. Furthermore, it adopts a 2K 165Hz Samsung M16 display, focusing on high-performance gaming and sustained performance retention. Details about the iQOO 16 are provided below.

iQOO 16 Specifications and Features

  • Display: 6.85-inch Samsung M16 AMOLED
  • Resolution: 3168 x 1440 (2K)
  • Refresh Rate: LTPO variable refresh rate up to 165Hz
  • Touch Sampling: Instant touch sampling up to 5000Hz
  • Brightness: 2,800 nits full-screen brightness, 10,000 nits peak local brightness
  • Dimming: Full brightness DC dimming, supports high-frequency PWM dimming up to 3300Hz

Performance is driven by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 processor, manufactured using TSMC's 2nm process, boasting CPU speeds of up to 5.01GHz. This is paired with LPDDR5X Ultra Pro memory and UFS 4.1 storage. The Q4 gaming chip, Monster Super Core Engine, and the 8,500mm² VC cooling system are designed for continuous performance enhancement.

The 8400mAh battery utilizes 5th generation Silicon-Anode technology and supports 100W wired charging and 40W wireless charging. Additionally, it features iQOO's proprietary Q-flex control technology, which optimizes six stages of the touch control pipeline and reduces gaming latency by integrating with the touch chip and gyroscope at the game engine level.

Camera

  • Rear Main Camera: 50MP OmniVision OV50Q, supports OIS
  • Rear Telephoto Camera: 50MP Sony LYTIA 600 periscope telephoto, supports OIS
  • Rear Ultra-Wide Camera: 50MP
  • Front Selfie Camera: 32MP
  • Video Recording: Rear camera supports up to 8K

The iQOO 16 runs Android 17 based on OriginOS 7. It is equipped with a 3D ultrasonic fingerprint sensor, Wi-Fi 7, USB 3.2 Gen 1, NFC, an infrared blaster, and stereo speakers. The device features X+Z dual-axis linear motors to provide more diverse and precise haptic feedback. For durability, it supports IP68 and IP69 dust and water resistance ratings.

iQOO 16 Pricing and Availability

  • 12GB RAM + 256GB Storage: ¥5,999 (Approx. $900)
  • 12GB RAM + 512GB Storage: ¥6,799 (Approx. $1,020)
  • 16GB RAM + 512GB Storage: ¥7,499 (Approx. $1,125)
  • 16GB RAM + 1TB Storage: ¥8,499 (Approx. $1,275)

The phone is available in three colors: Chasing Light, Legend, and Track.

A global version of the iQOO 16 was recently spotted passing European EEC certification; however, there has been no official announcement regarding its global release.

Summary

The iQOO 16 is a flagship smartphone designed for peak performance, featuring the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 Extreme processor and Q4 gaming chip. Its gaming experience is maximized by a 2K 165Hz Samsung M16 display and an enormous 8,400mAh battery. The device also boasts premium features such as a powerful multi-camera system and robust IP68/IP69 durability ratings, making it highly appealing to power users who prioritize performance and endurance.
