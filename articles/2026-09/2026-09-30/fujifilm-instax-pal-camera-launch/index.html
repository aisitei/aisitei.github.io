Design and Features

The Fujifilm Instax Pal 2 pocket camera has been released today. This new device features a retro design and offers a shooting experience reminiscent of the waist-level viewfinder found on traditional film cameras. It can connect with various devices, including smartphones and Fujifilm printers.

This camera supports both waist-level viewfinder and standard viewfinder shooting, allowing for mini, square, wide, and borderless photo capture. Furthermore, it includes 30 built-in effect filters that users can freely select from.

Specifications

  • Sensor: 1/3.06 inch, 10 million pixels
  • Internal Storage: Stores up to 100 photos
  • Expansion Slot: Supports microSD card
  • Top LCD Screen: 1.3 inches, supports remote shooting

Connectivity and Special Functions

The camera is equipped with a USB-C port for direct photo transfer to mobile phones, in addition to supporting wireless transmission via Bluetooth. By sliding the lever located on the upper right side of the body toward the display, the camera switches into "instax Animation" mode. Additionally, pressing and holding the shutter button allows users to take up to 10 continuous photos, creating a video effect similar to a flipbook animation—a fun way to record images despite its slightly choppy nature.

The product is currently available on JD.com for 1299 yuan.
