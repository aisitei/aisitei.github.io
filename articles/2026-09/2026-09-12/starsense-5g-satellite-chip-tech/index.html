기업 개요 및 기술 전략

2020년에 설립된 XingSi Semiconductor는 "5G 만물 인터넷 연결 칩"이라는 핵심 포지셔닝을 확고히 하고, 3GPP 기술 반복 추세와 시장의 실제 수요에 발맞춰 5G 기저대역 칩 기술을 심화하는 동시에 위성통신 단말 칩 분야에 선제적으로 진출하여 차별화된 기술 및 산업 우위를 구축했습니다. 핵심 팀원들은 평균 10년 이상의 이동 통신 산업 연구 개발 경험을 보유하고 있으며, 여러 무선 통신 칩의 성공적인 테이프 아웃 및 양산 경험을 갖추고 있어 안정적이고 강력한 역량을 자랑하며, XingSi Semiconductor 듀얼 모드 칩의 지속적인 반복 및 안정적인 양산을 위한 기반을 다지고 있습니다.

제품 및 기술 특징

깊이 있는 연구 개발 축적을 바탕으로 XingSi Semiconductor는 5G 및 위성 듀얼 모드 칩 제품 시스템을 구축했습니다. 핵심 제품인 CS7620 멀티 모드 위성 기저대역 칩과 CS6610 5G RedCap 기저대역 칩은 모두 완전 국산 고성능 RISC-V 아키텍처 프로세서를 채택했으며, 관련 솔루션과 함께 지상 5G 셀룰러 네트워크와 저궤도 위성 시스템의 원활한 융합을 실현합니다. 이 제품은 5G NR NTN, 국내 저궤도 위성군, 5G RedCap, 4G LTE 등 다양한 통신 방식을 지원하며, 높은 통합성, 낮은 전력 소비, 고성능의 제품 특성을 겸비하여 휴대폰 직결 위성, 위성 광대역 접속, 산업 인터넷, 스마트 웨어러블, 차량 통신 등 다양한 단말 시나리오에 적용될 수 있습니다.

기술 검증 및 시장 성과

업계의 단일 기술 배치와 달리, XingSi Semiconductor는 국내에서 궤도 테스트 및 검증을 거친 저궤도 광대역 위성과 5G 융합 기저대역 칩 플랫폼을 보유한 기업으로, 기술 구현 가능성과 시나리오 적합성이 충분히 검증되었습니다. 2025년 5월, XingSi Semiconductor는 듀얼 모드 칩 기술 시스템을 기반으로 5G NTN 표준 기반의 휴대폰 직결 위성 고화질 영상 통화를 성공적으로 구현하여 상징적인 기술 응용을 실현했으며, 국내 관련 분야의 기술 공백을 효과적으로 메우고 위성통신 단말 칩 기술의 단점을 해결하는 데 기여했습니다.

미래에 XingSi Semiconductor는 칩 자체 혁신에 지속적으로 집중하고, 산업 체인 협력을 심화하며, 듀얼 모드 칩 핵심 역량으로 저고도 경제, 산업 인터넷, 위성 IoT 등 신흥 분야에 지속적으로 힘을 실어줄 것입니다.

行业背景与发展趋势

随着卫星互联网被纳入国家战略新兴支柱产业，行业正从概念炒作阶段迈入技术落地和产业深耕的务实期。政策和资本资源持续向上游硬科技领域倾斜，为芯片研发、高端制造等核心环节提供了有力支撑。

星思半导体定位与技术积累

  • **公司定位：** 星思半导体自 2020 年成立以来，始终聚焦于“5G 万物互联连接芯片”的核心赛道。
  • **核心优势：** 公司在深耕 5G 基带芯片的同时，前瞻性布局了卫星通信终端芯片领域，构建了差异化的技术与产业优势。
  • **团队实力：** 团队核心成员平均拥有 10 年以上移动通信行业研发经验，具备多款无线通信芯片成功流片及量产经验。

双模芯片产品体系与规格

星思半导体打造了成熟的 5G 与卫星双模芯片产品体系，实现了地面蜂窝网络和低轨卫星系统的无缝融合。

  • **核心处理器：** 关键产品均采用全国产高性能 RISC-V 架构处理器。
  • **主打产品：**
    • CS7620 多模卫星基带芯片
    • CS6610 5G RedCap 基带芯片
  • **技术兼容性：** 产品兼容多种制式，包括 5G NR NTN、国内低轨卫星星座、5G RedCap 和 4G LTE 等。

行业领先的技术验证与落地

星思半导体是国内首家拥有经过在轨测试验证的低轨宽带卫星与 5G 融合基带芯片平台企业，技术落地性得到充分验证。

  • **里程碑事件：** 2025 年 5 月，公司成功实现了基于 5G NTN 标准的手机直连卫星高清视频通话，填补了国内相关领域的关键技术空白。

市场合作与未来战略布局

目前，星思半导体的双模芯片解决方案已获得业内认可，并与多家头部手机、通信设备和汽车厂商建立了深度合作关系。

対応する主な通信方式や応用分野は以下の通りです。

Introduction

As satellite internet becomes incorporated into national strategic and emerging core industries, industry development has moved beyond conceptual speculation and entered a practical stage of technological implementation and industrial deepening. Capital markets and policy authorities are continuously focusing on the hardware technology sector upstream of satellite internet, gradually establishing financial guarantee mechanisms that provide strong support for key areas such as chip R&D and advanced manufacturing. Against this industrial backdrop, domestic chip companies possessing core technologies are meeting development opportunities. XingSi Semiconductor is emerging as a critical player in Korea's space-ground converged communication field by leveraging its mature 5G and satellite dual-mode chip research and development capabilities, thereby promoting the commercialization of integrated air-ground-sea communication technology.

Company Overview and Technology Strategy

Established in 2020, XingSi Semiconductor solidified its core positioning as a "5G IoT Connectivity Chip." By aligning with the 3GPP technological iteration trends and actual market demand, the company deepened its expertise in 5G baseband chip technology while proactively entering the satellite communication terminal chip sector. This strategy allowed them to build differentiated technological and industrial advantages. The core team members boast an average of over 10 years of R&D experience in the mobile communication industry, possessing successful tape-out and mass production experience across various wireless communication chips. This establishes a stable and robust capability foundation for the continuous iteration and reliable mass production of XingSi Semiconductor's dual-mode chips.

Product and Technical Features

Building on deep R&D accumulation, XingSi Semiconductor has established a product system featuring 5G and satellite dual-mode chips. Its key products include:

  • CS7620 Multi-mode Satellite Baseband Chip
  • CS6610 5G RedCap Baseband Chip

Both core products utilize fully domestic, high-performance RISC-V architecture processors. Combined with related solutions, they achieve seamless convergence between terrestrial 5G cellular networks and Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite systems. This product supports diverse communication methods, including:

  • 5G NR NTN
  • Domestic LEO Satellite Constellations
  • 5G RedCap
  • 4G LTE

The chip combines high integration, low power consumption, and high performance, making it applicable to various terminal scenarios such as: phone-direct satellite connectivity, satellite broadband access, Industrial IoT, smart wearables, and vehicle communication.

Technical Validation and Market Achievements

Unlike industry players focused on single technologies, XingSi Semiconductor possesses a platform combining LEO broadband satellites with 5G converged baseband chips, which have undergone orbital testing and validation within Korea. This confirms the technical feasibility and scenario suitability of their technology. In May 2025, XingSi Semiconductor achieved a symbolic technological application by successfully implementing high-definition video calls via phone-to-satellite based on its dual-mode chip system utilizing the 5G NTN standard. This effort effectively bridged domestic technology gaps and contributed to solving limitations in satellite communication terminal chips.

Cooperation and Future Strategy

Currently, XingSi Semiconductor's dual-mode chip solution has gained industry recognition, leading to deep collaborations with several major Korean manufacturers of mobile phones, communication equipment, automotive components, and modules. Various commercialization projects are continuously underway. Furthermore, the company aligns perfectly with the nation's strategy for integrated air-ground-sea industrial development. XingSi is committed to continually deepening core 5G/6G satellite communication technologies, advancing industry innovation platforms through academia-industry-research cooperation, and completing its product matrix.

In the future, XingSi Semiconductor will continue to focus on chip self-innovation, deepen collaboration across the industrial chain, and provide sustained momentum for emerging fields—such as the LEO economy, Industrial IoT, and Satellite IoT—through its core dual-mode chip capabilities.

Summary

XingSi Semiconductor has emerged as a key player in the satellite internet industry by developing 5G and satellite dual-mode chips. The company's high-performance chips support diverse communication methods, including 5G NR NTN, LEO constellations, and 5G RedCap, enabling innovative applications like direct phone-to-satellite calling. By proving technical feasibility through collaborations with major domestic and international companies, XingSi is establishing itself as a core driver for the future era of integrated air-ground-sea communication.
