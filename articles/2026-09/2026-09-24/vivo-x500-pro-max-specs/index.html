Overview

The Vivo X500 Pro Max is a high-end smartphone featuring a large display, powerful hardware, and a camera-centric setup. The device runs on Android 17 based on OriginOS 7, offering up to 16GB of RAM and 1TB of storage. It is equipped with an 8,000mAh battery supporting 90W fast charging, making it ideal for users who demand both powerful performance and long battery life.

Pricing and Availability

The starting price for the Vivo X500 Pro Max is $1,299.00. It can be purchased from Giztop, which offers global shipping and express delivery options for international buyers.

Display and Performance

This phone features a 6.85-inch BOE Q11 OLED display that supports 2K resolution, a 144Hz refresh rate, and 1440Hz high-frequency dimming. The high refresh rate provides a smooth experience during scrolling, gaming, and animations. Internally, the device is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro chipset, combined with LPDDR5X Ultra RAM and UFS 4.1 storage. Storage options include 256GB, 512GB, and 1TB.

Camera System

Photography is a key focus of the Vivo X500 Pro Max. The rear camera array includes a 50MP Sony LYT-L910 main camera, a 50MP ultra-wide-angle camera, and a 200MP Samsung ISOCELL HPB periscope telephoto camera with an equivalent focal length of 85mm. Notably, the periscope telephoto camera supports long-distance macro shooting and features Zeiss APO optics and Zeiss T* coating. The front camera is a 50MP sensor dedicated to selfies and video calls. The 16GB/1TB version includes a full Photography Kit.

  • Main Camera: 50MP Sony LYT-L910
  • Ultra-wide-angle Camera: 50MP
  • Periscope Telephoto Camera: 200MP Samsung ISOCELL HPB (85mm equivalent focal length), featuring Zeiss APO optics, Zeiss T* coating, and macro support.
  • Front Camera: 50MP

Battery and Other Features

The 8,000mAh battery supports 90W fast charging. Vivo states that the phone can charge to 50% in approximately 10 minutes and fully charge in about 30 minutes. Additional features include NFC, HiFi audio, dual stereo speakers, X-axis linear motor, in-display fingerprint sensor, facial recognition, and support for dual Nano SIM cards.

Summary

The Vivo X500 Pro Max is a flagship smartphone equipped with the powerful MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro chipset and a 2K OLED display with a 144Hz refresh rate. Its standout features are the high-performance camera system, which includes a 200MP periscope telephoto lens, and an enormous 8,000mAh battery that supports 90W fast charging. Overall, it offers top-tier performance and extended usage time, making it highly appealing for photography enthusiasts.
