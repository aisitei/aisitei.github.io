Motorola Edge 70 Plus Specifications and Features

The Motorola Edge 70 Plus is equipped with a 6.78-inch Extreme AMOLED display, boasting Super HD resolution of 2772 x 1272 pixels, a 144Hz refresh rate, and a peak brightness of up to 5,200 nits. This panel supports HDR10+, 10-bit color, 100% DCI-P3 coverage, and Pantone Validated Colors.

The device also features an optical in-display fingerprint sensor, Gorilla Glass 7i, IP64 water resistance rating, and MIL-STD-810H durability.

Under the hood, the phone is powered by the Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chipset, paired with 8GB of LPDDR5X RAM and 256GB of UFS 3.1 storage. It offers RAM Boost for up to 24GB of virtual memory, and storage can be expanded up to 1TB using a microSD card. Running Android 16, the phone promises five years of security updates until September 2031.

The camera system includes a 20MP main rear camera supporting OIS, All Pixel Focus, and Ultra Pixel Technology, complemented by a 2MP macro camera and a dedicated optical sensor. The front-facing camera is a 32MP setup designed for selfies and video calls. Other key features include eSIM support, 5G, Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 6.0, NFC, and an in-display fingerprint sensor.

Motorola Edge 70 Plus Price and Availability

The Motorola Edge 70 Plus is available in a single configuration of 8GB+256GB for £529.99 / €599.99. It comes in Pantone colors such as Gray Lilac, Mountain View, and Milky Blue. Pre-orders are currently open, with shipping scheduled to begin on September 23rd. Buyers will receive a bundle including the moto watch fit and moto tag 2 at no extra cost.

Summary

The Motorola Edge 70 Plus is positioned as a mid-range smartphone featuring an impressive combination of hardware. Key highlights include the 144Hz Extreme AMOLED display, the Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chipset, and a 20MP OIS camera system. Its standout features are the massive 6,500mAh battery capacity and five years of promised security updates, further enhanced by the included moto watch fit and moto tag 2 bundle for pre-order customers.
