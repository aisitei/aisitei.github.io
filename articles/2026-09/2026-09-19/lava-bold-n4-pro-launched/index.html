Lava Bold N4 Pro 5G 发布

Lava 在其 Bold N4 系列中推出了新机型。继上个月发布了入门级 4G 手机 Bold N4 Lite 后，这次正式发布了 Lava Bold N4 Pro 5G，它配备了更快速的芯片组、5G 连接性、更大的显示屏以及超大容量电池。

  • 内存：6GB LPDDR4X RAM
  • 存储空间：128GB UFS 2.2 存储，支持高达 1TB microSD 卡
  • 摄像头：后置 50MP AI 双摄，前置 8MP 摄像头
  • 安全功能：侧边指纹传感器
  • 耐用性：IP64 等级防尘防水
  • 电池：6,000mAh，支持 18W 快充
  • 操作系统：Android 16
  • 连接性：Wi-Fi 802.11ac，蓝牙 5.0，USB Type-C 端口，双 SIM 卡

设计与配置

Lava Bold N4 Pro 5G 提供午夜蓝 (Midnight Blue) 和薰衣草到银色渐变（Lavender-to-Silver）两种颜色选择。该机型以 6GB RAM 和 128GB 存储的单一配置上市。

价格与发售信息

Lava Bold N4 Pro 5G 的特别发布价为 13,999 印度卢比，并通过 Amazon India 进行独家销售。预计将于 9 月 26 日 IST 中午 12 点开始发售。

与前代机型的对比

新款 Bold N4 Pro 5G 的定价与今年初发布的 Lava Bold N2 Pro 水平相当。但新机型在多个方面进行了升级，包括增加了 5G 支持、更大的 6,000mAh 电池、120Hz 显示屏以及最新的 Android 16 系统。

总结

Lava Bold N4 Pro 5G 是一款具有强大竞争力的入门级智能手机。它搭载了 Unisoc T8200 芯片组和 5G 连接性，并配备了 120Hz 显示屏和超大容量的 6,000mAh 电池。此外，机身还具备 IP64 等级的防尘防水功能以及 Android 16 系统，为追求高性价比、需要稳定耐用性的消费者提供了极具吸引力的选择。

Lava Launches Bold N4 Pro 5G

Lava has added a new model to its Bold N4 lineup. Following the release of the entry-level 4G phone, the Bold N4 Lite, Lava is now introducing the Lava Bold N4 Pro 5G, which features a faster chipset, 5G connectivity, a larger display, and a high-capacity battery.

Key Specifications

  • Display: 6.75-inch HD+ LCD, 120Hz refresh rate, up to 550 nits brightness
  • Processor: Unisoc T8200 chipset
  • Memory: 6GB LPDDR4X RAM
  • Storage: 128GB UFS 2.2 storage, supports up to 1TB microSD card slot
  • Camera: Rear 50MP AI dual camera, front 8MP camera
  • Security: Side-mounted fingerprint sensor
  • Durability: IP64 dust and water resistance rating
  • Battery: 6,000mAh, supports 18W charging
  • Operating System: Android 16
  • Connectivity: Wi-Fi 802.11ac, Bluetooth 5.0, USB Type-C port, Dual SIM

Design and Configuration

The Lava Bold N4 Pro 5G is available in two colors: Midnight Blue and Lavender-to-Silver gradient. It will be launched with a single configuration of 6GB RAM and 128GB storage.

Pricing and Availability

The Lava Bold N4 Pro 5G is priced at a special launch price of 13,999 rupees and will be sold exclusively through Amazon India. Sales are scheduled to begin on September 26th at 12:00 PM IST.

Comparison with Previous Models

The price point of the Bold N4 Pro 5G is similar to that of the Lava Bold N2 Pro from earlier this year. The new Bold N4 Pro 5G features several upgrades, including 5G support, a larger 6,000mAh battery, a 120Hz display, and Android 16.

Review

The Lava Bold N4 Pro 5G is a smartphone with strong competitiveness in the budget segment, equipped with the Unisoc T8200 chipset, 5G connectivity, a 120Hz refresh rate display, and a massive 6,000mAh battery. It enhances user convenience and durability by including Android 16 and an IP64 dust/water resistance rating. This makes it an attractive option for consumers looking for a 5G smartphone at a reasonable price.

Summary

The Lava Bold N4 Pro 5G enters the budget market with significant upgrades, featuring 5G connectivity, a Unisoc T8200 chipset, and a large 6,000mAh battery. Key highlights include a 120Hz display, IP64 durability rating, and Android 16 OS. Priced at 13,999 rupees, it offers strong value for consumers seeking modern features in an affordable package.
