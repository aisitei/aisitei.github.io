생체 인식

출시 행사

Honor Magic 그랜드 이벤트 및 Honor Magic9 시리즈 신제품 발표회는 9월 28일 베이징에서 개최될 예정입니다. 현재 블로거 @数码闲聊站가 Honor Magic9 시리즈의 사양 정보를 공개했으며, 다음과 같이 정리했습니다.

  • 프로세서: Snapdragon 8E5 (5세대 Snapdragon 8 至尊版)

  • 기타: 3D 얼굴 인식 + 3D 초음파 지문 인식, AI 버튼 + 카메라 버튼, 가로형 대형 매트릭스 Deco, 듀얼 1216 스피커, IP68/69/69K 방수방진

총평

Honor Magic9 시리즈는 3D 초음파 지문 인식과 3D 얼굴 인식을 포함한 듀얼 3D 생체 인식을 특징으로 합니다. 이 시리즈는 Snapdragon 8E5 또는 Snapdragon 8EE6 프로세서를 탑재하며, 모델에 따라 최대 11000mAh의 대용량 배터리와 고속 유무선 충전을 지원합니다. 또한, 200Mp 메인 카메라를 포함한 강력한 카메라 시스템과 IP68/69/69K 방수방진 등 플래그십 사양을 갖추고 있습니다.

新品发布与活动信息

  • Honor Magic9 系列手机将于 9 月 28 日在北京举行盛典暨新品发布会。

核心技术与安全特性

  • 独家生物识别： Honor Magic9 系列带来行业独家的双 3D 生物识别系统，包括 3D 超声波指纹识别和 3D 人脸识别。
  • 防护等级： 三款机型均具备 IP68/69/69K 的高标准防水防尘能力。

Honor Magic9 系列主要规格亮点

Honor Magic9 (基础版)

  • 屏幕与设计： 6.37英寸，2664*1236p 1.5K+120Hz LTPS 小바 타입；采用大 R 角极窄四等边。
  • 性能与续航： 配备骁龙 8E5 处理器（第五代骁龙 8 至尊版），搭载 8000mAh 大电池，支持 80W 有线快充和 50W 无线快充。
  • 影像系统： 后置三摄组合：200Mp 1/1.4" F1.9 主摄 + 50Mp F2.0 超广角 + 200Mp 1/1.56" 3.5X F2.6 潜望长焦；前置为 55Mp F2.0 1:1 方形。
  • 其他功能： 支持 3D 超声波指纹，以及 AI 键 + 拍照键等特色按键。

Honor Magic9 Pro Max

  • 屏幕与设计： 6.8英寸，2868*1320p 1.5K+120Hz LTPO 大바 타입；采用大 R 角极窄四等边。
  • 性能与续航： 配备骁龙 8EE6 处理器（第六代骁龙 8 超级至尊版），搭载 8800mAh 大电池，支持 100W 有线快充和 80W 无线快充。
  • 影像系统： 后置三摄组合升级：200Mp 1/1.28" F1.57 超대형 센서主摄 + 50Mp F2.0 超广角 + 200Mp 1/1.4" F2.6 大형 센서潜望长焦；前置为 55Mp F2.0 1:1 方形。
  • 特色功能： 支持双重生物识别（3D 人脸识别 + 3D 超声波指纹），配备双 1216 扬声器，以及横向大矩阵 Deco 设计。

Honor Magic9 超能版

  • 屏幕与设计： 6.81英寸，2788*1280p 1.5K+120Hz 바 타입；具备 1mm 级别极窄边框和横向大矩阵 Deco 设计。
  • 性能与续航： 配备骁龙 8E5 处理器（第五代骁龙 8 至尊版），搭载超大容量 11000mAh 电池，支持 80W 有线快充和 50W 无线快充；新增主动散热风扇。
  • 影像系统： 后置三摄组合：200Mp 主摄 + 12Mp 超广角 + 50Mp 3X 长焦；前置为 50Mp。

总结

Honor Magic9 系列在新品发布会上展示了三大型号，核心亮点在于行业独家的双 3D 生物识别系统和超高的防护等级。三款机型分别针对不同需求进行了配置优化，从基础版到Pro Max和超能版，提供了从续航、性能（骁龙8EE6）到影像规格的全面升级选择。

生体認証

Honorは、新型のHonor Magic9シリーズが業界初のデュアル 3D生体認証を搭載すると発表しました。これには、3D超音波指紋認識と3D顔認証が含まれます。

まとめ

Honor Magic9シリーズは、業界初のデュアル 3D生体認証（超音波指紋認識と顔認証）を主要な特徴としています。本シリーズはSnapdragon 8E5またはSnapdragon 8EE6プロセッサを搭載し、モデルによっては最大11000mAhという大容量バッテリーと高速有線・ワイヤレス充電に対応しています。さらに、200Mpのメインカメラを含む高性能カメラシステムやIP68/69/69Kといったフラッグシップ級の防水防塵性能を備えています。

Biometrics

Honor announced that the Honor Magic9 series provides industry-leading dual 3D biometrics, which includes both 3D ultrasonic fingerprint recognition and 3D face recognition.

Launch Event Details

The Honor Magic Grand Event and the Honor Magic9 series new product launch are scheduled to take place in Beijing on September 28th. Blogger @数码闲聊站 has released preliminary specifications for the Honor Magic9 series, which are summarized below.

Honor Magic9

  • Display: 6.37-inch 2664*1236p 1.5K+120Hz LTPS bar type, featuring wide R corners and extremely narrow all-around bezels.
  • Processor: Snapdragon 8E5 (5th Gen Snapdragon 8 Supreme Edition).
  • Battery: 8000mAh large capacity battery + 80W wired charging + 50W wireless charging.
  • Camera System:
    • Front: 55Mp F2.0 1:1 square camera.
    • Rear: 200Mp 1/1.4-inch F1.9 main camera + 50Mp F2.0 ultra-wide camera + 200Mp 1/1.56-inch 3.5x periscope telephoto camera.
  • Other Features: 3D ultrasonic fingerprint recognition, AI button + camera button, horizontal large matrix cooling plate Deco, IP68/69/69K dust and water resistance.

Honor Magic9 Pro Max

  • Display: 6.8-inch 2868*1320p 1.5K+120Hz LTPO large bar type, featuring wide R corners and extremely narrow all-around bezels.
  • Processor: Snapdragon 8EE6 (6th Gen Snapdragon 8 Super Supreme Edition).
  • Battery: 8800mAh large capacity battery + 100W wired charging + 80W wireless charging.
  • Camera System:
    • Front: 55Mp F2.0 1:1 square camera.
    • Rear: 200Mp 1/1.28-inch F1.57 large sensor main camera + 50Mp F2.0 ultra-wide camera + 200Mp 1/1.4-inch F2.6 large sensor periscope telephoto camera.
  • Other Features: 3D face recognition + 3D ultrasonic fingerprint recognition, AI button + camera button, horizontal large matrix Deco, dual 1216 speakers, IP68/69/69K dust and water resistance.

Honor Magic9 Supercharged Edition

  • Display: 6.81-inch 2788*1280p 1.5K+120Hz bar type, featuring an extremely narrow 1mm bezel and horizontal large matrix Deco.
  • Processor: Snapdragon 8E5 (5th Gen Snapdragon 8 Supreme Edition), Active cooling fan.
  • Battery: 11000mAh battery, 80W wired charging, 50W wireless charging.
  • Camera System:
    • Front: 50Mp camera.
    • Rear: 200Mp main camera + 12Mp ultra-wide camera + 50Mp 3x telephoto camera.

Summary

The Honor Magic9 series is defined by its dual 3D biometrics, incorporating both 3D ultrasonic fingerprint recognition and 3D face recognition. The lineup features powerful processors, either the Snapdragon 8E5 or Snapdragon 8EE6, alongside massive batteries that can reach up to 11000mAh and support high-speed wired/wireless charging. Furthermore, these devices are equipped with flagship camera systems, including a 200Mp main camera, and boast robust IP68/69/69K dust and water resistance ratings.
