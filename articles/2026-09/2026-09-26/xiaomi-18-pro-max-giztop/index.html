Introduction

The Xiaomi 18 Pro Max is Xiaomi's latest flagship model, introducing high-end performance, advanced photography features, and a large-capacity battery to the premium smartphone market. This model features a 2K LTPO AMOLED display, Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 processor, Leica-based 200MP camera system, and an 8,500mAh battery. It is available starting from $1,299 from Giztop, with global and express shipping options.

Display and Design

The Xiaomi 18 Pro Max is equipped with a 6.9-inch 2K LTPO AMOLED display, supporting a variable refresh rate of 1–120Hz. It supports HDR10+, HDR Vivid, Dolby Vision, 12-bit color, and a peak brightness of up to 4,000 nits. Additionally, the display includes privacy protection features and a 300Hz touch sampling rate. A secondary 2.9-inch auxiliary OLED rear display, which supports a 120Hz refresh rate, provides another way to check content and take selfies using the main camera.

Performance and Storage

The phone is powered by the 2nm Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 processor, boasting speeds up to 5.0GHz. It offers LPDDR6 RAM of either 12GB or 16GB, along with storage up to 1TB. Xiaomi HyperOS 4 is pre-installed, providing a fast interface that supports full-screen gestures. For connectivity, it supports 5G and Wi-Fi 7 with up to 6.9Gbps throughput.

Camera System

The Xiaomi 18 Pro Max features a Leica camera system composed of two 200MP cameras and a 50MP ultra-wide-angle camera.

  • Main Camera: 200MP, 1/1.28-inch sensor, f/1.67 aperture, OIS (Optical Image Stabilization)
  • Periscope Telephoto Camera: 200MP, 3.2x optical zoom, supports 12.8x lossless zoom
  • Ultra-Wide Camera: 50MP, autofocus, supports 5cm macro shooting
  • Front Camera: 50MP, autofocus, supports 4K video recording

Battery and Charging

The phone is equipped with a large 8,500mAh battery and supports 100W wired charging, 50W wireless charging, and 27W reverse wireless charging.

Summary

The Xiaomi 18 Pro Max combines the latest Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 processor, a Leica-based 200MP camera system, and an enormous 8,500mAh battery, positioning it as a strong contender in the premium smartphone market. Users can expect enhanced experience through the 2K LTPO AMOLED display and innovative dual-display design. It is set to be an attractive choice for users who prioritize high performance and advanced camera functionality.
