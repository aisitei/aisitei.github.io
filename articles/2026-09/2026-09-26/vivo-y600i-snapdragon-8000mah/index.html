Performance and Battery

The smartphone is powered by the Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 mobile platform and features an impressive 8000mAh battery capacity, supported by 44W fast charging (11V 4A).

Camera and Features

It is equipped with an 8MP front camera and a 50MP rear camera. Additionally, the device supports infrared remote control functionality.

Display and Design

The Y600i features a 6.87-inch LCD screen with a resolution of 1592×720. It offers a 120Hz refresh rate and a peak brightness of 1300 nits.

Summary

The vivo Y600i offers a combination of robust performance and extended usage time thanks to its Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 platform and massive 8000mAh battery. Key features include a 6.87-inch LCD display with a 120Hz refresh rate, a 50MP rear camera, and IP68/69 water and dust resistance rating.
