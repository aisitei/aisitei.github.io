イメージングモードの詳細解説

ソフトフォーカスモードは球面収差を維持することでハイライトのエッジが自然に滲む効果を生み出し、バブルボケは光学設計を活用してアウトフォーカスの光玉が明確なリング状の輪郭を持つように表現します。これら2つの効果は、レンズ固有の光学特性によるものであり、後処理でのデジタルシミュレーションではありません。

3つのモードはそれぞれ異なるフォーカスマークに対応しており、モードを切り替えることで実際の焦点距離が変化します。

  • SMOOTH ソフトフォーカスモード：人物や逆光のシーンに適しています。
  • PRIME 標準モード：一般的な50mmの焦点距離における自然な描写を提供します。
  • FOAMS バブルモード：夜景やボケを強調したいシーンで、アウトフォーカスの表現力を高めるために使用されます。

Key Features

This lens was previously unveiled at the CP+ exhibition in 2026. Its most notable feature is the inclusion of three built-in imaging modes. Users can switch between Soft Focus, Standard, and Bubble Bokeh effects by physically rotating a mode ring on the lens body, eliminating the need to change lenses or add filters. Furthermore, four official pattern inserts—Flower, Star, Arc Triangle, and Four-leaf Clover—are provided to further customize the shape of the out-of-focus bokeh.

Mount Compatibility

The lens is available in four mount versions: Sony E, Canon RF, Nikon Z, and Leica L. The focusing mechanism is purely manual focus, with no electronic contacts.

Imaging Mode Details

According to IT之家: The Soft Focus mode maintains spherical aberration, causing the edges of highlights to naturally bloom. Conversely, Bubble Bokeh utilizes optical design to ensure that out-of-focus bokeh exhibits distinct, ring-shaped edges. Both effects are derived from the lens's inherent optical characteristics and are not digital simulations applied in post-processing.

The three modes correspond to different focus markings, meaning switching modes actually changes the effective focal length. The SMOOTH Soft Focus mode is ideal for portraits and backlit scenes; the PRIME Standard mode provides natural imaging at a typical 50mm focal length; and the FOAMS Bubble mode enhances out-of-focus representation for nightscapes and bokeh photography.

Overall Review

The Brightin Star MF 50mm F2 TRI-SIGHT lens is a unique full-frame manual focus lens that allows users to switch between three imaging modes—Soft Focus, Standard, and Bubble Bokeh—without changing lenses. Featuring an optical structure of 6 groups in 7 elements and 11 aperture blades, it supports Sony E, Canon RF, Nikon Z, and Leica L mounts, offering flexible creative experiences for a wide range of camera users.
