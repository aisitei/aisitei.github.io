디스플레이

공식 발표된 정보에 따르면, iQOO 16은 세계 최초로 2K 165Hz Samsung 에베레스트 디스플레이를 탑재하며, Samsung 디스플레이 M16 발광 소재를 사용합니다. 이 디스플레이는 1100nits 수동 피크 밝기, 2800nits 전체 피크 밝기, 10000nits 부분 피크 밝기를 지원합니다. 또한 1.24배 P3 색역 커버리지를 지원하며, 개별 화면 색상 보정을 거쳤습니다. iQOO 16의 화면 밝기 균일성은 22% 향상되었고, 색상 균일성은 40% 향상되었으며, 자체 개발한 색상 밝기 보정 알고리즘을 탑재하여 서브 픽셀 단위 보정을 지원하고 ±0.001의 초고색도 정확도를 구현합니다.

Display

According to officially announced information, the iQOO 16 will feature the world's first 2K 165Hz Samsung Everest display, utilizing Samsung Display M16 emitting materials. This display supports a manual peak brightness of 1100nits, an overall peak brightness of 2800nits, and a partial peak brightness of 10000nits. Furthermore, it supports 1.24x P3 color gamut coverage and has undergone individual screen color calibration. The iQOO 16 boasts a 22% improvement in screen brightness uniformity and a 40% improvement in color uniformity. It also incorporates a self-developed color brightness correction algorithm that supports sub-pixel level correction, achieving ultra-high chromatic accuracy of ±0.001.

Performance

The iQOO 16 is set to be the first device to feature the Qualcomm 6th Gen Snapdragon 8 Super Extreme Edition processor, with the new product scheduled for announcement on September 29th.

Summary

The iQOO 16 is positioned as a flagship smartphone featuring a 6.85-inch 2K 165Hz Samsung M16 display and the Qualcomm 6th Gen Snapdragon 8 Super Extreme Edition processor. It is equipped with an enormous 8400mAh battery, supporting both 100W wired and 40W wireless charging, alongside a triple camera system headlined by a 50Mp main lens. Notably, the device emphasizes superior display performance, boasting excellent brightness uniformity and color accuracy.
