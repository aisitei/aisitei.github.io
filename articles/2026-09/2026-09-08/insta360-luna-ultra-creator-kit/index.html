设计与结构

首先从外观开始看起。Luna Ultra的前部配备了两个镜头，其设计让人联想到Wall-E。手持时能感受到相机的重量感，重达230g，比Pocket 3重了大约50g。

相机规格与性能

接下来我们谈谈相机本身。Luna Ultra的核心在于其双镜头设置。

  • 主摄像头：1英寸传感器，F1.8光圈，14档动态范围。支持8K 30fps视频拍摄。
  • 辅助摄像头：1/1.3英寸传感器，F2.0光圈，支持最高12倍数码变焦。

两个镜头都经过Leica色彩科学调校，相机还支持10bit I-Log录制。

那么实际拍摄的视频效果如何呢？

在白天，标准模式提供了平衡且可用的成像结果。三款Leica色彩配置文件为用户提供了多种选择以适应个人偏好。I-Log配置文件捕获了宽广的动态范围，这对创作者非常有用。

在低光环境下，大型1英寸传感器和F1.8光圈确实发挥了巨大的作用。与Pocket 3进行比较后，我认为其图像性能明显更优秀，后续评测中会深入探讨这一点。

实用功能

屏幕下方的两个按钮是可自定义的，方便快速访问特定功能。此外，相机还提供47GB的内置存储空间，非常实用；如果需要更多空间，可以使用microSD卡槽。

因此，这款可拆卸遥控器设计具有创新性，后续将进行更详细的测试。

Design and Build

Let's start with the exterior design. The Luna Ultra features two lenses on the front, giving it a look reminiscent of Wall-E. When held, the camera has a noticeable weight. At 230g, it is about 50g heavier than the Pocket 3. The screen is relatively large, and its shape feels comfortable in hand.

One of the most notable design features is the detachable screen. Unlike magnetic attachments, this uses a locking mechanism, which feels safer. The detached screen functions as a remote monitor and controller. We will explain how it operates in actual use later on.

Several accessories are included. There are two different bases, both featuring an integrated kickstand that allows the camera to be set up on flat surfaces. One base includes a useful 1000mAh additional battery. Additionally, there is a wide-angle lens attachment that expands the standard 20mm focal length down to 15mm. The package also includes Insta360's new E-ink microphone. While the protective case included in the box serves its purpose, it appears somewhat bulky.

Camera Specifications and Performance

Now let’s talk about the camera itself. The core feature of the Luna Ultra is its dual-lens setup.

  • Main Camera: 1-inch sensor, F1.8 aperture, 14-stop dynamic range. Capable of shooting 8K video at 30fps.
  • Auxiliary Camera: 1/1.3-inch sensor, F2.0 aperture, supports up to 12x digital zoom.

Both lenses are tuned with Leica color science, and the camera supports 10-bit I-Log recording.

So, what will actual footage look like? In daylight, the standard mode provides balanced and usable results. Three Leica color profiles offer several options depending on preference. The I-Log profile is particularly useful as it captures a wide dynamic range for us. In low light conditions, the large 1-inch sensor and F1.8 aperture are definitely helpful. When comparing this to the Pocket 3, we feel that the image performance is distinctly better, and we plan to dive deeper into this.

Practical Features

The detachable screen is perhaps another feature that deserves mention. As mentioned before, it attaches securely in place using a traditional locking mechanism. When detached, you can use the screen to compose shots, adjust settings, and control zoom. You can also record audio through the built-in microphone.

The two buttons located below the screen are customizable, which is great for quick access to specific functions. The camera also provides 47GB of internal storage, which is very useful, and a microSD slot is available if you need more space.

Overall, the detachable remote control is innovative, and we plan to test it in greater detail later.

Comparison with Pocket 4 Pro

Many people will naturally compare this product to the DJI Pocket 4 Pro. Since it has not been released yet, a direct comparison is impossible. What is clear, however, is that while both products feature dual lenses, the Luna Ultra includes this unique remote control.

So, who is the Insta360 Luna Ultra for? It’s for us—the creators. As long-time users of the DJI Pocket 3, we have been waiting for certain features that are desperately needed, such as better dynamic range, superior low-light performance, and flare control.
