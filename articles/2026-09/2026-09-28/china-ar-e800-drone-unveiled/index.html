개요

搭載されたコア技術

今回の飛行は、「航空工業計算所（Aviation Industry Computing Institute）」が提供する2つの国産主要部品によって支えられました。具体的には「飛行制御コンピューター」と「検知・回避システム」です。ドローンは、機動飛行、吊り下げての商品運搬、貨物コンテナの輸送など、複数のミッションを成功裏にこなしました。

Overview

CCTV's military channel reported that China's domestically developed 6-rotor drone, AR-E800, successfully completed its first public flight in Jingdi Town, Jiangxi Province.

Key Specifications and Capabilities

The AR-E800 is designed for various heavy-lifting and rescue scenarios. Its key specifications include:

  • Effective Payload Capacity: 320kg
  • Daily Transport Capacity (Single Drone): Up to 14 tons
  • High Altitude Capability: 4,800m
  • Applications: Suitable for various environments, including heavy object transport in complex terrain, short-distance high-efficiency material delivery, and aerial emergency rescue support.

Core Technology Support

The successful demonstration was supported by two key domestically produced components provided by the Aviation Industry Computing Institute (AICI): a flight control computer and a detection and avoidance system.

During the mission, the drone successfully executed multiple tasks, including maneuver flying, hanging cargo transport, and cargo bay transport. The operation demonstrated stable completion of heavy object missions, fully verifying the overall reliability and controllability in complex low-altitude environments.

The AICI components played crucial roles:

  • Flight Control Computer: Acts as the core for body control, completing attitude calculation and power distribution based on a redundant architecture. It executes active vibration cancellation control for swinging hanging loads to counteract disturbances caused by gusts and heavy objects.
  • Detection and Avoidance System: Integrates multi-source detection information to identify airspace obstacles. It transmits environmental data in real time to the flight control computer, supporting dynamic route replanning and autonomous avoidance, thereby mitigating risks during complex low-altitude operations.

Summary

China's domestically developed 6-rotor AR-E800 drone boasts an effective payload of 320kg and a high altitude capability of 4,800m, making it highly versatile for heavy object transport and emergency rescue missions. The system’s reliability in complex low-altitude environments was successfully proven by the integration of key components from the Aviation Industry Computing Institute, including its flight control computer and advanced detection/avoidance system.
