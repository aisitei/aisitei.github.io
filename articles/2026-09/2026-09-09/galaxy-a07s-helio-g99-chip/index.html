Overview

Samsung Electronics has launched a new entry-level smartphone based on its existing models, improving performance and strengthening software support. This product is aimed at users who desire long-term software support without paying a premium price.

Key Changes and Performance

The Galaxy A07s is positioned as an updated version of the Galaxy A07. The most significant change is the processor, which now uses the newly released MediaTek Helio G99+ instead of the previous G99. This processor supports faster LPDDR5X RAM (the previous model used LPDDR4X), offering a noticeable speed boost in general tasks like web browsing or social media while maintaining an affordable price point.

Display and Storage

  • Display: 6.7-inch PLS LCD, HD+ resolution, 90Hz refresh rate (Same as Galaxy A07)

  • Internal Storage: 64GB
  • Expandable Storage: microSD card slot (up to 2TB)

Camera

  • Rear Main Camera: 50MP sensor
  • Rear Auxiliary Camera: 2MP sensor
  • Front Camera: 8MP (For selfies and video calls)

Battery and Other Features

  • Battery: 5,000mAh (All-day use)
  • Charging: USB Type-C
  • Water/Dust Resistance: IP54 rating
  • Security: Side fingerprint sensor
  • Network: Dual SIM 4G support
  • Connectivity: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.3, GPS

Software Support

Samsung promises very robust software support for this price segment, offering 6 major OS upgrades and 6 years of security updates.

Pricing and Launch

The Galaxy A07s was launched in the Levant region, such as Jordan. It is available in Black, Light Violet, and Green colors, and is currently priced at JOD 119 (approximately $167 or ₹15,860). While higher memory options may be released in other markets, the initial launch begins with these accessible specifications.

Summary

The Galaxy A07s maintains Samsung's reliable entry-level formula but significantly boosts its future competitiveness through an upgraded MediaTek Helio G99+ processor and faster LPDDR5X RAM. It is designed for users seeking dependable performance, strong software support (6 major OS upgrades/6 years of security), and basic durability at a highly reasonable price point.
