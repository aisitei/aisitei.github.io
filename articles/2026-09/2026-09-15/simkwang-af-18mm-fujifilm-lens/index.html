조요 테크(Zhuoyao Tech)는 오늘 AF 18mm F2.2 LE APS-C 카메라 렌즈를 출시했습니다. Fujifilm X 마운트가 먼저 출시되며, 제품 페이지에 따르면 Sony E, Nikon Z, M43 마운트도 추후 출시될 예정입니다. 사전 판매 가격은 649위안입니다.

이 렌즈의 상세 스펙은 다음과 같습니다.

  • 렌즈 구성: 6군 8매 (ED 저분산 렌즈 1매, 고굴절률 렌즈 4매 포함)
  • 조리개 날개 수: 9매

Camera Lens

Zhuoyao Tech has announced the launch of its AF 18mm F2.2 LE APS-C camera lens. The lens initially launches for the Fujifilm X mount, with product pages indicating future releases for Sony E, Nikon Z, and M43 mounts. The pre-sale price is set at 649 CNY.

The lens features a metal body construction, weighing approximately 122g and measuring 29.5mm x 66mm. It boasts a minimum focusing distance of 0.18m. Optically, it consists of 6 groups and 8 elements, incorporating one ED low-dispersion lens and four high refractive index lenses. Furthermore, the design includes 9 aperture blades and an STM low-noise motor.

The detailed specifications for this lens are as follows:

  • Aperture Blades: 9 blades
  • Minimum Focusing Distance: 0.18m
  • Filter Diameter: 49mm
  • Weight: Approx. 122g
  • Dimensions: 29.5mm x 66mm
  • Mount: Fujifilm X (Sony E, Nikon Z, and M43 expected in future releases)
  • Motor: STM low-noise motor

Overview

The AF 18mm F2.2 LE APS-C lens from Zhuoyao Tech is characterized by its lightweight metal body and STM low-noise motor.
