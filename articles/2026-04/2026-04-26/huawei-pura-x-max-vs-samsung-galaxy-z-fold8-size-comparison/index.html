Size Comparison of Huawei Pura X Max and Galaxy Z Fold8 Wide

According to IT House, information source @i冰宇宙 shared a comparative analysis of the dimensions of two foldable smartphones, the Huawei Pura X Max and Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Wide, on Weibo on April 24.

Key Specifications and Design Comparison

The data indicates that both models exhibit different dimensions depending on whether they are in an unfolded or folded state. Notably, there are distinct differences in screen ratios and overall body width between the two devices.

  • Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Wide
    • Unfolded size: 123.9 x 161.4 x 4.3mm
    • Folded size: 123.9 x 82.2 x 9.8mm
    • Screen ratios: Inner screen 4:3, Outer screen 4.7:3
  • Huawei Pura X Max
    • Unfolded size: 120 x 166.5 x 5.2mm
    • Folded size: 120 x 85 x 11.2mm

Detailed Dimensions Analysis

IT House provided a detailed breakdown of the dimensions for both models based on official data.

  • Huawei Pura X Max
    • Horizontal: 166.5mm, Vertical: 120.0mm
    • Unfolded thickness: 5.2mm, Folded thickness: 11.2mm
    • Folded body width: 85mm
    • Outer screen ratio: 4.4:3, Inner screen ratio: 4.24:3
  • Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Wide
    • Horizontal: 161.4mm, Vertical: 123.9mm
    • Unfolded thickness: 4.3mm, Folded thickness: 9.8mm
    • Folded body width: 82.2mm
    • Outer screen ratio: 4.7:3, Inner screen ratio: 4:3

Overall Review

This comparison highlights the clear differences in physical size and design between these two flagship foldables. While the Huawei Pura X Max features a larger horizontal length and a thicker folded profile, the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Wide is characterized by its slimmer build and different outer screen ratio. These specifications allow users to choose their preferred model based on their priorities regarding portability and ease of use.

Summary

This analysis compares the physical dimensions and screen ratios of the Huawei Pura X Max and Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Wide. The Huawei model offers a larger horizontal footprint, while the Samsung device provides a slimmer profile with different aspect ratios. These differences provide potential users with clear options depending on whether they prioritize size or portability.
