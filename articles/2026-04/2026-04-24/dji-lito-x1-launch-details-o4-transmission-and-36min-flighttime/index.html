Design and Hardware

Both models feature a lightweight body weighing under 249g for excellent portability. The drone arms are foldable, allowing them to be easily stored in backpacks or small pouches. They boast a maximum wind resistance of 10.7m/s (Level 5).

Both drones are equipped with DJI O4 transmission technology, capable of transmitting high-quality 1080p/60fps video. An optional 4G augmented transmission module can be added if required.

Flight Performance and Safety Features

Both drones can take off quickly upon powering on without waiting for satellite signals. Thanks to powerful vision positioning, they can remember flight paths in bright environments and safely return to their original routes even in the absence of a satellite signal.

  • Battery: A standard smart flight battery provides 36 minutes of flight time, while an additional long-flight battery allows for up to 52 minutes of continuous flight.

The primary differences in safety features between the two models are as follows:

  • Lito 1: Features omnidirectional environmental sensing, which intelligently stops or maneuvers around obstacles on the flight path to provide users with more maneuvering space.
  • Lito X1: Includes an integrated omnidirectional vision detection system and supports front/rear infrared detection to avoid obstacles such as cliffs or walls. Additionally, it features a front visual radar to enhance obstacle detection accuracy.

Camera and Shooting Specifications

The Lito X1 offers more advanced capabilities in terms of video recording performance.

  • Sensor: Equipped with a 1/1.3-inch image sensor.
  • Lens: f/1.7 aperture.
  • Video Recording: Supports up to 8K resolution photos, 4K/60fps HDR video (up to 14 stops of dynamic range), 4K/100fps slow-motion video for dynamic scenes, and supports 10-Bit D-Log M format.
  • Special Modes: Provides a 2.7K vertical shooting mode optimized for mobile phone screens.
  • Video Recording (Lito 1): Capable of recording up to 8K resolution photos and 4K/60fps video.

Furthermore, the Lito X1 body includes 42GB of built-in storage for rapid content saving.

Connectivity and Software

Both drones support high-speed Wi-Fi 6 transmission, eliminating the need to carry extra equipment. Users can activate devices and download data via the DJI Fly App within Bluetooth range, featuring fast transfer speeds of up to 50MB/s.

In terms of software, both the Lito X1 and Lito 1 support smart tracking features for various filming scenarios, with a maximum tracking speed of 12m/h. They also offer several cinematic modes, including one-touch clip creation, Master Shots, Timelapse photos, and Panorama photos.

Design and Hardware

Both models feature a lightweight body weighing less than 249g, offering excellent portability. The foldable arms allow for easy storage in backpacks or small pouches. Both drones can withstand wind speeds of up to 10.7m/s (Level 5).

Equipped with DJI O4 transmission technology, both models can transmit high-quality 1080p/60fps video, with an optional 4G enhanced transmission module available.

Flight Performance and Safety Features

Both drones support rapid takeoff immediately after powering on, eliminating the need to wait for satellite signals. Thanks to powerful vision positioning capabilities, they can remember flight paths in bright environments and return safely even when a satellite signal is unavailable.

  • Battery: A standard smart flight battery providing 36 minutes of flight time is included; an optional long-flight battery allows for up to 52 minutes of continuous flight.

The primary differences in safety features between the two models are as follows:

  • Lito 1: Features omnidirectional environmental sensing, allowing it to intelligently stop or detour when encountering obstacles on its path, providing a more spacious operating area for users.
  • Lito X1: Includes an omnidirectional vision sensing system and supports front/rear infrared detection to avoid obstacles such as cliffs or walls. It also features a front visual radar to increase obstacle detection accuracy.

Camera and Shooting Specifications

The Lito X1 offers more advanced capabilities in terms of video recording performance.

  • Sensor: Equipped with a 1/1.3-inch image sensor
  • Lens: f/1.7 aperture
  • Video Recording (Lito X1): Supports up to 8K resolution photos, 4K/60fps HDR video (up to 14 stops of dynamic range), 4K/100fps slow-motion video for dynamic scenes, and supports 10-Bit D-Log M format.
  • Special Modes: Provides a 2.7K vertical shooting mode optimized for mobile phone screens.
  • Video Recording (Lito 1): Capable of recording up to 8K resolution photos and 4K/60fps video.

Additionally, the Lito X1 features 42GB of built-in storage for rapid content saving.

Connectivity and Software

Both drones support Wi-Fi 6 high-speed transmission, removing the need to carry extra equipment. Users can activate devices within Bluetooth range via the DJI Fly App and download data at speeds of up to 50MB/s.

In terms of software, both the Lito X1 and Lito 1 support smart tracking features for various shooting scenarios with a maximum tracking speed of 12m/s. They also offer several cinematic modes, including one-touch clip creation, Master Shots, TimeLapse photos, and Panorama photos.
