주요 재무 지표 (2026년 1월 1일 ~ 3월 31일)

당사의 통합 영업 실적에 따르면, 캐논은 매출액 증가와 순이익 감소를 동시에 기록했습니다. 주요 수치는 다음과 같습니다.

  • 총매출액: 1조 900억 엔 (전년 대비 3.3% 증가)
  • 영업 이익: 7,137억 엔 (전년 대비 26.1% 감소)
  • 순이익(모회사 소속): 4,830억 3천만 엔 (전년 대비 33.1% 감소)
  • 기본 주당 순이익(EPS): 55.20 엔/주 (전년 대비 28.6% 증가)
  • 희석 주당 순이익(EPS): 55.16 엔/주 (전년 대비 28.6% 증가)

또한, 보고 기간 말 기준 총자산은 6조 2,400억 엔으로 전년 말 대비 1.7% 증가했으며, 주주 자본금 역시 3조 4,300억 엔으로 1.7% 성장했습니다.

  • 예상 총매출액: 4조 7,700억 엔 (전년 대비 3.0% 증가)

총평

Canon Q1 FY2026 Financial Results

According to IT House, Canon has released its financial report for the first quarter of the 2026 fiscal year. The report summarizes the company's overall financial performance, highlighting several key figures.

Key Financial Indicators (January 1 – March 31)

Based on consolidated operating results, Canon recorded a simultaneous increase in revenue and a decrease in net profit. The primary metrics are as follows:

  • Total Revenue: 1.09 trillion yen (up 3.3% year-on-year)
  • Operating Profit: 713.7 billion yen (down 26.1% year-on-year)
  • Net Income (Parent): 483.03 billion yen (down 33.1% year-on-year)
  • Basic Earnings Per Share (EPS): 55.20 JPY (up 28.6% year-on-year)
  • Diluted Earnings Per Share (EPS): 55.16 JPY (up 28.6% year-on-year)

Additionally, as of the end of the reporting period, total assets reached 6.24 trillion yen, a 1.7% increase from the previous year's end, while shareholders' equity also grew by 1.7% to 3.43 trillion yen.

FY2026 Annual Performance Forecast

Canon has presented the following projections for the full fiscal year ending in December 2026:

  • Projected Total Revenue: 4.77 trillion yen (up 3.0% year-on-year)
  • Projected Operating Profit: 456 billion yen (up 0.1% year-on-year)
  • Projected Net Income (Parent): 333 billion yen (up 0.3% year-on-year)
  • Projected Basic Earnings Per Share (EPS): 388.42 JPY

General Overview

During the first quarter of fiscal year 2026, Canon demonstrated robust revenue growth; however, operating profit and net income saw a decline compared to the previous year. Despite this, the significant jump in both basic and diluted Earnings Per Share (EPS) provides a positive signal for investors. The company's annual forecast suggests expectations for stable operations with modest growth in revenue and net profits.

Summary

Canon reported steady revenue growth in Q1 FY2026, though operating and net profits decreased compared to the previous year. However, a substantial increase in Earnings Per Share (EPS) serves as a positive indicator for investors. The company projects stable performance with modest growth across key financial metrics for the remainder of the fiscal year.
