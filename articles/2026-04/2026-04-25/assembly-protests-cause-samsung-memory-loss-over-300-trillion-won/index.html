노사 갈등 현황 및 파업 경고

IT之家에 따르면, 《서울경제일보》의 보도에 의하면 삼성전자 노동조합원 약 4만 명이 어제 열린 '총회'에 참석했습니다. 삼성전자 노동조합 '연합투쟁본부'가 공개한 바에 따르면, 이번 집회 기간 동안 회사 반도체 생산량이 하루 만에 18% 이상 감소하는 등 심각한 상황이 발생했습니다.

노동조합은 이를 통해 경영진에게 압력을 가하며, 회사가 영업이익의 15%를 성과급으로 분배하자는 제안을 받아들이지 않을 경우, 다음 달에 진행할 예정인 18일간의 총파업을 통해 생산에 막대한 타격을 줄 것이라고 경고했습니다.

생산 영향 분석

상세한 수치로 보면, 저장 칩 공장의 야간 생산량은 18.4% 감소했으며, 화성 캠퍼스 내 저장 라인 중 15호선이 33.1%, 16호선이 11.3%, 17호선이 13.1% 하락했습니다.

  • 평택 캠퍼스 생산 라인 감소 현황:
  • P1D: 23.1% 감소, P1F: 10.0% 감소, P2D: 24.6% 감소, P2F: 3.2% 감소, P3D: 11.0% 감소

특히 웨이퍼 파운드리 공장의 생산량 하락 폭은 더욱 두드러져 전체적으로 58.1%가 감소했습니다. 이 중 기흥 S1 라인은 74.3%, 화성 S3 라인은 67.8%, 평택 S5 라인은 42.7%나 줄었습니다.

노동조합은 파운드리 공정이 저장 시설보다 자동화 수준이 낮아 인력 의존도가 높기 때문에, 생산 손실 규모가 훨씬 크다고 지적했습니다.

주요 쟁점 및 요구 사항

노동조합은 과거에 총파업 기간 동안 회사의 생산 손실액이 30조 원을 초과할 수 있다고 예측한 바 있습니다. 만약 경영진의 요구가 받아들여지지 않을 경우, 노동조합은 5월 21일부터 6월 7일까지 18일간의 총파업을 계획하고 있으며, 심지어 화학 물질 누출 방지 '안전 보호 시설' 운영 인력까지 동원하여 생산에 최대 영향을 미치겠다고 밝혔습니다.

요구 사항 비교

노동조합 요구:

  • 올해 영업이익의 15%를 성과급으로 지급할 것.
  • 성과급 상한선을 폐지할 것.

노동조합 측은 삼성전자의 올해 예상 영업이익이 약 300조 원(환율 기준)에 달하므로, 요구대로라면 약 45조 원의 성과급 지급이 필요하다고 주장했습니다.

현재 노동조합은 성과급 상한선이 연봉의 50%로 설정되어 있는 것에 반발하고 있으며, 다른 한국 대기업인 SK하이닉스는 이미 이 상한선을 폐지하는 데 동의했다는 점을 근거로 들었습니다.

경영진 제안:

  • 영업이익의 10%를 성과급으로 지급할 것.
  • 추가 자금을 배정하여 메모리 부문 직원의 올해 성과급이 경쟁사보다 높도록 보장할 것.

한편, 노동조합원 수는 현재 9만 명을 넘어 삼성전자 한국 직원 총원의 70% 이상을 차지하고 있으며, 전국 삼성전자 지회 책임자가 다음 달에 집회를 열 것이라고 전해졌습니다.

총평

노동조합이 생산량 감소 수치와 파업 계획을 근거로 강력히 압박하는 가운데, 경영진은 일부 자금 지원 및 경쟁사 대비 우위를 점하겠다는 방식으로 대응하며 협상 국면을 이어가고 있어 향후 상황에 귀추가 주목됩니다.

工会集会导致产量大幅下降

约 4 万名 Samsung 电子工会成员参加了“总集会”，导致公司半导体产量在单日内下降逾 18%。工会通过此举向管理层施压，要求将营业利润的 15% 分配为绩效奖金并取消奖励上限。若管理层不予接受，工会将于下月启动为期 18 天的总罢工。

具体产线产量变动情况

受集会影响，不同厂区的生产受到显著冲击：

  • 存储芯片工厂夜班产量下降 18.4%
  • 华城园区：第 15 号线下降 33.1%，第 16 号线下降 11.3%，第 17 号线下降 13.1%
  • 平泽园区：P1D 下降 23.1%，P1F 下降 10.0%，P2D 下降 24.6%，P2F 下降 3.2%，P3D 下降 11.0%
  • 晶圆代工厂（整体下降 58.1%）：器兴 S1 线下降 74.3%，华城 S3 线下降 67.8%，平责 S5 线下降 42.7%

双方诉求与财务背景

Samsung 电子工会要求将今年约 30 万亿韩元的营业利润中的 15%（约 4.5 万亿韩元）作为绩效奖金发放，并取消目前设定的“年薪 50%”的奖励上限。值得注意的是，竞争对手 SK Hynix 已同意取消该上限。

Samsung 管理层则提议将营业利润的 10% 用于绩效奖金，并额外拨出资金以确保存储部门员工的奖金高于竞争对手。目前工会成员人数超过 9 万人，占 Samsung 韩国员工总数的 70% 以上。

总结

由于 Samsung 电子工会要求提高奖金比例并取消奖励上限，近期举行的集会已导致半导体产量显著下降。若管理层未能与工会达成一致，公司可能面临为期 18 天的总罢工及巨大的生产损失。

Labor Conflict and Strike Warning

According to reports from IT Home and the Seoul Economic Daily, approximately 40,000 Samsung Electronics union members attended a general meeting held yesterday. The "United Struggle Headquarters" of the Samsung Electronics labor union revealed that during this period of assembly, semiconductor production fell by more than 18% in a single day, creating a critical situation for the company.

The union is using this as leverage to pressure management into accepting a proposal to distribute 15% of operating profits as performance bonuses. They warned that if the demand is not met, an upcoming 18-day general strike next month will cause significant damage to production.

Production Impact Analysis

Detailed figures indicate that night production at storage chip factories decreased by 18.4%. Within the Hwaseong campus, specific lines saw the following declines:

  • Line 15: 33.1%
  • Line 16: 11.3%
  • Line 17: 13.1%

The reduction in production lines at the Pyeongtaek campus is as follows:

  • P1D: 23.1% decrease
  • P1F: 10.0% decrease
  • P2D: 24.6% decrease
  • P2F: 3.2% decrease
  • P3D: 11.0% decrease

The decline in production at wafer foundry plants was even more pronounced, with an overall drop of 58.1%. Specific notable decreases include:

  • Giheung S1: 74.3%
  • Hwaseong S3: 67.8%
  • Pyeongtaek S5: 42.7%

The union pointed out that because foundry processes have lower levels of automation compared to storage facilities, they are more labor-dependent, resulting in much larger scales of production loss during strikes.

Key Issues and Demands

The labor union has previously estimated that production losses could exceed 30 trillion KRW during a general strike. If management's demands are not met, the union plans an 18-day general strike from May 21 to June 7. They even stated they would mobilize personnel in "safety protection facilities" for chemical leak prevention to maximize the impact on production.

Comparison of Demands

Union Demands:

  • Distribute 15% of this year's operating profit as performance bonuses.
  • Abolish the cap on performance bonuses.

The union claims that since Samsung Electronics' expected operating profit for this year is approximately 300 trillion KRW (based on exchange rates), their demand would require a payout of about 45 trillion KRW. They are currently protesting the existing cap, which limits bonuses to 50% of annual salary, citing that another major Korean corporation, SK Hynix, has already agreed to remove this cap.

Management Offer:

  • Distribute 10% of operating profit as performance bonuses.
  • Allocate additional funds to ensure that the performance bonuses for employees in the memory division are higher than those of competitors.

Currently, the number of union members exceeds 90,000, accounting for more than 70% of Samsung Electronics' total workforce in Korea. It has been reported that local leaders of various Samsung Electronics branches will hold a rally next month.

Summary

Samsung Electronics is facing a sharp labor dispute centered on the percentage of operating profits to be distributed as performance bonuses and the removal of current bonus caps. The union is leveraging significant production loss data from recent rallies to pressure management into a 15% payout, while management has countered with a 10% offer plus specific incentives for the memory division. The situation remains tense as both sides remain firm in their respective positions ahead of a planned 18-day strike.

The decline in production at wafer foundry plants was even more pronounced, with an overall drop of 58.1%. Specific notable decreases include:

  • Giheung S1: 74.3%
  • Hwaseong S3: 67.8%
  • Pyeongtaek S5: 42.7%

The labor union pointed out that because foundry processes have a lower level of automation and higher reliance on manpower compared to storage facilities, the scale of production loss in those areas is significantly larger.

Comparison of Demands

Labor Union Demands:

  • Distribute 15% of this year's operating profits as performance bonuses.
  • Abolish the current reward cap (currently set at 50% of annual salary).

The union argues that since Samsung Electronics' expected operating profit for this year is approximately 300 trillion KRW, their demand would require a payout of roughly 45 trillion KRW. They cited the fact that competitor SK Hynix has already agreed to abolish the reward cap as a primary reason for their stance.

Management Proposal:

  • Distribute 10% of operating profits as performance bonuses.
  • Allocate additional funds to ensure that rewards for memory division employees are higher than those offered by competitors.

Currently, the number of union members exceeds 90,000, accounting for more than 70% of Samsung Electronics' total workforce in Korea. Leaders from various regional branches are scheduled to hold further meetings next month.

Summary

Samsung Electronics is facing a tense labor dispute over performance bonus percentages and the removal of reward caps. The union is leveraging significant production losses in semiconductor and foundry lines to pressure management into a 15% profit distribution, while management has countered with a 10% offer and additional incentives to remain competitive against SK Hynix.
