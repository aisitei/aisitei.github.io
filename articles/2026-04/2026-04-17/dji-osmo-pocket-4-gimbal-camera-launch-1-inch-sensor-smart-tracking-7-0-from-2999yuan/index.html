Product Overview and Pricing

DJI has officially launched the Osmo Pocket 4, a new 1-inch pocket gimbal camera. This model features an upgraded 1-inch CMOS sensor, supporting a wide 14-stop dynamic range and professional 10-bit D-Log color modes. The price starts from 2,999 RMB.

Osmo Pocket 4 Configuration Options

There are two packages available depending on the user's needs:

  • Osmo Pocket 4 Standard Set: 2,999 RMB. Includes the Osmo Pocket 4 body, gimbal clip, 1/4-inch screw handle, carrying case, and a USB-C to USB-C PD fast charging cable (USB 3.1).
  • Osmo Pocket 4 All-around Set: 3,799 RMB. In addition to the Osmo Pocket 4 body, this set includes a DJI Mic 3 transmitter, auxiliary light, expansion lens, mini tripod, carrying case, gimbal clip, 1/4-inch screw handle, and a USB-C to USB-C PD fast charging cable (USB 3.1).

Camera and Video Specifications

The Osmo Pocket 4 has been upgraded in several areas to accommodate professional video production environments.

  • Sensor and Image Quality: The combination of a new 1-inch CMOS sensor and an f/2.0 large aperture expands the dynamic range to 14 stops and supports 10-bit D-Log color mode.
  • Video Recording Specs: Slow-motion recording has been upgraded to 4K/240fps, and features such as spatial audio recording and zoom mic pickup have been added.
  • Audio System: The device supports direct connection with DJI Mic transmitters via OsmoAudio, enabling up to 4 channels of audio recording.

Key Features and User Experience

In addition to camera performance, various features have been included to improve user convenience.

  • Smart Tracking: Equipped with Smart Tracking 7.0, it supports smart tracking for subjects at distances up to four times the original range.
  • Auto-Focus System: The upgraded Smart Focus system includes "Subject Lock Track" and "Registered Subject Priority" modes.
  • Gesture Control: Users can start or stop tracking and begin or end recording using simple gestures without touching the screen.

Portrait Shooting and Accessories

Features and accessories optimized for portrait filming have been added.

  • Improved Skin Representation: Fine-tuned to capture skin tones, providing a clear and natural look under various environments and lighting conditions.
  • Beauty Filters: Built-in beauty filters allow users to customize skin correction effects, whitening levels, and the warmth/coolness of skin tones to easily optimize portrait results.
  • External Lighting: Compatible with small portable external auxiliary lights, supporting 3-step color temperature and brightness adjustments to illuminate a subject's face with one touch.

Warranty and Service

DJI has officially launched the DJI Care "Suixinhuan" (随心换) warranty program specifically for the Osmo Pocket 4.

  • 1-Year Version: 219 RMB. Provides two low-cost replacement rights within one year.
  • 2-Year Version: 349

Summary

The DJI Osmo Pocket 4 features a new 1-inch CMOS sensor, expanded dynamic range, and advanced capabilities like Smart Tracking 7.0 and enhanced portrait modes. It offers multiple bundle options including professional audio and lighting accessories, supported by a dedicated DJI Care warranty program.
