According to IT Home (IT之家), blogger @数码闲聊站 has compiled the detailed specifications for the upcoming OPPO Find X9s Pro, which is scheduled to be unveiled on April 21.

Key Specifications of the OPPO Find X9s Pro

The primary specifications reported by IT Home are as follows:

  • Display: 6.32-inch, 1.5K+, 144Hz LTPS, Tianma U9 Pro, bar-type display
  • Camera: Front 32MP; Rear setup includes a 200MP HPE 1/1.4" main camera, 50MP ultra-wide lens, 200MP HP5 1/1.56" F2.6 2.8x periscope telephoto lens, and a 3.2MP multi-spectrum lens with 253mm zoom
  • Processor: Dimensity 9500
  • Battery and Charging: 7025mAh battery, 80W wired charging, 50W wireless charging
  • Other Features: 3D ultrasonic fingerprint recognition, IP68/69 rating, 8.4mm thickness, and a weight of 198g

Launch Information

The OPPO Find X9s Pro is scheduled to be revealed on April 21 at 7:00 PM. In addition to the Find X9s Pro and Find X9 Ultra smartphones, other products such as the OPPO Enco Clip2 ear-clip earphones will also be unveiled. Interested users should follow IT Home for further updates.

Summary

The upcoming OPPO Find X9s Pro is expected to feature a high-performance Dimensity 9500 processor and a massive 7025mAh battery. It will debut on April 21 alongside the Find X9 Ultra and new audio accessories like the Enco Clip2.
