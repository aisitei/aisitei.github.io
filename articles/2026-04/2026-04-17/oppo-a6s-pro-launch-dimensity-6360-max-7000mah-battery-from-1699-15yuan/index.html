Design and Durability

The OPPO A6s Pro is available in three color options and features a 6.57-inch bar-type display. The device weighs 194g with a thickness of 8.29mm. It boasts high durability, featuring an IP69K rating for dust and water resistance.

Performance and Battery

The new model is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 6360 MAX processor. This chipset consists of two large cores and six small cores, reaching a maximum frequency of 2.4GHz.

  • Memory and Storage: It combines LPDDR4X memory with UFS 2.2 storage for fast data processing and supports expansion via external memory cards.
  • Battery and Charging: The device features a high-capacity 7,000mAh battery and supports 80W ultra-fast charging, allowing users to use the device without worrying about power.

Camera and Display

The camera system provides a diverse shooting experience for users. The rear features a 50MP wide main camera paired with a 2MP monochrome camera, while the front is equipped with a 16MP sensor.

The display is a 6.57-inch AMOLED panel with a resolution of 2372x1080. It supports smart variable refresh rates of 60Hz/90Hz/120Hz to balance power efficiency with smooth screen transitions. Additionally, an in-display fingerprint sensor is integrated for convenient unlocking.

Overall Review

The OPPO A6s Pro stands out by combining a massive 7,000mAh battery with 80W ultra-fast charging to provide extensive usage time. With solid specifications such as the 6.57-inch AMOLED display and a 50MP main camera, combined with an IP69K rating for maximum durability in daily life, it is positioned as a high-value model.

Summary

The OPPO A6s Pro offers a compelling combination of a large 7,000mAh battery, 80W fast charging, and an IP69K-rated durable build. Featuring a high-quality AMOLED display and reliable camera specs, it serves as a highly durable and cost-effective option for users seeking long battery life.
