vivo Y600 Pro 출시 및 기본 정보

디자인 및 디스플레이

본 제품은 전면에 6.83인치 AMOLED 스크린을 탑재했으며, 해상도는 2800*1260입니다. 최대 주사율은 120Hz까지 지원하며, 화면 비율은 20:9입니다. 이 디스플레이는 HDR, 10bit 색 깊이, P3 광색역 등 다양한 기술을 지원하고, 명암비는 8000000:1에 달합니다.

외관 디자인 측면에서는 하월 블랙, 부광 골드, 성요 자줏, 호묘 블루 네 가지 색상을 제공하며, 미세한 무광(micro-satin) 가공 처리가 적용되었고 둥근 R 각을 채택했습니다. 후면에는 항공 알루미늄 소재를 사용하고 고급 시계 제작 공법이 적용된 "부광 월광" 카메라 링이 특징입니다.

성능 및 배터리

가장 큰 장점은 저온에 강한 10200mAh 초대용량 배터리가 탑재되었다는 점입니다. 일반 사용 환경에서는 24시간 이상 연속 사용이 가능하며, 고부하 작업 환경에서도 12시간 이상의 긴 사용 시간을 보장합니다. 이 배터리는 -20°C~40°C의 넓은 온도 범위에서 안정적으로 작동하며, 특히 -20°C 극저온 환경에서도 39시간 동안 연속 통화가 가능합니다.

충전 성능 면에서는 90W 유선 고속충전을 지원합니다. 프로세서는 MediaTek Dimensity 7300e 칩을 탑재했으며, 이는 8코어 구성에 6nm 공정 설계를 채택했습니다. 메모리는 LPDDR5 더블 채널, 저장 공간은 UFS 3.1 규격을 사용했습니다.

카메라 및 기타 기능

  • 후면 카메라: 5000만 화소 메인 카메라를 장착했으며, 10배 디지털 줌과 1080P/720P 동영상 녹화를 지원합니다.
  • 전면 카메라: 3200만 화소를 탑재하고 있으며, FF(Fixed Focus) 자동 초점을 지원합니다.

또한 이 모델은 IP68+IP69 등급의 강력한 방수방진 기능을 지원하며, 젖은 손에서도 터치가 가능한 기능도 갖추고 있습니다. 통신 강화 칩 E1을 탑재했으며, 공장 출하 시 OriginOS 6 운영체제가 기본 설치되어 있고, 모든 기능을 갖춘 NFC와 적외선 리모컨 기능까지 제공합니다. 내부에는 VC 액체 냉각 방열 시스템과 스테레오 더블 스피커 등 다양한 주변 사양이 적용되었습니다.

총평

vivo Y600 Pro는 10200mAh의 초대용량 배터리와 극저온 환경에서의 뛰어난 내구성이 가장 큰 강점입니다. MediaTek Dimensity 7300e 칩과 LPDDR5, UFS 3.1 조합으로 준수한 성능을 제공하며, IP68+IP69 등급의 강력한 방수방진 기능까지 갖춘 만능형 스마트폰입니다. 전반적으로 배터리 사용 시간과 내구성을 최우선으로 고려하는 사용자에게 적합한 플래그십 모델로 평가됩니다.

Design and Display

The device is equipped with a 6.83-inch AMOLED screen featuring a resolution of 2800x1260. It supports a maximum refresh rate of 120Hz and has an aspect ratio of 20:9. The display supports various technologies including HDR, 10-bit color depth, P3 wide color gamut, and offers a contrast ratio of 8,000,000:1.

In terms of exterior design, the device is offered in four colors (Haoyue Black, Fuguang Gold, Xingyao Purple, and Haomiao Blue) with a micro-satin finish and rounded R corners. The rear utilizes aerospace aluminum materials and features an "Fuguang Moonlight" camera ring crafted using high-end watchmaking techniques.

Performance and Battery

The standout feature is the 10200mAh ultra-large battery, which is highly resistant to low temperatures. It provides over 24 hours of continuous use in standard environments and more than 12 hours under heavy workloads. The battery operates reliably across a wide temperature range of -20°C to 40°C, specifically supporting up to 39 hours of continuous talk time in extreme cold (-20°C).

Regarding charging performance, it supports 90W wired fast charging. The device is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 7300e chip (8-core configuration, 6nm process), paired with LPDDR5 dual-channel memory and UFS 3.1 storage.

Camera and Other Features

  • Rear Camera: Equipped with a 50MP main camera supporting 10x digital zoom and 1080P/720P video recording.
  • Front Camera: Features a 32MP sensor with FF (Fixed Focus) support.

Additionally, the model supports IP68+IP69 ratings for water and dust resistance and features wet-hand touch functionality. It includes the E1 communication chip, comes pre-installed with OriginOS 6, and provides full NFC and infrared remote control functions. Internal hardware includes a VC liquid cooling system and stereo dual speakers.

Summary

The vivo Y600 Pro is characterized by its massive 10200mAh battery and exceptional durability in extreme temperature environments. Combining reliable performance with high-end features like IP68+IP69 ratings, it is an ideal choice for users who prioritize long battery life and ruggedness.

It is available in four color options: Haoyue Black, Fuguang Gold, Xingyao Purple, and Haomiao Blue, with a starting price of 1999 RMB.

Pricing and Model Specifications

According to IT Home, the pricing for the various configurations is as follows:

  • 8GB+128GB: Retail price 1999 RMB (1699 RMB after national subsidies)
  • 8GB+256GB: Retail price 2299 RMB (1954 RMB after national subsidies)
  • 8GB+512GB/12GB+256GB: Retail price 2599 RMB (2209 RMB after national subsidies)
  • 12GB+512GB: Retail price 2899 RMB (2464 RMB after national subsidies)

Design and Display

The device features a 6.83-inch AMOLED screen with a resolution of 2800x1260. It supports a maximum refresh rate of 120Hz and a 20:9 aspect ratio. The display is equipped with advanced technologies including HDR, 10-bit color depth, and P3 wide color gamut, achieving a contrast ratio of 8,000,000:1.

In terms of aesthetics, the Y600 Pro features a micro-satin finish and rounded corners. The back panel utilizes aviation-grade aluminum materials and features a "Fuguang" camera ring crafted with high-end watchmaking techniques.

Performance and Battery

The standout feature of the Y600 Pro is its 10200mAh ultra-large battery, which remains stable across a wide temperature range from -20°C to 40°C. Under normal usage, the device can last over 24 hours continuously; under heavy workloads, it provides more than 12 hours of use. Notably, even in extreme cold (-20°C), the phone can support up to 39 hours of continuous talk time.

The device supports 90W wired fast charging and is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 7300e chip (8-core, 6nm process). It features LPDDR5 dual-channel memory and UFS 3.1 storage.

Camera and Additional Features

  • Rear Camera: 50MP main camera supporting 10x digital zoom and 1080P/720P video recording.
  • Front Camera: 32MP sensor with FF (Fixed Focus) support.

The Y600 Pro is built for durability with IP68+IP69 water and dust resistance, including a feature that allows touch operation even with wet hands. It includes an E1 communication enhancement chip, comes pre-installed with OriginOS 6, and features full-function NFC and infrared remote control. Internal hardware includes a VC liquid cooling system and stereo dual speakers.

Summary

The vivo Y600 Pro is positioned as a high-durability smartphone featuring a massive 10200mAh battery capable of performing in extreme low-temperature environments. It combines solid mid-range performance via the MediaTek Dimensity 7300e chip with premium features like IP68+IP69 protection and a high-quality AMOLED display, making it an ideal choice for users prioritizing endurance and reliability.
