Xingsi Semiconductor는 광대역 위성통신과 셀룰러 통신 기술을 심층 융합하고, 맞춤형 소프트웨어/하드웨어 구조와 전용 알고리즘을 통해 단일 ASIC 하드웨어 회로로 위성과 셀룰러의 두 가지 통신 모드를 처리하며 핵심 계산 자원을 공유합니다. 이를 통해 칩 소비 전력을 최적화하고 생산 비용을 절감하며, 다양한 방식의 통신 기술을 효율적으로 호환시켰습니다.

Industry Trends and Company Overview

As the dense network phase for Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite constellations approaches in 2026, the space-ground integrated communication industry is moving beyond technical verification toward full commercialization. In the 6G ground-satellite fusion structure, satellite baseband chips that comply with the 3GPP 5G NR NTN standard are essential components for implementing ground-to-space communication and represent a core direction for innovation in the telecommunications industry. Xingsi Semiconductor is an outstanding company supporting the standardization and scaling of the domestic satellite internet industry through proactive strategic positioning and proprietary core technologies.

Strategic Positioning and Technical Support

Based on national strategies for commercial space and 6G development, Xingsi Semiconductor is precisely targeting the space-ground integration sector. The core team possesses an average of over 10 years of R&D experience in mobile communications and has a proven track record of successful tape-outs and mass production for various wireless communication chips, providing them with stable and powerful capabilities.

Through these capabilities, the company builds integrated channels between terrestrial 5G networks and LEO satellite systems, providing robust technical support for emerging fields such as the low-altitude economy, industrial internet, and Internet of Things (IoT). Xingsi Semiconductor is deeply involved in tailoring and implementing domestic NR NTN satellite communication standards, establishing a technical framework optimized for local LEO satellite application scenarios. This positions them as both an implementer of industry standards and a key participant in completing the domestic satellite communication technology ecosystem.

Technical Innovation and Hardware Optimization

In terms of R&D, while 5G NR NTN shares roots with 5G RedCap and 4G LTE technologies and may share some functional modules, the unique requirements of satellite communication scenarios necessitate new chip algorithms, software, and hardware architectures. Xingsi Semiconductor moved away from traditional research methods to solve technical challenges such as scheduling complexity and variable timing caused by multi-communication system integration.

By deeply integrating broadband satellite communication with cellular technology, Xingsi Semiconductor utilizes customized software/hardware structures and dedicated algorithms to process both satellite and cellular modes on a single ASIC hardware circuit while sharing core computing resources. This approach optimizes chip power consumption, reduces production costs, and ensures efficient compatibility between various types of communication technologies.

Key Achievements and Future Outlook

Through continuous R&D, Xingsi Semiconductor has achieved several key milestones. In May 2025, the company successfully conducted a high-definition video call test via direct smartphone-to-satellite connection based on the 3GPP 5G NTN standard, proving the stable performance of its chips in complex satellite channel environments.

This achievement helps address technical weaknesses in domestic satellite communication terminal chips and contributes to securing leadership in the global satellite communication market. Xingsi Semiconductor plans to continue researching 5G NR NTN technology and upgrading core products to support high-quality development in the space-ground integrated communication industry.

Summary

Xingisi Semiconductor plays a pivotal role in connecting ground and space networks by utilizing single ASIC designs to achieve high power and cost efficiency. The company has demonstrated its technical maturity through successful high-definition video call tests over 3GPP 5G NR NTN standards. Moving forward, they aim to strengthen their global competitiveness through continuous innovation in the LEO satellite and 6G communication markets.
