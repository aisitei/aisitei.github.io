모델 소개

Huawei는 2026년 6월 29일 시안에서 세계 최초의 상용 멀티모드 문화 관광 대규모 모델인 'BoK량(博观) 문화 관광 대규모 모델'을 본격적으로 활용한다고 발표했습니다. 올해 3월까지 'BoK량'이 지원하는 AI 동반자 서비스는 400만 명 이상의 사용자를 확보했으며, 이를 통해 제작된 무형문화유산 디지털 IP의 파생 상품 판매량은 200만 건을 넘어섰습니다.

Shaanxi 문화 투자와 Huawei가 2025년 9월 공동 개발한 'BoK량' 모델은 고품질 데이터셋과 Ascend 연산 기반을 바탕으로 구축되었습니다. 이는 문화 보호와 전승을 핵심 목표로 하는 중국 최초의 산업용 대규모 모델로 평가받고 있습니다.

데이터 및 기능

해당 데이터셋의 총량은 1.2PB를 돌파했으며, 다음과 같은 방대한 자료를 포함하고 있습니다.

  • 3,100만 장의 문화 관광 이미지
  • 440만 분의 문화 박물관 영상
  • 218만 분의 오디오 리소스
  • 510개의 3D 모델
  • 9억 6,000만 건의 구조화된 텍스트

'BoK량'은 고정밀 멀티모드 역사 콘텐츠 출력을 지원하며, 박물관 수준의 유물 콘텐츠를 정확하게 생성할 수 있습니다. 이를 통해 고품질 유물 활성화, 공정의 디지털 보존, 무형문화유산 디지털 IP 개발 등이 가능해집니다.

네트워크 인프라

인기 관광지인 '대당불야성(大唐不夜城)'에서는 China Telecom 산시 회사가 Huawei와 협력하여 3중 캐리어 집합 기반의 5G-A 네트워크를 구축했습니다. 상/하향 피크 속도는 각각 600Mbps와 3.5Gbps에 달하며, 이는 일반 5G보다 약 10배 빠른 속도입니다. 올해 노동절 연휴 기간 동안 해당 지역의 5G-A 네트워크는 약 2만 3천 명의 사용자가 동시에 접속하는 것을 지원했습니다.

총평

Huawei와 Shaanxi 문화 투자가 협력하여 개발한 'BoK량' 모델은 방대한 데이터셋과 Ascend 기술을 기반으로 한 세계 최초의 상용 멀티모드 문화 관광 대규모 모델입니다. 또한, 해당 지역에 구축된 5G-A 네트워크를 통해 고속 통신 환경을 제공하며 문화 콘텐츠의 디지털화와 사용자 경험 개선에 기여하고 있습니다.

Model Overview

Huawei announced the full-scale deployment of 'BoK량 (博观)' on June 29, 2026, in Xi'an. It is recognized as the world's first commercial multi-mode cultural tourism large model. As of March this year, the AI companion service supported by BoK량 has secured over 4 million users, and the sales of digital IP products for intangible cultural heritage created through the platform have exceeded 2 million units.

Developed jointly by Shaanxi Culture Investment and Huawei starting in September 2025, the BoK량 model is built upon high-quality datasets and Ascend computing power. It is regarded as China's first industrial large model specifically aimed at the protection and transmission of cultural heritage.

Data and Capabilities

The total volume of the dataset exceeds 1.2PB and includes a vast array of resources:

  • 31 million cultural tourism images
  • 4.4 million minutes of museum videos
  • 2.18 million minutes of audio resources
  • 510 3D models
  • 960 million structured text entries

BoK량 supports high-precision multi-mode historical content output and can accurately generate museum-grade artifact content. This enables high-quality artifact activation, the digital preservation of cultural processes, and the development of digital IP for intangible cultural heritage.

Network Infrastructure

In the popular tourist destination 'Great Tang All Day Mall' (大唐不夜城), China Telecom Shaanxi collaborated with Huawei to establish a 5G-A network based on triple carrier aggregation. The peak upload and download speeds reach 600Mbps and 3.5Gbps, respectively, which is approximately 10 times faster than standard 5G. During the Labor Day holiday this year, the 5G-A network in the area supported approximately 23,000 concurrent users.

Overall Review

The BoK량 model, developed through the collaboration between Huawei and Shaanxi Culture Investment, is a world-first commercial multi-mode cultural tourism large model based on massive datasets and Ascend technology. Furthermore, the 5G-A network infrastructure deployed in the area provides a high-speed communication environment, contributing to the digitalization of cultural content and an enhanced user experience.

Summary

Huawei's BoK량 is a pioneering multi-mode AI model for cultural tourism that leverages Ascend computing and massive datasets to preserve and promote cultural heritage. Supported by a high-speed 5G-A network, the system provides a robust infrastructure for digital content creation and improved visitor experiences in major tourist destinations.

As of March this year, the AI companion service powered by BoK량 has secured over 4 million users, and the sales of digital IP products for intangible cultural heritage created through the platform have exceeded 2 million units.

Developed jointly by Shaanxi Culture Investment and Huawei starting in September 2025, the BoK량 model is built upon high-quality datasets and Ascend computing power. It is recognized as China's first industrial large model specifically aimed at the protection and transmission of cultural heritage.

Data and Capabilities

The total volume of the dataset exceeds 1.2PB and includes a vast array of resources:

  • 31 million cultural tourism images
  • 4.4 million minutes of museum-related video
  • 2.18 million minutes of audio resources
  • 510 3D models
  • 960 million structured text entries

BoK량 supports high-precision multimodal output for historical content and can accurately generate museum-grade artifact content. This enables the activation of high-quality artifacts, the digital preservation of cultural processes, and the development of digital IP for intangible cultural heritage.

Network Infrastructure

In the popular tourist destination "Great Tang All Day Mall" (大唐不夜城), China Telecom Shaanxi collaborated with Huawei to establish a 5G-A network based on triple carrier aggregation. The network provides high transmission performance and robust capacity during peak periods.

  • Peak speeds of 600Mbps and 3.5Gbps (approximately 10 times faster than standard 5G)
  • Supported approximately 23,000 concurrent users during the Labor Day holiday this year

Summary

Huawei's BoK량 is a pioneering multimodal AI model that utilizes Ascend computing and massive datasets to preserve and promote cultural heritage. Combined with high-performance 5G-A infrastructure, it provides a robust platform for digital content creation and enhances the user experience in major tourist destinations.
