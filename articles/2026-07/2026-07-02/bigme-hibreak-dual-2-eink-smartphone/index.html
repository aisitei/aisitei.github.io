HiBreak Dual 2 주요 사양

이 제품의 가장 큰 특징은 두 개의 대형 디스플레이입니다. 전면에는 독서, 메모 작성 및 장시간 사용 시에도 눈의 피로가 적은 6.13인치 컬러 e-ink 스크린이 탑재되었습니다.

또한 스타일러스 지원을 통해 e-ink 화면 위에 종이에 쓰는 것처럼 직접 메모를 하거나 스케치를 할 수 있는 기능을 제공합니다.

내부 성능 또한 업그레이드되었습니다. 기존 HiBreak Dual이 MediaTek Dimensity 1080을 탑재했던 것과 달리, 새로운 HiBreak Dual 2는 더욱 강력한 Dimensity 8300 프로세서와 5G 네트워크 지원을 갖췄습니다. 운영체제 역시 이전 모델의 Android 14에서 한 단계 도약한 Android 16이 기본 탑재됩니다.

New Product Release

BigMe has unveiled its new e-ink smartphone, the HiBreak Dual 2, on Kickstarter. Marketed as the world's first large dual-screen color e-ink smartphone, this device serves as the successor to the recently released HiBreak Dual model.

Key Specifications of HiBreak Dual 2

The most prominent feature of this device is its two large displays. The front features a 6.13-inch color e-ink screen designed for reading, note-taking, and long periods of use with minimal eye strain.

The rear is equipped with a 5-inch full-color LCD panel intended for everyday tasks such as watching videos, navigating apps, and viewing photos. This represents a significant improvement over the original HiBreak Dual, which paired the front e-ink screen with a much smaller 1.85-inch circular LCD. With both screens now similar in size, users can enjoy two distinct smartphone experiences on a single device.

Furthermore, the device supports a stylus, allowing users to take notes or sketch on the e-ink display as if writing directly on paper.

Internal performance has also been upgraded. While the original HiBreak Dual was powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 1080, the new HiBreak Dual 2 features a more powerful Dimensity 8300 processor and supports 5G networks. The operating system has also advanced from Android 14 on the previous model to Android 16 as the default OS.

Pricing is structured into various tiers for backers:

  • Early Bird: $699 and $769
  • Standard Edition: Starting at $899
  • Kickstarter Exclusive Edition: Starting at $989

Over the past year, BigMe has steadily expanded its e-ink product lineup—including smartphones, tablets, readers, monitors, and digital frames—and the HiBreak Dual 2 stands as their most ambitious smartphone model to date.

Overall Assessment

The HiBreak Dual 2 is an innovative device that simultaneously satisfies both reading needs and multimedia consumption by combining a large front color e-ink display with a high-performance rear LCD. With the integration of the Dimensity 8300 and Android 16 support, it offers enhanced performance and serves as a flagship model in BigMe's ambitious expansion of its e-ink ecosystem.

まとめ

HiBreak Dual 2は、前面に6.13インチのカラーe-ink、背面に5インチLCDを搭載した革新的なデュアルスクリーンスマートフォンです。Dimensity 8300プロセッサとAndroid 16の採用により処理能力が向上しており、読書体験とマルチメディア消費の両立を目指すユーザーに向けた野心的なモデルとなっています。

New Product Launch

BigMe has unveiled the HiBreak Dual 2 on Kickstarter, marketing it as the world's first large dual-screen color e-ink smartphone. The device serves as the successor to the recently released original HiBreak Dual model.

Key Specifications of HiBreak Dual 2

The most prominent feature of this device is its dual-display configuration. The front features a 6.13-inch color e-ink screen designed for reading, note-taking, and long periods of use with minimal eye strain.

The rear is equipped with a 5-inch full-color LCD panel intended for everyday tasks such as watching videos, navigating apps, and viewing photos. This is a significant improvement over the original HiBreak Dual, which paired the front e-ink screen with a much smaller 1.85-inch circular LCD. With both screens now similar in size, users can enjoy two distinct smartphone experiences on a single device.

Additionally, the device supports a stylus, allowing users to take notes or sketch on the e-ink display as if writing directly on paper.

Performance Upgrades

Internal performance has also been upgraded. While the original HiBreak Dual was powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 1080, the new HiBreak Dual 2 features a more powerful Dimensity 8300 processor and supports 5G networks. The operating system has also advanced from Android 14 on the previous model to Android 16 as the default OS.

Pricing

The pricing for Kickstarter backers is structured into several tiers:

  • Early Bird: $699 and $769
  • Standard Edition: Starting at $899
  • Kickstarter Exclusive Edition: Starting at $989

Over the past year, BigMe has steadily expanded its e-ink product lineup—including smartphones, tablets, readers, monitors, and digital frames—and the HiBreak Dual 2 stands as their most ambitious smartphone model to date.

Summary

The HiBreak Dual 2 is a dual-screen smartphone featuring a 6.13-inch color e-ink front display for reading and a 5-inch LCD rear screen for multimedia consumption. It features significant hardware upgrades, including the Dimensity 8300 processor, 5G support, and Android 16.
