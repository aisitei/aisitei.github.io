배터리 및 냉각 시스템 사양은 다음과 같습니다.

  • 배터리: 8,100mAh
  • 충전 속도: 44W 고속 충전 지원

소프트웨어 및 기타 기능은 다음과 같습니다.

  • OS: Android 16 기반 OriginOS 6
  • 보안: 디스플레이 내장 광학 지문 인식 스캐너
  • 오디오: 듀얼 스테레오 스피커
  • 연결성: Bluetooth 5.4, Dual-band Wi-Fi, GPS, USB 2.0 포트, 3.5mm 헤드폰 잭
  • 네트워크 및 내구성: Dual nano-SIM 지원, IP68 및 IP69 방수/방진 등급, MIL-STD-810H 군용 표준 충격 저항

Vivo Y500 Launch News

Vivo has quietly unveiled the Vivo Y500 in Pakistan. While models such as the Y500, Y500i, Y500s, and Y500 Pro were previously introduced in the Chinese market, this device marks the first time a model from that lineup has debuted outside of its home country. One of its standout features is a massive 8,100mAh battery, which represents the largest capacity ever featured in a Vivo smartphone released outside of China. In addition to the substantial battery, the device features an AMOLED display, a 50MP dual-camera system, and runs on OriginOS 6.

Vivo Y500 Specifications and Key Features

The Vivo Y500 is equipped with a 6.83-inch AMOLED display providing 1.5K resolution.

  • Resolution: 2800 x 1260
  • Pixel Density: 449ppi
  • Peak Brightness: 5,000 nits (local peak)
  • Screen Ratio: 94.47%
  • Certification and Protection: Includes SGS Low Blue Light, SGS Five-Star Sunlight Readable Display certification, and Gaming Eye Protection for vision safety.

In terms of performance, the Vivo Y500 is powered by the Unisoc T7300 processor, offering 8GB of RAM and either 128GB or 256GB of storage. Due to the adoption of the Unisoc T7300 chipset, this device does not support 5G networks and is limited to 4G connectivity.

The battery and cooling system specifications are as follows:

  • Battery: 8,100mAh
  • Charging Speed: Supports 44W fast charging
  • Cooling System: Improved thermal management via 3,000mm² Vapor Chamber technology.

Software and other features include:

  • OS: OriginOS 6 based on Android 16
  • Security: In-display optical fingerprint scanner
  • Audio: Dual stereo speakers
  • Connectivity: Bluetooth 5.4, Dual-band Wi-Fi, GPS, USB 2.0 port, and a 3.5mm headphone jack
  • Network and Durability: Dual nano-SIM support, IP68 and IP69 water/dust resistance ratings, and MIL-STD-810H military standard shock resistance.

Camera Specifications

The Vivo Y500 features a camera system utilizing high-resolution sensors.

  • Main Camera: 50MP Sony IMX852 sensor
  • Secondary Camera: 2MP sensor
  • Front Camera: 32MP selfie camera

Pricing and Availability

The Vivo Y500 is available in Pearl White and Midnight Blue colors.

  • Pakistan Price (8GB+128GB): PKR 99,999 (approx. $360)
  • Pakistan Price (8GB+256GB): PKR 109,999 (approx. $400)

The smartphone is scheduled to be released in additional markets, including Saudi Arabia and Kazakhstan, in the near future.

Summary

The Vivo Y500 is a durability-focused device featuring a massive 8,100mAh battery and high-level protection ratings like IP69 and MIL-STD-810H. While it is limited to 4G connectivity due to its Unisoc T7300 chipset, it offers premium features such as an AMOLED display and a Sony IMX852 main camera. It serves as an ideal choice for users who prioritize long battery life and robust hardware over 5G connectivity.
