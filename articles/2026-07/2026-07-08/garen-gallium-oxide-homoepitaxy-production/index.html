Production Line and Technology

In June, Jianren Semiconductor announced the establishment of the world's first 6/8-inch gallium oxide homo-epitaxy mass production line. This achievement is based on their independently developed casting method for single crystal growth and MOCVD epitaxy processes. During the growth phase, the company utilized unique casting technology to achieve stable growth of thick gallium oxide crystals.

When combined with the company's ultra-thin substrate processing technology, production yields were increased by 3 to 4 times compared to traditional methods. Furthermore, the use of the precious metal iridium was reduced through the casting method, cutting substrate costs by more than 80% and significantly lowering material costs for downstream device manufacturers.

Epitaxy and Quality

During the epitaxy stage, Jianren Semiconductor's technical team optimized core process parameters tailored to (100) plane characteristics and designed a unique MOCVD epitaxy process. This has established the company as the world's first and only supplier of commercial 6-inch gallium oxide homo-epitaxy wafers.

The 6-inch gallium oxide homo-epitaxy wafers produced by the company feature an epitaxial layer thickness of over 10μm with a thickness variation of less than 1%, demonstrating excellent uniformity.

Supply Chain and Market Impact

Previously, gallium oxide homo-epitaxy wafers faced significant challenges in industrialization due to small sizes, low production volumes, and poor uniformity. Jianren Semiconductor has not only achieved the first shipment of 6-inch gallium oxide homo-epitaxy wafers but also secured stable mass production capabilities across the entire "single crystal-substrate-epitaxy" process.

By establishing the world's first 6/8-inch gallium oxide homo-epitaxy mass production line, they can now ensure consistent quality control between batches. Currently, several international companies and research institutions are placing orders, with many collaborative customers already beginning long-term and stable procurement agreements.
