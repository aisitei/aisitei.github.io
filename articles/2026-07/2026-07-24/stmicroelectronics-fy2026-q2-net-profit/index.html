실적 발표

STMicroelectronics는 2026 회계연도(2026년 1월 3일 ~ 2027년 1월 2일) 제2분기(2026년 3월 29일 ~ 2026년 6월 27일) 보고서를 발표했습니다.

성과 분석

이번 실적은 회사가 사전에 제시한 가이드라인의 중간치를 상회하며 시장 예상보다 우수한 성과를 거두었습니다. 제2분기 매출은 자동차 전자, CECP 부문 및 AI 관련 칩 사업이 주요 성장 동력으로 작용했습니다.

매출총이익률은 유휴 생산 능력 비용 감소와 제품 구조 최적화에 힘입어 34.80%로 상승했습니다. 특히 전년 동기 9,700만 달러의 순손실을 기록했던 것과 대조적으로 이번 분기에 성공적인 흑자 전환을 달성했습니다.

영업 활동 현금흐름은 전년 대비 41.81% 증가한 5.02억 달러를 기록했으며, 잉여 현금흐름 또한 7,500만 달러로 양수 전환되며 현금 흐름의 질이 크게 개선되었습니다. 부채비율은 33.22%로 전년 대비 6.71%포인트 하락하며 재무 구조가 지속적으로 최적화되고 안정적인 상태를 유지하고 있습니다.

2026 회계연도 제4분기 매출은 40억 달러를 초과할 것으로 예상되며, AI 데이터 센터 및 위성통신 관련 고객 프로젝트가 주요 성장 동력이 될 전망입니다. 특히 AI 데이터 센터 사업의 매출 목표를 상향 조정하여, 2026년 해당 부문 매출이 10억 달러를 넘어서고 2027년에는 20억 달러를 돌파할 것으로 기대하며 AI 반도체 분야에서의 시장 입지를 더욱 공고히 할 계획입니다.

STMicroelectronics 在 2026 财年第二财季表现强劲，营收超预期且成功扭亏为盈。公司受益于汽车电子和 AI 相关业务的推动，财务结构持续优化。未来将继续聚焦 AI 数据中心等高增长领域，以巩固其在 AI 半导体赛道的市场地位。

業績分析

今回の決算は、同社が事前に提示したガイダンスの中間値を上回り、市場の予想を上回る良好な成果を収めました。第2四半期の売上成長の主な要因は、車載用電子機器、CECP部門、およびAI関連チップ事業でした。

売上総利益率は、遊休生産能力コストの削減と製品構成の最適化により34.80%に上昇しました。特に前年同期には9,700万ドルの純損失を記録していたのに対し、今四半期は成功的な黒字転換を達成しています。

営業活動によるキャッシュフローは前年比41.81%増の5.02億ドルを記録し、フリーキャッシュフローも7,500万ドルとプラスに転じるなど、キャッシュフローの質が大幅に改善しました。負債比率は33.22%となり、前年比で6.71ポイント低下しており、財務構造は継続的に最適化され、安定した状態を維持しています。

今後の見通し

同社は2026会計年度第3四半期の純売上高の中間値を37億ドルと予測しており、これは前四半期比で約6.2%、前年同期比で約16.2%の増加を見込んでいます。また、売上総利益率は37.0%となる見通しです。

2026会計年度第4四半期の売上は40億ドルを超えると予想されており、AIデータセンターおよび衛星通信関連の顧客プロジェクトが主要な成長エンジンになると予測されています。特にAIデータセンター事業の売上目標を引き上げ、同部門の売上高は2026年に10億ドルを突破し、2027年には20億ドルを突破すると見込んでおり、AI半導体分野における市場の地位をさらに強固にする計画です。

Earnings Report

STMicroelectronics has released its Q2 report for fiscal year 2026 (January 3, 2026 – January 2, 2027), covering the period from March 29, 2026, to June 27, 2026.

  • Total Revenue: $3.487 billion (up 26.0% YoY, up 12.7% QoQ)
  • Gross Profit: $1.215 billion (up 31.1% YoY, up 16.3% QoQ)
  • Gross Margin: 34.8% (up 1.30 percentage points YoY, up 1.00 percentage point QoQ)
  • Net Income Attributable to Shareholders: $222 million (Turned profitable)
  • Adjusted Net Income: $291 million (up 410.50% YoY, up 138.5% QoQ)
  • Operating Cash Flow: $502 million (up 41.81% YoY)
  • Free Cash Flow: $75 million (Turned positive)
  • Basic Earnings Per Share (EPS): $0.25 (Turned profitable)
  • Diluted Earnings Per Share (EPS): $0.24 (Turned profitable)
  • Debt-to-Equity Ratio: 33.23%

Performance Analysis

The results exceeded the midpoint of the company's previously issued guidance and outperformed market expectations. Key growth drivers for Q2 included automotive electronics, the CECP segment, and AI-related chip businesses.

Gross margin rose to 34.80% due to a reduction in costs from idle production capacity and an optimized product mix. Notably, the company achieved a successful turnaround to profitability this quarter, contrasting with the net loss of $97 million recorded during the same period last year.

Operating cash flow reached $502 million, an increase of 41.81% compared to the previous year, while free cash flow turned positive at $75 million, significantly improving the quality of cash flow. The debt-to-equity ratio fell by 6.71 percentage points to 33.22%, indicating that the financial structure is being continuously optimized and remains stable.

Future Outlook

The company projects mid-point net sales of $3.7 billion for Q3 FY2026, representing an increase of approximately 6.2% QoQ and 16.2% YoY, with a projected gross margin of 37.0%.

Revenue for Q4 FY2026 is expected to exceed $4 billion, driven primarily by customer projects in AI data centers and satellite communications. In particular, the company has raised its revenue targets for the AI data center business, expecting this segment's revenue to surpass $1 billion in 2026 and exceed $2 billion in 2027, aiming to solidify its market position in the AI semiconductor sector.

Market Evaluation

As a major global semiconductor manufacturer, STMicroelectronics currently holds sufficient orders, with signs of supply shortages appearing in certain product lines. Inventory levels have fallen below the company's standard targets. Combined with the ongoing boom in demand for AI infrastructure, there is high certainty regarding future revenue growth and potential for further improvement in overall valuation.

Overall Review

STMicroelectronics outperformed its performance guidelines thanks to robust demand in automotive electronics and AI chips, while strengthening its financial structure through a successful turnaround to profitability and improved cash flow. The company is positioned for significant growth driven by the expansion of the AI data center and satellite communication markets.

Summary

STMicroelectronics reported strong Q2 results that exceeded market expectations, fueled by demand in automotive electronics and AI-related chips. The company successfully turned a profit and improved its cash flow position while lowering its debt ratio. Looking ahead, STMicroelectronics expects significant growth through 2027, particularly within the AI data center and satellite communication sectors.
