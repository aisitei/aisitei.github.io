Design and Durability

The device is available in four colors: Sky Blue, Nebula Purple, Black, and White. It features a thickness of 8.46mm and weighs 226g. Furthermore, it has achieved IP66/IP68/IP69/IP69K certifications for enhanced durability.

Display and Camera

The front is equipped with a 6.83-inch panel with 1.5K resolution, supporting a peak brightness of up to 3,500 nits. It incorporates Xiaomi QingShan eye protection technology, which can lower the brightness to as low as 1 nit in low-light environments. The sides feature a quad micro-curved frame, while the rear houses a rectangular camera module with a 50MP main camera.

Performance and Battery

The device is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 6s Gen 4 processor and features a massive 9,000mAh battery. It supports 67W wired charging and 22.5W wired reverse charging. To celebrate its launch, Xiaomi provides a 5-year battery upgrade guarantee: if the battery health falls below 80% within four years, it will be replaced for free; in the fifth year, users with battery health under 80% can receive a free upgrade to a high-capacity battery.

Overall Review

The Xiaomi REDMI Note 17 Pro is a robust model featuring a high-performance processor, a massive 9,000mAh battery, and strong durability. It features an eye-protection-enhanced display and provides a reliable user experience through its comprehensive 5-year battery upgrade program.
