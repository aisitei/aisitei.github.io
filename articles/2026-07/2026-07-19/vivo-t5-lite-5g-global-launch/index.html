  • 최대 6GB LPDDR4X RAM
  • 256GB UFS 2.2 저장 공간 (microSD 카드로 최대 2TB 확장 가능)
  • 6500mAh 배터리
  • 44W 유선 충전 지원

총평

vivo T5 Lite 5G는 Dimensity 6300과 6500mAh 대용량 배터리를 탑재한 입문형 스마트폰입니다. 고주사율 디스플레이와 확장 가능한 저장 공간을 갖추고 있습니다.

Product Overview

According to reports from IT Home, vivo has launched the T5 Lite 5G smartphone in international markets. Equipped with a Dimensity 6300 chipset and a 6500mAh battery, this device is aimed at the entry-level market.

Design and Display

The device is available in two colors and features the following physical specifications:

  • Weight: 209g
  • Thickness: 8.39mm

The front of the device features a 6.74-inch HD+ 120Hz LCD waterdrop display with a peak brightness of 1,200 nits. It is equipped with a 5MP selfie camera and a 50MP main camera powered by a Sony IMX582 sensor on the rear.

Performance and Battery

The device is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 6300 processor, featuring the following key specifications:

  • Up to 6GB LPDDR4X RAM
  • 256GB UFS 2.2 storage (expandable up to 2TB via microSD card)
  • 6500mAh battery
  • 44W wired charging support

Conclusion

The vivo T5 Lite 5G is an entry-level smartphone equipped with a Dimensity 6300 chipset and a high-capacity 6500mAh battery. It offers a high refresh rate display and expandable storage options.
