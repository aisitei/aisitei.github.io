야간 감시를 위해 화이트 LED와 적외선(IR) LED를 혼합하여 사용합니다. 시스템은 기본적으로 표준 적외선 야간 투시 모드를 사용하지만, 어둠 속에서 사람을 감지하면 화이트 LED가 켜져 컬러 영상으로 촬영됩니다.

Xiaomi Smart Solar Camera 4 Pro 4G 发布

Xiaomi Smart Solar Camera 4 Pro 4G 参数

该摄像头配备 9,900mAh 电池和 7.6W 太阳能电池板，可在无需手动充电的情况下持续供电。它采用内置的双 4G SIM 系统替代 Wi-Fi，并支持自动切换网络以保持连接。此外，通过 Mi Home 应用进行远程监控时提供免费数据流量。

摄像头详细规格与功能

该设备采用了双摄像头配置。两个摄像头均配备 5MP 传感器，结合 f/1.6 镜头可录制 3K 分辨率视频。

  • 传感器：5MP（2个）
  • 分辨率：3K
  • 光圈：f/1.6
  • 摄像头配置：固定广角镜头（用于常规监控）及 PTZ 摄像头（支持水平旋转 355 度，垂直旋转 95 度）

两个摄像头通过 AI 追踪技术协同工作。当一个镜头负责监控大范围区域时，另一个镜头则负责跟踪移动目标。

为了实现夜间监控，系统结合了白色 LED 和红外线（IR）LED。系统默认使用标准红外夜视模式，但在黑暗中检测到人时，会自动开启白色 LED 以拍摄彩色视频。

存储与耐用性

为管理高达 256GB 的 microSD 存储空间，该设备引入了“Always-On Video”系统。在环境安静时，采用每 2 秒记录一帧的延时摄影模式，可减少约 90% 的存储空间占用；一旦检测到人或车辆，将立即切换至常规录制模式。

硬件具备 IP66 级防尘防水功能，能够承受户外环境。此外，它支持带降噪功能的双向语音，并与 Xiaomi 的 HyperOS Connect 智能家居平台集成。在检测到异常情况时，内置的警报器和警示灯将启动，同时向用户的手机发送推送通知。

Xiaomi Launches Smart Solar Camera 4 Pro 4G

Following a successful crowdfunding campaign last month, Xiaomi has officially released the Smart Solar Camera 4 Pro 4G Dual-Camera Edition in China. Designed specifically for outdoor surveillance environments where Wi-Fi or power supply is difficult to provide—such as construction sites and orchards—the device is priced at 649 yuan (approximately $96).

Xiaomi Smart Solar Camera 4 Pro 4G Specifications

The camera is equipped with a 9,900mAh battery and a 7.6W solar panel to provide continuous power without the need for manual charging. Instead of Wi-Fi, it utilizes an integrated dual 4G SIM system that automatically switches networks to maintain a stable connection. Additionally, free data is provided for remote monitoring via the Mi Home app.

Detailed Camera Specifications and Features

This device features a dual-camera configuration. Both cameras are equipped with 5MP sensors combined with f/1.6 lenses to record in 3K resolution.

  • Sensors: 5MP (x2)
  • Resolution: 3K
  • Aperture: f/1.6
  • Camera Configuration: Fixed wide-angle lens (for general monitoring) and a PTZ camera (capable of rotating 355 degrees horizontally and 95 degrees vertically).

The two cameras work in tandem using AI tracking technology; while one lens monitors a broad area, the other tracks moving targets.

For night surveillance, the system utilizes a combination of white LEDs and infrared (IR) LEDs. The system defaults to standard IR night vision mode but automatically activates the white LEDs to capture color video when a human is detected in the dark.

Storage and Durability

To manage storage for up to 256GB of microSD space, the device features an "Always-On Video" system. When the surroundings are quiet, it uses a time-lapse method recording one frame every two seconds, which reduces storage consumption by approximately 90%. The system immediately switches to standard recording mode upon detecting people or vehicles.

The hardware is built with an IP66 rating for dust and water resistance to withstand outdoor environments. It also supports two-way audio with noise cancellation and integrates with Xiaomi's HyperOS Connect smart home platform. In the event of an anomaly, a built-in siren and warning lights will activate while sending push notifications to the user's phone.

Recently, Xiaomi also launched a smart bathtub heater featuring automatic lighting, heating, and airflow control. Additionally, they introduced a new smart water flosser featuring a color display, app connectivity, and a battery life of up to 105 days.

Summary

The Xiaomi Smart Solar Camera 4 Pro 4G is a robust security solution optimized for remote outdoor environments by combining solar power with 4G connectivity. It features an innovative dual-camera system with AI tracking and efficient storage management algorithms to ensure high performance in infrastructure-poor areas.
