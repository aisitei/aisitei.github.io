총평

OPPO K15 표준판은 Dimensity 7360 Super 칩과 8000mAh 배터리, 그리고 금속 프레임과 크리스탈 실드 글래스를 통해 강력한 성능과 고급스러운 디자인을 동시에 구현했습니다. 7월 24일 출시 예정이며, 고성능 카메라와 빠른 충전 기능을 갖춘 것이 특징입니다.

总结

OPPO K15 标准版将于 7 月 24 日开售。该机型搭载 Dimensity 7360 Super 芯片并配备 8000mAh 大容量电池，在提供高像素双摄和快速充电的同时，通过金属中框与晶盾玻璃提升了产品质感。

まとめ

Design and Colors

OPPO today unveiled two color options for the OPPO K15 Standard Edition: Speed White and Wind Grey.

Performance and Battery

The OPPO K15 Standard Edition is powered by the Dimensity 7360 Super chip and features an 8,000mAh Ice Glacier battery. It also incorporates a next-generation Wind Cooling Engine.

Display and Build

The device adopts a metal mid-frame and Crystal Shield glass to provide an excellent grip and premium texture. It is equipped with a 6.59-inch 1.5K LTPS bar-type display.

  • Weight: 205g
  • Thickness: 8.27mm

Camera and Other Features

  • 50MP front camera
  • 50MP main camera + 50MP ultra-wide camera on the rear
  • 80W wired fast charging support
  • RGB breathing LED, dual-band GPS, and triple-constellation BeiDou system
  • Single-shot optical fingerprint recognition

Summary

The OPPO K15 Standard Edition combines high performance with a premium design by featuring the Dimensity 7360 Super chip, an 8,000mAh battery, and a durable metal frame. Set to launch on July 24, it highlights its advanced camera system and fast charging capabilities.
