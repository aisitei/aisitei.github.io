이번 연기로 인해 여러분의 기대에 부응하지 못하고 구매 계획에 차질을 드린 점 깊은 사과의 말씀을 드립니다. 현재 전담팀이 심사 진행 상황을 밀착 모니터링하며 공식 기관과 실시간으로 소통하고, 인증 기간을 단축하기 위해 최선을 다하고 있습니다.

Delayed Release Announcement

According to reports from IT Home, BLUEFOX released a statement today regarding the Aura A1. The launch of the new Aura A1 mini smartphone will be postponed due to delays in obtaining 3C certification.

Official Statement

Dear valued customers, we sincerely thank you for your continued interest and support for BLUEFOX.

We would like to offer our sincere apologies regarding the launch of our new product. Due to delays in the mandatory national 3C certification process, the overall release schedule for the new device has been postponed.

3C certification is a critical compliance procedure that ensures the legal market entry and safety of electronic products; it cannot be bypassed or expedited. Due to a recent surge in official application volume, verification periods have significantly increased, preventing the certification process for our new product from being completed as scheduled.

To maintain our quality standards and protect the rights of our users, we decided to postpone the launch after careful internal review. We deeply apologize for failing to meet your expectations and for any inconvenience caused to your purchasing plans.

A dedicated team is currently monitoring the certification progress closely and communicating in real-time with official authorities to expedite the process as much as possible. We plan to begin shipping and distribution immediately upon completion of the certification, and all updates will be shared transparently through our official accounts and store notices.
