MotoG77 Power 사양 및 특징

MotoG77 Power는 120Hz 주사율을 지원하는 6.72인치 Full HD+ 디스플레이를 탑재했습니다. 최대 1,050니트의 피크 밝기와 Display Color Boost 기술이 적용되었으며, 물기가 있는 손으로도 원활한 조작이 가능한 Water Touch 기능을 지원합니다. 오디오 측면에서는 Dolby Atmos 및 Hi-Res Audio를 지원하는 스테레오 스피커가 탑재되었습니다.

성능 면에서 이 제품은 MediaTek Dimensity 6400 칩셋을 기반으로 하며, 8GB LPDDR4X RAM과 최대 24GB RAM Boost 기능을 제공합니다. 저장 공간은 128GB를 기본으로 제공하며, microSD 카드를 통해 최대 1TB까지 확장할 수 있습니다.

MotoG77 Power Specifications and Features

The MotoG77 Power has officially launched in the Indian market. By combining a high-capacity battery, AI-powered camera features, and a high refresh rate display, the device aims to establish a competitive edge in the mid-range smartphone segment. The following sections detail the device's specifications, key features, pricing, and availability.

The MotoG77 Power features a 6.72-inch Full HD+ display supporting a 120Hz refresh rate. It reaches a peak brightness of up to 1,050 nits and incorporates Display Color Boost technology. Additionally, it supports Water Touch functionality, allowing for smooth operation even with wet hands. For audio, the device is equipped with stereo speakers that support Dolby Atmos and Hi-Res Audio.

In terms of performance, the device is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 6400 chipset, paired with 8GB of LPDDR4X RAM and a maximum 24GB RAM Boost feature. It comes with 128GB of internal storage as standard, which can be expanded up to 1TB via a microSD card.

Camera Specifications

The MotoG77 Power is equipped with a powerful camera system designed to deliver high-quality results.

  • Main Camera: 50MP Sony LYTIA 600 sensor (utilizing Quad Pixel technology)
  • Ultra-Wide Camera: 8MP
  • Front Selfie Camera: 32MP

Hardware and Software Specifications

The following are the additional hardware and software specifications:

  • Battery and Charging: 7,000mAh high-capacity battery, 30W TurboPower charging support, and 6W wired reverse charging
  • Durability and Protection: Corning Gorilla Glass 7i, SGS-certified MIL-STD-810H durability standards, and IP64 dust and water resistance
  • Software: Android 16, one OS upgrade provided, three years of security updates guaranteed, with Moto Secure and ThinkShield integrated

MotoG77 Power Price and Launch Information

The MotoG77 Power is available in three colors: Pantone Nautical Blue, Pantone Fuchsia Red, and Pantone Impenetrable, offered in a single 8GB+128GB configuration. The official retail price is Rs 25,999 (approximately $275), but it can be purchased for Rs 23,999 (approximately $250) when applying launch promotions. Sales begin on July 13 through Flipkart, the Motorola India official website, and major retail stores nationwide.

Summary

The MotoG77 Power is a robust mid-range smartphone featuring a massive 7,000mAh battery and a high refresh rate display. It stands out with its Sony LYTIA 600 sensor camera system and durable build quality, offering a balanced mix of performance and practicality.
