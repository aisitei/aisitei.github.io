Oppo Reno 16 and Reno 16c Launch

Oppo has launched the Reno 16 and Reno 16c in India. These two models place a heavy emphasis on artificial intelligence while featuring upgrades to camera systems, battery capacity, and hardware. Furthermore, these are the first devices in the Reno series to feature the "AI Snap Key," a dedicated shortcut for AI-powered tools, along with various features designed to enhance photography and productivity.

Oppo Reno 16 Specifications and Features

  • Display: 6.32-inch 1.5K AMOLED, 120Hz refresh rate, up to 3,600 nits peak brightness, Crystal Guard protection
  • Processor: Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset
  • Memory and Storage: Up to 12GB LPDDR5X RAM, 256GB UFS 3.1 storage
  • Battery: 6,700mAh, 80W SuperVOOC charging
  • Rear Camera:
    • 50MP Sony LYT-600 main sensor (with OIS)
    • 50MP ultra-wide camera
    • 50MP 3.5x telephoto camera
  • Front Camera: 50MP (supports 4K video recording)
  • Operating System: ColorOS 16 based on Android 16
  • AI Features: AI Mind Pilot, AI Mind Space, AI Bill Manager, and AI Translation (supporting ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity)

Oppo Reno 16c Specifications and Features

  • Display: 6.57-inch AMOLED, 120Hz refresh rate
  • Processor: MediaTek Dimensity 7300
  • Memory and Storage: Up to 12GB RAM, 256GB storage
  • Battery: 7,000mAh, 80W charging
  • Rear Camera:
    • 50MP main sensor (with OIS)
    • 8MP ultra-wide camera
    • 50MP 3.5x telephoto lens
  • Front Camera: 50MP selfie camera
  • Durability Rating: IP66, IP68, IP69, and IP69K (applicable to both models)

Oppo Bubble Accessory

Alongside the phones, Oppo introduced the "Bubble" accessory. This device attaches magnetically to the smartphone and features a 1.73-inch AMOLED display that allows users to preview photos taken with the rear camera. It also functions as a wireless remote shutter and is equipped with a 550mAh battery.
