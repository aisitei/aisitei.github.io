Release Information

iQOO has unveiled the Z11 Lite, a budget-friendly smartphone for the Indian market. The device is scheduled to launch on July 24 and will be priced under 20,000 rupees (approximately 1,407 yuan).

Display and Camera

The key specifications for this model include:

  • Waterdrop display with 120Hz high refresh rate support
  • Maximum brightness of 1200nits
  • 50MP main camera
  • Up to 6GB RAM and 256GB storage

Performance and Battery

The device features the following performance specifications:

  • MediaTek Dimensity 6300 chipset
  • Large 6,500mAh battery
  • 44W wired fast charging and bypass charging support

Software and Durability

The smartphone comes pre-loaded with OriginOS 6 and officially supports 2 years of OS updates and 4 years of security updates. Additionally, it features an IP65 rating for dust and water resistance.
