According to a report by IT Home, blogger @体验more has revealed the specifications for the vivo X300E. Pre-orders for the device are scheduled to begin tomorrow at 9:00 AM, with three models available: 8GB+256GB, 12GB+256GB, and 12GB+512GB.

Design and Display

The vivo X300E is offered in three color options: Moonlight White, Sunlight Orange, and Night Black. The device measures 157.52 x 74.33 x 7.99mm and weighs 203g.

The front of the device features a 6.59-inch 1260p (1.5K) OLED single punch-hole bar display with an integrated 50MP selfie camera. The build quality includes a metal mid-frame and a glass back cover.

Camera

It features a large 7200mAh battery supporting 90W wired charging, along with premium features such as ultrasonic fingerprint recognition and an X-axis motor.
