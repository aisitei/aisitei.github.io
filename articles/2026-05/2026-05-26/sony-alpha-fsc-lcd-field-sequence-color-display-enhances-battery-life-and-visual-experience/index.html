FSC-LCD 기술의 원리와 장점

FSC(Field Sequential Colour)는 '장면 순차 색상'을 의미하며, 이 기술은 기존의 RGB 컬러 필터 구조를 고투과율 그레이스케일 액정 패널로 대체하는 것이 핵심입니다. 이 방식을 통해 빨강, 초록, 파랑 세 가지 발광 다이오드(LED)가 1초에 720번씩 스위칭될 수 있으며, IPS LTPS 액정 패널과 결합하여 깜빡임 없는 시각적 경험을 제공하는 240Hz 주사율의 컬러 화면 구현이 가능합니다.

쉽게 설명하자면, 기존 LCD는 RGB 컬러 필터를 이용해 색상을 표시했지만, FSC-LCD는 RGB 백라이트를 고속으로 전환하여 색상을 나타내는 방식입니다.

또한 이 기술은 디스플레이 모듈의 전력 소비를 70%까지 낮출 수 있어, 카메라의 실시간 라이브 뷰(Live View) 작동 시간을 대폭 늘릴 수 있다는 장점이 있습니다.

FSC-LCD技術の原理とメリット

FSC（Field Sequential Colour）は「フィールド順次色」を意味する技術であり、この技術の核心は、従来のRGBカラーフィルター構造を高透過率のグレースケール液晶パネルに置き換えることにあります。この方式を採用することで、赤、緑、青の3色のLEDが1秒間に720回切り替わることが可能になります。さらにIPS LTPS液晶パネルと組み合わせることで、ちらつきのない視覚体験を提供する240Hzのリフレッシュレートのカラー画面を実現できます。

簡単に説明すると、従来のLCDはRGBカラーフィルターを用いて色を表示していましたが、FSC-LCDはRGBバックライトを高速度で切り替えることで色を表現する仕組みです。

また、この技術はディスプレイモジュールの消費電力を最大70%まで削減できるため、カメラのリアルタイムなライブビュー（Live View）の動作時間を大幅に延ばすことができるという利点があります。

Principles and Advantages of FSC-LCD Technology

FSC (Field Sequential Colour) replaces traditional RGB color filter structures with high-transparency grayscale liquid crystal panels. This method allows three types of LEDs—red, green, and blue—to switch 720 times per second. When combined with IPS LTPS panels, it enables a color display with a 240Hz refresh rate that provides a flicker-free visual experience.

In simpler terms, while conventional LCDs use RGB filters to display colors, FSC-LCD displays colors by rapidly switching the RGB backlights.

Principles and Advantages of FSC-LCD Technology

FSC (Field Sequential Colour) is a display technology that replaces traditional RGB color filter structures with high-transparency grayscale liquid crystal panels. Unlike conventional LCDs that use filters to display colors, FSC-LCD displays colors by rapidly switching the RGB backlights.

When combined with IPS LTPS panels, this system can achieve a 240Hz refresh rate, providing a flicker-free visual experience for users.

  • Switching frequency: Red, green, and blue LEDs switch 720 times per second.
  • Power efficiency: The technology can reduce display module power consumption by up to 70%.
  • Live View performance: Significantly extends the operation time for real-time Live View in cameras.

Industry Trends and Future Outlook

Sony is currently co-developing FSC-LCD technology with TCL and Hisense. Specifically, TCL plans to launch TV products equipped with RGB-LED backlights and FSC-LCD; it is reported that prototypes for 2025 have already been completed.

Furthermore, reports suggest that Sony's BRAVIA TV division may establish a joint venture with TCL in 2027. If these details are confirmed, the implementation of this technology in Alpha cameras would provide users with a more power-efficient Live View experience and significantly improved visual quality.

However, as no official announcement has been made regarding Sony's mirrorless camera display specifications, these reports should be treated as rumors for now.

Summary

Sony is reportedly developing FSC-LCD technology for its Alpha series cameras to replace traditional color filters with high-speed RGB switching. This innovation aims to reduce power consumption by up to 70% while providing a flicker-free 240Hz refresh rate. If implemented, it would significantly enhance both the battery life and visual quality of the Live View experience.
