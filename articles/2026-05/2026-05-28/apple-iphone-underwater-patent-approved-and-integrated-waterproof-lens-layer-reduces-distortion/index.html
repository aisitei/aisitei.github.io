Apple, 수중 촬영 왜곡을 줄이는 일체형 렌즈 보호층 특허 확보

IT之家에 따르면, 기술 매체 Appleinsider는 지난 5월 26일 블로그를 통해 Apple이 새로운 특허를 획득했음을 보도했습니다. 이 특허는 iPhone이 수중에서 촬영할 때 발생하는 왜곡을 최소화하는 일체형 수중 카메라 보호층과 관련되어 있습니다.

기존 수중 촬영 액세서리의 문제점

Apple은 이번 특허를 통해 수중 이미징의 왜곡 문제를 중점적으로 다루었습니다. 기존 시장에서 사용되는 서드파티(third-party) 수중 촬영 액세서리들은 공통적인 문제점을 가지고 있습니다.

  • 보호층의 역할: 렌즈 앞에 보호층을 추가하여 물속 환경으로부터 카메라를 격리하는 것이 필수적입니다.
  • 왜곡 유발 가능성: 하지만 이 구조물 자체가 이미지 왜곡(distortion)을 일으킬 수 있습니다.

전통적인 돔 포트(dome port)는 침수 후 발생하는 이미징 변화를 완화하기 위해 설계되었지만, 실제로 부착되는 과정에서 오히려 새로운 종류의 왜곡이 발생할 위험이 있었습니다.

Apple이 제시하는 혁신적인 해결책: 단일 광학 구조

Apple이 제안한 솔루션은 여러 개의 렌즈가 하나의 통합된 방수 광학층을 공유하도록 하는 것입니다. 이 구조는 여러 카메라 모듈을 모두 덮으면서도 각 렌즈 스택의 이미징 과정에 참여합니다.

이 보호층은 다음과 같은 방식으로 유연하게 설계될 수 있습니다:

  • 평면 배치 시: 여러 개의 카메라가 평면에 배열되어 있을 경우, 이 통합 광학 부품은 기본적으로 평면 형태로 제작되며, 국소적인 부분에만 볼록한 구조를 유지합니다.
  • 곡면 배치 시: 만약 카메라 모듈이 곡면에 분포되어 있다면, 이 광학층 역시 그 곡면을 따라 성형될 수 있으며, 각 국부 돔의 곡률은 아래쪽 렌즈와 정확하게 일치하도록 설계됩니다.

이 특허를 통해 가장 강조된 또 다른 핵심 포인트는 보호층 전체를 단일 재질과 단일 부품으로 만드는 것입니다. 이는 기존 디자인에서 흔히 볼 수 있는 이음매(seams), 접착제, 기타 연결 재료들을 제거할 수 있게 합니다.

결과적으로 이러한 설계 개선은 침수 위험을 낮출 뿐만 아니라, 연결 구조물로 인해 발생하던 추가적인 왜곡까지 줄이는 효과를 가져옵니다.

专利概况

Apple获批一项关于一体式水下镜头保护层的专利，旨在解决iPhone在水下拍摄时因现有配件导致的图像畸变问题。

核心技术方案

该专利针对目前主流第三方水下拍摄配件存在的结构性缺陷。传统穹顶罩（dome port）虽然能隔绝水体，但其复杂的连接结构往往会引入额外的成像失真。Apple提出的替代方案包括：

  • 采用单块一体式光学保护层覆盖多个摄像头模组。
  • 该层结构不仅作为物理屏障，还参与各镜头堆栈的成像过程。
  • 支持多种布局适配：若摄像头排布在平面上，保护层可做成平面；若分布在曲面上，则可随曲面成型并匹配每个局部穹顶的曲率。

结构优化优势

该方案的核心在于将整块保护层设计为单一材料和单一部件，具有以下技术优势：

  • 去除传统设计中的接缝、胶水及其他连接材料。
  • 显著降低因进水风险带来的安全隐患。
  • 减少由复杂连接结构引起的额外畸变，提升成像质量。

总结

Apple获批的这项新专利旨在通过一体式光学保护层取代传统的单镜头穹顶罩。该设计通过采用单一材料和简化结构，在降低水下进水风险的同时，能有效减少因连接件导致的图像畸变，提升iPhone的水下拍摄体验。

Apple、水中撮影の歪みを低減する一体型レンズ保護層の特許を取得

IT之家の報道によると、技術メディアのAppleinsiderが5月26日のブログで、Appleが新たな特許を獲得したことを報じました。この特許は、iPhoneを水中で撮影する際に発生する画像の歪みを最小限に抑えるための「一体型水中カメラ保護層」に関するものです。

既存の水中撮影アクセサリーの問題点

Appleはこの特許を通じて、水中撮影におけるイメージの歪み問題に焦点を当てています。現在市場で流通しているサードパーティ製の水中撮影用アクセサリーには、共通の課題が存在します。

  • 保護層の役割： レンズの前に保護層を追加し、水中の環境からカメラを隔離することは不可欠な要素です。
  • 歪みの発生可能性： しかし、この構造体自体が画像に歪み（distortion）を引き起こす原因となることがあります。

従来のドームポートは、浸水によって生じるイメージの変化を緩和するために設計されていますが、実際に装着する過程で、かえって新たな種類の歪みが発生するリスクがありました。

Appleが提示する革新的な解決策：単一の光学構造

Appleが提案するソリューションは、複数のレンズが単一の統合された防水光学層を共有する構造です。この構造は、複数のカメラモジュールすべてを覆いつつ、各レンズスタックの撮像プロセスに適合するように設計されています。

この保護層は、以下のような構成で柔軟に対応可能です。

  • 平面配置時： 複数のカメラが平面状に並んでいる場合、この統合光学部品は基本的に平らな形状で製造され、特定の箇所のみ凸状の構造を維持します。
  • 曲面配置時： カメラモジュールが曲面に配置されている場合、この光学層もその曲面に沿って成形され、各局所的なドームの曲率は下のレンズと正確に一致するように設計されます。

この特許において最も強調されているポイントは、保護層全体を単一の素材および単一の部品として構築することです。これにより、従来の設計で一般的だった継ぎ目（seams）、接着剤、その他の接合材料を排除することが可能になります。

結果として、この設計の改善は浸水のリスクを下げるだけでなく、構造体による接続部から発生していた追加の歪みをも低減する効果をもたらします。

まとめ

Appleは、水中撮影時の画像歪みを最小限に抑えるための「一体型レンズ保護層」に関する特許を取得しました。この技術は、単一の素材と構造を用いることで、従来のアクセサリーで見られた継ぎ目や接着剤による歪みを排除し、よりクリアな水中撮影を実現することを目指しています。

Apple Secures Patent for Integrated Lens Protection Layer to Reduce Underwater Shooting Distortion

According to IT之家, tech media outlet Appleinsider reported on May 26 that Apple has obtained a new patent. This patent relates to an integrated underwater camera protection layer designed to minimize distortion when filming underwater with the iPhone.

Issues with Existing Underwater Photography Accessories

Apple's patent focuses specifically on the problem of image distortion during underwater imaging. Third-party underwater photography accessories currently available on the market share common issues:

  • Role of the Protection Layer: It is essential to add a protective layer in front of the lens to isolate the camera from the aquatic environment.
  • Potential for Distortion: However, these structures themselves can cause image distortion.

While traditional dome ports are designed to mitigate imaging changes caused by immersion, they often risk introducing new types of distortion during the attachment process.

Apple's Innovative Solution: A Single Optical Structure

The solution proposed by Apple involves multiple lenses sharing a single integrated waterproof optical layer. This structure covers all camera modules while participating in the imaging process for each individual lens stack.

This protection layer can be designed flexibly based on the camera's layout:

  • Flat Arrangement: When multiple cameras are arranged on a flat plane, this integrated optical component is manufactured as a flat piece with only localized convex structures.
  • Curved Surface: If the camera modules are distributed across a curved surface, the optical layer can be molded to follow that curve, ensuring the curvature of each local dome matches the underlying lens exactly.

Another key point emphasized in this patent is manufacturing the entire protection layer from a single material and a single part. This allows for the removal of seams, adhesives, and other joining materials commonly found in existing designs.

Ultimately, these design improvements not only lower the risk of water ingress but also reduce the additional distortion caused by connecting structures.

Summary

Apple has patented an integrated, single-piece optical protection layer for its camera system to improve underwater photography. By replacing multi-part accessories with a unified material structure, Apple aims to eliminate seams and adhesives that cause image distortion. This design ensures high-quality imaging across both flat and curved camera arrangements while providing better protection against water.

技术方案细节

与传统的单镜头防护结构不同，Apple提出的解决方案是让多个摄像头共用一个统一的光学保护层：

  • 针对平面排列：若摄像头分布在平面上，该保护层可做成基本平面的结构，仅在局部保留凸起。

Apple Secures Patent for Integrated Lens Protection Layer to Reduce Underwater Distortion

According to reports from IT之家 and Appleinsider, Apple has obtained a new patent regarding an integrated underwater camera protection layer. The technology is designed to minimize image distortion that occurs when filming with an iPhone in underwater environments.

Issues with Existing Underwater Photography Accessories

Apple’s patent specifically addresses the problem of image distortion during underwater imaging. Third-party underwater photography accessories currently available on the market share common structural flaws:

  • Role of the Protection Layer: It is essential to add a protective layer in front of the lens to isolate the camera from the aquatic environment.
  • Potential for Distortion: These physical structures can often cause image distortion.

While traditional dome ports are designed to mitigate imaging changes caused by water immersion, the complex construction and attachment processes of these parts often risk introducing new types of distortion into the final image.

Apple's Innovative Solution: A Single Optical Structure

The solution proposed by Apple involves multiple lenses sharing a single integrated waterproof optical layer. This structure covers all camera modules while remaining fully integrated into the imaging process for each individual lens stack.

This protection layer is designed to be adaptable based on the hardware layout:

  • Flat Arrangement: When cameras are arranged in a flat plane, the integrated optical component is manufactured as a single flat piece with only localized convex structures.
  • Curved Surface: If camera modules are distributed across a curved surface, the optical layer can be molded to follow that curve, ensuring the curvature of each local dome matches the underlying lens exactly.

A key highlight of this patent is the construction of the entire protection layer from a single material and a single part. This design eliminates the seams, adhesives, and other joining materials commonly found in traditional designs.

Ultimately, these improvements not only lower the risk of water ingress but also reduce the additional distortion caused by complex connecting structures.

Summary

Apple has patented an integrated, single-piece optical protection layer to replace multi-part underwater accessories. By utilizing a unified material and structure, the design eliminates seams and adhesives that cause image distortion while improving durability against water ingress. This innovation aims to provide a clearer, more consistent imaging experience for iPhone users in aquatic environments.
