소개

IT之家 5월 8일 소식, 조요 테크(Zhong Yi Optics)는 오늘 Pittura 시리즈 30mm F2.4 중형 렌즈를 출시했습니다. 이 신제품은 대구경 디자인과 ED 저분산 렌즈를 특징으로 하며, Fujifilm GFX / 하셀블라드 XCD 마운트에 호환됩니다.

IT之家는 공식 위챗 계정을 통해 이 렌즈의 풀프레임 환산 초점 거리가 24mm이며, 풍경, 건축, 인문 및 환경 인물 사진 촬영에 적합하다는 것을 확인했습니다. F2.4 조리개는 더 풍부한 빛과 부드러운 배경 흐림 효과를 제공합니다.

Introduction

According to reports from IT Home on May 8, Zhong Yi Optics has released the Pittura series 30mm F2.4 medium format lens. This new product features a large-aperture design and ED low-dispersion lenses, and it is compatible with both Fujifilm GFX and Hasselblad XCD mounts.

IT Home confirmed via its official WeChat account that the lens has a full-frame equivalent focal length of 24mm, making it suitable for landscape, architecture, and humanistic/environmental portrait photography. The F2.4 aperture provides more light intake and smooth bokeh effects.

Specifications

  • Optical Structure: 12 groups, 13 elements
  • ED Low-Dispersion Lenses: 2
  • ASPH Aspherical Lenses: 1
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.2m
  • Weight: 550g
  • Filter Size: φ58mm
  • Maximum Magnification: 0.31x
  • Autofocus: Not supported

Price and Availability

The lens will be released on May 9. The manufacturer's suggested retail price is 3,580 RMB, with a special launch discount price of 3,380 RMB.

Summary

Zhong Yi Optics has launched the Pittura series 30mm F2.4 medium format lens for Fujifilm GFX and Hasselblad XCD mounts. Featuring ED low-dispersion lenses and a wide aperture, it provides a versatile 24mm equivalent focal length suitable for various photography styles including landscapes and portraits.
