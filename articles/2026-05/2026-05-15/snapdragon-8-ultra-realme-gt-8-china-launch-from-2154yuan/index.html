Design and Display

The realme True GT8 is available in three color options. Thickness varies slightly by model, and weight also differs depending on the selected color.

  • Thickness: 8.20mm (White / Navy), 8.30mm (Green)
  • Weight: Approx. 214g (White / Navy), Approx. 209g (Green)

The device features a 6.79-inch 2K, 144Hz "Sky Display," which shares the same specifications as the Pro model.

Performance and Durability

The hardware configuration is highly robust.

  • Processor: Snapdragon 8 Ultimate Edition x Gaming-specific GPU chip R1
  • Memory/Storage: LPDDR5X memory + UFS 4.0 storage
  • Battery and Charging: 7,000mAh battery with 100W ultra-fast charging support
  • Durability: IP69+IP68+IP66 dust and water resistance ratings
  • Security: Equipped with an ultrasonic fingerprint sensor

Camera System (Based on Ricoh GR)

The realme True GT8 integrates the Ricoh GR imaging system, favored by professional photographers, to provide a premium camera experience. The overall design and layout of the camera module emphasize ease of use for professional-grade shooting.

  • Main Lens: 50MP OIS main camera
  • Ultra-wide Lens: 8MP ultra-wide camera
  • Telephoto Lens: 50MP Super Light OIS Periscope telephoto lens (supporting 3.5x optical zoom)

The device implements key Ricoh GR features through both software and hardware optimizations.

  • Five Film Tones: Offers five unique film tones, including High Contrast B&W, Standard Photo, Negative, Normal, and Monochrome.
  • Customization Mode: Utilizes built-in 28mm and 40mm classic focal lengths; users can activate the signature Ricoh GR shutter style and immersive viewfinder mode with a single swipe.
  • Anti-Glare Main Lens: Developed in collaboration with Ricoh GR, this lens has passed Ricoh GR optical standard certification for anti-glare performance and high light transmission.
  • Filters and Watermarks: Provides exclusive filter combinations such as 'Ricoh Blue' and 'Ricoh Green,' along with five types of customizable photo watermarks to personalize the shooting experience.

Summary

The realme True GT8 is a high-performance device featuring the Snapdragon 8 Ultimate Edition, a massive 7,000mAh battery, and an advanced camera system developed in collaboration with Ricoh GR. It offers premium hardware specifications like UFS 4.0 storage and IP69 durability, while providing specialized photography features such as multiple film tones and anti-glare lens technology.
