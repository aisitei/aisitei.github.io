Product Introduction

Kandao Technology has launched the "Meeting Pro 2," a new video conferencing device priced at 9,899 RMB.

Key Features

  • Supports 4K resolution and HDR video

The Meeting Pro 2 by Kandao Technology is a video conferencing device that supports 4K HDR video and 360-degree person tracking. Additionally, it enhances meeting convenience by offering real-time multilingual subtitles and automated transcript generation.

The Meeting Pro 2 is a high-end video conferencing solution featuring 4K HDR imaging and advanced AI tracking to identify speakers automatically. It integrates sophisticated audio hardware with multi-language support to streamline professional communication across various platforms.
