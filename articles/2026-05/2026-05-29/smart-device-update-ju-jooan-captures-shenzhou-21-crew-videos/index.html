최근 낙하 지점 시계측 시스템은 새로운 장소로 이전하고 장비를 업그레이드하여, 귀환정 재진입, 낙하산 전개, 최종 착륙 등 주요 단계에서 정확한 측정 제어 능력을 확보했다고 밝혔습니다.

낙하 지점 시계측 시스템의 역할 및 개선 사항

인간 승무원 우주 임무 귀환에 필수적인 측정 제어 수단인 낙하 지점 시계측 시스템은 주로 다음과 같은 중요한 기능을 수행합니다:

  • 귀환정 고공 목표 포착
  • 자세 감지 및 궤적 추적
  • 실시간 영상 전송

이전 대비 하드웨어 구성의 업데이트는 물론, 협력 관측 모드와 지능형 식별 수준까지 진화했습니다. 새롭게 도입된 유도 알고리즘은 목표 검색, 인식 잠금, 연속 추적을 빠르고 정확하게 수행할 수 있습니다.

첨단 기술 적용: 딥러닝 및 고기동성 확보

술취안 위성 발사센터의 전문가에 따르면 이번 업그레이드를 통해 시스템은 다음과 같은 기술적 진보를 이루었습니다:

  • 지능형 서보 시스템 도입: 딥러닝 기반 목표 식별 알고리즘을 적용하여 대량의 샘플로 학습시켰습니다.
  • 정확도 향상: 이 알고리즘은 목표 특징을 빠르게 인식하고 정확하게 잠금으로써, 구름이나 장애물, 잡파 등의 영향을 효과적으로 줄여줍니다.
  • 추적 안정성 강화: 수동 보조 추적 방식보다 더 안정적이고 높은 정밀도를 가지며 오차를 최소화하여 신뢰성과 정확성을 크게 높였습니다.

또한, 다양한 돌발 상황에 대비하기 위해 고기동 측정 제어 지점을 추가했습니다. 이 지점들은 고비 사막이나 황무지 같은 복잡한 지역에 유연하게 배치되어 통신, 측정, 전송의 전체 과정을 독립적으로 수행할 수 있습니다.

탑재된 적외선 시스템에는 여러 필터가 추가되었으며, 이를 통해 화면 대비도와 선명도를 높여 귀환정이 재진입하여 낙하산 전개에 이르기까지의 상태 변화와 주변 환경을 정밀하게 기록합니다.

落点景象测量系统功能升级

作为载人航天返回任务的关键测控手段，落点景象测量系统主要负责在飞船从高空（100公里至着陆）过渡阶段提供核心保障。该系统承担以下关键职能：

  • 高空目标捕获与姿态检测
  • 轨迹跟踪与实时景象回传
  • 通过硬件更新、协同观测模式及智能识别水平的提升，实现更高效的自动化测控。

智能化测控技术创新

针对复杂环境下的信号弱、速度快等挑战，酒泉卫星发射中心对系统进行了多项关键技术升级：

  • 算法优化：引入基于深度学习的目标识别算法，通过大量样本训练实现快速搜索、识别锁定和连续跟踪，降低云层或障碍物的干扰。
  • 高机动测控点位：新增可部署于戈壁、荒漠等复杂地域的测控点位，具备独立完成通信、测量、传输全流程的能力。
  • 红外系统增强：加装多组滤光片以提高画面的对比度和清晰度，确保准确记录返回舱从再入到开伞前的状态变化。

任务准备与背景情况

目前，落点景象测量系统已完成联调联试，各项技术指标符合要求，具备执行神舟二十一号乘组返回任务的能力。

相关历史背景：2026年5月24日，神舟二十三号载人飞船成功发射；5月25日，中国航天史上第8次“太空会师”举行，包含首位来自香港的航天员入驻。

总结

酒泉卫星发射中心通过升级落点景象测量系统，引入深度学习算法和高机动测控点位，显著提升了在复杂环境下对神舟二十一号返回舱的跟踪精度。该系统已完成各项技术指标验证，为确保航天员安全返回提供了坚实的测控保障。

新天地21号の宇宙飛行士帰還およびシステムのアップグレード

IT之家の報道によると、中国中央電視（CCTV）のニュースにおいて、新天地21号宇宙飛行士グループの帰還が間近に迫っていることが報じられました。宇宙船の帰還艇は、大気圏再突入後の高高度遷移段階（地上から約100km）において、信号が弱まり降下速度が加速する際に、複数の測定制御エリアを通じてリアルタイムの座標と機体の姿勢を記録します。

最近、落地点時計測定システムは新しい場所へ移設され、機器のアップグレードが行われました。これにより、帰還艇の再突入、パラシュートの展開、最終着陸といった主要な段階において、正確な測定・制御能力を確保したことが明らかになりました。

落地点時計測定システムの役割と改善点

有人宇宙ミッションの帰還に不可欠な測定手段である「落地点時計測定システム」は、主に以下のような重要な機能を果たします。

  • 帰還艇の高高度目標捕捉
  • 姿勢検知および軌跡追跡
  • リアルタイム映像伝送

以前の構成と比較してハードウェアの更新が行われただけでなく、協調観測モードやインテリジェントな識別レベルまで進化しました。新たに導入された誘導アルゴリズムにより、目標の探索、認識のロック、および継続的な追跡を迅速かつ正確に実行することが可能になりました。

最先端技術の適用：ディープラーニングと高機動性の確保

術川衛星発射センターの専門家によると、今回のアップグレードを通じてシステムは以下の技術的進歩を遂げました。

  • インテリジェント・サーボシステムの導入： ディープラーニングに基づく目標識別アルゴリズムを適用し、大量のサンプルを用いて学習を行いました。
  • 精度の向上： このアルゴリズムはターゲットの特徴を迅速に認識して正確にロックするため、雲や障害物、ノイズなどの影響を効果的に低減します。
  • 追跡安定性の強化： 手動補助による追跡よりも安定性が高く、精度も高いため、誤差を最小限に抑え、信頼性と正確性を大幅に向上させました。

また、さまざまな突発的な事態に対応するため、高機動測定ポイントが追加されました。これらの地点は、ゴビ砂漠や荒野のような複雑な地域に柔軟に配置され、通信、測定、送信の全プロセスを独立して遂行することができます。

搭載されている赤外線システムには複数のフィルターが追加され、画面のコントラストと鮮明度が向上しました。これにより、帰還艇が再突入してからパラシュートを展開するまでの状態変化や周囲の環境を精密に記録することが可能になります。

Return of Shenzhou 21 Crew and System Upgrades

According to reports from CCTV as cited by IT Home, the return of the Shenzhou 21 crew is imminent. During the high-altitude transition phase—approximately 100km above the Earth's surface where signal strength weakens and descent speed increases—the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center utilizes multiple measurement control zones to record the real-time coordinates and attitude of the spacecraft.

The landing point surveying system has been relocated to a new site and upgraded with advanced equipment. These improvements ensure precise measurement and control during critical phases, including reentry, parachute deployment, and final landing.

Role and Improvements of the Landing Point Surveying System

As a vital measurement and control tool for manned space missions, the landing point surveying system performs several critical functions:

  • High-altitude target acquisition
  • Attitude detection and trajectory tracking
  • Real-time video transmission

In addition to hardware upgrades, the system has evolved to include collaborative observation modes and intelligent identification capabilities. Newly implemented guidance algorithms enable rapid search, recognition locking, and continuous tracking of the target.

Advanced Technology: Deep Learning and High Mobility

Experts at the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center stated that these upgrades have resulted in several technical advancements:

  • Intelligent Servo System: A deep learning-based target identification algorithm was implemented, trained on a vast amount of data samples.
  • Enhanced Accuracy: This algorithm quickly identifies and locks onto target features, effectively minimizing the impact of clouds, obstacles, and noise.
  • Improved Tracking Stability: The system offers higher precision and stability compared to manual-assisted tracking methods, significantly reducing errors and increasing reliability.

Furthermore, high-mobility measurement control points have been added to handle various contingencies. These points can be flexibly deployed in complex environments such as the Gobi Desert or wasteland, where they can independently perform communication, measurement, and transmission processes.

The integrated infrared systems now feature multiple filters to enhance contrast and clarity. This allows for precise recording of the spacecraft's status and surrounding environment from reentry through parachute deployment.

System Verification and Future Plans

The Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center confirmed that the landing point surveying system has completed integration testing and meets all mission requirements for the Shenzhou 21 return. The center plans to continue optimizing processes and refining standards to ensure the successful completion of measurement and control missions with high-level technical performance.

Summary

The upgrade of the landing point surveying system for the Shenzhou 21 mission marks a significant shift toward integrating AI and deep learning into space exploration infrastructure. By enhancing target identification, tracking stability, and environmental adaptability, these advancements significantly improve the safety and precision of spacecraft recovery operations.

During the high-altitude transition phase—where signal strength weakens and descent speed increases at approximately 100km above the ground—the Shuzhou Satellite Launch Center utilizes multiple measurement control zones to record the real-time coordinates and attitude of the spacecraft.

The landing site observation system has been relocated and upgraded with new equipment. These improvements ensure precise measurement and control capabilities during critical stages, including reentry, parachute deployment, and final landing.

Role and Improvements of the Landing Site Observation System

As a vital measurement and control means for crewed space missions, the landing site observation system performs several essential functions:

  • High-altitude target capture
  • Attitude detection and trajectory tracking
  • Real-time image transmission

Beyond hardware updates, the system has evolved to include collaborative observation modes and enhanced intelligent identification levels. Newly implemented guidance algorithms allow for rapid target searching, recognition locking, and continuous tracking.

Advanced Technology: Deep Learning and High Mobility

Experts from the Shuzhou Satellite Launch Center stated that these upgrades have resulted in several technical advancements:

  • Intelligent Servo System: The system now utilizes deep learning-based target identification algorithms trained on massive datasets.
  • Improved Accuracy: These algorithms can quickly identify target features and lock onto them, effectively reducing the impact of clouds, obstacles, and noise.
  • Enhanced Tracking Stability: By providing higher precision and stability than manual assistance methods, the system minimizes errors and significantly improves reliability.

To prepare for various contingencies, high-mobility measurement control points have been added. These points can be flexibly deployed in complex environments, such as deserts or wastelands, to independently perform communication, measurement, and transmission processes.

Furthermore, the integrated infrared systems now include multiple filters to increase contrast and clarity. This allows for precise recording of the spacecraft's status and its surrounding environment from reentry through parachute deployment.

System Verification and Future Plans

The Shuzhou Satellite Launch Center confirmed that the landing site observation system has completed integration tests, with all technical indicators meeting mission requirements for the Shenzhou 21 return. The center plans to continue optimizing processes and standardizing procedures to ensure the successful completion of measurement and control missions in the future.

Summary

The Shenzhou 21 spacecraft's return is supported by a significantly upgraded landing site observation system that integrates deep learning algorithms for improved target tracking. These advancements, including high-mobility points and enhanced infrared filtering, ensure precision during critical phases like reentry and parachute deployment. These upgrades demonstrate an increased level of reliability and technical sophistication in China's space exploration infrastructure.
