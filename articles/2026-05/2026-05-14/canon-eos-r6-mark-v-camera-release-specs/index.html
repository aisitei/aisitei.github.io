产品定位与核心特性

Canon 正式推出面向视频创作场景的 EOS R6 V。该机型搭载 3250 万像素 7K 传感器，并配备 DIGIC X 图像处理器。为了优化视频拍摄体验，它取消了电子取景器和机械快门，仅提供约 162 万点的 3.0 英寸可翻转 LCD 屏幕作为监看设备。此外，机身集成了电动变焦拨杆、Tally 指示灯以及专为自拍创作者设计的录制按钮。

物理规格与设计

  • 尺寸：141.8 × 83.3 × 79.8mm
  • 重量：约 688 克（含电池及单张存储卡）

EOS R6 V 支持多种高规格的 RAW 视频录制模式：

  • 超采样 4K DCI / UHD Fine：最高 60p
  • 非超采样 4K：最高 120p（支持录音）

该机配备主动散热系统，风扇提供 Off、On、Auto 三种模式及多档转速调节。在开启风扇的情况下，4K 及 7K 等多种录制模式基本无过热限制。续航方面，支持新款 LP-E6P 电池（兼容旧款但部分功能受限），并支持通过 USB-C Power Delivery 进行外接供电。

防抖与存储配置

在防抖技术上，EOS R6 V 内置 IBIS 五轴机身防抖，配合具备光学防抖的 RF 镜头可实现高强度补偿。同时提供多种 Movie Digital IS 模式（Standard、Enhanced 及结合对焦数据的 Subject Tracking）。

  • 双卡槽设计：一个支持最高 8TB 的 CFexpress Type B (VPG400) 卡槽，一个为 UHS-II SD 卡。
  • 录制功能：支持接力录制、主+代理、主+副卡或同步备份等多种模式。

总结

Canon EOS R6 V 是一款专为视频创作者设计的专业机型，核心优势在于其强大的 7K RAW 录制能力、主动散热系统以及针对竖拍场景优化的硬件设计。虽然放弃了机械快门和取景器，但它通过高性能传感器、丰富的防抖算法和双卡槽配置，提供了极高的视频创作灵活性。国行版本定价为 13999 元。

Overview and Design

Canon has officially released the EOS R6 V, a flagship model specifically designed for video production environments. The camera features a 32.5-megapixel 7K sensor and supports in-camera RAW recording, In-Body Image Stabilization (IBIS), an active cooling system, and a dedicated interface for vertical grip mounting.

The device measures 141.8 x 83.3 x 79.8mm and weighs approximately 688g with the battery and one memory card installed. The grip includes a built-in interface for vertical shooting, while the top is equipped with a dedicated electronic zoom dial and a Tally lamp. Additionally, a recording button has been added to the front of the body for the convenience of video creators.

Key Features and Limitations

According to the official announcement, this camera does not feature an Electronic Viewfinder (EVF) or a mechanical shutter. The only device for image preview and monitoring is a 3.0-inch vari-angle LCD screen with approximately 1.62 million dots. Furthermore, the camera relies entirely on an electronic shutter, which supports speeds up to 1/8000s.

Consequently, this model may not be suitable for users who primarily rely on viewfinders to compose shots or those who frequently use flash for still photography.

Sensor and Core Performance

The EOS R6 V utilizes the same 32.5-megapixel full-frame CMOS sensor as the EOS C50 and EOS R6 Mark III, paired with the DIGIC X image processor. The maximum effective resolution reaches approximately 6960 x 4640 pixels.

  • Sensor Size and Megapixels: Full-frame CMOS sensor, 32.5 megapixels
  • Image Processor: DIGIC X

Video Recording Specifications

The camera supports a wide range of resolutions and frame rates, offering high flexibility through various RAW formats.

  • 7K RAW Video: Can record 7K RAW video directly to CFexpress cards.
  • Supported Formats: Supports both Standard RAW and Light RAW formats.

[Maximum Specifications by RAW Mode]

  • 7K Light RAW Mode: Supports up to 60p (17:9, 6960 x 4640) recording.
  • Standard RAW Mode: Supports up to 30p recording.

Additionally, 7K Open Gate recording in a 3:2 aspect ratio is supported in both RAW formats, capable of up to 30p.

  • 4K DCI / UHD Fine (Under 7K): Supports up to 60p.
  • 4K Uncompressed (Non-oversampling): Supports up to 120p (includes audio recording).
  • 2K DCI or Full HD: Supports up to 180p.

[Codecs and Data Rates]

  • Compressed Codecs: Provides XF-HEVC S (4:2:2 10-bit or 4:2:0 10-bit) and XF-AVC S (4:2:2 10-bit or 4:2:0 8-bit).
  • Intra Coding Maximum Data Rates: Supports up to 1200 Mbps for 4K Intra and 1800 Mbps for 2K Intra, maintaining high bitrates.

The EOS R6 V utilizes the same 32.5 million pixel full-frame CMOS sensor as the EOS C50 and EOS R6 Mark III, powered by the DIGIC X image processor. The maximum effective resolution reaches approximately 6960 x 4640 pixels.

  • Sensor Size and Pixels: Full-frame CMOS sensor, 32.5 million pixels
  • Image Processor: DIGIC X

Video Recording Specifications

The camera supports a wide range of resolutions and frame rates, offering high flexibility through multiple RAW formats.

  • 7K RAW Video: Supports direct 7K RAW video recording to CFexpress cards.
  • Supported Formats: Supports both Standard RAW and Light RAW formats.

[Maximum Specifications by RAW Mode]

  • 7K Light RAW Mode: Supports up to 60p (17:9, 6960 x 4640) recording.
  • Standard RAW Mode: Supports up to 30p recording.

Additionally, 7K Open Gate recording in a 3:2 aspect ratio is supported in both RAW formats at up to 30p.

  • 4K DCI / UHD Fine (Below 7K): Supports up to 60p.
  • 4K Unoversampled (Non-oversampling): Supports up to 120p (includes audio recording).
  • 2K DCI or Full HD: Supports up to 180p.

[Codec and Data Rates]

  • Compressed Codecs: Provides XF-HEVC S (4:2:2 10-bit or 4:2:0 10-bit) and XF-AVC S (4:2:2 10-bit or 4:2:0 8-bit).
  • Intra Coding Max Data Rates: Supports high bitrates with up to 1200 Mbps for 4K Intra and up to 1800 Mbps for 2K Intra.

Cooling and Battery

The camera features an active cooling system where the fan supports Off, On, and Auto modes, with adjustable rotation speeds including High, Medium, Low, or Stop.

According to Canon's test data at 23°C, recording limitations due to overheating were rarely encountered in 4K DCI 59.94p Standard Long-GOP, 4K DCI 29.97p, and 2K DCI 179.8p modes when the fan was active.

However, in 7K Light RAW + 2K Proxy recording mode, the recording time is approximately 33 to 37 minutes with the fan off, but exceeds 120 minutes of stable recording when the fan is activated.

[Battery and Power]

  • Battery: When equipped with the new LP-E6P battery, it provides approximately 1 hour and 10 minutes of usage in 7K Light RAW 60p or 4K DCI Fine Standard Long-GOP 60p modes.
  • Compatibility: Older LP-E6N and LP-E6NH batteries are compatible, though some features like Wi-Fi, fan settings, and percentage power display will be disabled.
  • External Power: Supports USB-C Power Delivery (PD), allowing for external power connection via the Canon PD-E2 adapter or use in live streaming and remote shooting.

Image Stabilization and Storage

[Image Stabilization]

  • IBIS (In-Body Image Stabilization): Features built-in 5-axis body image stabilization.
  • Stabilization Performance: When used with the RF24-105mm F2.8 L IS USM lens, it provides up to 7.5 stops of correction in the center and approximately 7 stops at the edges.
  • Cooperative Stabilization: Automatically activates cooperative stabilization when paired with RF lenses featuring optical image stabilization.
  • Digital Modes: Movie Digital IS offers Standard, Enhanced, and Subject Tracking modes. Specifically, Subject Tracking IS utilizes autofocus data to stabilize specific subjects, though it results in some cropping of the field of view.

[Storage Devices]

  • Slot Configuration: Features two card slots.
  • CFexpress Type B Slot: A CFexpress Type B (VPG400) slot supporting cards up to 8TB.
  • SD Card Slot: Includes a UHS-II SD card slot, enabling various modes such as relay recording, primary/backup camera setups, or synchronized backup.

Summary

The Canon EOS R6 V is a high-performance flagship camera specifically engineered for video creators, featuring a 32.5MP full-frame sensor and advanced 7K RAW recording capabilities. While it omits an EVF and mechanical shutter to prioritize its video features, it compensates with an active cooling system, robust IBIS stabilization, and specialized hardware like Tally lights and vertical shooting support. It is designed to provide professional-grade results in demanding environments while offering high flexibility for diverse production needs.
