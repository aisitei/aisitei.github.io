후지필름 미국 공장, 해킹당해 약 8TB 규모의 1,100만 건 이상 파일 유출

2026-05-14

폭스콘 미국 공장 사이버 공격 발생

IT之家에 따르면, 기술 매체 Appleinsider는 지난 5월 12일 보도자료를 통해 Fujifilm의 협력사 Foxconn이 미국 위스콘신주 마운트 플레전트(Mount Pleasant)에 위치한 공장이 이번 달(2026년 5월) 사이버 공격을 받았다고 전했습니다.

유출된 데이터 규모 및 내용

랜섬웨어 조직 Nitrogen가 이 공격의 배후를 자처하며, 총 8TB에 달하는 1,100만 건 이상의 파일이 유출되었다고 주장했습니다. Nitrogen는 탈취된 자료에 Intel, Apple, Google, Dell, NVIDIA 등 다수의 고객과 관련된 기밀 지침, 프로젝트 자료 및 설계 도면이 포함되어 있다고 밝혔습니다.

IT之家가 분석한 샘플 자료를 바탕으로 볼 때, 유출 파일들은 특히 Fujifilm의 전기 엔지니어링 팀 자료에 치우쳐 있었습니다. 주요 내용은 다음과 같습니다:

  • 온도 센서 및 집적 회로(IC) 관련 정보
  • 회로 기판 배치 설계 자료
  • 텍사스주 휴스턴 공장의 재무 관련 문서

더욱 우려되는 점은, 샘플에서 AMD, Intel, Google 프로젝트와 관련된 네트워크 토폴로지 문건이 발견되었다는 것입니다. 이 자료에는 서버 프로세서, 슬롯 및 기타 구성 요소에 대한 내용이 포함되어 있습니다.

공격의 심각성과 잠재적 위험

전문가들은 이러한 자료들이 단순한 설계 스케치가 아니라 실제 인프라 구조와 유사한 아키텍처 지도에 가깝다고 분석했습니다. 만약 이 정보가 사실이라면, 공격자들은 이를 이용해 데이터센터의 취약점을 파악하고 후속 공격 범위를 넓힐 수 있습니다.

다만, 해당 매체는 현재 공개된 샘플 자료만을 기준으로 볼 때 이번 보안 사고가 Apple사에 미치는 직접적인 충격은 상대적으로 작다고 지적했습니다. 마운트 플레전트 공장은 주로 TV와 데이터 서버를 생산하며, Apple 기기를 주력으로 다루지 않기 때문입니다. 따라서 유출된 샘플에서는 Apple의 원형 도면, Fujifilm-Foxconn의 Apple 제품 개발팀 문서, 또는 Apple 품질 관리 데이터는 발견되지 않았습니다.

하지만 위험이 완전히 사라진 것은 아닙니다. Foxconn은 여러 지역 공장을 내부 가상 사설망(VPN)으로 보호하며, 공장 간 또는 Apple과의 업무 소통은 이메일을 통해 이루어지기 때문에, 공격자가 휴스턴 등 다른 공장의 파일을 확보했다는 사실 자체가 침투 범위가 한 곳에 국한되지 않았음을 의미합니다. 따라서 공장 간의 메일이나 파일 공유 서버가 향후 위험 요소로 남을 수 있습니다.

网络攻击概况

Foxconn 位于美国威斯康星州 Mount Pleasant 的工厂在 2026 年 5 月遭遇勒索组织 Nitrogen 的网络攻击。该组织声称窃取了超过 1100 万份文件，总计约 8TB。这些内容包含大量机密指令、项目资料和图纸。

涉及客户与泄露数据

被盗取的数据涉及多个知名科技公司：

  • Intel
  • Apple
  • Google
  • Dell
  • NVIDIA
  • AMD

具体泄露内容包括电气工程团队的温度传感器、集成电路、板卡布局，以及德克萨斯州 Houston 工厂的财务文件。此外，还包含涉及服务器处理器、插槽等组件的网络拓扑文档，这些数据可能被用于识别数据中心的薄弱环节。

生产影响与风险评估

由于 Mount Pleasant 工厂主要生产电视和数据服务器而非 Apple 设备，目前初步分析显示对 Apple 的直接冲击相对较小，尚未发现相关产品的研发或质量控制数据。然而，跨工厂的内部网络（VPN）及邮件系统仍可能成为后续的风险点。

此次攻击已导致实际生产中断。由于 Wi-Fi 被切断且核心基础设施受损，该厂的制造环节在 5 月初受到干扰，直到 5 月 12 日才恢复正常。

总结

Foxconn 威斯康星州工厂遭勒索组织 Nitrogen 攻击，导致约 8TB 的机密数据外泄。虽然目前涉及 Apple 的核心产品数据较少，但由于包含多个大厂的硬件架构图及生产线受到中断，该事件对供应链安全构成了显著威胁。

Foxconn米国工場でサイバー攻撃が発生

IT之家の報道によると、テックメディアのAppleinsiderは5月12日のプレスリリースにおいて、FujifilmのパートナーであるFoxconnのウィスコンシン州マウントプレザント（Mount Pleasant）にある工場が、今月（2026年5月）サイバー攻撃を受けたと報じました。

流出したデータの規模と内容

ランサムウェアグループ「Nitrogen」がこの攻撃の犯行を主張しており、合計8TBに達する1,100万件以上のファイルが流出したと主張しています。Nitrogenは、奪取されたデータにはIntel、Apple、Google、Dell、NVIDIAなど、多数の顧客に関連する機密指示、プロジェクト資料、および設計図が含まれていると明らかにしました。

IT之家が分析したサンプルデータに基づくと、流出したファイルは特にFujifilmの電気エンジニアリングチームのデータに集中していました。主な内容は以下の通りです：

  • 温度センサーおよび集積回路（IC）に関する情報
  • プリント基板（PCB）の配置設計資料
  • テキサス州ヒューストン工場の財務関連文書

さらに懸念される点として、サンプルの中からAMD、Intel、Googleのプロジェクトに関連するネットワークトポロジの文書が見つかったことです。これらの資料には、サーバープロセッサ、スロット、およびその他のコンポーネントに関する内容が含まれています。

攻撃の深刻さと潜在的なリスク

専門家は、これらの資料が単なる設計スケッチではなく、実際のインフラ構造に近いアーキテクチャマップであると分析しています。もしこの情報が事実であれば、攻撃者はこれを利用してデータセンターの脆弱性を特定し、後続の攻撃範囲を広げることが可能です。

しかし、同メディアは現在公開されているサンプル資料のみに基づけば、今回のセキュリティ事案がApple社に与える直接的な影響は比較的小さいと指摘しています。マウントプレザント工場は主にテレビやデータサーバーを製造しており、Apple製品を主力として扱っていないためです。そのため、流出したサンプルからは、Appleの原型図、Fujifilm-FoxconnのApple製品開発チームの文書、またはAppleの品質管理データなどは発見されていません。

しかし、リスクが完全に消えたわけではありません。Foxconnは複数の地域工場を内部仮想プライベートネットワーク（VPN）で保護しており、工場間やAppleとの業務上のやり取りはメールを通じて行われるため、攻撃者がヒューストンなど他の工場のファイルを入手したという事実は、侵入範囲が一箇所に限定されていないことを意味します。したがって、工場間のメールやファイル共有サーバーが今後のリスク要因として残る可能性があります。

生産および運営への影響

今回の攻撃はすでに生産ラインにも影響を及ぼしています。現地メディアによると、当該工場は2026年5月初旬にネットワーク遮断を経験し、生産が約1週間中断された後にようやく正常化したとのことです。

別の報道によれば、5月1日の米国東部時間午前7時頃からWi-Fiが遮断され、午前11時には基幹インフラまで妨害を受けたため、製造工程が5月12日まで正常化できなかった可能性も指摘されています。

総評

今回の事件は、グローバルなITサプライチェーンの脆弱性を改めて浮き彫りにしました。現在流出したサンプルだけではAppleへの直接的な脅威は低いと評価されるものの、攻撃者が複数の工場や基幹インフラを網羅する広範囲な情報を奪取した点は深刻です。企業には、物理的なセキュリティだけでなく、内部ネットワークやファイル共有システム全体にわたる強力なサイバー防御体制の構築が不可欠です。

まとめ

Foxconnのウィスコンシン州工場で大規模なサイバー攻撃が発生し、IntelやAppleなどを含む主要企業の機密データが流出しました。生産ラインへの影響により数日間の稼働停止が発生しており、サプライチェーン全体の脆弱性が露呈した形です。企業は、広範囲なネットワーク侵入を防ぐため、より強固な内部セキュリティ体制の構築が求められます。

Cyberattack Hits Foxconn Factory in the United States

According to IT Home, tech outlet Appleinsider reported on May 12 that a Foxconn factory—a partner of Fujifilm located in Mount Pleasant, Wisconsin—was targeted by a cyberattack this month (May 2026).

Scale and Content of Leaked Data

The ransomware group Nitrogen claimed responsibility for the attack, asserting that over 11 million files totaling approximately 8TB were leaked. Nitrogen stated that the stolen data includes confidential instructions, project materials, and design blueprints related to several major clients, including Intel, Apple, Google, Dell, and NVIDIA.

Based on an analysis of sample data provided by IT Home, the leaked files are heavily concentrated on records from Fujifilm's electrical engineering team. Key contents include:

  • Information regarding temperature sensors and integrated circuits (IC)
  • Circuit board layout design materials
  • Financial documents related to the Houston, Texas factory

More concerning is the discovery of network topology documents related to AMD, Intel, and Google projects within the samples. These documents contain information regarding server processors, slots, and other components.

Severity and Potential Risks

Experts analyze these documents as architectural maps similar to actual infrastructure rather than simple design sketches. If this information is accurate, attackers could utilize it to identify vulnerabilities in data centers and expand the scope of subsequent attacks.

However, the outlet noted that based on the currently released samples, the direct impact on Apple is relatively small. This is because the Mount Pleasant factory primarily produces TVs and data servers rather than a high volume of Apple devices. Consequently, no original blueprints for Apple products, Fujifilm-Foxconn internal development documents for Apple products, or Apple quality control data were found in the leaked samples.

Nevertheless, the risk is not entirely eliminated. Foxconn protects its various regional factories through an internal Virtual Private Network (VPN), and communication between factories or with partners like Apple is conducted via email. The fact that attackers obtained files from other locations, such as Houston, suggests that the breach was not confined to a single site. Therefore, inter-factory emails and shared file servers remain potential risk factors.

Impact on Production and Operations

The attack has already impacted production lines. According to local media, the factory experienced network outages in early May, with production only returning to normal after approximately one week of downtime.

Other reports indicate that Wi-Fi was cut off around 7:00 AM ET on May 1, and core infrastructure was disrupted by 11:00 AM. This may have caused manufacturing processes to remain disrupted until May 12.

Summary

A cyberattack by the Nitrogen ransomware group has resulted in the theft of approximately 8TB of data from a Foxconn factory in Wisconsin, involving sensitive information for major tech giants like Apple and Intel. While the immediate impact on specific Apple product lines appears limited due to the facility's primary focus on TVs and servers, the breach highlights significant vulnerabilities in cross-factory networks and infrastructure. The incident underscores the necessity for robust cyber defenses across all internal networks and file-sharing systems within the global supply chain.

Cyberattack on Foxconn's U.S. Factory

According to IT Home, the tech outlet Appleinsider reported that a Foxconn factory located in Mount Pleasant, Wisconsin—a manufacturing partner of Fujifilm—was targeted by a cyberattack in May 2026.

Scale and Content of Leaked Data

The ransomware group Nitrogen claimed responsibility for the attack, asserting that over 11 million files totaling approximately 8TB were exfiltrated. The stolen data reportedly includes confidential instructions, project materials, and design blueprints related to several major clients, including Intel, Apple, Google, Dell, and NVIDIA.

Based on sample data analyzed by IT Home, the leaked files were primarily concentrated on Fujifilm's electrical engineering team records. Key contents included:

  • Information regarding temperature sensors and integrated circuits (IC)
  • Circuit board layout design materials
  • Financial documents from a factory in Houston, Texas

More concerningly, network topology documents related to AMD, Intel, and Google projects were discovered. These documents contain details regarding server processors, slots, and other components.

Severity of the Attack and Potential Risks

Experts analyzed these materials as being close to "architecture maps" rather than simple design sketches. If accurate, attackers could utilize this information to identify vulnerabilities in data centers and expand the scope of subsequent attacks.

However, the outlet noted that based on currently available samples, the direct impact on Apple is relatively low. The Mount Pleasant factory primarily produces TVs and data servers rather than focusing on Apple devices. Consequently, no original blueprints for Apple products, internal documents from the Fujifilm-Foxconn Apple product development team, or Apple quality control data were found in the leaked samples.

Nevertheless, the risk is not entirely eliminated. Foxconn protects its various regional factories via an internal Virtual Private Network (VPN), and communication between factories or with Apple is conducted through email. The fact that attackers successfully obtained files from other locations, such as Houston, suggests that the breach was not confined to a single site. Therefore, cross-factory emails and shared file servers remain potential risk factors.

Impact on Production and Operations

The attack has already impacted production lines. Local reports indicate that the factory experienced a network outage in early May 2026, with production only returning to normal after approximately one week.

Other reports suggest that Wi-Fi was cut starting around 7:00 AM ET on May 1st, and core infrastructure was disrupted by 11:00 AM. This may have prevented the manufacturing process from normalizing until May 12th.

Summary

The incident highlights significant vulnerabilities within the global IT supply chain following a massive data breach at a Foxconn facility in Wisconsin. While the immediate impact on Apple appears limited due to the specific products manufactured at that site, the theft of extensive infrastructure and multi-factory data poses a serious long-term risk. The event underscores the critical need for robust cyber defenses across internal networks and shared communication systems.
