구글 AI 스크린샷 앱 'Pixel Screenshots', 모바일 넘어 Aluminum OS 데스크톱 진출 기대감

2026-05-13

Google Pixel Screenshots, 데스크톱 확장 테스트 중

IT之家에 따르면, 기술 매체 AndroidAuthority가 지난 5월 11일 보도한 바에 의하면, Google이 'Pixel Screenshots' 앱을 데스크톱 기기로 확장하는 테스트를 진행하고 있는 것으로 알려졌습니다. 이 기능은 더 이상 Pixel 스마트폰에만 국한되지 않을 것이며, 향후 Aluminum OS의 기반을 마련할 가능성이 제기되고 있습니다.

해당 매체는 Play 스토어 버전의 Pixel Screenshots 앱에서 1.26.151.00.desktop.release라는 단서를 발견했습니다. 이 업데이트가 2026년 4월 14일로 지정된 점을 미루어 볼 때, Google이 해당 앱에 데스크톱 형태를 개발하고 있음을 시사합니다.

참고로, Pixel Screenshots는 Google이 Pixel 스마트폰 사용자에게 제공하는 스크린샷 관리 기능입니다. 현재 이 기능은 Pixel 9 및 Pixel 10 시리즈에서만 지원되며, 구형 Pixel 사용자는 여전히 사용할 수 없습니다.

기능 및 활용성

이 앱의 핵심은 단순히 스크린샷을 저장하는 것을 넘어섭니다. 온디바이스 AI를 활용하여 내용을 자동으로 정리해주므로, 사용자가 나중에 정보의 단서를 따라 스크린샷을 검색할 수 있는 '검색 가능한 자료실' 역할을 합니다.

Google이 공개한 시나리오에 따르면, 사용자가 웹페이지에서 특정 정보를 발견했지만 당장 메모하거나 복사/붙여넣기를 할 시간이 없을 때, 단순히 스크린샷만 찍어두면 나중에 해당 내용을 쉽게 다시 확인할 수 있습니다.

  • 자동 분류 및 처리: Pixel Screenshots는 캡처된 스크린샷을 자동으로 분류하고 온디바이스 AI 모델로 처리합니다.
  • 실용성 증대: 주소, 상품 정보, 여행 일정 등 스크린샷으로 저장하는 경우가 많은 사용자에게 기존의 일반 앨범보다 훨씬 유용한 기능을 제공할 것으로 예상됩니다.

데스크톱 환경 적응과 Aluminum OS

현재 테스트 과정에서는 눈에 띄는 새로운 기능은 발견되지 않았으며, 심지어 일반적인 휴대폰 버전처럼 앱 서랍에 진입점이 표시되지 않는다는 점도 주목할 만합니다. 이는 Google이 우선적으로 하위 구조(Underlying Adaptation)만 완성했을 가능성을 시사하며, 인터페이스나 사용 방식 등은 아직 완전히 준비되지 않은 상태일 수 있습니다.

AndroidAuthority는 Google 내부에서 데스크톱 버전의 Pixel Screenshots를 개발 중이며, 목표 플랫폼으로 Aluminum OS가 거론된다고 분석했습니다. Aluminum OS는 루머로만 전해지는 Google의 데스크톱 시스템으로, 일반적인 노트북이나 데스크톱 환경에 최적화되어 있으며 안드로이드 앱 실행을 지원하는 것이 특징입니다.

총평

Google이 Pixel Screenshots 기능을 데스크톱 환경으로 확장하려는 움직임은 모바일 스크린샷 관리의 개념을 한 단계 발전시키고 있습니다. 온디바이스 AI를 활용한 검색 가능한 자료실 기능은 사용 편의성을 극대화하며, Aluminum OS와 같은 새로운 플랫폼에서도 강력한 생산성 도구로 자리매김할 잠재력을 보여줍니다.

Pixel Screenshots 桌面版测试

科技媒体 AndroidAuthority 报道，谷歌正在测试将 Pixel Screenshots 应用扩展至桌面设备。通过挖掘 Play 商店中的应用信息，发现了一个版本号为 1.26.151.00.desktop.release 的线索（更新日期标注为 2026 年 4 月 14 日），表明谷歌正在尝试为该应用开发桌面形态。

核心功能与技术特性

Pixel Screenshots 不同于传统的截图保存工具，它利用端侧 AI 模型对内容进行自动整理。用户可以根据信息线索回查截图，从而建立一个可搜索的资料库。目前该功能在移动端仅支持 Pixel 9 和 Pixel 10 系列。

  • 核心技术：集成端侧 AI 处理能力
  • 应用场景：快速记录并检索地址、商品信息或行程内容
  • 当前状态：桌面版测试版本尚未出现在应用抽屉中，可能仍处于底层适配阶段

战略布局与系统关联

该应用的桌面化尝试被认为可能为 Aluminum OS 铺路。Aluminum OS 是传闻中谷歌正在开发的、支持运行安卓应用的桌面或笔记本专用系统。通过将 Pixel Screenshots 带入桌面端，谷歌有望增强其在生产力领域的生态布局。

总结

谷歌正通过发布特定版本的 Pixel Screenshots 来测试该应用在桌面端的适配情况。该工具利用 AI 技术将截图转化为可搜索的数据库，并被视为推动新桌面系统 Aluminum OS 的重要步骤。

Google Pixel Screenshots、デスクトップへの拡張をテスト中

ITのニュースサイト「IT之家」および技術メディア「AndroidAuthority」の報道によると、Googleは「Pixel Screenshots」アプリをデスクトップデバイスへ拡張するためのテストを実施していることが判明しました。この機能は今後スマートフォンに限定されることはなく、将来的にAluminum OSの基盤となる可能性も浮上しています。

同メディアは、Playストア版のPixel Screenshotsアプリにおいて「1.26.151.00.desktop.release」というバージョンを確認しました。このアップデートが2026年4月14日に設定されていることから、Googleが当該アプリのデスクトップ形態の開発を進めていることが示唆されます。

なお、Pixel ScreenshotsはGoogleがPixelスマートフォンユーザー向けに提供するスクリーンショット管理機能です。現在、この機能はPixel 9およびPixel 10シリーズでのみサポートされており、旧モデルのPixelユーザーはまだ利用することができません。

機能と利便性

このアプリの核心は、単にスクリーンショットを保存するだけではありません。オンデバイスAIを活用して内容を自動的に整理するため、ユーザーが後から情報の断片を辿って検索できる「検索可能なアーカイブ」としての役割を果たします。

Googleが公開したシナリオによれば、ユーザーがウェブページで特定の情報を見つけたものの、その場ですぐにメモしたりコピー＆ペーストしたりする時間がない場合でも、単にスクリーンショットを撮っておくだけで、後から該当する内容を簡単に再確認できるようになります。

  • 自動分類と処理： Pixel Screenshotsはキャプチャされたスクリーンショットを自動的に分類し、オンデバイスAIモデルで処理します。
  • 利便性の向上： 住所、商品情報、旅行の予定など、スクリーンショットとして保存するケースが多いユーザーにとって、従来の一般的なアルバムよりもはるかに便利な機能を提供すると予想されます。

デスクトップ環境への適応とAluminum OS

現在のテスト過程では目立った新機能は見つかっておらず、一般的なスマートフォン版のようにアプリドロワーにエントリーポイントが表示されない点も注目に値します。これはGoogleが優先的に基礎となる構造（Underlying Adaptation）の構築を進めており、インターフェースや操作方法などはまだ完全には準備されていない可能性を示唆しています。

AndroidAuthorityは、Google内部でデスクトップ版のPixel Screenshotsの開発が進んでおり、ターゲットプラットフォームとしてAluminum OSが挙がっていると分析しています。Aluminum OSは噂されているGoogleのデスクトップシステムであり、一般的なノートPCやデスクトップ環境に最適化され、Androidアプリの実行をサポートするのが特徴です。

まとめ

Googleは「Pixel Screenshots」をデスクトップ環境へ拡張するテストを行っており、オンデバイスAIを活用した高度なスクリーンショット管理機能を提供しようとしています。この動きはAluminum OSなどの新プラットフォームを見据えたものであり、モバイルの枠を超えて生産性を高めるツールとしての展開が期待されています。

Google Pixel Screenshots Testing Desktop Expansion

According to reports from IT之家 and AndroidAuthority, Google is reportedly testing the expansion of the "Pixel Screenshots" app to desktop devices. This move indicates that the feature will no longer be exclusive to mobile phones and may serve as a foundation for the upcoming Aluminum OS.

The source identified a specific version code, 1.26.151.00.desktop.release, within the Play Store version of the Pixel Screenshots app. The inclusion of a date set for April 14, 2026, suggests that Google is actively developing a desktop-compatible iteration of the application.

Currently, Pixel Screenshots is a tool provided to Pixel smartphone users to manage captured images. This feature is presently supported only on the Pixel 9 and Pixel 10 series; older Pixel models do not have access to this functionality.

Features and Utility

The core functionality of this app goes beyond simple storage. By utilizing on-device AI, it automatically organizes content, serving as a "searchable archive" where users can find information based on specific cues later on.

According to scenarios shared by Google, if a user finds specific information on a webpage but lacks the time to copy or paste it immediately, they can simply take a screenshot. This allows them to easily retrieve and reference that content at a later time.

  • Automatic Classification and Processing: Pixel Screenshots automatically categorizes captured screenshots using on-device AI models.
  • Enhanced Practicality: For users who frequently save information such as addresses, product details, or travel itineraries via screenshots, this feature is expected to be much more useful than a standard photo gallery.

Desktop Adaptation and Aluminum OS

During the current testing phase, no prominent new features have been discovered, and notably, there is no entry point in the app drawer similar to the mobile version. This suggests that Google may be prioritizing the completion of the underlying infrastructure while the interface and user experience are still being finalized.

AndroidAuthority analyzes that Google is developing a desktop version of Pixel Screenshots internally, with Aluminum OS as the target platform. Aluminum OS is a rumored Google desktop system optimized for laptop and desktop environments that features support for running Android applications.

Summary

Google is expanding its "Pixel Screenshots" tool to desktop environments, potentially integrating it into the rumored Aluminum OS. By leveraging on-device AI to create a searchable archive of captured information, the feature aims to enhance productivity across both mobile and desktop platforms.

Google Pixel Screenshots Testing Desktop Expansion

According to reports from IT之家 and AndroidAuthority, Google is reportedly testing the expansion of the "Pixel Screenshots" app to desktop devices. This move indicates that the feature will no longer be exclusive to mobile phones and may serve as a foundation for the upcoming Aluminum OS.

The source identified a specific version code, 1.26.151.00.desktop.release, within the Play Store version of the Pixel Screenshots app. The inclusion of a date set for April 14, 2026, suggests that Google is actively developing a desktop-compatible iteration of the application.

Currently, Pixel Screenshots is a tool provided to Pixel smartphone users to manage captured images. This feature is presently supported only on the Pixel 9 and Pixel 10 series; older Pixel models do not have access to this functionality.

Features and Utility

The core functionality of this app goes beyond simple storage. By utilizing on-device AI, it automatically organizes content, serving as a "searchable archive" where users can find information based on specific cues later on.

According to scenarios shared by Google, if a user finds specific information on a webpage but lacks the time to copy or paste it immediately, they can simply take a screenshot. This allows them to easily retrieve and reference that content at a later time.

  • Automatic Classification and Processing: Pixel Screenshots automatically categorizes captured screenshots using on-device AI models.
  • Enhanced Practicality: For users who frequently save information such as addresses, product details, or travel itineraries via screenshots, this feature is expected to be much more useful than a standard photo gallery.

Desktop Adaptation and Aluminum OS

During the current testing phase, no prominent new features have been observed, and notably, there is no entry point in the app drawer like the mobile version. This suggests that Google may be prioritizing "underlying adaptation," meaning the backend infrastructure is being built while the interface and user experience are still being finalized.

AndroidAuthority analysis suggests that the development of a desktop version of Pixel Screenshots is intended to pave the way for Aluminum OS. Aluminum OS is a rumored Google desktop system optimized for laptops and desktops that supports the execution of Android applications. By bringing Pixel Screenshots to the desktop, Google aims to strengthen its ecosystem in the productivity sector.

Summary

Google is testing the expansion of "Pixel Screenshots" to desktop devices, utilizing on-device AI to turn captured images into a searchable archive for information like addresses and travel plans. This initiative is closely linked to the rumored Aluminum OS, signaling Google's intent to bridge mobile utility with desktop productivity. The current development phase focuses on backend infrastructure as part of a broader expansion beyond mobile-only features.
