Product Announcement: Nikon Z 120-300mm f/2.8 TC VR S

According to IT Home, Nikon has announced the development of the "Nikon Z 120-300mm f/2.8 TC VR S," a telephoto zoom lens compatible with full-frame (FX format) mirrorless cameras.

Key Features and Specifications

This lens belongs to Nikon's Z mount S-Line series and is designed to meet the rigorous demands of professional photographers.

  • Model: Nikon Z 120-300mm f/2.8 TC VR S
  • Compatibility: Full Frame / FX format mirrorless cameras
  • Series: S-Line
  • Key Feature: Equipped with a built-in 1.4x Teleconverter
  • Focal Length Expansion: Beyond the standard zoom range, it can expand the usable focal length from 120mm up to 420mm.

Expected Impact and Development Goals

Nikon stated that this lens combines superior optical performance with portability, aiming to support richer creative possibilities in professional fields such as sports photography.

Furthermore, Nikon aims to meet user demands while continuously pursuing new dimensions of optical performance. The goal is to expand the boundaries of video expression and ultimately contribute to the advancement of both photography and video culture.

Summary

The Nikon Z 120-300mm f/2.8 TC VR S is a high-performance telephoto zoom lens featuring a built-in teleconverter and full-frame compatibility, making it an ideal solution for capturing distant subjects in sports and wildlife photography. It demonstrates Nikon's commitment to balancing optical excellence with portability while expanding the capabilities of its flagship lineup.
