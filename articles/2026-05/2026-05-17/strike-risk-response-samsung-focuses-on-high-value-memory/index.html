IT之家에 따르면, Trendforce(集邦咨询)는 5월 15일 보고서를 통해 Samsung Electronics가 5월 21일로 예정된 파업 위험에 대응하기 위해 이미 예방 조치를 취하고 있다고 보도했습니다. 삼성전자는 5월 14일부터 일부 웨이퍼 공장에서 '웜다운(warm-down)'이라는 비상 절차를 가동하며, 신규 웨이퍼 투입을 줄이고 생산 능력을 고부가가치 메모리인 HBM 등으로 집중시키고 있습니다.

‘웜다운’ 절차의 의미와 목적

IT之家는 '웜다운'이 공장이 정전되거나 외부적인 혼란 위험에 직면했을 때 취하는 비상 대응 방식이라고 설명했습니다. 이 과정은 장비를 즉시 멈추게 하는 것이 아니라, 웨이퍼 투입량과 설비 부하를 점진적으로 낮추고 온도나 압력 같은 핵심 공정 변수들을 단계적으로 조정하는 것을 포함합니다.

이는 갑작스러운 가동 중단에 비해 웨이퍼 폐기 및 장비 손상 위험을 줄여주는, 사실상 공장과 생산 라인의 안정성을 보호하기 위한 임시 조치로 해석됩니다. 실제로 반도체 장비가 급정지할 경우 내부의 온도와 압력 균형이 깨져 막대한 웨이퍼 손실을 초래할 수 있기 때문입니다.

생산 중심 이동 및 시장 반응

삼성전자는 생산 중점을 조정하여 DRAM과 HBM처럼 핵심적이고 수익성이 높은 제품의 공급을 최우선으로 확보하고 있습니다. 또한, Apple이나 HP 같은 주요 고객사들은 이미 삼성전자 측에 파업이 미칠 영향에 대해 문의를 해왔으며, HBM 관련 고객들 역시 시장 상황 변화를 면밀히 주시하고 있는 것으로 알려졌습니다.

다만 언론 매체별로 파업 충격에 대한 판단에는 큰 차이가 있습니다. 일부 매체는 반도체 생산 라인이 전면적으로 멈출 경우 최대 손실액이 수십조 원에 달할 수 있다고 경고하는 반면, 다른 분석에서는 메모리 투입량이 5%만 둔화되어도 분기 매출에 미치는 영향은 상대적으로 적을 것이라는 상반된 의견도 제시되고 있습니다.

총평삼성전자는 임박한 파업 위험에 대비하여 생산 라인의 안정성을 최우선으로 확보하기 위해 '웜다운'과 같은 선제적 비상 절차를 가동하고 있습니다. 이로 인해 단기적으로 신규 웨이퍼 투입을 줄이고, DRAM 및 HBM 등 고수익 제품군으로 생산 초점을 이동시키는 전략을 취하고 있습니다. 시장의 불확실성이 높은 상황 속에서 삼성전자는 핵심 메모리 부문 공급망 안정화에 집중하며 리스크를 관리하는 모습입니다.

Samsung 防御性生产调整

为应对 5 月 21 日可能发生的罢工风险，Samsung 已于 5 月 14 日在部分晶圆厂启动“warm-down”预防性降载。该措施并非立即停机，而是通过降低投片和机台负载，并逐步调整温度、压力等关键参数，以保护设备稳定性并减少因突然断电或停工导致的晶圆报废风险。

在生产策略上，Samsung 正在采取以下行动：

  • 减少新晶圆投片。
  • 将产能进一步转向 HBM 等高价值、高利润的内存产品。

物流与设备保护措施

为了确保核心产线的安全，Samsung 在平泽园区采取了具体的预防性动作：

  • 在 DRAM 产线专用物流系统中，已将约 15,000 个晶圆存储舱移出。

客户影响与经济风险评估

由于罢工可能带来的不确定性，Apple、HP 等大客户以及 HBM 相关客户均在密切关注局势并询问相关影响。针对罢工造成的潜在损失，不同媒体给出了不同的预测：

  • Money Today 预测：若产线全面停摆，损失最高可达 10 万亿韩元；若受到法院禁令限制，损失约为 1-2 万亿韩元。
  • Chosun Biz 观点：即便投片量放缓 5%，对单季营收的影响可能低于 1 万亿韩元（Samsung 单季度总销售额约为 23 万亿韩元）。

总结

Samsung 为应对 5 月下旬的罢工风险，提前启动“warm-down”预防性降载并强化 HBM 等高价值产品的生产。此举旨在通过降低负载和优化物流安排来保护核心产线稳定。尽管市场对罢工造成的具体经济损失估算不一，但 Samsung 已采取主动措施以保障关键业务的连续性。

「ウォームダウン」手順の意味と目的

IT之家は、「ウォームダウン」が停電や外部的な混乱のリスクに直面した際に講じられる緊急対応策であると説明しています。このプロセスは、装置を即座に停止させるのではなく、ウェハーの投入量と設備の負荷を段階的に下げ、温度や圧力といった主要な工程変数を調整するものです。

これは急な稼働停止と比較して、ウェハーの廃棄や装置の損傷リスクを低減し、工場および生産ラインの安定性を保護するための暫定的な措置と解釈されます。半導体装置が急停止した場合、内部の温度と圧力のバランスが崩れ、膨大な量のウェハ損失を招く可能性があるためです。

まとめ

Samsung Electronics Preemptively Adjusts Production Amid Strike Risks

According to a report from Trendforce via IT Home on May 15, Samsung Electronics is taking preemptive measures to address potential strike risks scheduled for May 21. Starting May 14, several wafer plants have initiated an emergency "warm-down" procedure. This involves reducing the intake of new wafers and concentrating production capacity on high-value memory products such as HBM.

Meaning and Purpose of the 'Warm-down' Procedure

IT Home explains that a "warm-down" is an emergency response protocol used when a factory faces risks such as power outages or external disruptions. Rather than stopping equipment abruptly, this process involves gradually reducing wafer input and equipment load while systematically adjusting key process variables like temperature and pressure.

This is interpreted as a temporary measure to protect the stability of factories and production lines by minimizing the risk of wafer waste and equipment damage during an unplanned shutdown. If semiconductor equipment stops suddenly, the balance of internal temperature and pressure can be disrupted, leading to significant losses of wafers.

Shift in Production Focus and Market Reaction

Samsung Electronics is prioritizing the supply of high-demand, profitable products by adjusting its production focus. Key target products include:

  • DRAM
  • HBM

Major customers, including Apple and HP, have already inquired about the potential impact of the strike, while HBM-related clients are closely monitoring changes in market conditions.

However, there is a significant divide among media outlets regarding the expected impact of the strike. The following perspectives were reported:

  • Some reports warn that a total shutdown of production lines could result in losses reaching tens of trillions of won.
  • Other analyses suggest that even if memory input slows by only 5%, the impact on quarterly revenue would be relatively minor.

Summary

Samsung Electronics is implementing "warm-down" procedures to safeguard its production lines against potential strike risks starting May 21. By reducing new wafer intake and prioritizing high-margin products like DRAM and HBM, the company aims to stabilize its supply chain amidst market uncertainty. While some experts fear massive losses from a full shutdown, others believe the impact on quarterly revenue may be limited.

「ウォームダウン」とは、停電や外部的な混乱のリスクに直面した際に講じられる緊急対応策です。このプロセスは装置を即座に停止させるのではなく、ウェハーの投入量と設備の負荷を段階的に下げ、温度や圧力といった主要な工程変数を調整するものです。

これは急な稼働停止と比較して、ウェハーの廃棄や装置の損傷リスクを低減し、工場および生産ラインの安定性を保護するための措置です。半導体装置が急停止した場合、内部の温度と圧力のバランスが崩れ、膨大な量のウェハ損失を招く可能性があるためです。

生産拠点のシフトと物流・設備保護

Samsungは生産の重点を調整することで、DRAMやHBMといった核心的で収益性の高い製品の供給を最優先で確保しています。また、ピョンタンク（Pyeongtaek）拠点では以下の具体的な予防措置が取られています。

  • DRAM生産ライン専用の物流システムにおいて、約15,000個のウェハー保管容器を移動。

顧客への影響と経済的リスクの評価

AppleやHPなどの主要顧客、およびHBM関連の顧客は、ストライキが及ぼす影響についてSamsungに問い合わせを行っており、市場状況の変化を注視しています。なお、ストライキによる潜在的な損失についてはメディアによって予測が分かれています。

  • 一部の報道：生産ラインが全面的に停止した場合、最大で数兆ウォン（数十兆ウォン）に達する可能性があると警告。
  • 別の分析：ウェハー投入量がわずか5%減速した程度であれば、四半期売上への影響は1兆ウォン未満にとどまるという見解。

まとめ

Samsungは5月21日のストライキに備え、生産ラインの安定性を確保するための「ウォームダウン」措置を導入しています。この戦略により、新規ウェハーの投入を抑制しつつDRAMやHBMといった高付加価値製品へのリソース集中を図ることで、不確実な市場環境におけるリスク管理を行っています。

Samsung Electronics Preemptively Adjusts Production Amid Strike Risks

According to a report from Trendforce via IT Home on May 15, Samsung Electronics is taking preemptive measures to address potential strike risks scheduled for May 21. Starting May 14, several wafer plants initiated an emergency "warm-down" procedure. This strategy involves reducing the intake of new wafers and concentrating production capacity on high-value memory products such as HBM.

The Purpose of 'Warm-down' Procedures

A "warm-down" is an emergency response protocol used when a factory faces risks such as power outages or external disruptions. Rather than stopping equipment abruptly, this process involves gradually reducing wafer input and equipment load while systematically adjusting key process variables like temperature and pressure.

This is interpreted as a protective measure to ensure the stability of production lines by minimizing the risk of wafer waste and equipment damage during an unplanned shutdown. If semiconductor equipment stops suddenly, the balance of internal temperature and pressure can be disrupted, leading to significant losses of wafers.

Shift in Production Focus and Market Reaction

Samsung is prioritizing the supply of high-demand, profitable products by adjusting its production focus. Key target products include:

  • DRAM
  • HBM

Major customers, including Apple and HP, have already inquired about the potential impact of the strike, while HBM-related clients are closely monitoring changes in market conditions.

Logistics and Economic Risk Assessment

To ensure the safety of core production lines at its Pyeongtaek site, Samsung has taken specific preventive actions:

  • Moved approximately 15,000 wafer storage bins out of the dedicated DRAM line logistics system.

Media outlets offer conflicting views regarding the potential economic impact of a strike:

  • Some reports warn that a total shutdown of production lines could result in losses reaching up to 10 trillion won (or 1-2 trillion won if restricted by court injunctions).
  • Other analyses suggest that even if wafer input slows by only 5%, the impact on quarterly revenue would be less than 1 trillion won (given Samsung's total quarterly sales of approximately 23 trillion won).

Summary

Samsung Electronics is implementing "warm-down" procedures and prioritizing high-value products like DRAM and HBM to mitigate risks from a potential strike on May 21. These measures are designed to protect equipment integrity and ensure supply chain stability during periods of uncertainty. While the exact financial impact remains debated, Samsung's proactive stance focuses on safeguarding its core manufacturing operations.
